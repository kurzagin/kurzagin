## Volume 1: The Sky-Sea & The Oblivious Current

| Chapter | Title | Type | Summary | File |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00** | Prologue: Where the Blue Sky Drowns | Prologue | Tsubasa Kaidō introduces the strange, breathtaking reality of Shiozaki, where the ocean does not end at the horizon but spills into the sky. Accompanied by his fiery childhood friend Matsuri Isaki on their steep morning climb to Kaiyō High School, the town's wildlife and weather forecast an extraordinary atmospheric shift before an ancient, fifty-meter Celestial Humpback breaches through the cloud canopy above them. | [prologue.md](./volume-01/prologue.md) |
| **01** | Formaldehyde, Fish Scales, and Disciplinary Committees | Chapter | Tsubasa and Matsuri dash through the school gates just in time, running into the stern Student Council President Shiori Ryūgū, whose hidden seasickness Tsubasa diagnoses with clinical precision. After surviving classes and dissecting Matsuri's homemade bento with reverent ichthyological analysis, Tsubasa retreats to the Marine Specimen Club, where club president Chihaya Kujirazaki is obsessed with analyzing the morning's whale mist—leading to an explosive academic clash and a foreboding rumor from the coastline. | [chapter-01.md](./volume-01/chapter-01.md) |
