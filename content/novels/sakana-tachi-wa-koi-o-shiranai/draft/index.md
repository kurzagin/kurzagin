## Volume 1: The Sky-Sea & The Oblivious Current

| Chapter | Title | Type | Summary | File |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00** | Prologue: Where the Blue Sky Drowns | Prologue | Tsubasa Kaidō introduces the strange, breathtaking reality of Shiozaki, where the ocean does not end at the horizon but spills into the sky. Accompanied by his fiery childhood friend Matsuri Isaki on their steep morning climb to Kaiyō High School, the town's wildlife and weather forecast an extraordinary atmospheric shift before an ancient, fifty-meter Celestial Humpback breaches through the cloud canopy above them. | [prologue.md](./volume-01/prologue.md) |
| **01** | Formaldehyde, Fish Scales, and Disciplinary Committees | Chapter | Tsubasa and Matsuri reach the school gates just ahead of the morning bell, intercepted by Student Council President Shiori Ryūgū over uniform infractions and harbor smells. In navigation class, Tsubasa corrects the homeroom proctor's atmospheric models before enduring Matsuri's volatile bento critique. After school, Tsubasa heads to the Marine Specimen Club, where an empirical clash with club president Chihaya Kujirazaki over celestial whale vapor is interrupted by the quiet arrival of Umika Minagi. | [chapter-01.md](./volume-01/chapter-01.md) |
| **02** | The Driftwood Girl and the Benthic Anomaly | Chapter | Umika Minagi's arrival with an abyssal ghost-hermit crab sparks an academic clash between Chihaya and Student Council President Shiori Ryūgū, who places Umika under club custody to investigate deep-water signs. The ideological fight between shrine ritual and laboratory science is shattered when a deep hydrogen sulfide upwelling turns the school flume crimson, trapping a decompressed 7-meter giant oarfish and threatening Shiozaki Bay. | [chapter-02.md](./volume-01/chapter-02.md) |
