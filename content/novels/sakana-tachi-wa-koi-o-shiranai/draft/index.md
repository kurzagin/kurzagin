## Volume 1: The Sky-Sea & The Oblivious Current

| Chapter | Title | Type | Summary | File |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00** | Prologue: Where the Blue Sky Drowns | Prologue | Tsubasa Kaidō introduces the strange, breathtaking reality of Shiozaki, where the ocean does not end at the horizon but spills into the sky. Accompanied by his fiery childhood friend Matsuri Isaki on their steep morning climb to Kaiyō High School, the town's wildlife and weather forecast an extraordinary atmospheric shift before an ancient, fifty-meter Celestial Humpback breaches through the cloud canopy above them. | [prologue.md](./volume-01/prologue.md) |
| **01** | Formaldehyde, Fish Scales, and Disciplinary Committees | Chapter | Tsubasa and Matsuri dash through the school gates just in time, running into the stern Student Council President Shiori Ryūgū, whose hidden seasickness Tsubasa diagnoses with clinical precision. After surviving classes and dissecting Matsuri's homemade bento with reverent ichthyological analysis, Tsubasa retreats to the Marine Specimen Club, where club president Chihaya Kujirazaki's analysis of whale mist sparks an explosive academic clash—interrupted by the barefoot arrival of Umika Minagi, the mysterious sea-drifter Tsubasa found weeks ago. | [chapter-01.md](./volume-01/chapter-01.md) |
| **02** | The Driftwood Girl and the Clubroom Storm | Chapter | Umika Minagi's unannounced arrival in Preservation Lab 3 sends Chihaya Kujirazaki into an academic fury, which only worsens when Student Council President Shiori Ryūgū arrives with official transfer papers. When Matsuri Isaki barges in with harbor tackle, the basement lab turns into a battleground of clashing egos—until a strange, bioluminescent signal from Umika's bucket and the harbor aqueducts hints at a disturbance in the benthic depths. | [chapter-02.md](./volume-01/chapter-02.md) |
