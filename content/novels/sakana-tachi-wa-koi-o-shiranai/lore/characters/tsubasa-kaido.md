**Tsubasa Kaidō (海道 翼)** is a second-year student at Shiozaki Prefectural Kaiyō High School. While his grades in standard humanities subjects hover just above failing due to dozing off in class, his practical mastery of marine biology, fishing craftsmanship, and celestial navigation surpasses even the academy's tenured faculty.

## Personality & Habits
- **Marine Savant:** Has spent his mornings since childhood working the 5:00 AM auction docks with veteran harbor fishermen. He can gut a bonito in four seconds, tie fluorocarbon knots in complete darkness, and identify deep-sea fish solely by scent or scale texture.
- **Romantic Radar of Zero:** Completely incapable of recognizing romantic tension. When a girl blushes around him, he assumes she has low blood sugar, mild heat stroke, or an allergic reaction to coastal plankton.
- **Fish Metaphors:** Has an unfortunate habit of complimenting girls by comparing their features to marine organisms:
  - *"Your complexion has the pristine silver iridescence of a freshly caught Japanese Spanish mackerel!"* (promptly resulting in him getting hit with a school bag).
- **The Reliable Protector:** The moment a genuine crisis occurs—such as a runaway sky-whale calf or an incoming riptide—his dorky demeanor vanishes into calm, authoritative leadership.

## Special Abilities
- **Acoustic Mimicry:** Can mimic low-frequency cetacean clicks and whale calls using hollow sea-snail shells, calming agitated sky-whales. (This was the acoustic signal that drew the drifting Umika to shore at Moon-Crescent Bay before he rescued her).
- **Swell Prediction:** Detects minute barometric drops and atmospheric salinity shifts hours before coastal sirens sound.

## Quotes
- *"Why are you standing in the breeze with flushed cheeks like that? Based on the humidity and your breathing rate, your core body temperature has spiked. Here, have an iced seaweed gel pack."*
- *"People say the ocean is chaotic and terrifying, but fish are honest. If you respect their currents, they never betray you. Humans... are considerably more baffling."*
