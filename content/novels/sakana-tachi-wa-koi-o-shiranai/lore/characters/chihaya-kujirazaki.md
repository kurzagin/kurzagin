**Chihaya Kujirazaki (鯨崎 千早)** is an elegant third-year honors student, club president, and the granddaughter of the founder of the Kujirazaki Marine Science Institute and luxury oceanarium chain.

## Personality & Archetype
- **The Pompous Scholar:** Stylish red-rimmed glasses, pristine white lab coat worn over her uniform, and a clipboard filled with meticulous statistical charts. She prides herself on scoring 100% on national academic marine taxonomy exams.
- **The Chronic Inferiority Crisis:** Deeply exasperated that Tsubasa—a slacker who sleeps through civics and has never published a peer-reviewed paper—consistently out-identifies, out-navigates, and solves real-world marine emergencies that baffle her computerized scientific models.
- **Desperate for his Validation:** Convinced she can "civilize" Tsubasa into an elite research assistant. She repeatedly corners him for "after-school tutoring sessions" in the dimly lit specimen laboratory, only to get flustered whenever Tsubasa leans in close to inspect the slides she prepared.

## Comedy Highlights
- Frequently pushes up her glasses with theatrical flair right before making an incorrect diagnosis, which Tsubasa immediately debunks with an offhand harbor tip.
- Secretly keeps a journal ranking all of Tsubasa's habits, rating his fish-dissecting form an "A+" and his social decorum an "F-minus".

## Quotes
- *"Listen closely, Kaidō-kun! Real ichthyology is a discipline of rigorous data and peer-reviewed journals, not crude harbor superstition! ...Now explain to me why your hand-tied nylon feather caught twelve flying snappers while my five-hundred-thousand-yen sonar rig detected nothing!"*
- *"I am merely observing your biometrics for scientific research! My elevated pulse is an autonomic response to formaldehyde exposure! Do not flatter yourself!"*
