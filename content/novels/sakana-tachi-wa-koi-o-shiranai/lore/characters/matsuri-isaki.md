**Matsuri Isaki (伊佐木 茉莉)** is Tsubasa's lifelong neighbor, classmate, and the fiercely energetic daughter of Shiozaki's harbor union chieftain. 

## Personality & Archetype
- **Harbor Tomboy:** Sun-kissed skin, perpetually rolled-up uniform sleeves, and sporting a lucky braided fishing line around her wrist. She can steer a diesel skiff through heavy chop with one hand and toss mooring ropes with pinpoint precision.
- **Childhood Bond:** Has fished side-by-side with Tsubasa since they were toddlers. To everyone in the harbor, they resemble an old married couple whose banter sounds like squabbling seagulls.
- **The Romantic Meltdown:** While she has zero problem punching Tsubasa's shoulder or dragging him by his collar, she completely short-circuits whenever the mood turns feminine or tender. If Tsubasa touches her hands to adjust a lure knot, she burns crimson, drops her tackle box, and yells nonsense before running away.

## Comedy Highlights
- Wakes Tsubasa up by throwing wet dried-squid snacks at his bedroom window from her adjacent balcony.
- Repeatedly cooks handmade lunchboxes for him featuring whole grilled sea bream heads to show off her domestic skills, only for Tsubasa to dissect the anatomical otoliths (ear bones) with admiration while ignoring her blushing face.
