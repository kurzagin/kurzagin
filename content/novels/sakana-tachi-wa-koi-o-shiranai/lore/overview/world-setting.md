The town of **Shiozaki (潮崎町)** is a picturesque coastal enclave situated along a dramatic rias coastline. Famous for its terraced hillside streets, bustling dawn fish markets, and cliff-hanging stone staircases, Shiozaki exists at the convergence point of earthly currents and the supernatural **Celestial Tides (*Tenkai-ryū*)**.

## Daily Life Beneath the Sky-Sea
In Shiozaki, seeing marine megafauna swim through low-hanging coastal cloud banks is an ordinary fact of life. Whales, schools of glass-squid, and floating manta rays drift across the sky during humid mornings, propelled by buoyant vapor thermals. 

Locals take this in stride:
- Street vendors sell vapor-tackle lures alongside fresh takoyaki.
- Weather forecasts broadcast both atmospheric pressure and **Aerial Tide Density (*Tenkai-shio*)**, alerting laundry owners when floating jellyfish drifts are predicted overhead.
- Town roofs are reinforced with traditional marine timber to endure accidental brushings from drifting sunfish.

## Shiozaki Prefectural Kaiyō High School (海陽高校)
Perched dramatically atop the highest sea bluff overlooking the bay, **Kaiyō High School** is Japan's premier coastal institute. 

The campus features:
- **Sea-Facing Tiered Classrooms:** Windows designed with wide sliding panes so students and instructors can track passing sky-currents during lectures.
- **Campus Aqueducts:** Seawater canals routed through courtyards to nurture live specimens for classroom observation.
- **Rooftop Sky-Pier:** Replaces the standard athletic field observation deck, allowing the school's maritime clubs to cast practice lines and moor sky-skiffs.
- **Integrated Curriculum:** Alongside standard Japanese high school subjects (Mathematics, Modern Japanese, English, Civics), students must pass specialized coursework in:
  - *Marine Biology & Taxonomy*
  - *Celestial Current Navigation*
  - *Maritime Shinto Rituals & Harbor Safety*
  - *Tackle Engineering & Net Maintenance*
