Because celestial marine creatures swim through moisture-laden vapor banks rather than liquid ocean water, standard heavy lead sinkers and conventional nylon lines are useless. Instead, Shiozaki's anglers and students use specialized **Cloud-Fishing Tackle (雲釣具)**.

## Core Components
- **Vapor-Buoyant Floats (*Kiri-Uki*):** Crafted from ultra-lightweight hollow balsa wood and aerogel, these bobbers suspend baits precisely within the moisture layer of a passing cloud.
- **Micro-Fluorocarbon Cloud Lines:** Extra-thin, high-tensile braided lines treated with a hydrophobic silicone coating that glides through wind currents without catching drag.
- **Barbless Feather Lures:** In accordance with town regulations and Shinto sanctification laws, all hooks used within municipal airspace must be barbless and wrapped in natural peacock and mackerel quill fibers to ensure catch-and-release safety.

## School Practical Exams
- High school students are evaluated on their casting arcs from the school's elevated rooftop docks. Tsubasa's homemade custom hand-wrapped rigs are legendary on campus, capable of hitting a moving cloud-swell seventy meters out with pinpoint accuracy.
