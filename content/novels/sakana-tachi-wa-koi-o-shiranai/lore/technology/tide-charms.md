The **Sea-Glass Talismans (*Shiogarasu Omamori*)** are traditional Shinto amulets made from fragments of colorful glass polished smooth by centuries of ocean surf along Shiozaki's rocky coves.

## The Torii Ribbon Ritual
The town's most famous romantic superstition revolves around the submerged vermilion torii of Ryūgū Shrine:
- During the **Summer Equinox Spring Low Tide (*Ōshio*)**, the ocean recedes for exactly ninety minutes, opening a narrow sandbar path to the torii's stone foundations.
- Local high school lore dictates that if two people tie a sea-glass charm bound with a twin-braided silk ribbon to the torii's beam before the rising tide submerges it again, their fates will remain bound no matter where the ocean currents carry them.

## The Harem Scramble
- Every summer, the rumor of who Tsubasa will accompany across the sandbar causes utter mayhem across the student body, with Shiori, Matsuri, Chihaya, and Umika all preparing their own custom charms while Tsubasa thinks they're just inviting him to gather hermit crabs.
