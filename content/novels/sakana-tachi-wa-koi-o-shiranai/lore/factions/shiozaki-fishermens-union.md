The **Shiozaki Fishermen's Guild (潮崎漁業協同組合)** is the economic and cultural backbone of the port town, housing hundreds of veteran captains, line-fishermen, and seafood wholesalers.

## Daily Routine & Traditions
- **The 5:00 AM Fish Auction:** The energetic center of town life, where flashing hand signals, ice-covered wooden crates, and auction bells echo through the mist.
- **Ebisu Traditions:** Guild members strictly adhere to archaic fishermen's taboos—never speak of foxes aboard ship, always pour a cup of sake into the harbor before departing for distant banks, and give the first catch of every season to the sea.

## Relationship with Tsubasa Kaidō
- Tsubasa is practically an honorary junior boss among the grizzled dock hands. While other teenagers sleep until 7:30 AM, Tsubasa has been helping unhook nets, repair engine impellers, and sort mackerel on the wet concrete since he was seven.
- The fishermen constantly tease Tsubasa and Matsuri, treating their marriage as an inevitable harbor event.
