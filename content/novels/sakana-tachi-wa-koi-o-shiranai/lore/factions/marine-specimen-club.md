The **Marine Specimen & Ecology Club (海洋標本生態部)** is officially dedicated to cataloging and preserving rare oceanic and celestial life forms discovered along the Shiozaki coastline.

## The Clubroom Atmosphere
Located in the cool, subterranean ground level of the old school wing, the clubroom is filled with:
- Giant glass formaldehyde specimen jars holding glowing deep-sea squid, preserved oarfish jaws, and fossilized sky-whale barnacles.
- High-powered electron microscopes and dissecting trays where Chihaya attempts serious scientific research.
- A worn leather couch where Tsubasa naps after early dock shifts, and where Umika sits watching dust motes floating in sunlight.
- Stacks of bait buckets, fishing tackle catalogs, and half-eaten bento boxes left by Matsuri.

## The Harem Battleground
Despite its academic mandate, the club functions as the primary battleground where the heroines vie for Tsubasa's attention:
- Chihaya demands private research sessions.
- Matsuri barges in with homemade harbor snacks under the pretense of "checking on the bait."
- Shiori uses Student Council club budget audits as an excuse to inspect the premises every afternoon.
- Umika silently curls up next to Tsubasa, causing everyone else's composure to collapse.
