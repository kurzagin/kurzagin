**Shiozaki Prefectural Kaiyō High School (海陽高校)** is a storied secondary academy established during the Meiji era to bridge traditional coastal maritime lore with modern navigation and marine sciences.

## Campus Architecture
- **The Cliffside Terrace:** Built directly into the sea crags, featuring balconies overlooking both the harbor waters below and the open cloudways above.
- **The Sky-Mooring Spire:** A tall clock tower equipped with mooring cables for emergency rescue skiffs and sky-buoys.
- **Saltwater Aqueduct Atrium:** An open-air garden where fresh coastal water constantly circulates through tiered pools holding native tide pool creatures and flora.

## Unique School Traditions
- **Sky-Catch Practical Exam:** Held every autumn; students must successfully identify, track, and hook a non-lethal celestial mackerel from the school roof within a twenty-minute window.
- **The Sea-Glass Ribbon Exchange:** An informal romantic tradition where third-year seniors offer polished tide-glass charms to their crushes before graduation to ensure the sea guides their futures back together.
