**Otohime Ryūgū Shrine (乙姫竜宮神社)** is an ancient Shinto sanctuary perched on the jagged crags of Dragon-Gate Promontory. For over eight centuries, the shrine has served as the spiritual beacon of Shiozaki's fishing fleets and the celestial currents.

## Sacred Landmarks
- **The Submerged Vermilion Torii:** Standing forty meters offshore in the bay, the iconic torii gate is submerged to its crossbeams at high tide and accessible by foot across a rocky tombolo sandbar only during spring neap lows.
- **The Bell of the Tide-Dragon:** Cast from ancient sea-bronze, its resonance carries across low-frequency sound channels, soothing celestial whales and signaling changes in atmospheric salinity.

## Ceremonial Roles
- **The Great Tide Blessing (*Ōshio Shinji*):** Conducted at each new moon to ensure passing sky-whales do not disrupt coastal navigation or harbor shipping lanes.
- **Tide-Glass Purification:** The shrine distributes consecrated sea-glass charms believed to protect fishermen from squalls and bring destined lovers together across changing tides.
- **Sanctuary of the *Yoriko*:** In accordance with ancient maritime covenants, castaways or children gifted by the sea (*Yoriko*) are granted divine sanctuary and legal sponsorship by the Ryūgū clan (such as the recent enrollment of Umika Minagi at Kaiyō High School).
