Known scientifically as the Giant Oarfish, the **Ryūgū no Tsukai (竜宮の使い / Messenger of the Dragon Palace)** is a ribbon-like deep-sea teleost reaching up to eight meters in length, crowned with a brilliant crimson dorsal crest and shimmering silver scales.

## Mythic Significance in Shiozaki
- In ancient Shiozaki folklore, this creature never surfaces without purpose. Its arrival in the shallow reefs or low-hanging mist signifies a seismic shift in the **Celestial Currents**, an impending typhoon, or an omen that the Dragon Palace is calling an emissary back to the depths.
- Coastal Shinto rites dictate that whenever an oarfish is sighted alive near the submerged torii, the shrine maiden must perform the sacred purification dance before sunset.

## In the Story
- While superstitious townspeople panic and gather at the seawalls to pray, Tsubasa approaches the magnificent creature with calm reverence, examining its gill condition and dorsal spines to determine which deep abyssal tremor forced it to ascend.
