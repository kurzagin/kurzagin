The **Phosphorescent Cloud-Jellyfish (*Amatsukurage* / 天津水母)** are translucent, saucer-sized cnidarians that drift along low-altitude coastal thermal layers when summer humidity reaches 85% or higher.

## Characteristics & Glow
- **Living Lanterns:** They emit a soft cyan and pale lilac bioluminescence, creating ethereal, floating starry nights whenever dense fog rolls into Shiozaki's harbor streets.
- **Vapor Stings:** While they possess no venomous tentacles, their bells release microscopic salt-crystals that cause harmless tingling and itchy red bumps on exposed skin.

## School Hazard & Romance Catalyst
- Weather forecasts frequently issue **Jellyfish Drifting Advisories**, prompting students to bring clear umbrella domes or hooded rain ponchos to school.
- During unexpected sudden drifts between classes, sharing an umbrella or huddling under school blazers to dodge the falling medusae is a notorious catalyst for romantic tension—and fierce disputes among Tsubasa's harem.
