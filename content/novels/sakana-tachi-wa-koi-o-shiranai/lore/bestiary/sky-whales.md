The **Celestial Humpback (*Amanokujira* / 天乃鯨)** is the most magnificent and iconic creature of Shiozaki's skies. Measuring between thirty to fifty meters in length, these gentle giants glide effortlessly through atmospheric rivers and dense cumulus cloud banks.

## Biology & Buoyancy
- **Aerostatic Baleen:** Possesses microscopic chambered gas sacks within its blubber, allowing it to regulate buoyancy in response to changes in air humidity.
- **Vapor Plumes:** When an Amanokujira breaches through a cloud layer, it vents warm, sea-salted mist that creates temporary localized rainbows across the school courtyards.
- **Low-Frequency Songs:** Their resonant calls travel across tens of kilometers through the moisture currents, sounding like deep cello chords vibrating in the chest of anyone standing on open cliffs.

## Daily School Impact
- Under academy regulations, sky-whales have the complete right-of-way. If an Amanokujira pod passes directly over the sports field or classroom spires, outdoor gym classes are paused so students do not cast stray tennis balls or sky-nets into their flukes.
- Tsubasa is one of the few individuals capable of communicating with lost juvenile calves by blowing into a conch shell at specific harmonic intervals.
