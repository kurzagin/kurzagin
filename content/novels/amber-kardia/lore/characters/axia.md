**Axia** is a specialized **Guardian Class Android** engineered by **[The Professor](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/the-professor.md)**. Designed with a smaller, more compact chassis than standard combat automatons, she was officially designated by her creator as **[Amber's](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/amber.md)** younger sister.

Because Amber's existence is a tightly guarded secret that must never be exposed beyond the estate, Axia serves as The Professor's primary personal bodyguard whenever he is forced to travel outside the mansion for engineering consultations, materials trading, or client negotiations with figures like **[Ms. Atea](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/atea.md)**.

## The Field Guardian: Protecting The Professor Outside

Unlike Amber, who is permanently confined to the mansion to hide her impossible 5-Cubereum core from the world, Axia operates regularly in public:

- **Public Pretext:** To outside observers, corporate inspectors, and clients like **[Organicosys](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/factions/organicosys.md)**, Axia appears to be nothing more than a standard, commercially legitimate Guardian Class unit purchased or constructed by an aging engineer for personal defense. Her standard 1-Cubereum core does not trigger high-density energy scanners, allowing her to move through urban checkpoints without raising suspicion.
- **Vigilant Escort for a Dying Master:** Axia is keenly aware that The Professor is an old man whose time is running out. Knowing that his body is fragile and his days are numbered, Axia's protective instincts border on obsessive. She never leaves his side during excursions, scanning every passerby and acoustic frequency, determined to ensure that not a single scratch, indignity, or ounce of stress accelerates his fading life.

## Dual Demeanor: Loving Sister and Berserker Bodyguard

Axia possesses one of the most stark behavioral divides among the mansion's android retinue:

### The Affectionate Little Sister
Within the safety of the ruined mansion, Axia displays a cheerful, affectionate, and clingy personality toward Amber and The Professor. She actively seeks Amber's praise, follows her through the corridors while Amber performs her dusting duties, and happily accepts maintenance and oil calibrations from her elder sister. To Amber, Axia's childlike attachment is another fascinating puzzle in her pursuit of understanding family and emotional bonds.

### The Short-Fused Brawler
In contrast to her domestic warmth, Axia's core programming incorporates a hyper-volatile threat-response algorithm:
- The moment a weapon is drawn, hostile intent is detected, or an outsider speaks condescendingly toward The Professor, Axia's demeanor flips completely.
- Her sweet cadence vanishes instantly, replaced by a foul-mouthed, bloodthirsty combat fury. She takes any insult directed at The Professor as a personal offense worthy of immediate kinetic obliteration, shouting verbal threats and diving headfirst into hand-to-hand brawls without a shred of hesitation.
- More than once, The Professor has had to physically restrain Axia by her collar to prevent her from beating disrespectful corporate couriers or underworld clients into scrap metal in broad daylight.

---

## Sisterly Dynamic with Amber

- **Mutual Devotion:** Axia deeply respects Amber as the heart of the household. Although she recognizes that Amber is vastly more powerful due to her 5-Cubereum matrix, Axia takes immense pride in her role as the "outdoor protector," boasting that she guards the family out in the harsh world while Amber keeps the hearth safe.
- **Protective Jealousy:** Axia is fiercely defensive of Amber's safety. She strictly enforces the secrecy of the estate, routinely double-checking perimeter sensors to ensure no curious trespasser catches a glimpse of her older sister through the mansion's barred windows.
