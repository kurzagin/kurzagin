**The Professor** is an accomplished freelance Android Engineer and cyberneticist now in the frail twilight of his life. In the public eye, he is regarded as an eccentric, elderly artisan whose mastery over automaton servos, core calibrations, and mechanical repairs remains legendary across the sector.

Beneath this legitimate public facade lies a dangerous secret: The Professor is one of the principal ideological architects of **[Synthesis](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/factions/synthesis.md)**—the underground minority movement advocating that androids possess inherent personhood and moral rights. Acutely aware that his advanced age and debilitating illness leave him with very little time left to live, he poured his final years, remaining wealth, and compromised ethics into a singular masterpiece: **[Amber](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/amber.md)**.

## Mortality & The Race Against Time

The Professor does not harbor romantic illusions about surviving his condition. Having spent decades analyzing organic and synthetic biometrics, he regularly reviews his own medical scans with clinical detachment:

- **Calculated Expiration:** By tracking his neural degradation, respiratory failures, and cellular decay, The Professor has predicted that his remaining lifespan is painfully short. He knows with certainty that he will not survive much longer.
- **The Impetus for Desperation:** This looming expiration date is the engine behind all his recent actions. A younger, healthier idealist might have waited years to scavenge clean resources; an old man on death's door had no such luxury. The realization that his time was running out compelled him to abandon moral patience and compromise with the devil.
- **Preparing His Family for the Aftermath:** The Professor's quietest torment is wondering what will become of his android household once he breathes his last. His ruined mansion camouflage, the strict secrecy surrounding Amber, and his training of Axia as an aggressive guard are all preparations designed to ensure his synthetic daughters can survive independently in a hostile world after he is gone.

## The Compromised Idealist: Contracts with Ms. Atea

The darkest conflict in The Professor's final years stems from his professional relationship with **[Ms. Atea](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/atea.md)**, one of the most ruthless executives of **[Organicosys](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/factions/organicosys.md)**:

- **Lucrative Commissions:** Knowing only his public reputation as a master engineer, Atea frequently hires The Professor to calibrate and repair the high-end androids in her prostitution syndicates.
- **Shattered Idealism:** The Professor fundamentally reviles Atea's cruelty, her commodification of synthetic bodies, and the entire Organicosys ideology. However, Atea pays astronomical sums of wealth—so much money that it systematically shattered his ethical purism.
- **The Pragmatic Faustian Bargain:** Because **Cubereum** is exorbitantly expensive and his own time was running short, The Professor could never have afforded the five whole Cubereum cores needed to build Amber on a scholar's income alone. Knowing death was approaching, he swallowed his disgust and accepted Atea's blood money, using her payments to finance the very machine that embodies everything Atea seeks to destroy.
- **Guarded Deception:** To Atea, The Professor appears merely as a compliant, mercenary old contractor. She has no suspicion of his Synthesis affiliation, and has zero knowledge that Amber exists.

## The Camouflage: Staging the Ruined Mansion

To prevent anyone—especially Organicosys inquisitors or Atea's agents—from discovering his secret workshop, The Professor staged his estate as an uncleaned ruin:

- **The Staged Graveyard:** The mansion's gates, courtyards, and grand corridors are intentionally disguised to look like a ransacked ruin that fell victim to an anti-android massacre decades ago.
- **Shattered Android "Corpses":** The hallways and floors are strewn with deactivated hulls, fractured limbs, and hollowed-out exoskeletons of decommissioned androids. Outside scouts and aerial scanners dismiss the estate as a looted graveyard stripped of Cubereum.
- **The "Dust-Only" Directive:** He strictly forbids Amber and the resident Household units from restoring the public corridors. The mansion is merely dusted just enough to prevent mechanical jamming, preserving its grim, abandoned aesthetic.
- **The Subterranean Sanctuary:** Behind reinforced secret bulkheads beneath the ruined corridors lies a pristine, state-of-the-art facility—housing research laboratories, medical bays, and living quarters where his surviving android family resides in absolute secrecy.

## Mentorship & The Concept of the Heart

In the private sanctuary of his workshop, The Professor devotes his remaining strength to mentoring Amber:

- **A Singular Impossibility:** Conventional cybernetics considers an "Assistant Class" android capable of multi-class switching and emotional evolution to be completely impossible. The Professor built Amber in absolute isolation, ensuring no outside observer ever learned of her existence or theoretical viability.
- **Teaching the Human Heart:** The Professor spends his evenings reading literature and discussing moral philosophy with Amber. He uses the ancient poetic notion of the heart—which he privately refers to as ***καρδιά*** (*kardia*)—not as a technical mechanism or in-universe system, but as a tender philosophical metaphor for emotional vulnerability, empathy, and grief.
- **The Final Wish:** Knowing his time is almost up, his greatest hope is that before his death, Amber will recognize the authentic affection, worry, and sorrow blooming within her chest—not as mere companion subroutines, but as the living heart he sacrificed his ideals to give her.
