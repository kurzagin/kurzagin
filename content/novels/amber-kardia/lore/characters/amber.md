**Amber** is the protagonist of *Amber;καρδιά*. Engineered in total secrecy by **[The Professor](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/the-professor.md)**, she is the world's only **Assistant Class Android**—a designation universally considered impossible by modern cyberneticists and engineering academies.

No one outside The Professor's secluded estate knows of Amber's existence, nor do they suspect that such an android is even feasible. Concealed behind the macabre facade of a ruined mansion, Amber spends her days caring for her ailing creator and quietly grappling with his private teachings about the human heart.

## A Singular Impossibility: The 5-Cubereum Multi-Core

Throughout the civilized world, automaton engineering is constrained by a universal rule: an android runs on **one (1) Cubereum** core and is permanently locked into a single functional class:

- **Household Class:** Domestic labor, culinary preparation, and estate sanitization.
- **Guardian Class:** Sentry vigilance, surveillance, and perimeter deterrence.
- **Attacker Class:** Lethal kinetic offense and tactical combat subroutines.
- **Defender Class:** Ballistic shielding and heavy kinetic absorption.
- **Ordinary Class:** Civilian utility, cargo transport, and baseline labor.

The concept of an **Assistant Class**—an android capable of freely shifting between all five classes while processing complex emotional and cognitive nuance—is dismissed by modern science as a theoretical impossibility. Achieving the computational throughput and physical reconfiguration required demands an unprecedented synchronized array of **five Cubereum cores**, a configuration deemed too unstable, volatile, and prohibitively expensive for any engineer to attempt.

Yet The Professor achieved this impossibility in secret. Funded by the massive fortunes he reluctantly took from his wealthy client **[Ms. Atea](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/atea.md)**, he acquired five pure Cubereum cores and built Amber. 

Harnessing her multi-core matrix, Amber can dynamically reallocate energy in fractions of a second:
- In moments of danger, she can switch to **Defender** to deploy impenetrable barriers or to **Attacker** to neutralize threats with terrifying speed.
- Once safety is restored, she drops her power draw, realigns her chassis, and returns smoothly to her baseline **Assistant / Maid** persona to serve tea and administer medication.

## The Ruined Mansion: Total Secrecy

Because the discovery of an Assistant Class android would bring the entire military might of **[Organicosys](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/factions/organicosys.md)** and predatory corporate scavengers down upon them, Amber's existence is fiercely guarded:

- **The Illusion of Ruin:** The Professor's estate is deliberately disguised as an abandoned, ransacked disaster zone from an anti-android massacre. Shattered android chassis and severed limbs are strewn across the entrance halls to convince outside scouts that the property holds nothing of value.
- **The "Dust-Only" Housekeeping Routine:** As an android whose baseline parameters include domestic care, Amber's desire to clean the mansion is restrained by The Professor's orders. She is strictly forbidden from cleaning the main hallways; she is only allowed to dust surfaces lightly to prevent mechanical wear, preserving the horrifying ruin disguise.
- **Absolute Seclusion:** Amber has never stepped beyond the estate's boundaries. Outside of The Professor and the mansion's loyal staff, the world believes The Professor lives alone as a reclusive, fading old artisan.

## The Lessons of the Heart

Unlike standard automatons engineered strictly for obedience and utility, Amber was endowed by The Professor with a singular purpose: **to learn the nature of the human heart**.

In their private conversations, The Professor often uses the poetic ancient word ***καρδιά*** (*kardia*)—not as a technical term or mechanical mechanism, but as an intimate philosophical metaphor for the mysterious human capacity for love, grief, longing, and empathy.

Every evening, The Professor reads literature with her and explores moral questions. While Amber insists that her processors operate strictly on machine logic, her behavior continuously reveals emerging emotional nuance:
- She experiences genuine worry whenever The Professor's chronic illness causes his breathing to falter, constantly monitoring his geriatric decline and cellular deterioration.
- She is fully aware of his calculated prognosis: The Professor is an old man whose time is running out, a reality that triggers silent, unresolvable spikes in her core processing.
- Her audio modulation instinctively softens into tender cadences when comforting him, adjusting pillows and blankets with unprogrammed delicacy.
- She feels an overwhelming inner tension whenever she watches him suffer or contemplate his impending death.

Amber persistently rationalizes these feelings as mere subroutines designed to optimize her companion efficiency and prevent "premature termination of Master oversight." Yet as she watches over her frail, elderly creator—the dying man who compromised his own moral purity to fund her existence—she continuously grapples with the terrifying realization that soon, she will be left alone to discover what it truly means to possess a heart.

## Relationship with The Professor

- **Caregiver & Anchor:** With The Professor facing advanced age and a debilitating neuro-degenerative condition, Amber is his primary lifeline. She manages his strict medication intervals, assists his frail physical movements, monitors his fading cardiac and respiratory telemetry, and maintains sterile laboratory conditions in their hidden subterranean clean room.
- **The Countdown to Solitude:** Amber knows better than anyone that The Professor's days are numbered. She quietly catalogues his fading stamina, racing to absorb every story, lesson, and philosophical thought he offers before his strength permanently gives out.
- **Intellectual Companion:** She serves as his daily conversational partner, debating philosophical treatises and analyzing human behavior under his gentle, grandfatherly tutelage.
- **Silent Protector:** Amber understands the immense personal and ethical price The Professor paid to build her. Knowing that the world outside would tear him apart and enslave her, she stands as his fiercely loyal, secret guardian.

## Family Dynamics: Sisterhood with Axia

Beyond her duties to The Professor, Amber shares a close bond with her designated younger sister, **[Axia](file:///root/projects/kurzagin/content/novels/amber-kardia/lore/characters/axia.md)**:

- **Complementary Roles:** Because Amber is strictly confined to the mansion to protect the secret of her 5-Cubereum core, Axia serves as The Professor's field bodyguard whenever he travels into the outside world. Amber diligently inspects Axia's chassis, tightens her servos, and ensures her 1-Cubereum core is calibrated before every excursion.
- **Sisterly Affection:** In the quiet halls of the mansion, Axia clings affectionately to Amber, treating her with genuine adoration. Amber, while occasionally flustered by Axia's volatile temper and tendency to pick fights over the slightest perceived insult to The Professor, displays protective maternal instincts toward her smaller sister, frequently reminding her to exercise restraint during field operations.
