**Wine of Eridu** is a sacred crimson vintage pressed from the grapes of the primordial Eridu tree in **[Eridu Garden](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/eridu-garden.md)** during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**.

---

## Attributes & Distinctions

- **Contrast with Tea:** Where **[Tea of Eridu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/tea-of-eridu.md)** offers clarity and physical longevity, Wine of Eridu offers divine intoxication of primordial memory.
- **Visions of Infancy:** Drinking the vintage grants visions of the world's dawn, loosens mortal boundaries, and unlocks ancient memories of the tree itself.
