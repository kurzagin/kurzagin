**Enkiantium** is the hardest material ever discovered on Earth, a substance so resilient that it defies all conventional smithing and processing.

---

## Mining History & Royal Naming

The history of Enkiantium dates back to ancient **Mesopotamia** during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**:

- **The Discovery:** While mining for gold, **[Dwarven](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/dwarf.md)** miners unearthed a mysterious, transparent crystal of peerless strength.
- **Consultation & Honor:** Recognizing its extraordinary resilience, the Dwarves consulted with Mesopotamian high officials. In a gesture of immense respect, King **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** granted the Dwarves the right to name the discovery. To honor his reign, they named it **"Enkiantium."**
- **Dwarven Monopoly:** Only the Dwarves possess the secret ancient techniques required to shape and temper Enkiantium.

---

## The Enkiantium Needle

The **Enkiantium Needle** represents the pinnacle of Dwarven craftsmanship. Using secret methods, Dwarven smiths refine raw Enkiantium into strands as thin as a thread, which retain the absolute, unbreakable durability of its original state.
