Forged from the ethereal **[Twilight Crystal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/twilight-crystal.md)**, **Twilight Bullets** behave with a chilling, dual nature on the battlefield:

- **Soft Target Permeation:** When striking flesh or any substance of lesser density, the bullet slips through as if the target were mere mist, leaving a clean, lethal exit.
- **Hard Surface Ricochet:** Should it strike a surface of superior hardness, the crystal refuses to yield—instead, it ricochets with violent grace, seeking a softer path.
