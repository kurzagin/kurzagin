**Longus** is a custom-made polearm designed specifically for **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**.

---

## Inception & Design

Conceived by the mage **[Merlyne](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/merlyne.md)**, who observed Azure's inefficient combat habits:

- **Combat Analysis:** Azure frequently threw her short knives and swords in the heat of battle, leaving herself unarmed.
- **Prototype Testing:** Merlyne tested wooden prototypes before crafting a specialized double-edged spear.
- **Detachable Chains:** Equipped with detachable chains connected to the shaft, allowing Azure to hurl the spear with devastating force and instantly retrieve it mid-combat.
