The **Sword of Abramelin** is a legendary divine blade forged by **[En-sirsir](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sirsir.md)** (**Abramelin Valentine**).

---

## Magical Capabilities & Combat Arsenal

- **True Death:** Feared across all realms as one of the few artifacts capable of inflicting **True Death**—an absolute, unalterable destruction that permanently eradicates a soul beyond reincarnation or spiritual recovery.
- **Levitation Magic:** The blade possesses sophisticated levitation magic capabilities, allowing it to float autonomously, maneuver at high velocities in mid-air, and be remotely controlled or directed during combat without physical contact.
- **Duplication Magic:** Integrated with high-level duplication magic, the blade can generate multiple physical clones or spectral duplicates of itself, creating relentless swirling storms of levitating swords that overwhelm targets.
- **Anti-Mage Specialization & "The Tyrant" Historical Debate:** Modern scholars of Elven history strongly debate En-sirsir's legacy. Many argue that she might have actually been a ruthless **tyrant**—or at least engineered her weapon as a specialized tool for state-sanctioned mage hunting. Her entire capability kit (True Death to bypass soul recovery, autonomous remote levitation, swarming duplication magic, and spell disruptions) is suspiciously hyper-optimized to counter, suppress, and hunt down spellcasters, leaving traditional mages with zero defense or casting window.

---

## Metamorphosis

- **Metamorphosis:** Upon Abramelin's demise after 60,000 years, the blade fell to the earth and transformed into **[The Great Tree of Abramelin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/great-tree-of-abramelin.md)**, leaving behind its lethality to become a living sanctuary.
