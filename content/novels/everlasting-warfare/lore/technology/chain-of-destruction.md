The **Chain of Destruction** is a legendary divine artifact forged during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**. Designed as a formidable, heavy chain tipped with a lethal spearhead, it possesses powerful binding and subduing properties capable of restraining high-tier celestial and primordial threats.

---

## Origin & Commission by Diapatri

- **Commissioned by Diapatri:** The weapon was originally commissioned during the Antediluvian Era by **[Diapatri](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/diapatri.md)**, the Iudex of Mesopotamia and adopted daughter of King Enki.
- **Dwarven Craftsmnam-an:** Diapatri sought out master smiths of the **[Dwarven Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/dwarf.md)** to forge the chain, using advanced metallurgy and arcane binding formulas.
- **Original Purpose:** The artifact was specifically engineered to capture, bind, and tame hostile **[Elemental Beasts](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elemental-beast.md)** that roamed the realm and posed severe dangers to civilization.

---

## Preserved in Ubbshu-Ukkinna & Entu Relic Tradition

- **Sanctuary Storage:** Following its initial era of use, the Chain of Destruction was placed within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)** (The Chamber of Fate), remaining preserved in the sacred sanctuary alongside the divine records.
- **Traditional Entu Weapon:** Because of its permanent housing in Ubbshu-Ukkinna, the chain became the canonical weapon available to those holding the **ME** of **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)**. Successive Entu across historical eras inherited and wielded the Chain of Destruction to carry out their divine duties.
- **The Sole Exception (En-sirsir):** Every Entu throughout history drew upon the Chain of Destruction as their designated weapon with one notable exception: **[En-sirsir](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sirsir.md)** (Abramelin Valentine). En-sirsir forged her own distinct weapon, the **[Sword of Abramelin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/sword-of-abramelin.md)**, leaving the chain in Ubbshu-Ukkinna during her era.

---

## Current Wielder: En-gurnun (Anna ni Euphratea)

- **Wielded by the VIII Entu:** In the present era, the current active Entu, **En-gurnun** (**[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)**), draws upon the Chain of Destruction as her primary divine weapon.
- **Combat Capability:** Endowed with divine binding magic and the piercing spearhead tip, the Chain of Destruction grants En-gurnun formidable offensive, defensive, and subduing capabilities in battle.
