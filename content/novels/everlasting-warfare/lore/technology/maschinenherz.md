The **Maschinenherz** (*German for "Machine Heart"*) is an advanced cybernetic mechanical heart core engineered by the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** bio-surgical division within the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**.

Designed as an extreme life-sustainment apparatus, the core was transplanted into high-priority human test subjects whose biological hearts collapsed or failed under the immense trauma of White House physical and physiological experiments.

---

## Origin & Operational Necessity

- **Emergency Life Preservation:** Because subjects like **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** (`ZR4N-4`) and **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** were deemed indispensable assets for the imperial program to engineer the "next Gilgamesh," White House scientists refused to let them perish when experimental procedures pushed their biological bodies past fatal limits.
- **Surgical Replacement:** When near-fatal experimental shocks destroyed their biological hearts, surgical teams excised their organic hearts and surgically implanted the **Maschinenherz** directly into their thoracic cavities.

---

## Known Recipients

1. **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md):** Received a Maschinenherz transplant following severe systemic collapse during early intelligence and Vigortio conditioning trials.
2. **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md):** Received a Maschinenherz transplant after near-fatal trauma during intensive physical modification and flight trials.

---

## Mechanics & Properties

- **Circulatory Regulation:** The core pumps synthetic blood matrix and oxygenated fluid through the body, sustaining vital functions independent of biological cardiac rhythm.
- **Vulnerability & Power Source:** While granting cardiac stability, the Maschinenherz requires internal bio-energy or synthetic fluid maintenance, serving as a permanent mechanical reminder of their time under White House experimentation.
