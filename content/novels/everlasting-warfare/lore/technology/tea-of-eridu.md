**Tea of Eridu** (known as the *"Liquid of Life"*) was a sacred beverage brewed from the leaves of the ancient Eridu tree in **[Eridu Garden](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/eridu-garden.md)** during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**.

---

## Properties & Guardian Siduri

- **Fountain of Youth Legend:** Celebrated throughout Mesopotamia for its unparalleled flavor and health-restoring properties.
- **Elixir Catalyst:** Served as the primary catalyst in synthesizing the **Elixir of Life**, halting biological aging.
- **Guarded by Siduri:** Overseen and protected by **[Siduri](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/siduri.md)**, who ensured the secrets of eternal youth remained under King Enki's protection.
