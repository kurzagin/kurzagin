**The Lesser Key of Solomon** is a series of ten legendary artifacts forged from raw, abyssal materials of **[The Underworld](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/underworld.md)**.

Beyond their individual powers, these items serve as the metaphysical keys to the **Great Gate**—the veil separating the Underworld from the Middleworld (**[An-Ki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/underworld.md)**).

---

## The Ten Abyssal Artifacts

1. **[The Blades of Despair](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/lesser-key-of-solomon.md):** (ME: *"The King's Weapon"*) Crafted in **[Ereshkigal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ereshkigal.md)**'s domain outside Middleworld laws; even **[Nam-shub](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-shub.md)** holds no sway over them. Forged for **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** to seal his own Nam-shub, generating a localized *"Anti-Nam-shub"* field in combat.
2. **The Ten Rings of Commandment:** Spiritual suppressors worn by Enki designed to seal ten specific "Forbidden Orders" that must never be spoken.
3. **The Crown of Dominion:** Grants total authority over all Underworld denizens. Locked behind lineage requiring the ME *"Goddess of the Underworld"* (**[Ereshkigal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ereshkigal.md)**).
4. **The Mirror of Truth:** Reflects reality stripped of masks, showing past, present, and glimpses of future (accuracy fades further into the living realm).
5. **[The Chain of Destruction](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md):** Spectral chains used to hunt and restrain escaped Underworld entities; held by VIII Entu **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)** (En-gurnun).
6. **The Scroll of Forbidden Knowledge:** Ancient parchment containing buried truths capable of rewriting history.
7. **The Staff of Balance:** *(Properties Unknown)*
8. **The Veil of Illusions:** *(Properties Unknown)*
9. **The Chalice of Eternity:** *(Properties Unknown)*
10. **The Bell of Awakening:** The final key; when struck, its resonance pierces the deepest abyss to rouse slumbering gods and primordial entities.
