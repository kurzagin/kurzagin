**Eridu Garden** was a breathtaking marvel of **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**—an expansive sanctuary located at the heart of ancient Mesopotamia.

---

## Botanical Jewels & Divine Favors

More than a collection of flora, Eridu Garden represented the crown jewel of the Mesopotamian empire.

- **Tea of Eridu:** Grown near the central spring, **[Tea of Eridu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/tea-of-eridu.md)** was the favorite brew of King **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)**, and was equally favored by **[Ereshkigal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ereshkigal.md)** (The Goddess of Kur). A single cup infused with pure mana sharpened the mind to a supernatural degree.
- **Architect & Guardian:** Designed and overseen by **[Siduri](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/siduri.md)**, who guarded its secrets and maintained its splendor before the world was unmade.
