**The Seven Realms** (also known as the **Seven Earths**) represent the spatial and metaphysical division of the mortal world executed by **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** prior to the cataclysmic world reset. Sealed within isolated dimensional spheres bounded by the impenetrable barrier known as the **Firmament**, each mortal race was assigned its own Earth to develop, cultivate power, and survive independently.

---

## Enki's Strategy & The Necessity of Separation

During the **[Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**, King Enki unified Earth's mortal lineages under a single global Mesopotamian banner. However, during the invasion by the extraterrestrial **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**, Enki found himself losing the war. He did not possess future foresight, but he understood the practical reality of what his defeat and the impending world reset would mean:

1. **The Lesson of the Early Days (The Six Being Era):** Enki remembered the primordial history before his kingship—detailed in **[Six Signs of Being](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/six-signs-of-being.md)**—when the original six lineages lacked governance and engaged in relentless, destructive inter-race hatred and warfare.
2. **Risk of Coexistence Without a King:** Enki knew that when he fell and was no longer present to enforce order, allowing all seven mortal races to continue coexisting in a single realm would cause them to relapse into ancestral inter-species warfare.
3. **The External Aethernian Threat Outside the Firmament:** Because the Aethernians still existed outside the Firmament in outer space waiting to conquer Earth, inter-species warfare among mortals would lead to total annihilation.

To prevent this doom, Enki logically executed the **Seven Realms Separation**. By enclosing the world in seven distinct Earths behind the **Firmament**, Enki forced each race to focus internally and prepare themselves for the ultimate struggle without destroying one another in inter-race conflict.

## The Seven Earths & Demographics

The physical world was partitioned into Seven Realms corresponding to the seven mortal races:

- **[Elf Realm](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elf.md):** The domain of ancient Elven arcana and long-lived magical refinement.
- **[Dwarf Realm](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/dwarf.md):** The subterranean realm of master metallurgy and **[Enkiantium](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/enkiantium.md)** crafting.
- **[Beastmen Realm](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/beastmen.md):** The realm of physical prowess and lycanthropic lineages.
- **[Demon Realm](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/demon.md):** The subterranean and infernal domain of demonic hierarchy.
- **[Spirit Realm](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/spirit.md):** The intangible realm of essence and ethereal life.
- **[Elemental Beast Realm](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elemental-beast.md):** The primordial realm bound to natural forces.
- **[Human Realm](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/human.md):** The realm of short-lived mortals, defined by rapid innovation, adaptability, and technological progression.

---

## Knowledge Disparity & Preparation for the Firmament's Fall

Enki designed the separation with precise constraints regarding world knowledge and readiness:

- **Awareness of the Aethernian Threat:** The non-human races (Elves, Dwarves, Beastmen, Demons, Spirits, and Elemental Beasts) were passed down the core imperative that an apocalyptic off-world threat—the **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**—awaits them outside the Firmament. They spend their epochs preparing themselves for the eventual confrontation when the Firmament breaks.
- **Ignorance of Other Mortal Races:** Because each non-human race is isolated within its own realm behind the Firmament, they do not understand or know about the existence of the other mortal races. Each race lives believing it is the sole terrestrial force preparing to face the Aethernians.

### Why Humanity Does Not Know

Humanity occupies a unique and isolated position in Enki's cosmic grand design:

1. **Erasure of the First Entu:** The first human candidate, **[En-zagin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-zagin.md)** (Amethyst Lorelei), succumbed to selfishness and suffered divine erasure—having her true name (*Shumu*) completely wiped from existence.
2. **The Eighth Entu:** Because of En-zagin's total erasure, Humanity became the last race to receive an Entu in the divine succession. The Human Entu is **En-gurnun** (**[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)**), appointed only in the present Eighth Era.
3. **Ignorance of the Past:** Lacking an active Entu guide throughout the previous six eras, Humanity possessed no divine conduit to transmit records of the antediluvian era. Consequently, **Humans naturally do not know what happened back then**—they remain oblivious to Enki's grand separation, the existence of the other six Earths, the true nature of the Firmament, and the ancient origin of the Aethernian threat.

## The Present Era: The Breaking of the Firmament & Realm Re-Convergence

In the present Eighth Era, the ancient **Firmament is broken**, bringing Enki's millennia-long era of isolation to a sudden, dramatic end. 

### Anna's Divine Revelation

When **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)** (Anna Wisteria) ascends to her divine station as **VIII Entu (En-gurnun)** and receives spiritual entry into **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)**, she gains access to all world records across all Seven Earths and the memories of all seven predecessor Entu.

Through this awakening, Anna discovers the awful truth:
- Humanity was left as the least/last race to have an Entu and is completely oblivious to the ancient world reset, arcana, and the Aethernians.
- With the Firmament broken, the **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)** are poised to invade. While the other six races spent thousands of years honing their combat arcana and metallurgy for this war, Humanity is hopelessly unprepared and will be instantly crushed and extinguished.

### The Forced Maintenance Event & Catastrophic Convergence

Realizing that waiting for the Aethernians means total human extinction, Anna takes a drastic, ruthless action. Exercising her divine **Tu** authority and Ubbshu-Ukkinna's metaphysical framework, she initiates a global **Maintenance** event—**forcibly merging all Seven Realms back into a single world**.

The physical consequences of this sudden spatial re-convergence are violent and catastrophic:
- **Spatial Collisions:** Geography from all seven realms violently overlaps in real time. For example, **[The Great Tree of Abramelin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/great-tree-of-abramelin.md)** from the Elven Realm suddenly manifests directly inside human settlements, its massive roots and ancient trunk violently crushing human buildings and cities.
- **Cultural Shock:** Subterranean Dwarven bastions, Demonic under-domains, Spirit nexus points, Beastmen territories, and Elemental Beast environments crash into human urban geography, instantly forcing all seven distinct cultures together in raw, chaotic proximity.
- **Immediate Human Casualties:** Humanity suffers the first and heaviest casualties ("the first to die") as fragile human cities crumble beneath the overlapping lands of elder races.
- **Anna's Rationalization:** Although the convergence causes immense devastation and immediate human casualties, Anna accepts this outcome. In her calculation, a violent merging that forces all seven races to collide and fight alongside each other is the only way to prevent Humanity from being completely wiped out to total extinction by the Aethernians.
