**Pseudo-Knowledge** is a dangerous phenomenon involving information, belief, or understanding that exists independently of—or in direct contradiction to—the absolute cosmic truth bound within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)** (The Chamber of Fate).

---

## Nature & Threat

- **Unbound Truths:** While Ubbshu-Ukkinna contains the hyper-accurate past, present, and future of the world, Pseudo-Knowledge introduces unwritten anomalies into mortal perception.
- **Cosmic Variance:** Can manifest as unrecorded memories, forbidden techniques, or dangerous paradoxes that defy preordained **[ME](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/me.md)** decrees.
