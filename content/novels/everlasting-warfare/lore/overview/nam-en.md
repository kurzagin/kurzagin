**Nam-en** is the divine governance system created by the primordial deity **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)**—built upon the **[Nam-shub](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-shub.md)** authority bestowed upon him by **[Anship](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/anship.md)**—to select specific mortal individuals, grant them the sacred title of **Nam-en**, and elevate them to become an **Entu**.

---

## Divine Authority: Tu & Nam-shub

Those chosen as Entu are women selected directly through Enki's systemic framework. Because Enki cannot speak freely within the cosmos, world governance requires a mortal proxy—so he appoints an Entu and grants her a fraction of **Nam-shub** power, known as **Tu**.

- **Primacy of Nam-shub:** The universe intrinsically prioritizes **Nam-shub** above **Tu**. Consequently, when an Entu speaks, her spoken words carry no automatic reality-warping effect unless she explicitly wills her Tu to manifest the command.
- **Shared Divine Spark:** This divine framework bestows upon all chosen Entu a small portion of Nam-shub authority to execute their sacred duties.
- **Cross-Dimensional Boundary:** Neither **Tu** nor **Nam-shub** can directly alter or affect **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**. Since Aethernians originate from the **Ohr Ein Sof** universe, Anship-based reality warping commands are ineffective against them directly.

---

## The Eight Rules of Entu

Enki established strict, unalterable laws governing the existence and responsibilities of every Entu:

1. **Selfless Duty:** The Entu must act solely for the safety and balance of the world and must not pursue personal desires. Violation results in divine punishment—permanent, unhealable damage to their spiritual body within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)** (The Chamber of Fate).
2. **Subconscious Access:** Entu gain direct access to the records housed within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)** through their subconscious minds.
3. **Eternal Preservation:** When an Entu dies, she does not undergo reincarnation. Her soul becomes permanently embedded within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)**, remaining there for eternity.
4. **Memory Sharing:** Entu possess the innate ability to share memories and experiences directly with one another.
5. **Gender Restriction:** Only mortal women may be chosen as Entu.
6. **Candidate Mandate:** Before official appointment, a candidate must first become an "Entu Candidate." She must lead a nation, organization, or be publicly recognized as a sovereign leader. Only upon fulfilling this requirement is she formally consecrated as an Entu.
7. **Lifelong Window:** The time limit for completing the "Entu Candidate" requirement extends until her physical death. If a candidate fails before death, a new candidate is selected in her place.
8. **Monotheistic Era Rule:** Only one active Entu exists in the physical world at any given time. The selection of a new Entu marks the beginning of a new historical Era, signaling a fundamental shift in world destiny.

## The Seven Realms & Realm Entu Allocation

As detailed in **[The Seven Realms](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/seven-realms.md)**, Enki separated the mortal world into seven isolated Earths enclosed by the **Firmament**. Each of the seven mortal races was assigned an Entu across the sequential historical eras:

- **Elven Realm:** Governed in Era II by **[En-sirsir](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sirsir.md)**.
- **Dwarven Realm:** Governed in Era III by **[En-sig](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sig.md)**.
- **Beastmen Realm:** Governed in Era IV by **[En-babbar](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-babbar.md)**.
- **Demon Realm:** Governed in Era V by **[En-hulal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-hulal.md)**.
- **Spirit Realm:** Governed in Era VI by **[En-giznu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-giznu.md)**.
- **Elemental Beast Realm:** Governed in Era VII by **[En-guran](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-guran.md)**.
- **Human Realm:** Due to the erasure of **[En-zagin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-zagin.md)**, Humans were the last to receive an Entu, inheriting **En-gurnun** (**[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)**) only in the Eighth Era. This lack of an early Entu guide left Humanity completely ignorant of ancient history, the world reset, and the other six Earths behind the Firmament.

## The Erasure of En-zagin

While eight names appear in the divine lineage, only seven seats remain recognized by world history. **En-zagin**, once known as **Amethyst Lorelei**, was cast down from her station.

Driven by overwhelming selfishness in violation of Rule #1, she was not merely executed—she was subjected to the ultimate divine punishment. Her **Shumu** (True Name and Essence) was completely erased from the fabric of existence, leaving a blank void where the first Entu once stood. Because of this total erasure, mortal history recognizes only seven Entu, moving the legacy forward until ending with **En-gurnun** (Anna Wisteria / Anna ni Euphratea).
