## Shumu (Title) Dynamics

- **Shumu Breaker:** An individual who attempts to defy destiny dictated by their Shumu. History records no one who has successfully broken their title.
- **Shumu Writer:** Rare authority exclusive to **"The King"**, capable of bestowing a Shumu upon individuals or inanimate objects, turning ordinary items into preordained legendary artifacts.
