**The Great Tree of Abramelin** is a colossal, silver-canopied tree that towers over the planet, serving as the capital of the **[Elven Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elf.md)**.

---

## Origin & Cosmic Role

- **Origin:** Born at the exact moment of **[En-sirsir](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sirsir.md)**'s death, when the **[Sword of Abramelin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/sword-of-abramelin.md)** fell to the earth and transformed into a living tree.
- **Sanctuary of Life:** Replaced the lethality of True Death with an oxygen-rich, thriving cosmic metropolis and sanctuary bridging worlds.
