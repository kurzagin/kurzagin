**The Eye** is a massive, unblinking cosmic eye featuring a swirling iris that manifests exclusively in the peripheral vision of **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)**.

---

## Dual Truths

- **Enki's Perception:** Enki perceives it as an inescapable, terrifying curse stalking his every step.
- **Neurological Reality:** A manifestation of **Schizophrenia**—a splintering of perception in an ancient era before medical terminology existed.
- **Meta-Verse Reality:** Represents the literal collective gaze of **the audience/readers** watching Enki from beyond the narrative boundary.
