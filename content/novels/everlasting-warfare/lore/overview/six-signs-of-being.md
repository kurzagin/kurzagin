In dawn-time during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**, **[Anship](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/anship.md)** created the original **Six Races** of Earth. These six original lineages were unnamed at their creation and possessed a deep-rooted, instinctive racism toward one another triggered merely by physical presence. Lacking any structure of government, law, or leadership, they waged endless, brutal wars against each other.

---

## The Grand Maintenance & The Rise of Enki

- **The Grand Maintenance:** To halt the endless bloodshed, Anship instituted a cosmic event known as **Maintenance**—plunging the entire world and all six races into a profound, heavy slumber.
- **Appointment of Enki:** Upon waking from Maintenance, Anship introduced a new **Plain Race**—a single mortal man whom Anship appointed as **The King**, known across the realm as the **Lord of Earth**: **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)**.
- **Kingship & Unification:** Enki introduced the concepts of **Kingship**, law, and **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)** governance to Earth, successfully unifying the warring six races under structured leadership.
- **Creation of the Human Race:** Following unification, Enki forged the **[Human Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/human.md)** in his own image—basing them on his Plain Race morphology.

---

## The Unified Six Earth Lineages

The six original terrestrial lineages unified by Enki are codified under the Six Signs of Being:

1. **[Elven Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elf.md):** Long-lived magical master lineage.
2. **[Dwarven Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/dwarf.md):** Subterranean master smiths exclusively capable of shaping **[Enkiantium](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/enkiantium.md)**.
3. **[Beastmen Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/beastmen.md):** Lycanthropic physical race.
4. **[Demon Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/demon.md):** Inhabitants of the Underworld.
5. **[Spirit Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/spirit.md):** Incorporeal essence beings between flesh and void.
6. **[Elemental Beast Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elemental-beast.md):** Beings bound to primordial nature forces.

*(Note: **Humans** were created by Enki post-unification in his image, while **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)** are off-world invaders and are strictly excluded from the Six Earth Lineages. Prior to the world reset, Enki separated all seven mortal races into **[The Seven Realms](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/seven-realms.md)** behind the **Firmament** to prevent ancestral racism from destroying them after his death.)*
