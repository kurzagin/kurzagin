The **Euphry** is the official currency unit of the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)** and the supreme global economic unit established following the conclusion of the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)** (1634). Serving as the universal monetary standard for international banking, trade routes, and military contracts, the Euphry solidified Euphrates' post-war position as the dominant financial power on the continent.

---

## Production & Minting: The World Treasure Company

The formal minting, stamping, and distribution of the Euphry currency is managed around the **World Treasure Company**:

- **Institutional Foundation:** The **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md)** is a global financial institution established ten years prior. Founded and governed by **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** ("The Gilded Plague"), it originated as an apothecary clinic and exploded into a monetary hegemon after she engineered and cured an itching pestilence among Euphrates nobility.
- **Top-Secret Executive Leadership:** Zyfel secretly governs the global monetary standard behind a veil of corporate secrecy. Confidential internal communications address her exclusively under formal honorifics: *"Young Lady"*, *"Madame"*, or *"Mademoiselle"*.
- **Precision Stamping:** Operating state-of-the-art mints, coining presses, and printing mills under her sovereign corporate oversight, the company enforces immaculate weight, purity, and anti-counterfeiting standards across all minted coins and printed banknotes.

## Physical Forms & Spread

The Euphry circulates in two distinct physical formats, though their public adoption and spread vary significantly:

### 1. Gold Coins (Most Common)
- **Primary Circulation:** Minted from high-purity gold alloys by the World Treasure Company, gold coins constitute the vast majority of currency actively circulating among the general public.
- **Universal Trust:** Due to post-war economic shifts and widespread preference for hard bullion in everyday market transactions, gold coins are by far the most common and universally accepted denomination across cities, rural settlements, and foreign markets.

### 2. Paper Banknotes (Varying Spread)
- **Institutional Medium:** Printed paper certificates backed by central gold reserves.
- **Varying Distribution:** Paper banknotes have a varying spread. While common among international trading houses, scientific institutions, and high-level banking transactions in **Euphratea**, paper notes are far less common in provincial regions where physical gold coins remain dominant.

## Iconography: Sovereign Prominence & The Hidden Ouroboros

Every piece of Euphry currency carries a stark contrast in its visual design:

1. **Ex-Queen [Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md) (Prominent):** The bold profile of the former queen is prominently stamped on the face of the gold coins and printed on the front of paper notes, representing sovereign victory and Euphrates' political reign.
2. **The [Ouroboros](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md) (Subtle & Obscure):** 
   - **Hidden Design:** The Ouroboros (the snake consuming its tail, crest of **[House Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)**) is micro-engraved along the outer rim bevel of the gold coins or subtly woven into the fine background guilloché patterns of the paper notes.
   - **Public Ignorance:** The symbol is so minute and disguised that ordinary people using the currency daily do not even know it exists on the coin or note.
   - **Enigmatic Lineage:** Even among the rare collectors or bankers who manage to spot the tiny mark, no ordinary person knows what the symbol actually belongs to—completely unaware of its link to the exiled noble family working in the secret subterranean facility beneath the capital.
