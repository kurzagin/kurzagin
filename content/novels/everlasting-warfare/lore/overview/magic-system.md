In this world, magic does not require a "caster" in the traditional biological or internal sense. It exists as a pervasive environmental force fueled directly by **Mana**. 

To channel and shape this raw power into tangible magical effects, a practitioner must transfer a **Magic Formula** into a physical **Catalyst**—such as a staff, sword, or crystal. However, this definition of magic represents the practical knowledge as codified and understood primarily by **[Human](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/human.md)** scholars.

---

## The Imbuing Process & Ancient Runes

Magic Formulas consist of **Ancient Runic Letters**, which represent the literal, intrinsic meaning of the desired effect. For example, inscribing the specific runes for *"Eternal Flame"* grants the target catalyst the permanent capacity to manifest inextinguishable fire.

> *"To imbue a catalyst, place it at the center of a magic circle inscribed with the chosen runes. The letters will vanish, sinking into the object. To use it, simply visualize the effect—imagine the fire erupting, and the catalyst will manifest it."*  
> — **[Merlyne](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/merlyne.md)**

## Magic Classification

Magic is categorized systematically by its trigger behavior and the depth of structural proficiency built into its formula:

### 1. Operational Types

- **Active Magic (Spelled Magic):** Requires conscious activation, verbal incantation, or a deliberate trigger. Pioneered by **[En-sirsir](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sirsir.md)**, who first conceived of weaving mana into structured, programmable actions rather than relying solely on innate, instinctual mana flow.
- **Passive Magic:** Pre-programmed to remain continuously active at all times, or set to trigger automatically when precise environmental or situational conditions are met. Pioneered in human scholarship by **[Merlyne](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/merlyne.md)** of the Human Abraxas Academy.

### 2. Proficiency Ranks

- **Advanced Rank:** Highly complex formulas that have been refined and enhanced over centuries. For long-lived species such as the **[Elven](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elf.md)** race, reaching this level of absolute mastery typically requires thousands of years of dedication. Historically, only **[En-sirsir](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sirsir.md)** (Abramelin Valentine) is known to have attained this tier of mastery.
- **Intermediate Rank:** Standard, fully mastered formulas whose structural mechanics and outputs are thoroughly comprehended by experienced mages.
- **Beginner Rank:** Basic or newly learned formulas producing standard, unrefined magical outputs.
