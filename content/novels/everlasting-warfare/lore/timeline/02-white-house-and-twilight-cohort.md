Deep within imperial forests, the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)** established the secret **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** facility under the guise of an orphanage to engineer the "next Gilgamesh" through brutal conditioning and **[Vigortio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/vigortio.md)**.

---

## Sequential Admission & Serial Codes

Upon entry into the facility, all children were forcibly stripped of their real birth names and assigned alphanumeric test codes:

1. **Zeeran (`ZR4N-4`):** Admitted at ~6 years old (~1615).
2. **Azure (`ZR3-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~1616).
3. **Liezt (`LZT-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~1617).
4. **Zell (`ZLL-4`) & Chizu (`CZ-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~1618, twins).
5. **Zyfel (`ZFL-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~1619, ~4 years old; recovered from the destroyed neutral sanctuary borderland village).
6. **Zephyr (`ZP-4`):** Admitted +6 months later (~1619).

---

## Zeeran's Naming & The "Twilight Room"

- **Phonetic Derivation:** Refusing to allow his siblings to exist as nameless test subjects, patriarch Zeeran Twilight personally derived proper names for each sibling from their serial codes (`ZR4N-4` → *Zeeran*, `ZR3-4` → *Azure*, `LZT-4` → *Liezt*, `ZLL-4` → *Zell*, `CZ-4` → *Chizu*, `ZFL-4` → *Zyfel*, `ZP-4` → *Zephyr*).
- **Origin of the Surname:** All seven siblings were assigned to reside together in a research ward designated as the **"Twilight Room"**. Zeeran claimed the room's title as their official family surname, establishing **[House Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)**.

---

## Dr. Griffin's Hypothesis: Compete, Unify, or Become Gods?

While the Tigris Empire sought basic martial super-soldiers, lead scientist **[Dr. Griffin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-griffin.md)** used the Twilight Room cohort for a grand socio-evolutionary trial. By confining seven enhanced human geniuses in one space, Griffin sought to see whether they would compete, unify, or transcend into gods. Though the physical facility burned down in 1634, Griffin's experiment remains active in real-time as the siblings shape the world.

---

## Dr. Phoenix: The Maternal Guardian of the Cohort

Working alongside Griffin was his wife, senior scientist **[Dr. Phoenix](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-phoenix.md)** (Parischt Chartrenix). Revered as **"Mother"** by the children of the White House and the Twilight Room, she attended to their physical trauma and psychological stability, nursing them through grueling drug regimens and surgical augmentations while providing warm meals, gifts, and emotional guidance. Her warmth proved to be the vital anchor that kept the children human and fostered the deep familial devotion that bound them together.
