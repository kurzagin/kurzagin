The **Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War** stands as one of the most severe and pivotal conflicts in continental history. Initiated in **1534** and concluding in **1634**, the century-long struggle pitted the vast, traditionalist **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)** against the modernist breakaway **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**.

---

## The 1634 Turning Point & Invasion of Euphratea

Near the conclusion of the war in **1634**, Supreme Commander **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** launched a massive, coordinated Tigris counter-offensive. Vanguard commander **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** spearheaded the attack, shattering Euphrates defense lines and invading to the gates of **Euphratea**. Zeeran, who hated **[ur-sag̃](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ur-sag.md)** as a "monster in human form," meticulously formulated strategies to avoid triggering his deployment. Paradoxically, the harder Zeeran tried to avoid him, the more inevitably ur-sag̃ appeared on the battlefield, where only Azure was capable of holding him. This brilliant push brought the Tigris Empire to the absolute brink of total victory.

---

## The Burning of the White House & Zell's Tragedy

At the climax of the offensive, critical intelligence reached Supreme Commander Zeeran: the location of the top-secret **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** experimental facility had been traced, revealed, and set ablaze—putting his sister **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** (`ZLL-4`) in immediate mortal danger.

Prioritizing Zell over imperial conquest, Zeeran abandoned the frontlines and rushed to the White House, only to discover that Zell had already died from severe burns.

---

## Queen Elizabeth's Whisper & Tigris Capitulation

As Zeeran carried Zell's body outside the burning facility, he was immediately surrounded by an elite division of Euphrates Cavalry led by Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**.

When Queen Elizabeth demanded he end the war, Zeeran responded with grim desperation, asking why he should stop when Azure was on the verge of taking Euphratea. Queen Elizabeth then leaned close and whispered a secret revelation into Zeeran's ear. Overwhelmed by what he heard, Zeeran immediately ordered all Tigris forces to stand down, officially concluding the war with Euphrates declared victorious.

---

## Aftermath & Displacement of House Twilight

- **Euphrates Ascendancy:** Euphrates secured lucrative territory and resource concessions, establishing the **[Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md)** as the new global economic currency.
- **Exile of House Twilight:** House Twilight lost their ancestral lands and imperial titles in Tigris. Patriarch Zeeran relocated his surviving sisters to live in exile within **Euphratea**, housed in a secret subterranean R&D facility beneath the capital.
- **Post-War Palace Visit & Undocumented Encounter:** Directly after the war's conclusion seven years ago, Zeeran (then **23 years old**) visited the Euphratea palace for the first time. Inside the palace grounds, **[Erika ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)** (who was just **14 years old** at the time) accidentally ran into him without knowing his identity—an encounter that remained completely undocumented in official royal history, but resurfaced when Erika met him again in exile after her regicide.
