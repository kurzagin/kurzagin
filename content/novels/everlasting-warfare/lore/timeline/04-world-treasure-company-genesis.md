In **1624** (17 years before Present Day 1641 / 10 years before the 1634 war end), House Twilight launched a covert biological and financial campaign in the heart of the enemy capital, **Euphratea**.

---

## Liezt's Nurse Cover & Espionage Matrix

- **Infiltration as a Nurse:** **[Liezt Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md)** (`LZT-4`, ~13 years old) used her chameleon persona abilities to assume the identity of a dedicated nurse in Euphratea.
- **Clinic Operations & Staff Command:** Liezt commanded a small medical clinic staff established under **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** (`ZFL-4`, ~10 years old), providing healthcare to local citizens.
- **Double Identity & Spy Operations:** Caring for the sick provided Liezt with an ideal cover environment to gather political and military intelligence, traveling back and forth to supply covert intel to Supreme Commander **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)**.

---

## Water Route Contamination & Royal Charters

- **Targeted Water Contamination:** Zyfel contaminated the specific river channel feeding directly into the Euphratea nobility's private quarters.
- **Liezt's Recommendation:** When an agonizing affliction struck the aristocracy, Liezt positioned herself inside the medical system to recommend a "foreign practitioner" possessing a specialized antidote.
- **Acquisition of Banking Charters:** The royal court authorized the importation of Zyfel's remedy. Curing the affliction earned total trust, tax immunities, and royal banking charters, transforming the modest clinic into the **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md)**.
