The **Great Schism** marks the ancient, foundational split that defined continental history nearly **900 years ago**.

---

## The Rebellious Exodus

- **Rebellion Against Dogma:** Frustrated by the rigid, iron-headed elders of the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)** who enforced strict religious traditionalism and suppressed all foreign innovation, the young populace of Tigris staged a massive cultural rebellion.
- **The River Crossing:** The rebels fled across the great river boundary, establishing an independent sovereign state that eventually became the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**.
- **Cultural & Genetic Intermingling:** Out beyond the river, the refugees mingled and reproduced with neighboring foreign nations, developing a diverse population recognized by their iconic **blonde hair and red eyes**.

---

## Spiritual & Technological Paradigms

- **Spiritual Schism:** Breaking away from Tigris's veneration of the Elder Triad (**An**, **Enlil**, **Enki**), Euphrates embraced a modernist pantheon worshipping **Ishtar** (goddess of beauty, passion, and freedom) and **Marduk** (god of fierce ambition and storm).
- **Centuries of R&D:** Rather than undergoing a sudden overnight technical explosion, Euphrates spent centuries in quiet, dedicated scientific research and metallurgy, laying the groundwork for firearm development.
