Set **seven years following the conclusion of the 100 Year War** (and 3 years post-regicide), the present-day era is defined by covert scientific intrigue, economic monopoly, and shifting shadow warfare.

---

## The Secret Underground R&D Division

- **Subterranean Exile:** Patriarch **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** (30 yrs old; 23 yrs old at war end 7 years ago) and **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** (30 yrs old) reside within a top-secret underground facility beneath the capital of **Euphratea**.
- **Resentful Supervision:** Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)** (Age 90) exploits their unmatched scientific intellect for Euphrates projects while viewing the family with intense contempt, derogatorily calling them **"Tigris rats"**.
- **Zeeran's Degeneration:** End-stage *LähmKraftum* (Vigortio toxicity) has left Zeeran permanently paralyzed and wheelchair-bound, relying on his mechanical **[Maschinenherz](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/maschinenherz.md)** core while protecting Azure during her wing-fracturing rituals.

---

## Global Financial Dominance & The Clandestine Odyssey

- **Monetary Monopolization:** CEO **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** (`ZFL-4`, 24 yrs old) governs the **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md)** from the shadows, controlling the global **[Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md)** currency micro-engraved with the House Twilight **Ouroboros** crest.
- **The Crusade for the Elixir of Life & Missing Sister:** Leaving the boardrooms of Euphratea, Zyfel travels incognito alongside her enforcer sister **[Chizu Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/chizu-twilight.md)** (`CZ-4`, 27 yrs old) in a **western country**, hunting for ancient alchemical secrets to synthesize the *Elixir of Life* to cure Zeeran, while tracking leads for her missing sister **[Zephyr Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zephyr-twilight.md)** (`ZP-4`), who went missing in Antarctica at age 6 (missing for 16 years).
- **The Sovereign Crown Crisis & Erika's Organization:** Three years after committing regicide at age 18 and being rescued from royal knights by **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**, **[Erika ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)** is now **21 years old** and commands her own formidable exiled organization. Ex-Queen Elizabeth rules Euphrates directly while preparing Second Princess **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)** (~17 yrs old; ~14 at regicide) for the throne.
