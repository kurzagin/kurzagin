**The Postdiluvian Era** is the current Modern Era born from the ashes of the old world following the catastrophic Great Flood.

---

## Political Fragmentation & Modern Conflicts

Unlike **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)** when Mesopotamia held global hegemony:

- **Fractured Hegemony:** Global power is fractured among diverse competing nations, most notably the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)** and the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**.
- **Primary Setting:** Serves as the primary timeline setting for central historical events, including the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)** and present day 1641.
