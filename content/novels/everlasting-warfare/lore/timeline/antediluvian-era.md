**The Antediluvian Era** was the primordial golden age of the world, a time when **Mesopotamia** stood under **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** as the sole, absolute superpower spanning the entire globe.

---

## Unified World Order

- **Global Banner:** Under the singular rule of **King Enki**, all lands were unified beneath one Mesopotamian banner.
- **Governance:** Reality was governed directly by physical tablets of **[ME](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/me.md)** and spoken **[Nam-shub](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-shub.md)** commands.
- **Sanctuaries:** Featured **[Eridu Garden](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/eridu-garden.md)**, **[The Abzu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/abzu.md)**, and trade with **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**.

---

## Cataclysmic End: Enki's Scorched Earth

Upon his final battle and prayer to Nam-an, Enki destroyed the world to force a universal rebirth. However, Enki's soul did **not** vanish. 

Because of his cosmic archetype as **The King**, Enki's soul shattered into divine fragments and entered the eternal cycle of reincarnation. As a result, every great leader, monarch, and conqueror across human and continental history carries a fragment of Enki's soul—the true origin of their instinctual drive to lead, conquer, and unify the world under a single **World Order**.
