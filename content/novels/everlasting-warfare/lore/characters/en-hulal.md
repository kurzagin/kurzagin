**En-hulal**, whose true name (*Shumu*) is **Luna Diamond**, was a woman of the Demon race who served as the **Fifth Entu** (Rank V) under the **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)** divine decree system created by Enki.

---

## Known Facts

- **Rank & Lineage:** Rank V Entu in the divine hierarchy.
- **True Name:** Luna Diamond.
- **Race:** Demon.
- **Divine Power:** Granted **Tu** (a fraction of **Nam-shub** authority) by Enki upon her appointment as Entu.
- **Afterlife:** Governed by the rules of Entu; upon her death, her soul did not reincarnate and now resides permanently within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)** (The Chamber of Fate).
