**Ereshkigal** (known by her divine title as **The Goddess of Kur**) is the absolute Queen and sovereign ruler of **[The Underworld](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/underworld.md)**.

---

## Divine Authority & Preferences

- **Nam-shubkur:** Wields the omnipotent **Nam-shubkur**, an authority overriding even **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)**'s Nam-shub within her realm.
- **Eridu Tea Favor:** Along with King Enki, Ereshkigal favored **[Tea of Eridu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/tea-of-eridu.md)** above all beverages.
- **Abyssal Hierarchy & Non-Punitive Realm:** Holds exclusive entitlement to wear *The Crown of Dominion* via her ME title *"Goddess of Kur"*, commanding the abyssal floors. Contrary to mortal fear, Ereshkigal did not design her domain for eternal torment; instead, she transformed floors of the Underworld (such as the Shadow District) into lavish **entertainment districts** filled with nightlife, relaxation, and grand venues.
- **Impact on Mortal Law:** Rumors of this non-punitive, paradise-like Underworld caused a widespread wave of mortal suicides, forcing **[Diapatri](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/diapatri.md)** to decree that "Suicide is a Sin" and abolish capital punishment across Mesopotamia to prevent criminals from escaping to Ereshkigal's entertainment districts.
