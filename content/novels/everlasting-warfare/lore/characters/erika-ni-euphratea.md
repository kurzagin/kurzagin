**Erika ni Euphratea** is the former Crown Princess of the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)** and the eldest daughter of the late King and Queen. Once destined to inherit the sovereign throne, her catastrophic betrayal—murdering both of her parents—led to her immediate banishment and stripped her of all royal succession rights.

---

- **Titles / Aliases:** Morgenstern Erika (Moe), Supreme Leader of The Crows, Former Crown Princess, The Traitor Princess, Banished Heir
- **Gender:** Female

## Timeline, Encounters & Organization

As the firstborn daughter of the King and Queen, Erika was groomed from youth to assume leadership of the Euphrates Kingdom as Crown Princess.

- **First Meeting at War's End (7 Years Ago — Erika Age 14, Zeeran Age 23):** At the conclusion of the 100 Year War seven years ago, when Erika was just **14 years old** (a child), **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** (then **23 years old**) visited the Euphratea palace directly after the war ended. Erika accidentally ran into him inside the palace, though she did not know who he was at the time *(this encounter remains entirely undocumented in official realm records)*.
- **The Succession Crisis & Regicide (3 Years Ago — Age 18):** Three years ago, at **18 years old**, when her parents chose not to pass the sovereign throne to Erika as heir, her political fury culminated in parricide: Erika assassinated both of her parents, the reigning King and Queen.
- **Hunted by Knights & Rescue by Azure:** Following the regicide, royal Euphrates knights launched a relentless manhunt across the capital to execute Erika. While fleeing, she was unexpectedly saved by **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**, who brought her into House Twilight's secret **underground facility**.
- **The Underground Base & The Sudden Memory:** Inside the facility, Erika had no idea who Azure or Zeeran were at first. However, the moment she was introduced to **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** (then 27 yrs old at regicide), her memory flashed back—and she suddenly remembered that he was the exact man she had accidentally encountered at the palace four years prior (seven years before present day) when she was 14.
- **Formation of The Crows & Present Day (Age 21):** Adopting the name **Erika Morgenstern**, she established **[The Crows](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/the-crows.md)**, which has rapidly grown into the largest criminal organization in the world. In the present day, Erika is **21 years old**, actively directing high-profile terror operations and attacks targeting the Euphrates Kingdom.

## Legacy & Impact on the Throne

Erika's heinous action forced her grandmother, Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**, to step back onto the throne and take full supreme command of the realm, as Erika's younger sister **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)** (~17 years old in present day; ~14 at regicide) was far too young and unready to rule.
