**ur-sag̃** (whose true name bound by the **[ME](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/me.md)** of ur-sag̃ is **Cain Goliath**) is the legendary giant General of the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**.

---

## Military Significance & Zeeran's Avoidance

- **Monstrous Prowess:** Normally refrains from combat due to seeing no urgency in standard warfare.
- **Zeeran's Hatred:** **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** harbored an intense hatred for Cain Goliath, viewing him as a monster in human form and designing low-intensity maneuvers specifically calculated to avoid forcing Euphrates to deploy him.
- **Battlefield Climax:** Deployed during the climax of the 100 Year War when Zeeran's pressure placed Euphrates on the brink of collapse, stalled only by **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**.
