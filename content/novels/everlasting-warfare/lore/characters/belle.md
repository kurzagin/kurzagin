**Belle** (Full Name: **Isabelle Rätsel**) is the closest friend, confidante, and trusted logistics aide to **[Erika ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)** within **[The Crows](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/the-crows.md)**.

---

## Personality & Relationship with Erika

At first glance, Belle presents an almost unreliable appearance—jogging clumsily to fulfill tasks and exhibiting a mild-mannered, quiet presence. However, beneath her awkward exterior lies a keen analytical mind and absolute loyalty.

Belle is the only person within The Crows who knows Erika’s true background as the exiled Crown Princess of Euphrates, as well as Erika's hidden vengeful and hot-tempered side. While other members see only their supreme leader's cold command, Belle has accompanied Erika into the secret subterranean training rooms and knows the full weight of Erika's burdens.

---

## Story Role & Key Actions

- **Chapter 3 (The Contract Renegotiation):** Belle fetched the leather parchment and quill set for Erika during the intense contract renegotiation with **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)**. She later accompanied Erika to her secret room containing plushies and the subterranean firing range, expressing shock at how Zeeran could survive a bullet wound near his heart.
- **Chapter 4 (Sanctuary Logistics):** Organized gas mask distribution and emergency medical assistance for wounded Black Pigeon division members when toxic gas canisters were unsealed.
- **Chapter 9 (The Sanctuary Meeting):** Following the surface battle beneath the World Tree, Belle joined the leadership circle around Zeeran and Erika. As heavy tension mounted over flying monsters, low fuel, and the perilous expedition to Antarctica, Belle adjusted her glasses and deadpanned, *"Guys, we need food."* Her practical observation broke the suffocating gloom, bringing a moment of lighthearted relief to the fifty survivors before their march to Babylon Harbor.
