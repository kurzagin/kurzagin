**Sirius** was once a noble Archangel of **[Aethernia](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**.

---

## Origin, Mind-Wipe & Invasion Command

Originally, the Aethernians dispatched Sirius to Earth during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)** to secure access to the realm's rich **[Nam-an](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-an.md)** reserves—the physical manifestation of Ohr Ein Sof needed to reconstruct their destroyed universe, **Olam HaBa**. When diplomatic negotiations broke down, the Aethernian leadership stripped Sirius of her memories and emotions, transforming her into a hollow, mindless weapon. The leaders commanded her to execute total planetary Nam-an siphoning across Earth at once.

In a bitter twist of irony, Sirius turned **[Longinus](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/longinus.md)**—the double-ended spear forged by **[Dwarven](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/dwarf.md)** master smiths as their absolute flagship masterpiece of craftsmnam-an—against its creators. Used as a mindless weapon of the invasion, she wielded Longinus to ruthlessly subjugate the Dwarves and seize control of their subterranean Nam-an crystal veins, planting a deep-rooted, ancestral hatred toward Sirius within the Dwarven race.

---

## The Last Battle Against Enki, Cosmic Reset & Memory Awakening

As the Aethernian invasion tore across the realm, King **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** rose to defend Earth against the off-world invaders. As Enki found himself losing the war, he offered a desperate prayer to **[Nam-an](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-an.md)** and executed a cataclysmic world reset. 

Through this divine intervention, Enki separated each of the seven races into its own individual Seventh Earth realm, contained the realms within the primordial waters of **[Abzu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/abzu.md)**, and forged a cosmic firmament above them to protect mortals while the Aethernians remained outside.

As the world was restructured, every entity not rooted in Nam-an was rejected by the new cosmic order. Sirius and her fellow Aethernian invaders were violently expelled beyond the firmament into the void of outer space. It was at this exact moment—thrown across the void amid the shock of the world's destruction—that Sirius's mental shackles shattered, and her long-suppressed memories and emotions came rushing back. 

Recognizing the atrocities committed by her leaders, Sirius refused to return to Aethernia. However, her sister **[Kamael](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/kamael.md)**, a fellow Archangel, had always been deeply suspicious that Sirius would eventually break free of her conditioning and regain her memories. The moment Sirius showed defiance, Kamael reported her to the Aethernian leaders and was granted command of a specialized military division to hunt down her sister.

---

## The Void Pursuit & The Firmament Anomaly

Desperate and hunted across the void of space, Sirius was pursued relentlessly by Kamael’s division into **[The Postdiluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/postdiluvian-era.md)**. Overwhelmed by her former kin, Sirius sustained critical, life-threatening wounds, her celestial wings completely shattered in the brutal engagement. 

As her broken body plummeted toward the reborn Earth, an unprecedented cosmic anomaly occurred: the barrier of the **[Abzu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/abzu.md)** firmament, which strictly repelled and rejected all non-Nam-an rooted off-world beings, **did not reject Sirius**. Breaching the barrier without resistance, she fell like a dying star into the desolate, frozen atmosphere of Earth.

She crashed into the snowy reaches near the Tigris River, where she was discovered by **[Dr. Griffin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-griffin.md)**.

---

## Transformation into Azure Twilight

In the wake of the crash, Sirius's mind fractured once more—her memories vanished and her emotions flickered like a dying flame. Dr. Griffin brought her to the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)**, where she was introduced to a young boy named **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)**. 

Over time, as she lingered between her forgotten past and an uncertain future, Zeeran gave her a new name: **"[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)"**.
