**Zsofia**, officially designated as **`ZSF-4`**, was an experimental human subject confined within the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)**—the subterranean black-site laboratory in the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**. Her original birth name was wiped from all records upon her capture. 

Before Zyfel's elevation to the elite **Twilight Room**, Zsofia lived alongside little Zyfel in the **Candlelight Room**, acting as a protective, nurturing older sister. Named **"Zsofia"** by **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** upon Zyfel’s plea, she was considered by Zeeran to be officially adopted into **[House Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)**. However, her life ended in horrific tragedy when **[Dr. Phoenix](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-phoenix.md)** used her as a disposable "blood stock," systematically draining her blood to death for immortality trials.

## Background: The Candlelight Room & Family Bond

### 1. Life in the Candlelight Room
Within the subterranean hierarchy of the White House, test subjects were segregated into different housing wards based on cognitive grading and genetic adaptability. The **Candlelight Room** was a ward graded far below the premier **Twilight Room**. The children relegated to the Candlelight Room lacked the apex cognitive or physiological markers demanded by Dr. Griffin's highest evolutionary trials, leaving them exposed to harsher conditions and less privileged care.

Her original name stolen by imperial captors and replaced with the cold alphanumeric tag **`ZSF-4`**, she found purpose not in survival alone, but in protecting the younger wards around her. Among these children was a traumatized, penniless four-year-old girl designated **`ZFL-4`** (the future **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)**), who had been dragged from a blood-soaked border village.

To little Zyfel, `ZSF-4` became a warm, indispensable older sister. In a freezing, sterile subterranean facility where death and excruciating surgical procedures were daily occurrences, the older girl formed a makeshift family for Zyfel and the other Candlelight children—shielding them from callous wardens, sharing meager food rations, and offering tender reassurance in the dark.

### 2. Zyfel's Elevation, Visits, & Zeeran's Naming
When facility researchers conducted a second battery of rigorous cognitive and physiological re-evaluations, Zyfel’s hidden alchemical genius and staggering analytical capability were detected. The imperial overseers immediately removed Zyfel from the Candlelight Room, promoting her into the elite **Twilight Room** alongside **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** and the other apex subjects.

Even after entering the elite Twilight cohort, Zyfel was granted permission to regularly visit her former ward and check in on her Candlelight family. Zyfel spoke to Zeeran with earnest devotion about `ZSF-4`, explaining how the older girl had been her family and emotional sanctuary in the Candlelight Room.

Moved by Zyfel's heartfelt devotion and respecting anyone who shielded his younger siblings, Zeeran took action:
- Just as he had derived names for his fellow Twilight subjects from their alphanumeric codes, Zeeran personally transformed `ZSF-4` into the beautiful name **Zsofia**.
- Zeeran resolved to formally adopt Zsofia into **[House Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)** as one of their own sisters, fully intending to bring her up into the Twilight Room once the necessary arrangements and medical clearances could be made. 
- However, bureaucratic delays, security restrictions within the compound, and sudden illness prevented the formal adoption ceremony from ever being concluded before tragedy struck.

## The Illness & The Tragedy of the "Blood Stock"

### 1. The Illness, Zyfel's Despair, and Zeeran's Knife Threat to Dr. Griffin
During one of her visits to the Candlelight Room, young Zyfel discovered that Zsofia had fallen critically ill with a severe, life-threatening physiological breakdown. Horrified and desperate, Zyfel ran frantically through the White House corridors, approaching the facility scientists and begging them on her hands and knees to treat and heal Zsofia. The imperial researchers, viewing Candlelight children as disposable sub-grade test material, cold-heartedly dismissed her pleas.

Left with no other recourse, little Zyfel ran back to the Twilight Room in tears. Falling to her knees before ten-year-old **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)**, she sobbed hysterically, clutching his clothes and begging him to order the scientists to treat Zsofia.

Seeing his little sister kneeling and weeping before him ignited a profound, volcanic rage within Zeeran. To Zeeran, a brother's sacred duty was absolute protection; seeing his own sister forced to beg on her knees was not merely painful—he saw it as his own unforgivable failure as her elder brother. Stifling his fury with rigid resolve, Zeeran gently lifted Zyfel from the floor and made an unyielding promise: he would handle everything.

Without hesitation, Zeeran armed himself with a blade and marched directly into the analytical office of Chief Scientist **[Dr. Griffin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-griffin.md)**. Knowing Griffin valued him above all else as the irreplaceable crown jewel of the White House evolutionary project, Zeeran drew the knife, aimed the sharp edge directly against his own carotid artery, and issued a lethal ultimatum: if Griffin did not immediately mobilize medical care to save Zsofia, Zeeran would slit his own throat on the spot.

However, Dr. Griffin remained chillingly unyielding. He told Zeeran that even if he killed himself, it could not be helped: the ongoing 100-Year War had completely drained imperial government resources and laboratory supplies. Griffin bluntly declared that with rations and medical stores depleted, imperial doctrine dictated efficiency—every scarce vial had to be conserved exclusively for the elite Twilight Room rather than wasted on lower-grade subjects like those in the Candlelight Room.

Faced with Griffin's cold, calculating refusal, Zeeran was crushed by absolute helplessness and lost all hope. 

It was at that desperate juncture that **[Dr. Phoenix](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-phoenix.md)** (Parischt Chartrenix) intervened. Stepping into the office with soothing maternal warmth, Mother Phoenix gently comforted Zeeran and promised that she would personally treat and nurse Zsofia back to health using her own private reserves and experimental resources. Overwhelmed with gratitude and relief, Zeeran placed his complete, unquestioning faith in the woman he called Mother.

Tragically, Zeeran would later uncover the horrifying reality of what Parischt's "private treatment" entailed.

From that day onward, a permanent, defining principle was forged in young Zyfel's psychology. Her bottomless greed and hoarding of global riches acquired a singular, unshakeable dogma: ***"Everything you call valuable is not as valuable as Zsofia."*** Because no amount of gold, resources, or imperial prestige had been deemed "efficient" enough by the world to save her older sister, Zyfel resolved to hoard all wealth on earth—mocking mortal definitions of value while proving that nothing in existence could ever equate to the life of the sister they lost.

### 2. Commodification as Living Blood Stock
Tragically, Dr. Phoenix’s profound obsession with the **defiance of mortality** eclipsed her moral sanity. Examining Zsofia's blood and physiology, Dr. Phoenix discovered an extraordinary cellular profile with unique biological vitality. Rather than administering medicine to cure her ailment, Parischt saw in the sick girl the long-sought catalyst for conquering human aging and cellular decay.

Dr. Phoenix locked Zsofia inside a hidden, soundproof subterranean laboratory:
- Zsofia was reduced to a **living "blood stock"** (*Blut-Reservoir*).
- She was strapped into an airtight glass containment chamber, catheterized with fluid-harvesting lines, and continually subjected to invasive extraction machinery that systematically siphoned her vital fluids.
- Parischt utilized Zsofia's continuous blood draw to fuel radical immortality serums and cellular regenerative trials.

Trapped in the dark, unable to reach Zyfel or Zeeran, Zsofia's body grew progressively pale and frail. Day after day, her blood was drained until total biological exhaustion, acute anemia, and organ collapse ended her life in the glass chamber.

### 3. The Confrontation & Zyfel's Discovery
When Zeeran arrived to check on Zsofia's progress and bring her into House Twilight, he uncovered the horrifying reality: inside the secluded treatment chamber, Zsofia lay in a gruesome, hollowed state—already dead, her vitality completely siphoned away into harvesting vats by the woman he regarded as his mother. Overwhelmed by cold wrath and profound grief, Zeeran confronted Dr. Phoenix right beside Zsofia's lifeless body and carried out her execution.

Alerted by the sudden commotion and violent noises echoing from the treatment sector, young **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** rushed immediately to the room. Crossing the threshold, she was met with a scene of indelible horror: **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** striking down Dr. Phoenix, and beside them, Zsofia in the worst, most devastating condition imaginable, drained of life.

With her razor-sharp intellect, Zyfel needed no explanation—she immediately understood everything that had transpired. The older sister who had protected her in the Candlelight Room had been turned into livestock, and their beloved "Mother" had butchered her for science.

The trauma of that moment irrevocably shaped both siblings:
- For **Zeeran**, executing Dr. Phoenix was his heaviest moral burden—a grim testament that he would personally execute even those he loved if they dared exploit those under his protection.
- For **Zyfel**, witnessing Zsofia's drained corpse and Phoenix's death cemented the foundational dogma of her existence: that tenderness, goodwill, and love are utterly powerless without absolute leverage, monopoly, and unassailable power. She swore that no one she loved would ever be reduced to harvested prey again.
