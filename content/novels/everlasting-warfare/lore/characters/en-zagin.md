**En-zagin**, whose true name (*Shumu*) was **Amethyst Lorelei**, was a Human woman who served as the **First Entu** (Rank I) under the **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)** divine decree system created by Enki.

---

## Known Facts

- **Rank & Lineage:** Rank I Entu, standing at the beginning of the divine Entu lineage.
- **Race:** Human.
- **Divine Penalty & Erasure:** Driven by overwhelming selfishness, En-zagin violated the fundamental first rule of the Entu mandate (which requires acting solely for the safety of the world and prohibiting the pursuit of personal desires). 
- **The Ultimate Punishment:** Rather than simple execution, she was subjected to the ultimate divine penalty: her **Shumu** (True Name and Essence) was completely erased from the fabric of existence, leaving a void where the first Entu once stood.
- **Historical Discrepancy:** Because of her total erasure, mortal history recognizes only seven Entu, despite eight appearing in divine records.
