**Anna ni Euphratea** is the Second Princess of the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)** and the younger daughter of the late King and Queen. Following the tragic murder of her parents by her elder sister **[Erika ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)** and Erika's subsequent exile, Anna became the primary heir to the throne.

## Royal Lineage & The Succession Crisis

Anna was born as the second daughter of the King and Queen of Euphrates. Her grandmother, **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**, is the revered former Queen of the realm.

- **Trauma & Succession:** When her elder sister Erika murdered their parents three years ago (when Anna was only **14 years old**) and fled into exile, Anna was thrust into the position of immediate heir to the crown.
- **Unprepared to Rule:** Due to being only **14 years old** at the time and unready for sovereign governance following the tragic demise of her parents, Anna was deemed not yet ready to take the throne.

---

## Connection to Systemist & Priest Azazel

- **Initial Encounter (8 Years Ago):** Around eight years ago (~age 9), Anna met **[Azazel Vorgenstein](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azazel-vorgenstein.md)**, a priest of the **Systemist** order—a major religious institution in Euphrates branching from Marduk and Ishtar worship that also honors all Mesopotamian gods equally.
- **Undocumented Purpose & Perpetual Visits:** The reason for their initial meeting remains undocumented in royal records. However, from that pivotal day onward, Princess Anna has continually frequented the Temple of Systemist, maintaining regular visits to the sacred grounds.

## Current Position & Regency / Royal Command

Because Anna is not yet prepared to rule the kingdom independently:

- Her grandmother, **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**, stepped back in to assume the throne and direct command of the Euphrates Kingdom.
- Anna remains under Elizabeth's royal tutelage in Euphratea, preparing for the day when she will eventually be ready to fulfill her duties as queen.
- She is served by her loyal vanguard knight, **[Ultima](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ultima.md)**, who joined her knights seven years ago and often wears a maid's attire over her armor because Anna loves her cooking.

## The VIII Entu, The Broken Firmament & Forced Realm Re-Convergence

In the present era, the ancient cosmic barrier known as the **Firmament is broken**. When Anna officially receives her divine consecration as **VIII Entu (En-gurnun)** and gains spiritual access to **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)** (The Chamber of Fate), she accesses all of the world's hyper-accurate archives across the **[Seven Realms](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/seven-realms.md)** and the accumulated memories/wisdom of all previous Entu.

Through this divine awakening, Anna comes to understand the terrifying cosmic reality:
1. **Humanity's Fatal Blindspot:** Because Humanity's original candidate (**[En-zagin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-zagin.md)**) was erased, Humans were left without an Entu guide across six whole epochs. Consequently, Humanity was left completely ignorant of the ancient antediluvian era, Enki's world reset, and the looming invasion of the extraterrestrial **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**.
2. **The Imminent Threat:** Now that the Firmament is broken, the Aethernians will inevitably invade. While the other six races had spent ages preparing their magic, metallurgy, and physical prowess for this cosmic war, Humanity remains hopelessly unprepared and will be instantly crushed and extinguished.

### The Forced Maintenance Event

Faced with the imminent extinction of her species, Anna decides that waiting for the Aethernians means guaranteed annihilation for Humanity. Channeling her divine **Tu** authority and utilizing the metaphysical framework of Ubbshu-Ukkinna, Anna executes a desperate, cold-blooded decision: **she initiates a universal Maintenance event, forcibly merging all Seven Realms back into a single world**.

### Catastrophic Impact & The Merging of Cultures

The sudden spatial convergence of seven distinct Earths into one shared physical plane produces immediate, violent destruction:
- **Spatial Overlap:** Ancient landscapes and architectural monuments from other realms materialize inside human territory. For example, **[The Great Tree of Abramelin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/great-tree-of-abramelin.md)** from the Elven Realm suddenly manifests directly within human urban centers, its massive roots and trunk crushing human buildings and infrastructure.
- **Cultural & Demographic Collision:** Subterranean Dwarven strongholds, Demonic infernal domains, Spirit nexus points, Beastmen territories, and Elemental Beast environments crash into human cities and geography, instantly throwing all seven distinct cultures together in chaos.
- **Human Casualties & Anna's Calculation:** Humanity suffers the first and heaviest casualties ("the first to die") as fragile human structures crumble under the physical overlapping of realms. Yet, in Anna's cold calculation, a catastrophic re-convergence with heavy initial casualties is far better than allowing Humanity to be completely wiped out to the last soul by the incoming Aethernians without any combined defense.
