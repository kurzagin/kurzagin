**Nam-en En-sirsir**, born as **Abramelin Valentine** (recorded in the divine Entu registry as **Ruby Valentine**), was a legendary figure of the Elven race and the **Second Entu** appointed under the **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)** divine decree system. 

Renowned across millennia for her unparalleled mastery over [Magic](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/magic-system.md), she stands as the only recorded mortal ever to reach the **Advanced Rank** of magic formula proficiency.

---

## Pioneer of Spelled Magic (Active Magic)

En-sirsir was the first Elf in history to conceive the concept of **spellcaster magic (Active Magic)**. 

Historically, the [Elven](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elf.md) race did not rely on incantations, runes, or structured spell formulas. Because mana naturally circulated within their very veins, Elves used magic instinctually as an extension of their bodies—spontaneously watering gardens, igniting wood for fires, levitating heavy objects to assist in daily work, and manipulating raw energy at will. Because magic was so inherently seamless, there was no urgency or felt need among Elves to "spell" or formalize their arcane actions.

En-sirsir recognized the limits of pure instinct and envisioned a revolutionary approach: **Spelled Magic**. Fundamentally, she defined spelled magic as the art of weaving and shaping mana into structured, **programmable actions**—allowing magical effects to be engineered, optimized, and consistently replicated (laying the groundwork for active magic formulas, though not yet passive magic).

During the early phase of her career, En-sirsir spent years traveling among Elven settlements to preach and convince her people of this new doctrine. She argued that spelled magic was vastly more convenient, versatile, and precise than relying solely on raw biological mana flow. Though met with initial indifference from an ancient race accustomed to effortless innate magic, her persistent advocacy and supreme mastery eventually transformed Elven arcane tradition forever.

## The Sword of Abramelin & True Death

Unlike the other Entu—who inherited and wielded the **[Chain of Destruction](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/chain-of-destruction.md)** stored within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)**, as currently wielded by **En-gurnun** (**[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)**)—Abramelin was the sole exception who forged her own distinct path by creating a unique artifact: the **[Sword of Abramelin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/sword-of-abramelin.md)**.

- **True Death:** The *Sword of Abramelin* is feared across all realms as one of the exceedingly rare artifacts capable of inflicting **True Death**—an absolute, unalterable destruction that permanently eradicates a soul beyond reincarnation or spiritual recovery.
- **Levitation & Duplication Magic:** Forged with Advanced Rank spell formulas, the blade incorporates autonomous levitation magic (allowing remote, high-speed telekinetic flight) and duplication magic (enabling it to multiply into swarms of blades).
- **Anti-Mage Kit & The "Tyrant" Debates:** Modern scholars of Elven history point out that her weapon's toolkit is suspiciously hyper-optimized for hunting down and countering mages. By combining autonomous swarming blade duplications, remote pressure, and soul-eradicating True Death, the weapon leaves spellcasters no window to weave spells or recover. Consequently, contemporary historians fiercely debate whether En-sirsir was truly just a benevolent Entu pioneer, or actually a tyrant who designed her arsenal specifically to suppress and hunt down rogue mages.
- **Craftsmanship & Arcane Mastery:** Forged using Advanced Rank magic formulas, the weapon stood as the pinnacle of Elven magical catalyst engineering.

## Lifespan, Demise & Physical Monument

Abramelin lived for **60,000 years**, guiding her people and fulfilling her duties as Entu across ages before finally meeting her demise.

- **Soul in Ubbshu-Ukkinna:** Following her physical death, her soul did not undergo reincarnation; instead, in accordance with the laws of **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)**, her essence became permanently embedded within **[Ubbshu-Ukkinna](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/ubbshu-ukkina.md)** (The Chamber of Fate), where she continues to reside alongside other past Entu spirits.
- **The Great Tree of Abramelin:** At the exact moment of her death, the *Sword of Abramelin* fell to the earth from her hands. Upon striking the soil, the divine blade transformed into **The Great Tree of Abramelin**—a colossal, living arboreal monument that endures as a sacred sanctuary and testament to her ancient power.
