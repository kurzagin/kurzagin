**Enki** was originally a man of the **Plain Race**, introduced by **[Nam-an](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-an.md)** following the cosmic **Grand Maintenance** event. Appointed by Nam-an as **The King** and proclaimed the **Lord of Earth**, Enki introduced governance and kingship to Earth, unifying the six primordial warring races and creating the **[Human Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/human.md)** in his image.

---

## Known Facts & Origin

- **The Plain Race & Appointment:** Introduced by Nam-an post-Maintenance as the sole member of the Plain Race. Nam-an appointed him as The King ("Lord of Earth") to lead Earth's inhabitants out of chaos.
- **Unification of the Six Races:** Before Enki, the six unnamed original races created by Nam-an possessed deep physical hatred toward one another and lacked government, causing endless war. Enki introduced kingship, governance, and the **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)** framework, successfully unifying the six lineages.
- **Creation of Humanity:** Following the unification, Enki created the **[Human Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/human.md)** in his own image, basing their mortal form on his Plain Race morphology.
- **Wielder of Nam-an's Power:** Received **[Nam-shub](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-shub.md)** (the divine power of Nam-an) and **Shumu** (True Name authority) to establish physical decrees (ME tablets).
- **Accidental Physicalization of Nam-an:** While using Nam-shub in the Antediluvian Era, Enki accidentally condensed Nam-an's omnipotence into a physical **clay cup**, which he preserved at the bottom of **[The Abzu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/abzu.md)**.
- **Communication & Entu System:** Enki cannot speak freely within the physical cosmos; he governs through mortal proxies known as **Entu**, granting each chosen Entu a fraction of Nam-shub designated as **Tu**.
- **The Seven Realms Separation:** During the **[Antediluvian War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**, Enki was losing against the invading off-world **[Aethernians](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**. Lacking future foresight, Enki acted based on the painful history of the early days before his kingship (the **[Six Signs of Being](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/six-signs-of-being.md)** era), knowing that without a King present, the seven races would succumb to ancestral inter-race hatred and destroy each other. Because the Aethernians still existed outside the barrier in outer space, Enki logically divided the world into **[The Seven Realms](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/seven-realms.md)** behind the **Firmament**, isolating each race so they could survive and prepare independently without destroying one another.
- **Adopted Daughter & Heir (Diapatri):** Named **[Diapatri](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/diapatri.md)** ("Through The Father") as his adopted daughter and appointed heir, entrusting her as the Iudex of Mesopotamia with the ME of Lawship.

## Global Mesopotamian Sovereignty & Shattered Reincarnation

- **Sole Global Civilization:** Under Enki, **Mesopotamia** was the sole, unified civilization and kingdom on Earth, with his territory and reign encompassing the entire globe.
- **Shattered Soul Reincarnation:** When the Antediluvian Era fell, Enki's soul did **not** vanish into nothingness. Bound by his sacred cosmic role as **The King**, his soul shattered into divine fragments and entered the cycle of reincarnation.
- **Legacy in Historical Leaders & Conquerors:** Because leadership is his fundamental nature, every great leader, emperor, and conqueror throughout history carries a fragment of Enki's shattered soul. This eternal soul fragment is the true source of their innate leadership charisma and their insatiable, driving ambition to establish a unified **World Order** or bring the entire world under a single rule.
