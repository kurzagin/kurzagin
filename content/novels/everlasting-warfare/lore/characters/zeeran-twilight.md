**Zeeran Twilight** is the Ex Supreme Commander of the **Tigris Empire** forces, key figure of the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)**, and the patriarch of the prestigious **Twilight Family**. Currently working as a Scientist for the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**, he was the **First Child** (`ZR4N-4`) enrolled in the White House covert human experimentation project, where he underwent intense intelligence grading and consumed the physical enhancement drug **[Vigortio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/vigortio.md)** since early childhood. At thirty years old (standing at 171 cm), his physical condition is severe and chronic, leaving him wheelchair-bound, yet his scientific brilliance, tactical genius, manipulative political intellect, and unyielding refusal to lie make him one of the most formidable figures in Euphrates.

- **Status & Condition:** Severely crippled (Wheelchair-bound) / Scientist for Euphrates / Absence of biological heart (fitted with mechanical **[Maschinenherz](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/maschinenherz.md)**) / Severe chronic physical degeneration due to long-term consumption of **Vigortio** since childhood (dampened via regular doses of **[Resiliexcuratio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/resiliexcuratio.md)**) / Post-war psychological illness (Temporal Dissociation & Trauma-induced delusion regarding Zell).
- **Experimental Status:** First Child (`ZR4N-4`) of the **White House** Human Experimentation Program.
- **Psychological Condition:** Severe post-war trauma and temporal dissociation. Unable to separate past and present, he firmly believes his deceased sister **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** is still alive and residing in the secret underground facility alongside him, **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**, and **[Liezt Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md)**.

## Background & Military Career

Zeeran Twilight was brought to the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** at around **~6 years old**—a secret human experiment facility hidden deep within the forests of the Tigris Empire and disguised as an orphanage. Stripped of his real name, he was given the experimental serial code **ZR4N-4**. 

As the first subject of the cohort, Zeeran was housed in a research ward designated as the **"Twilight Room"**, a title he adopted to forge their family surname, **House Twilight**. Over the following years, as younger test subjects arrived, Zeeran personally named each sibling by phonetically deriving names from their assigned serial codes:
- **ZR4N-4** → **Zeeran** (~6 yrs old)
- **ZR3-4** → **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** (+1 yr later)
- **LZT-4** → **[Liezt Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md)** (+1 yr later)
- **ZLL-4** → **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** & **CZ-4** → **[Chizu Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/chizu-twilight.md)** (+1 yr later)
- **ZFL-4** → **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** (+1 yr later)
- **ZP-4** → **[Zephyr Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zephyr-twilight.md)** (+6 months later)

Subjected to exhaustive cognitive testing, Zeeran achieved top-tier marks in strategic reasoning. To enhance his physical capabilities for future military leadership, Zeeran was administered **[Vigortio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/vigortio.md)** since early childhood. Dispatched by the White House under a cover identity from an unknown village to demonstrate the facility's engineered superiority to the military, he entered the Tigris Military Academy. Rising rapidly through the military hierarchy, he earned the rank of Supreme Commander before turning twenty, feared across the battlefield as a cold, unrelenting commander. It was on the battlefield at around **~20 years old** that Zeeran first encountered musket weaponry—a relatively new invention developed by Euphrates specifically for the 100-Year War.

### Command in the Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War & The 1634 Defeat
Zeeran's greatest strategic test came during the climax of the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)**. Appointed supreme commander of the Tigris forces, Zeeran deployed brilliant asymmetrical maneuvers, terrain traps, and aggressive vanguard strikes led by his sister **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**. 

Zeeran's overwhelming strategic pressure was so severe that it created an unmatched crisis for Euphrates high command—finally compelling **[ur-sag̃](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ur-sag.md)**, the giant half-naked General of Euphrates who normally refused to fight due to seeing no urgency in standard warfare, to personally march onto the battlefield. Azure's relentless push shattered enemy defense lines and brought the Tigris Army to the very threshold of **Euphratea** (the capital of the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**), placing the Tigris Empire on the verge of total victory.

However, at the decisive moment of invasion, critical intelligence reached Supreme Commander Zeeran: the location of the top-secret **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** facility had been traced, revealed, and placed under heavy assault—while his sister **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** was inside.

Prioritizing his sister's life over imperial conquest, Zeeran immediately abandoned the frontline command post and rushed to the White House. Rushing inside the burning compound, he made the tragic discovery that Zell had already died from severe burns. Heartbroken, Zeeran carried Zell's body out of the ruined facility, only to be immediately surrounded by an elite force of Euphrates Cavalry led by Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**.

Queen Elizabeth confronted him and commanded him to end the war immediately. In a state of grim desperation, Zeeran questioned her: *"Why? We were invading your country and about to end the war. Azure was right there and almost won."*

In response, Ex-Queen Elizabeth leaned close and whispered a secret revelation directly into his ear. Overwhelmed by what he heard, Zeeran yielded and ordered the immediate stand-down of all Tigris forces. Following the war's conclusion seven years ago (when Zeeran was **23 years old**):
- **Post-War Palace Visit & Undocumented Encounter:** Zeeran visited the Euphratea palace directly after the war ended *(having never set foot in Euphrates prior to that moment)*. Inside the palace, 14-year-old Princess **[Erika ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)** accidentally ran into him without knowing his true identity—an encounter that remained undocumented in royal archives.
- **Relocation & Exile:** The **[Twilight Family](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)** lost their ancestral house, estate, and imperial titles in Tigris. Patriarch Zeeran relocated the surviving family to **Euphratea**, capital of the victorious Euphrates Kingdom, where they live in exile within a secret underground facility built by Queen Elizabeth as a secret R&D scientist division. Recognizing Azure's lifelong passion for research and development, Zeeran and **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** (the former Lead Chief Scientist of Tigris) work as scientists within this secret division.
- **Reunion with Exiled Erika in the Underground Base (3 Years Ago):** Three years ago, following 18-year-old Erika's regicide against her parents, royal Euphrates knights hunted her through the capital. **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** saved Erika from the knights and brought her down into the secret underground facility to meet Zeeran (then 27 yrs old). Erika did not know who Azure or Zeeran were at first, but upon seeing Zeeran, she suddenly remembered that he was the exact man she had accidentally encountered at the palace four years earlier when she was 14. Over the following three years, Erika went on to forge her own powerful organization.

Meanwhile, the cumulative toxicity of Vigortio continuously degraded his body. At thirty years old in the present day, his physical condition has reached a severe chronic state, leaving him permanently paralyzed and confined to a mechanical wheelchair. Living as an exiled scientist inside the underground facility beneath Euphratea, Zeeran provides strategic and scientific intellect for Elizabeth's projects while protecting his sisters, despite Elizabeth's deep resentment and contemptuous labeling of them as **"Tigris rats"**.

## Psychological Profile & Belief System

The tragic loss of **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** in the White House fire inflicted a catastrophic psychological trauma on Zeeran. Following the war, alongside his severe physical degeneration, he developed an incurable post-war psychological illness: a severe temporal dissociation where he cannot separate the past from the present.

In his fractured mind, Zeeran cannot process or accept Zell's death. He lives under the firm, unshakeable delusion that Zell is still alive, residing and working alongside him, **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**, and **[Liezt Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md)** in the secret underground facility beneath Euphratea. Because Zeeran's code of absolute pride forbids lying under any circumstance, his statements about Zell being alive are not deliberate deception—to his perception, Zell is genuinely present in the room with them. Azure and Liezt carefully protect and preserve this delicate reality; Liezt occasionally uses her psychological mimicry to recreate Zell's presence or voice from afar, shielding their patriarch from a complete mental collapse.

- **The Execution of "Mother" (Dr. Phoenix):** Compounding his internal scars was the tragic execution of **[Dr. Phoenix](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-phoenix.md)** (Parischt Chartrenix). Having trusted his beloved childhood caretaker to treat **Zsofia**, Zeeran discovered that Parischt had succumbed to her obsession with defying mortality and turned Zsofia into a harvested "blood stock" for cellular trials. Unflinching in his moral judgment, Zeeran personally put down the only mother he had ever known—a grim testament that he will execute even those he loves if they cross the line of exploiting those under his protection.

## Grand Ambition: The New World Order

Zeeran Twilight's ultimate, unyielding goal for the **[Twilight Family](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)** is the establishment of a **[New World Order](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/new-world-order-project.md)**. Born directly from his childhood promise to **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** to transform the world into one she could love, the project is not a radical political ideology—it is the deliberate architectural expansion of the only space Azure ever loved: **the Twilight Room**.

- **Expanding the Twilight Room to the World:** Because Azure hates the world and only loves her family, Zeeran's logic is absolute: if Azure can only love what belongs to House Twilight, then everything on Earth must be brought under the control of or total dependency upon House Twilight. By extending the family's reach until the entire globe becomes an extension of the Twilight Room, Zeeran seeks to build a world where his sister can finally stand anywhere and feel safe.
- **Total Control & Systemic Dependency:** Rather than conventional military conquest, Zeeran architects systemic reliance—ensuring that continental trade, banking systems, technological advancements, and medical solutions cannot function without House Twilight's permission and oversight.
- **Universal Family Alignment & Zyfel's Support:** All Twilight siblings understand the loving origin of Zeeran's ambition. **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** serves as the direct economic enforcer of his ideals, wielding the **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md)** and the global **[Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md)** currency standard to turn Zeeran's promise into economic reality.

## Character Traits & Unique Behaviors

- **Absolute Pride & Unvarnished Honesty:** Zeeran operates under a code of absolute pride. In his worldview, lying is an act of hiding, and to hide something implies acknowledging a higher authority or fearing consequences. Because his pride permits nothing to stand above him, he refuses to lie under any circumstance.
- **Uncompromised Disclosure & The Arrogance Dilemma:** If an enemy happens to ask Zeeran what his plan is, Zeeran will tell them without hesitation. He will never give a vague or evasive answer; instead, he will hand them every single detail of the plan, laying out all the information they could possibly need. However, how the enemy interprets it is a completely different matter. If the enemy disbelieves his plan, thinking it is a trap or a bluff, it proves they are beneath Zeeran because their suspicion stems from their own deceitful nature and inability to comprehend absolute pride. Conversely, if the enemy actually believes him and accepts his plan as truth, it is *also* a matter of pride—it forces them to confront whether they are willing to admit their inferiority by taking his word. Either way, whether they believe him or reject his truth, it solely serves to prove that they are beneath Zeeran.
- **Temporal Dissociation & Illusion of Zell's Survival:** His post-war psychological illness blurred past and present entirely. To Zeeran, Zell is not dead; she is actively living in the secret underground facility alongside him, Azure, and Liezt. Because his absolute pride prevents him from lying, his insistence on Zell's living presence is a tragic manifestation of his trauma.
- **Tactical Ownership:** When military operations fail or miscalculate, Zeeran admits errors with uncompromising pride rather than deflecting blame onto weather, terrain, or subordinates.
- **Equal Supremacism & Manipulation:** Zeeran holds a deeply rooted supremacist belief, looking down on all outsiders equally. His prejudice is not based on physical appearance or skin color, but rather on an absolute conviction of his family's superiority. He conceals this contempt behind polished aristocratic manners and calculated manipulation.
- **Hatred of ur-sag̃ & Deployment-Avoidance Strategy:** Zeeran harbors an intense, profound hatred for **[ur-sag̃](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ur-sag.md)**, viewing him not as an honorable soldier or general, but as an uncontrollable "monster in human form." To prevent this monster from ruining carefully structured operational balances, Zeeran repeatedly designed complex, low-intensity tactical maneuvers specifically calculated to keep conditions below the threshold that would force Euphrates to deploy him.
- **The Paradox of Appearance:** Despite his supreme tactical brilliance, Zeeran was bound by a frustrating paradox: whenever he tried his hardest to craft strategies to avoid deploying ur-sag̃, ur-sag̃ would inevitably appear on the battlefield anyway.
- **Reliance on Azure:** When ur-sag̃ inevitably breached the field, Zeeran knew that standard Tigris forces stood no chance. **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** was the only warrior in existence physically capable of holding and stalling the monster in combat.
- **Messianic Perception:** Because the Tigris Empire high command and White House are rife with corruption and political deception, Zeeran's absolute refusal to lie has led the common populace and soldiers to view him as a messianic leader—the single truthful pillar of the Empire.
- **House Twilight Devotion & Protection of Azure:** He is fiercely proud of House Twilight and intensely protective of his sisters. Despite his crippled physical state, Zeeran remains steadfastly at his sister **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**'s side every single time she undergoes the agonizing ritual of fracturing her regenerating, non-functional wings to keep them concealed.

> The lieutenant inspected the walls, roof, and entrance carefully through the high-powered glass, searching for any ritual iconography or sacrosanct markings. After a long moment, he shook his head:
>
> *"No, sir. There are no religious marks visible in particular."*
>
> Zeeran adjusted his gloves, his voice completely cold and devoid of hesitation:
>
> > *"Then it is not a temple under the law. Continue the attack."*
>
> Without a second thought, the artillery fire commenced, flattening the sector in seconds. While Tigris high command viewed laws as meaningless suggestions, Zeeran defined laws and sanctuaries purely by objective, verifiable facts—if a building bore no mark, it held no protection under his standard.
