**Ishtar** is one of the divine rulers of **Mesopotamia**, governing the realm alongside **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** and the other gods during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**. 

---

## Divine Domain & ME Ownership

- **Divine Ruler of Mesopotamia:** Stands as a paramount sovereign within the divine pantheon of ancient Mesopotamia, co-ruling the cosmos with Enki.
- **Wielder of Various ME:** Possesses multiple **[ME](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/me.md)** (Divine Decrees / Physical Tablets of Destiny), granting her cosmic authority over her divine domains.
- **Domains of Beauty and War:** Her foundational authority encompasses both transcendent **Beauty** and ferocious **Warfare**, embodying both enticing harmony and unyielding battle prowess.
- **Goddess of Ambition and Love:** Ishtar instills passion, fierce ambition, and romantic love within mortal hearts. Under her divine ethos, **protecting your loved one is held as a sacred act of divine virtue**.

## Anecdotes: The Sky Paradise & The Dragged Ear

### The Request for Heaven on Earth
During the height of the Antediluvian Era, Ishtar approached King **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** with a flamboyant request: she wished for a "heaven on earth" where she could relax away from cosmic duties. Enki, believing that the hardworking goddess truly deserved a well-earned vacation, agreed to her plea. Utilizing his divine engineering, Enki constructed a breathtaking floating paradise anchored high in the sky above Mesopotamia.

### The Non-Stop Sky Party
Overjoyed by the gift, Ishtar immediately ascended to the sky heaven, bringing along a huge entourage of her mortal friends and followers. What was meant to be a quiet retreat swiftly turned into a perpetual, non-stop grand party filled with magnificent feasts, endless music, and unrestrained merriment that lasted day and night.

### The Crisis of Ambition & Diapatri's Scolding
As the festivities raged on in the sky, **[Diapatri](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/diapatri.md)**, the supreme Iudex of Mesopotamia, noticed a concerning phenomenon across the mortal realm: people down on Earth were losing their work ethic and drive, suffering from a severe lack of ambition as they spent their days gazing upward and daydreaming about the celestial party.

Investigating the source of this widespread apathy, Diapatri marched straight to Enki's court, only to discover that her father was the one who had built the sky paradise for Ishtar in the first place. Enraged by the disruption to legal order and mortal societal productivity, Diapatri thoroughly scolded her father for enabling Ishtar's reckless indulgence.

### "Five More Minutes!"
Chastised and embarrassed by his adopted daughter's stern lecture, Enki floated up to the sky paradise to end the party. Ignoring Ishtar's complaints, the Lord of Earth reached out, grabbed Ishtar firmly by her ear, and dragged the squirming goddess all the way back down to Earth. Throughout the entire descent, Ishtar whined and pleaded desperately:

> *"Just five more minutes! Enki, please, just five more minutes!"*
