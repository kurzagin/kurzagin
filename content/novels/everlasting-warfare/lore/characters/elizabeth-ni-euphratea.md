**Elizabeth ni Euphratea** is the former sovereign queen and active ruling matriarch of the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**. A central figure in continental history, her direct personal intervention at the climax of the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)** fundamentally altered the fate of both nations and House Twilight.

- **Key Characteristics:** Commanding royal presence, iconic Euphrates features (blonde hair and red eyes), possessor of top-level classified intelligence, intense hatred toward Tigris.

## Royal Succession & Retaking Supreme Command

While Elizabeth had previously stepped down from active rule in favor of her child (the reigning King) and his Queen, a devastating royal tragedy forced her to re-ascend the throne:

- **The Royal Assassination & Exile:** Her eldest granddaughter, **[Erika ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)**, was designated as the Crown Princess. However, when the King and Queen chose not to pass the throne to Erika at age 22, Erika committed regicide by murdering her parents, leading to her immediate banishment and exile.
- **Assuming the Throne:** With the King and Queen dead and Erika exiled, the second daughter **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)** became the rightful heir. However, because Anna was only **16 years old** and not yet ready to shoulder the responsibilities of sovereign governance, Elizabeth retook full command of the realm and assumed the throne to stabilize the kingdom.

## Interception at the White House (1634)

During the final days of the war in **1634**, while Tigris vanguard commander **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** was pushing Euphrates forces to the brink of defeat at the gates of Euphratea, Queen Elizabeth led an elite division of Euphrates Cavalry deep into imperial forest territory to the secret **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** compound.

- **Surrounding the Ruins:** As Supreme Commander **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** emerged from the engulfed facility carrying the burnt corpse of his sister **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)**, he found himself instantly surrounded by Elizabeth's cavalry detachment.
- **The Ultimatum & The Whisper:** Queen Elizabeth confronted the grief-stricken commander, ordering him to stop the war immediately. When Zeeran protested with grim desperation—asking why he should surrender when Tigris had invaded Euphratea and Azure was on the verge of total victory—Elizabeth leaned in and whispered a secret revelation directly into his ear.
- **The End of the War:** Whatever truth Elizabeth shared in that whisper struck Zeeran so deeply that he immediately ordered the Tigris Army to cease all offensive operations. The war officially concluded with the Euphrates Kingdom declared victorious, leading to the capitulation treaty and House Twilight's exile in Euphratea.

## Post-War Rule: Secret Division & Resentment Toward Tigris

Following the war, Ex-Queen Elizabeth established a top-secret scientist division housed within a hidden **underground facility** beneath **Euphratea**.

- **Secret Scientist Division:** She placed the exiled **[Twilight Family](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)**—specifically Zeeran Twilight and Azure Twilight—to work as research scientists within this secret subterranean facility to exploit their intellect for Euphrates' technological projects.
- **Resentment & "Tigris Rats":** Despite using House Twilight for scientific research, Elizabeth harbors deep-seated resentment and hatred toward Tigris as a whole. Viewing the imperial exiled nobility with contempt, she sees them as nothing more than vermin and derogatorily refers to them as **"Tigris rats"**.
- **The Sovereign Face of the [Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md):** Following the war, Elizabeth oversaw the creation of the **Euphry**, the new global economic currency. Her royal image was engraved on the primary face of all minted gold coins and paper banknotes alongside the **Ouroboros** crest of House Twilight, immortalizing her post-war economic reign.
