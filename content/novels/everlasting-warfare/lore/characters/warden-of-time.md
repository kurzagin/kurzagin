The **Warden of Time** is the celestial guardian elevated to cosmic responsibility and bound by the **[ME](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/me.md)** of Shumu to rule time itself.

---

## Station & Isolation

- **Domain of Anshar:** Resides in **Anshar** (Heaven), which functions as both a throne room and an inescapable cage.
- **The Timeship:** Keeper of the metaphysical **Timeship**, through which causality flows.
- **The Curse of Dominion:** Exercises absolute authority to rule, observe, and maintain temporal order, yet is strictly forbidden and unable to leave Anshar or join normal mortal existence.
