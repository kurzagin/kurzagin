**Ultima** is a prominent Royal Knight of the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)** and a devoted personal guardian of Second Princess **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)**. Joining the Knights of Anna seven years ago, Ultima has developed an exceptionally close bond with the young heir, serving as both her protective vanguard and trusted culinary attendant.

---

## Profile & Demographics

- **Full Name:** Ultima
- **Titles / Aliases:** Knight of Anna, The Maid Knight
- **Age:** 27 years old (Present Day; joined Anna's service at age 20)
- **Gender:** Female
- **Race:** Human
- **Affiliation:** Euphrates Kingdom Royal Court / Knights of Anna
- **Status:** Active / Royal Knight
- **Key Physical Characteristics:** Striking blonde hair and vivid red eyes.
- **Attire:** Frequently seen wearing a maid's outfit layered over her formal knight uniform whenever she presents herself before Princess Anna.

---

## Personality & Relationship with Anna

- **Absolute Loyalty:** Ultima is fiercely dedicated to Princess **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)**, guarding her with unyielding resolve through the political turbulence of the Euphrates court.
- **Close Bond:** Having served Anna since the princess was a child (~10 years old), Ultima is one of Anna's most trusted confidantes and pillars of support.
- **Culinary Skill & Maid Attire:** Beyond her martial prowess as a knight, Ultima is an extraordinary cook. Because Princess Anna particularly adores her cooking, Ultima routinely dons a maid's outfit over her knight uniform when visiting Anna to prepare meals and care for her.

---

## Service History & Timeline

- **Joining the Knights of Anna (7 Years Ago — Age 20):** Seven years ago, following the conclusion of the 100 Year War, Ultima officially entered into service as one of the knights sworn to Princess Anna (~10 years old at the time).
- **The Succession Crisis (3 Years Ago):** When Crown Princess Erika committed regicide and was banished, leaving 14-year-old Anna as heir under Ex-Queen Elizabeth's regency, Ultima stood steadfast by Anna's side to protect her.
- **Present Day (Age 27):** Continues her loyal duty as an elite defender of the realm and personal culinary knight to Princess Anna.
