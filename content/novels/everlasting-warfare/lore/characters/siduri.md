**Siduri** was the architect, master gardener, and primary guardian of **[Eridu Garden](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/eridu-garden.md)** during **[The Antediluvian Era](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/antediluvian-era.md)**.

---

## Intellect & Stewardship

- **Cover & Genius:** Appeared to be a humble village woman, yet possessed an intellect surpassing the greatest scholars of Mesopotamia.
- **Guardian of Longevity:** Formulated the cultivation techniques for **[Tea of Eridu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/tea-of-eridu.md)** and protected the secrets of the **Elixir of Life**.
