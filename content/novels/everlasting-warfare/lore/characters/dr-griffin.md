**Dr. Griffin** was the Chief Lead Scientist and director of the human experimentation program at the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)**, the top-secret imperial black-site hidden in the deep forests of the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**. 

While the Tigris Imperial High Command funded the facility with the pragmatic goal of forging brute-force "super-soldiers" to achieve military supremacy, Dr. Griffin held a far more audacious, philosophical, and unhinged vision. To Griffin, creating obedient physical weapons was a waste of rare human anomaly. Instead, he designed a grand socio-evolutionary incubator to answer a single question: *What happens when enhanced human geniuses are confined together—will they destroy each other through competition, forge absolute unification, or transcend humanity to become gods?*

## Divergence from Imperial Directives

When the Tigris Imperial High Command commissioned the White House black-site under the cover of an isolated orphanage, their orders were explicit: produce an army of enhanced, brainwashed super-soldiers embodying the martial might of the legendary hero Gilgamesh.

Dr. Griffin outwardly complied, delivering physical enhancement drugs like **[Vigortio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/vigortio.md)** (*Vigor-Kraftum*) and performing radical bio-prosthetic surgeries like the **[Maschinenherz](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/maschinenherz.md)**. However, internally, he despised the government's pedestrian obsession with simple super-soldiers. Griffin viewed true genius and enhanced human capacity not as weapons for border expansion, but as catalytic elements of evolutionary transcendence.

## The Core Hypothesis: "Compete, Unify, or Become Gods?"

Griffin established the **Twilight Room**—a centralized, shared living and research ward within the White House compound—where he gathered seven ultra-graded child subjects with radically distinct cognitive, physical, and psychological enhancements.

His secret master trial was governed by a single overarching hypothesis:

> *"If you place a group of artificially enhanced human geniuses into a single isolated room, what is their inevitable dynamic? Do they turn inward and compete until only one supreme apex remains? Do they coalesce into an unbreakable, single-minded collective entity? Or do their combined intellects shatter human limitations entirely, elevating them to gods?"*

To test this:
1. **Intense Pressure & Individuality:** Each child was subjected to unique trials—Zeeran's supreme tactical intelligence, Azure's mass/gravitational surges, Liezt's blank-slate persona mirroring, Chizu's pain-severed nerve pathways, and Zell's refined noble bloodline.
2. **Forced Proximity:** Rather than isolating the test subjects in solitary holding cells, Griffin forced them to live, eat, and endure recovery together in the Twilight Room.
3. **Observation of Emergent Order:** Griffin watched as **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** rejected imperial serial numbers (`ZR4N-4`, `ZR3-4`, etc.), phonetically assigned them names, and forged the shared identity of **House Twilight**. To Tigris generals, this was an intriguing side-effect of cohort bonding; to Dr. Griffin, it was the first proof of unification and collective self-deification.
4. **The Essential Counterbalance & Scientific Authorship:** While Griffin orchestrated the macro-level evolutionary experiment, roughly 80% of the actual experimental variant designs (including Azure's wings and legs, Zeeran's Vigortio regimen, Liezt's blank-slate cognition, and chimera splicing) were authored by his wife, **[Dr. Phoenix](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-phoenix.md)** (Parischt Chartrenix), driven by her obsession to defy human mortality. In a remarkable paradox, Parischt genuinely, deeply loved the children as a true mother—nursing them through the agony of her own blueprints with warm food, tender medical care, and emotional grounding. Griffin quietly relied upon her maternal devotion, recognizing that without her love preserving the children's sanity and physical lives, his experiment would have ended in catastrophic failure before it ever began.

## The Destruction of the White House (1634) & Real-Time Continuation

During the climax of the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)** in 1634, external forces raided and torched the secret facility. The White House was reduced to ash, killing subject **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** (`ZLL-4`) and causing Supreme Commander Zeeran to abandon the frontlines, precipitating the fall of Tigris.

To the imperial military, the burning of the White House was a catastrophic failure that destroyed years of black-budget research.

To **Dr. Griffin**, however, the physical burning of the facility was merely the transition from **Phase 1 (Laboratory Incubation)** to **Phase 2 (Real-Time Global Experiment)**.

Griffin recognized that the laboratory walls were no longer necessary. The destruction of the White House cast the surviving Twilight siblings out into the wide world—manipulating kingdoms, leading armies, engineering revolutionary technologies, waging war, and constructing shadow operations like the **[New World Order Project](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/new-world-order-project.md)**. 

Even long after the fires have cooled, Dr. Griffin considers his experiment to be operating in **real-time**:
- **Compete:** The siblings struggle against external empires, internal psychological scars, and physical degeneration.
- **Unify:** Their absolute loyalty to House Twilight and their shared trauma binds them beyond national allegiance.
- **Become Gods:** Through master plans to reshape global order and alter reality itself, the cohort is actively fulfilling Griffin's ultimate question.
