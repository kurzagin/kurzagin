The **White House** is a top-secret classified black-site facility and human experimentation program fully facilitated and secretly funded by the **Tigris Empire** government. Operating deep within dense, uncharted forest territory far beyond imperial urban centers, its physical location remains completely undetected by ordinary civilians and the general public.

The name *"White House"* designates both the physical facility structure and the overarching covert research project.

---

## Religious Mythos & The Pursuit of the Next Gilgamesh

Unlike conventional military research centers, the founding philosophy of the White House is anchored in ancient imperial theology:

- **Cult of Gilgamesh:** The iron-headed leaders of Tigris venerate **Gilgamesh**, the legendary ancient god-king renowned for unmatched martial strength, divine endurance, and heroic perfection.
- **Project Goal — The Next Gilgamesh:** Believing that victory should be achieved through absolute physical and mental supremacy rather than reliance on mechanical firearms, the White House was created to forge the **"next Gilgamesh"**—a supreme human asset who embodies the divine power of the ancient hero.

---

## Cover & Operational Structure

To maintain absolute secrecy while securing testing subjects, the Tigris Empire government established the White House under the guise of an isolated orphanage. 

- **Orphanage Front:** The facility systematically gathers orphaned children across imperial territories, presenting itself as a charitable institution offering sanctuary and education.
- **Clandestine Research Site:** Behind closed doors, the institution operates as a cold, rigorous experimental complex dedicated to shaping elite military assets and evaluating human limits.

---

## Research Focus & Human Experimentation

The White House project focuses heavily on physiological enhancement and multi-dimensional cognitive development to awaken Gilgamesh-like potential:

1. **Intelligence Grading & Evaluation:** Children enrolled in the program undergo exhaustive testing to measure, categorize, and grade various forms of intelligence—ranging from tactical reasoning and spatial awareness to emotional manipulation and raw cognitive processing.
2. **Physical Capability Enhancement & Vigor-Kraftum:** Subjects are subjected to brutal physical conditioning and experimental drug regimens—most notably **[Vigortio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/vigortio.md)** (*Vigor-Kraftum*), a performance-enhancing combat drug designed to force muscle tissue expansion, though causing eventual paralysis (*LähmKraftum*).
3. **Sensory & Pain Processing Experiments:** Subject **[Chizu Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/chizu-twilight.md)** underwent sensory enhancement trials that backfired, completely severing her nervous system's ability to process pain (modified Congenital Analgesia).
4. **Psychological Re-formatting (The Mirror Experiment):** Subject **[Liezt Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md)** was subjected to total psychological re-formatting to evaluate whether a human could become a blank slate capable of synthesizing any persona.
5. **Noble DNA Refinement:** Subject **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** was transferred from Euphrates noble bloodlines into the program for genetic and cognitive refinement.
6. **Avian Adaptation & Flight Trials (Failed):** Experimental biological trials aimed at engineering human flight. Subject **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** was subjected to these trials, resulting in vestigial, regenerating wing structures; Tigris scientists officially designated the project a complete failure.
7. **Emergency Cybernetic Surgery & Maschinenherz Transplants:** When extreme physiological trials caused cardiac collapse in premier subjects like **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** and **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**, surgical teams excised their biological hearts and implanted a **[Maschinenherz](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/maschinenherz.md)** (*mechanical heart*) to keep their key experimental assets alive.

## Subject Designations, Naming & The Twilight Room

Upon entry into the White House, all subjects were forcibly stripped of their birth names and official identities, assigned purely alphanumeric serial designations:

- **Admission Timeline & Serial Designations:**
  - **Zeeran (`ZR4N-4`):** Admitted at ~6 years old as the first subject of the Twilight cohort.
  - **Azure (`ZR3-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~Zeeran age 7).
  - **Liezt (`LZT-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~Zeeran age 8).
  - **Zell (`ZLL-4`) & Chizu (`CZ-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~Zeeran age 9).
  - **Zyfel (`ZFL-4`):** Admitted +1 year later (~Zeeran age 10).
  - **Zephyr (`ZP-4`):** Admitted +6 months later (~Zeeran age 10.5).

- **Origin of Names:** Rather than letting his siblings remain nameless subjects of imperial research, Zeeran personally named each sibling one by one by phonetically deriving names from their serial codes: `ZR4N-4` (*Zeeran*), `ZR3-4` (*Azure*), `LZT-4` (*Liezt*), `ZLL-4` (*Zell*), `CZ-4` (*Chizu*), `ZFL-4` (*Zyfel*), and `ZP-4` (*Zephyr*).
- **Origin of the Surname "Twilight":** All seven subjects were housed together in a designated research ward known within the compound as the **"Twilight Room"**. Zeeran adopted the name of their room as their permanent family surname, forging the identity of **House Twilight**.

## Key Personnel & Subject History

### Lead Scientist: Dr. Griffin
**[Dr. Griffin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-griffin.md)** served as the Chief Lead Scientist and director of human experimentation within the White House facility. 

While the imperial government demanded conventional super-soldiers, Dr. Griffin pursued a far more complex evolutionary agenda. He conceived the "Twilight Room" cohort experiment to answer a fundamental philosophical question: *What happens if enhanced human geniuses are placed together in a single room—do they compete against each other, unify into an unbreakable bond, or transcend into gods?* 

### Senior Scientist & "Mother": Dr. Phoenix (Parischt Chartrenix)
**[Dr. Phoenix](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-phoenix.md)**, real name **Parischt Chartrenix**, was a senior medical and evolutionary scientist at the facility and the wife of Dr. Griffin. 

Driven by a philosophical obsession with the **defiance of human mortality**—firmly believing human beings should not be bound by physical frailty, decay, or death—she was the scientific architect behind roughly **80% of all experimental variant proposals** executed at the White House. Her blueprints included **[Azure Twilight's](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** avian wings and hyper-dense leg structures, **[Zeeran Twilight's](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** Vigortio cellular regimen, **[Liezt Twilight's](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md)** blank-slate psychological architecture, and numerous chimera bio-splicing trials. 

Yet in an extraordinary and heartbreaking paradox, Dr. Phoenix **genuinely, deeply loved the children** as a true mother. Universally beloved and called **"Mother"** by all facility subjects, she spent her days and nights tending to their physiology and minds, healing the very suffering her proposals caused. She nursed surgical incisions, alleviated the toxicity of Vigortio, fed them warm home-cooked meals, brought them toys and storybooks, and gave them unconditional love. Her warmth was the crucial anchor that kept the children human and allowed House Twilight to bond as a unified family rather than destroying one another.

Her life ended in profound tragedy. When trusted by Zeeran Twilight to treat **Zsofia**, Parischt—blinded by her obsession to conquer death—used Zsofia as a living "blood stock" for cellular regeneration experiments. Upon discovering this betrayal, Zeeran personally executed the woman he had loved as a mother.

### The First Subject: Zeeran Twilight (ZR4N-4)
**[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** (`ZR4N-4`) was brought to the White House facility at around **~6 years old**. 

Raised within the facility from age six, Zeeran achieved top-tier marks in all tactical and cognitive intelligence grading evaluations. As the premier subject of the White House, he was dispatched under a cover identity from an unknown village to infiltrate the Tigris Military Academy and showcase the facility's engineered human perfection to the imperial army. As an early recipient of Vigortio, he rose rapidly through the military hierarchy to become Supreme Commander before turning twenty, while the drug ultimately caused the chronic physical degeneration that left him wheelchair-bound at estimated age twenty-seven.

## Compromise & Destruction (1634)

During the climax of the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)** in **1634**, the secret location of the White House facility was traced, revealed, and targeted in a violent attack.

- **Zell Twilight's Presence:** **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md)** was inside the compound during the raid.
- **The Burned Ruins:** News of the assault reached Supreme Commander Zeeran while his sister **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** was leading the Tigris vanguard to the gates of Euphratea. Zeeran abandoned the frontlines to protect Zell, arriving at the facility only to see it engulfed in flames and reduced to ashes ("burned house"). 
- **Legacy & The Real-Time Experiment:** While the destruction of the facility ended the Tigris government's official super-soldier program, **[Dr. Griffin](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/dr-griffin.md)** viewed the physical fire as merely the transition to Phase 2. Released into the world, the surviving Twilight siblings' ongoing actions—competing, unifying, and altering geopolitical reality—mean Dr. Griffin's experiment continues to run in real-time.
