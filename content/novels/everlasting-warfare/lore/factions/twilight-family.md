The **Twilight Family** (House Twilight) is a noble bloodline formerly situated at the apex of military and political influence within the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**. Headed by patriarch **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** alongside his six younger sisters, the family was historically revered for strategic brilliance and unyielding pride. Because no official birth records exist, all ages across the family are estimated, and all siblings officially chose **29 February (leap year)** as their shared birthday. They intentionally chose this date occurring only once every four years because they refused to waste time celebrating annually—preferring that each sibling remain relentlessly focused on their duty, research, and individual work. Exactly four years later, on leap day, the family members gather together to share and recount all that they have achieved during their four years of relentless pursuit.

## Noble Crest & Symbolism

- **Noble Crest:** A snake eating its own tail (**Ouroboros**).
- **Symbolic Significance:** The Ouroboros represents eternal cycles, self-containment, endless resilience, and perpetual renewal. For House Twilight, it signifies the unbroken continuity of their ancient bloodline and pride—enduring through catastrophic war, loss of ancestral lands, and exile within foreign borders.
- **Subtle Insignia on Global Currency:** Following the 1634 victory, a tiny micro-engraved **Ouroboros** mark was quietly incorporated onto the **[Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md)** currency minted by the **World Treasure Company**. The mark is so subtle that ordinary citizens using the currency daily do not even notice it, and even if noticed, the public is entirely ignorant of its historical tie to House Twilight.

## The Fall & Loss of the Ancestral House

Following the tragic conclusion of the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)** in **1634**, the Tigris Empire suffered a definitive military defeat against the **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**. 

In the wake of imperial capitulation and political restructuring:
- **Loss of Ancestral House:** House Twilight lost their ancestral lands, house, and noble titles within the Tigris Empire.
- **Relocation to Euphratea:** Deprived of their imperial hold, Patriarch Zeeran Twilight relocated the family to **Euphratea**, the capital city of the victorious Euphrates Kingdom.
- **Post-War Palace Visit & Undocumented Meeting:** Directly after the war ended four years ago, Zeeran (then **23 years old**) visited the Euphratea palace for the first time, where 14-year-old Princess **[Erika ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)** accidentally ran into him without knowing his identity *(undocumented)*.
- **Exile Reunion in Underground Base:** Following 18-year-old Erika's regicide, Azure Twilight rescued her from pursuing royal knights and brought her to House Twilight's secret underground facility. Not knowing who Azure or Zeeran were at first, Erika suddenly recognized Zeeran upon meeting him as the man she had run into at the palace four years earlier when she was 14.

## Origins of Naming & The "Twilight Room"

The identity of House Twilight was born inside the cold experimental halls of the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** facility:

- **Stripped Names & Serial Designations:** Upon enrolment, all seven children were stripped of their real birth names and assigned alphanumeric serial codes (`ZR4N-4`, `ZR3-4`, `LZT-4`, `ZLL-4`, `CZ-4`, `ZFL-4`, `ZP-4`).
- **Zeeran's Phonetic Naming:** Refusing to let his siblings exist as nameless test subjects, patriarch Zeeran Twilight personally derived proper names for each of them based on their serial codes:
  - `ZR4N-4` → **Zeeran** (Admitted ~6 yrs old)
  - `ZR3-4` → **Azure** (+1 yr later)
  - `LZT-4` → **Liezt** (+1 yr later)
  - `ZLL-4` → **Zell** & `CZ-4` → **Chizu** (+1 yr later)
  - `ZFL-4` → **Zyfel** (+1 yr later)
  - `ZP-4` → **Zephyr** (+6 months later)
- **The "Twilight" Surname:** All seven siblings were assigned to live together in a research ward designated as the **"Twilight Room"**. Zeeran claimed the room's title as their official family surname, establishing **House Twilight**.

## Family Structure & Key Members

- **Patriarch — [Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md) (`ZR4N-4`):** Ex Supreme Commander of the Tigris Empire, brought to the White House at ~6 years old, and crippled master strategist who now works as a Scientist in the secret underground R&D division.
- **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md) (`ZR3-4`):** Second eldest child of House Twilight, former military vanguard commander and former Lead Chief Scientist of the Tigris Empire, currently working as a Scientist in the secret underground R&D division.
- **[Liezt Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md) (`LZT-4`):** Known as "The Faceless Sister" and "The Thousand-Soul Spy", Chief of Intelligence and master of psychological warfare who synthesized over 100 distinct personalities.
- **[Zell Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zell-twilight.md) (`ZLL-4`):** Former Military Strategist and Second-in-Command of the Tigris Army, eldest twin sister of Chizu Twilight, who tragically perished in the 1634 White House fire.
- **[Chizu Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/chizu-twilight.md) (`CZ-4`):** Known as "The Crazy Girl", younger twin sister of Zell. Unofficial enforcer who feels zero pain due to modified Congenital Analgesia.
- **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md) (`ZFL-4`):** Known as *"The Gilded Plague"*, CEO of the **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md)** and lead alchemical toxicologist governing global monetary systems, who travels the world searching for the Elixir of Life and her missing sister Zephyr.
- **[Zephyr Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zephyr-twilight.md) (`ZP-4`):** The youngest Twilight sister, admitted to the White House 6 months after Zyfel. Subjected to dragon chimera experimentation, she has been missing for 16 years (since age 6 in Antarctica), though the family steadfastly refuses to mark her as dead.
- **The Twilight Sisters:** Zeeran's six younger sisters, who form the core lineage of House Twilight. Zeeran is fiercely protective of them, shaping all political and scientific maneuvers around safeguarding their future.

## Secret Underground Facility & Status in Euphratea

House Twilight currently resides within a top-secret **underground facility** beneath **Euphratea**, operating as a clandestine scientific R&D division established by Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**.

- **Secret Scientist Division:** Operating beneath the capital, Zeeran and Azure Twilight conduct cutting-edge scientific research, utilizing their brilliant minds to design technology for Euphrates while protecting the family lineage.
- **Royal Resentment & "Tigris Rats":** Though Elizabeth leverages House Twilight's unmatched scientific capabilities, she harbors deep-seated hatred and resentment toward Tigris as a whole. Viewing the exiled family with intense hostility, Elizabeth regards them as mere vermin and derogatorily calls them **"Tigris rats"**.

## Grand Ambition: The New World Order

The ultimate goal of Patriarch **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)** for House Twilight is the establishment of a **New World Order**.

- **Global Subjugation & Total Dependency:** Zeeran aims to bring the entire world under the control of House Twilight or make all global powers completely dependent on him. Through strategic planning, technological breakthroughs, and financial manipulation, the family seeks to ensure that no empire or nation can operate without House Twilight's overarching influence.
- **Family Alignment & Awareness:** Every sibling in House Twilight is fully aware of Zeeran's goal to achieve a New World Order. Each sister works in unison, channeling her unique abilities—whether in scientific R&D, intelligence, biological alchemy, or raw force—toward realizing their patriarch's vision.
- **Zyfel's Economic Pillar:** **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** serves as the direct enforcer of Zeeran's ideals through the global economy. By orchestrating monetary dominance via the **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md)** and embedding the **[Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md)** currency worldwide, Zyfel ensures that sovereign nations become inextricably bound to House Twilight's financial grid.
