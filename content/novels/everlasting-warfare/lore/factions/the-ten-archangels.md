# The Ten Archangels of Aethernia

The **Ten Archangels** represent the highest precision military force and sacred heralds of the **[Aethernian Race](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)**. Enclosed in luminous metallic frames with wings of pure light, each Archangel corresponds directly to one of the ten spheres (Sephirot) of the cosmic Tree of Life, serving under the supreme command of **[The One](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)** and led into battle by their Supreme Vanguard Commander, **[Sirius](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/sirius.md)**.

## Operational Mechanics & Merkabah Starships

- **Merkabah Command:** Each Archangel commands a flagship **Merkabah** (Divine Chariot) starship, equipped with advanced energy cannons and dimensional transit drives.
- **Formations:** When deployed together, the Archangels project a unified celestial resonance shield powered by sacred geometry.
