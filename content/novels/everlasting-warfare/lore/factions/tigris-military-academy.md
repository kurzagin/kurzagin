The **Tigris Military Academy** is the premier military training ground and officer candidate institution of the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**. Codifying the Empire's iron-fisted martial philosophy, the academy trains commanders, vanguard officers, and tacticians for imperial campaigns.

---

## Institutional Brutality & Seniority Culture

Built on the empire's reverence for physical strength and personal combat supremacy, the Tigris Military Academy enforces a ruthless internal hierarchy:

- **Power-Driven Culture:** The institution operates under an unwritten code that rewards raw physical power above all else. Moral restraint is viewed as weakness, while martial dominance is venerated.
- **Systemic Hazing & Instructor Backing:** Seniority is strictly enforced through systemic hazing and bullying. Academy instructors actively support and encourage these practices as a mandatory "greeting treatment" designed to break new recruits and filter out the weak.
- **Live-Steel Regulations:** Academy regulations permit all enrolled cadets and officers to carry swords within academy grounds. Resolving disputes through duels fought with live steel is widely accepted.

---

## Zeeran Twilight's Entrance Incident

The academy's institutional hazing culture faced a brutal disruption during the arrival of **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)**:

- **White House Covert Assignment:** Dispatched by the **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)** under a synthetic cover identity as a common boy from an unknown village, Zeeran was tasked with infiltrating the academy to demonstrate the facility's engineered human perfection to the imperial military.
- **The "Greeting Treatment" & Live-Steel Challenge:** Upon arrival at the academy gates, senior cadets cornered Zeeran to initiate the traditional hazing ritual. Zeeran answered every interrogation question with calm, unnerving precision. Seeking to humiliate him, the senior cadet demanded a live-steel duel, which Zeeran accepted immediately.
- **The Execution:** As the senior cadet arrogantly boasted about the academy's traditional culture, Zeeran drew his blade and decapitated him mid-sentence without saying a word.
- **Massacre & Impalement:** Before the remaining seniors could react, Zeeran turned on them with relentless efficiency, slaughtering and impaling the entire group until their corpses littered the courtyard.
- **Onlookers & Cleanup:** The surrounding recruits and spectators watched in absolute horror, with several vomiting from the gruesome carnage. The Academy Commander arrived at the scene, surveyed the bloodbath, and calmly ordered the corpses cleared away. Without acknowledging the horror, Zeeran wiped his blade, stepped over the bodies, and walked through the gates to begin his enrollment.
