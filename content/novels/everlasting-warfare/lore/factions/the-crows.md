**The Crows** is currently the largest criminal organization in the world. Originally established by **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)**, the supreme command was later transferred to **[Erika Morgenstern](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/erika-ni-euphratea.md)** (the exiled former Crown Princess *Erika ni Euphratea*). The syndicate operates on a global scale and is experiencing explosive growth in the present day.

---

## Origins, Leadership & The Eagle Hierarchy

- **Founding & Azure's Leadership (3 Years Ago):** Following Princess Erika's regicide and subsequent rescue by **[Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md)** three years ago, Azure originally founded and organized the syndicate, serving as its true initial leader to build its core operational foundation.
- **Handover to Erika Morgenstern:** Once the foundation of the organization was firmly established, Azure stepped back and relinquished the leadership role, allowing Erika to assume full supreme command under her alias **Erika Morgenstern**.
- **The "Eagle" Rank & Respect for Azure:** Within The Crows' internal hierarchy, **Eagle** represents the highest rank—reserved for founders, top commanders, and elite leadership. Every member holding the rank of Eagle knows and respects Azure Twilight deeply, revering her as the true founder and ultimate authority behind the syndicate's inception.
- **Present-Day Growth:** Under Erika's command, The Crows has grown at an unprecedented rate, consolidating smaller syndicates and establishing deep illicit roots across international borders to become the world's premier criminal empire.

---

## Symbol, Member Pin & Philosophy

- **Official Crest & Chest Pin:** The emblem of The Crows—featured on their official crest and on a metal pin worn attached to every member's chest—depicts a crow in front of a treasury chest in the background.
- **The Single Wing Design:** Critically, the crow on both the crest and member chest pins features **only one wing**, with the second wing visibly non-existent.
- **Symbolism of Being Trapped:** The single-winged crow serves as an overt symbol that the members of The Crows are trapped, shackled, and denied true freedom by the reigning global powers and socio-political orders.
- **Core Goal — Chasing Freedom:** The foundational objective of The Crows is to eventually "grow wings"—expanding their strength, shattering their chains, and relentlessly chasing true freedom.

---

## Operational Methods & Terror Campaign

- **Shadow Operations:** Historically, The Crows operated almost exclusively in the shadows—conducting clandestine smuggling, political blackmails, and covert assassinations out of public view.
- **Visible Acts of Terror:** Recently, the organization drastically altered its strategy, launching bold, highly visible acts of terror targeting entire sovereign countries to project power and destabilize regional authority.
- **The Target — Euphrates Kingdom:** The **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)** was chosen as their primary and most aggressive target. Despite facing relentless terror campaigns, Euphrates security forces and knights have managed to arrest a few members of The Crows, gaining intelligence on their structure.

---

## Signature Uniform, Pins & Mask Protocol

- **Chest Pin:** Every member wears an official pin attached to their chest depicting the single-winged crow in front of the treasury chest.
- **Bird-Shaped Mask Mandate:** Every single operative and member belonging to The Crows is required to wear a mask at all times during missions and gatherings.
- **Mask Variations:** While mask designs vary among members—including pitch-black masks, detailed crow masks, eagle masks, owl masks, and other predatory bird motifs—the mandatory rule is that **they always use bird-shaped masks**.

---

## Weapon Supply Line & Key Operatives

- **Primary Weapon Supplier:** The organization's vast arsenal of firearms and tactical weaponry is supplied by master gunsmith **[John Jeremiah (J.J)](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/john-jeremiah.md)**.
- **Subterranean Supply Engine:** J.J operates a mass-production firearm factory concealed directly beneath his neutral bar in Euphratea, ensuring a continuous supply of high-grade guns for The Crows' global campaigns.
- **Erika's Confidante & Logistics:** **[Belle (Isabelle Rätsel)](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/belle.md)** serves as Erika's closest friend, managing executive assistant duties, internal logistics, and sanctuary supply distribution.
