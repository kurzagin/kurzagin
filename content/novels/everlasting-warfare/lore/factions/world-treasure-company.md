The **World Treasure Company** is an immensely influential international banking conglomerate, central monetary mint, and global financial superpower. Headquartered in **[Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)** with hundreds of full-service branches operating across every sovereign territory, the company manages sovereign wealth reserves, imperial loans, foreign currency exchanges, and the physical minting of the global currency standard, the **[Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md)**.

Despite its public stature as an institutional central bank, the company was forged as an instrument of biological and financial dominion by **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)**.

---

## Origin: From Local Clinic to Financial Hegemon (Year 1624)

Established in **1624** (17 years before Present Day 1641 / 10 years before the 1634 war end), the World Treasure Company began as a clandestine medical operation inside Euphratea:

1. **Liezt's Nurse Infiltration (1624):** **[Liezt Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/liezt-twilight.md)** (`LZT-4`, ~13 years old) used her master disguise abilities to infiltrate Euphratea, successfully assuming the persona of a dedicated nurse. She commanded a small medical clinic staff established under **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** (`ZFL-4`, ~10 years old).
2. **Espionage Cover & Community Service:** While caring for the sick people of Euphrates, Liezt used her nursing position as an ideal cover environment to gather vast amounts of political and military intelligence, traveling back and forth to supply covert intel to **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)**.
3. **Targeted Water Contamination:** Zyfel took the operation further by contaminating the specific river channel feeding directly into the Euphratea nobility's private quarters, knowing the royal elite relied exclusively on that supply.
4. **Liezt's Recommendation & Foreign Medicine Charter:** Positioned inside the medical system, Liezt mentioned to the panicked elite that she knew a foreign practitioner who possessed a specialized remedy. The royal court authorized the importation of Zyfel's antidote. Curing the affliction earned total trust, tax immunity, and royal banking charters.
5. **Metamorphosis into a Global Bank:** Flush with royal capital and political leverage, Zyfel transformed the clinic into wealth management, bullion storage, and central minting—rapidly expanding into the **World Treasure Company**.

## Global Banking Expansion & Minting of the Euphry

- **Continental Footprint:** The company established branches across foreign empires, neutral trade hubs, and provincial territories, rendering international commerce entirely dependent on its vaults.
- **Minting of the [Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md):** Following the 1634 defeat, Zyfel secured total sovereign rights as sole minting body for the global monetary standard, stamping gold coins and printing banknotes under strict corporate oversight.
- **Iconography & Micro-Engraving:** Currency minted by the company bears Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**'s portrait alongside Zyfel's hidden masterpiece: a micro-engraved **Ouroboros** symbol (the noble crest of **[House Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)**), forcing all nations to trade under the subtle seal of their secret rulers.

## Secret Leadership: The Sovereign Executive

While the public perceives the World Treasure Company as a corporate board of senior bankers, ultimate authority rests in **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)**:

- **The Secret Individual:** Zyfel's identity as CEO is scrubbed from public registries. Ordinary citizens and bank clerks operate without ever knowing her real name or connection to House Twilight.
- **Epistolary Protocol & Formal Titles:** In internal correspondence, executive ledgers, and secret board directives, she is addressed exclusively with supreme reverence as:
  - **"Young Lady"**
  - **"Madame"**
  - **"Mademoiselle"**
- **Utmost Reverence:** Every letter, voucher, or corporate decree is written in immaculate honorific syntax, concealing her identity beneath a cloak of sovereign prestige and corporate secrecy.
