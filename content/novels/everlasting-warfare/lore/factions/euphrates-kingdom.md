The **Euphrates Kingdom** is a modernist, highly formidable sovereign nation situated along the western borders of the **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**, centered around its capital city of **Euphratea**. Rejecting the stagnant traditionalism of the past, Euphrates is defined by its progressive scientific engineering, modern religious doctrine, and fierce ambition.

---

## Origin: The Rebellious Exodus & Modernist Identity

- **Origins in Tigris (~900 Years Ago):** The Euphrates nation originated directly from the Tigris Empire nearly nine centuries ago. Frustrated by the stubborn, "iron-headed" elders of Tigris who constantly enforced rigid tradition and suppressed change, the youth of Tigris staged a grand rebellion and crossed the river.
- **The Crossing & Intermingling:** Fleeing beyond the great river boundary, the young rebels established an independent nation. Out beyond the river, they mingled and reproduced with neighboring foreign nations, creating a diverse and culturally adaptable populace over centuries.
- **Modernist Faith — Worship of Marduk & Ishtar:** Breaking away from the Elder Triad of Tigris (Enki, Enlil, An), Euphrates embraced a modernist pantheon centered on **Marduk** (God of fierce ambition, storm, and sovereign power) and **Ishtar** (Goddess of divine beauty, passionate war, and freedom).
- **The Systemism Institution:** Prominent alongside mainstream Marduk and Ishtar worship is **Systemism**, a major religious order that branched from Marduk and Ishtar veneration while continuing to worship all Mesopotamian deities equally. Led historically by the **Entu** (High Priestess), the position of supreme leader has been vacant for an unknown duration across uncounted ages (no one has ever kept count), with senior males serving as **En** (High Priest), such as **[Azazel Vorgenstein](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azazel-vorgenstein.md)**. Because Systemism lacks access to **Ubbshu-Ukkinna**, the order relies on **Pilgrims**—such as Sir Alden—to seek out promising candidates, though without cosmic verification tools, no candidate pursued has ever actually been a true Entu and the clergy possesses no proof whatsoever for any candidate they approach (including Princess Anna).

## Physical Traits & The Blessing of Ishtar and Marduk

- **Iconic Appearance:** The population of Euphrates is uniquely recognized by their **blonde hair and red eyes**.
- **Cultural Pride vs. Imperial Stigma:** While the conservative Tigris Empire views their mixed lineage with disdain, condemning them as "impure," the citizens of Euphrates wear their appearance with immense pride. 
- **Divine Symbolism:** To Euphrates, blonde hair represents the radiant **blessing of Ishtar's beauty**, while red eyes symbolize **Marduk's fierce ambition** and unyielding drive for progress.

---

## Technological Superiority & Musket Warfare

- **Centuries of R&D:** Technology in Euphrates did not explode overnight; rather, they spent centuries in dedicated research and development.
- **Muskets as a Recent Invention:** Engineered specifically for the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)** (1534 – 1634), muskets were a relatively new weapon system that offset Tigris's physical armor supremacy.
- **Resource Constraints:** The kingdom's small footprint and thin resource deposits created constant economic strain, compelling Euphrates to seek territorial expansion and vital resources from wealthier neighbors.

---

## Victory in 1634 & Capital City Euphratea

Driven by their acute need for resources and empowered by their overwhelming firearm technology, Euphrates engaged in the **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/05-tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)**.

Equipped with superior muskets, line infantry formations, and advanced ballistics, Euphrates armies shattered the sword-and-shield legions of the Tigris Empire. Despite counter-tactics led by Supreme Commander **[Zeeran Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zeeran-twilight.md)**, Euphrates emerged victorious in **1634**, forcing Tigris to sign a peace treaty yielding territorial and resource concessions.

Following the defeat of Tigris, the displaced **[Twilight Family](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)** lost their ancestral house in Tigris and relocated to **Euphratea**. Under the directive of Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**, they were placed in a secret **underground facility** beneath the capital to serve as a covert scientist R&D division. While leveraging their scientific intellect, Elizabeth harbors deep-seated resentment toward Tigris, viewing the family with contempt and calling them **"Tigris rats"**.

## High Command & Military Figures

- **Ex-Queen [Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md):** Sovereign matriarch who took back direct royal command after Crown Princess Erika committed regicide at age 22 (when denied the succession) and was exiled, as Second Princess Anna was only 16 years old and unready to rule.
- **[ur-sag̃](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ur-sag.md):** A legendary General whose real name and age are completely unknown. Giant, muscular, usually half-naked, and covered in ancient red tattoos, he possesses raw human strength and red hair that resembles a burning flame in the sun and wind. Zeeran Twilight detested him as a "monster in human form" and repeatedly engineered battle strategies to avoid triggering his deployment, yet paradoxically, whenever Zeeran tried hardest to prevent his entry, ur-sag̃ always appeared on the battlefield anyway. During these clashes, only [Azure Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/azure-twilight.md) was physically capable of holding him.

## Post-War Economic Hegemony & The Euphry

Following their 1634 victory, the Euphrates Kingdom transformed its military victory into permanent economic dominance over the continent:

- **The [Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/euphry.md) Standard:** Euphrates instituted the **Euphry** as the official global monetary unit. Replacing fragmented imperial barter and provincial coins, the Euphry quickly became the universal reserve currency for global commerce.
- **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md):** Formally produced and stamped around the World Treasure Company (established 10 years prior). Originally a modest local clinic, the company achieved explosive global expansion after sponsoring imported foreign medicine to cure a severe itching skin rash epidemic among Euphrates nobility. Today, it operates branches worldwide and mints gold coins (the common, widely spread daily currency) and paper banknotes (varying spread). The company's ultimate leader is a top-secret young female referred to in confidential documents with utmost respect as *"Young Lady"*, *"Madame"*, or *"Mademoiselle"*.
- **Insignia & Secret Markings:** Prominently displays the portrait of Ex-Queen **[Elizabeth ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/elizabeth-ni-euphratea.md)**, while carrying an extremely subtle micro-engraved **Ouroboros** symbol. Ordinary citizens using the currency are entirely unaware the symbol exists, and even those who discover it do not know what family or entity it belongs to.
