**Humans** are the mortal species created by **[Enki](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/enki.md)** in his own image—modeled after his Plain Race morphology—following his unification of Earth's six primordial races. Possessing relatively short lifespans compared to elder races like the [Elves](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/elf.md), humans offset their brevity of life through relentless innovation, rapid generational turnaround, strategic organization, and technological adaptation.

---

## Magical Tradition & Scholarship

Human magical theory is defined by practical application and structured categorization, treating magic as an environmental force driven by **Mana** and channeled via physical **Catalysts** (such as staves, blades, or crystals) inscribed with Ancient Runic Letters.

- **The Legacy of Merlin:** Foundational human magic was established by **Merlin**, the first human mage, who received direct instruction under an **Elf Master**. His written legacy, the *Diary of Merlin*, serves as the core curriculum for institutions like the clandestine **Human Abraxas Academy**.
- **Modern Arcane Evolution:** While traditional institutions strictly follow Merlin's elven-derived teachings, human prodigies like **[Merlyne](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/merlyne.md)** have pushed human magic beyond traditional boundaries by pioneering **Passive Magic**—pre-programming spells with conditional triggers.

## Technological & Military Adaptability

Humans are responsible for major technological advances that shape continental geopolitical power:
- **Firearms & Metallurgy:** Developed musket technology and field artillery during the 100-Year War.
- **Alchemy & Pharmacology:** Synthesized advanced combat enhancers such as **[Vigortio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/vigortio.md)** and therapeutic counter-compounds like **[Resiliexcuratio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/resiliexcuratio.md)**.
- **Biomechanical Engineering:** Engineered life-sustaining mechanical heart cores like the **[Maschinenherz](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/maschinenherz.md)**.

## Role in Divine Governance (Nam-en) & The Seven Realms

Humans hold a unique, isolated place within the divine **[Nam-en](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/nam-en.md)** framework and **[The Seven Realms](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/seven-realms.md)** system:
- **The First Entu (En-zagin):** The first Entu candidate, Amethyst Lorelei, was human before her selfishness led to divine punishment and the total erasure of her name (*Shumu*) from existence.
- **The Eighth Entu (En-gurnun):** Because of En-zagin's total erasure, Humanity was left without an Entu guide across six whole epochs, becoming the last race to receive an Entu. The current and final Entu of the present era is **[Anna ni Euphratea](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/anna-ni-euphratea.md)** (Anna Wisteria), Second Princess of Euphrates and active VIII Entu (**En-gurnun**).
- **Human Ignorance of Ancient History:** Because humans were the least to have an Entu guide during the ages post-reset, **humans naturally do not know what happened back then**. Unlike the other races who were taught about the impending **[Aethernian](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/races/aethernian.md)** threat, humans live behind the **Firmament** completely oblivious to Enki's world reset, the existence of the other six Earths, and the grand cosmic battle awaiting them.
