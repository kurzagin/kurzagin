The **Demon Race** consists of the primordial denizens born of the abyssal depths of **[The Underworld](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/underworld.md)**.

---

## Hierarchy & Entu Lineage

- **Underworld Hierarchy:** Divided into Ars Goetia (Nobility/Demon Kings), Ars Theurgia Goetia (Infernal Knights), and Ars Pauline (Shadow District).
- **Sovereignty:** Governed by Queen **[Ereshkigal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/ereshkigal.md)**.
- **Entu Representative:** **[En-hulal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-hulal.md)** (Luna Diamond), V Entu of the Divine Lineage.
