The **Dwarven Race** is one of the core lineages established under the **[Six Signs of Being](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/six-signs-of-being.md)**.

---

## Master Metallurgy & Entu Lineage

- **Enkiantium Masters:** Sole species capable of shaping **[Enkiantium](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/enkiantium.md)**, the hardest metal on Earth.
- **Forging Masterworks:** Forged **[Longinus](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/longinus.md)** for Archangel Sirius.
- **Entu Representative:** **[En-sig](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-sig.md)** (Emerald Caleste), III Entu of the Divine Lineage.
