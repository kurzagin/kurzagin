The **Spirit Race** represents beings whose fundamental nature exists untethered from mortal flesh, born of pure essence and consciousness in the spaces between flesh and void.

---

## Characteristics & Governance

- **Incorporeal Essence:** Lack mortal organs or blood; immune to standard physical harm.
- **Entu Representative:** **[En-giznu](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-giznu.md)** (Jenneate Garnet), VI Entu of the Divine Lineage.
- **Governance:** Ruled by the **Spirit Queen**, who selects souls from the 5th Floor of the **[Underworld](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/underworld.md)** for spiritual reincarnation.
