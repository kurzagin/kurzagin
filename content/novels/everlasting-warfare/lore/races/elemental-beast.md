The **Elemental Beast Race** is a primordial lineage bound directly to raw environmental mana and natural elemental forces under the **[Six Signs of Being](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/overview/six-signs-of-being.md)**.

---

## Characteristics & Entu Lineage

- **Elemental Affinity:** Physical forms naturally embody elemental forces (fire, earth, water, air, lightning).
- **Entu Representative:** **[En-guran](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/en-guran.md)** (Alexandrite Novah), VII Entu of the Divine Lineage.
- **Subduing Artifact:** During the Antediluvian Era, the **[Chain of Destruction](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/chain-of-destruction.md)** was specifically commissioned by **[Diapatri](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/diapatri.md)** and crafted by Dwarves to capture, bind, and tame hostile wild Elemental Beasts.
