When standing at the height of a snowy hill, the cold wind seemed to whisper with a voice that pierced to the bone. The dark night sky and the stars scattered like jewel dust were not enough to provide illumination, and the moon was merely a pale silhouette helpless against the dense darkness.


However, in this situation, what rendered people unable to move was not the freezing wind, but—


—Fear


Around the base of that tall snowy hill, a flickering light was visible from a distance—black smoke rose slowly, billowing like a dancing phantom among the snow, turning the cold air increasingly warm, then hot, almost scorching as one drew closer.


Approaching the source of the thickening smoke, the scene that unfolded grew ever more terrifying, surpassing all darkest nightmares. The air was thick with the sharp stench of burning, gripping the throat and piercing the lungs. The air felt toxic, forcing every breath into a deadly struggle.


From afar, a human figure could be seen, but their entire body was charred and burnt. They stood with hands reaching forward, as if trying to grasp something. Coming closer, suddenly...


"YOU LEFT ME TO DIE, BROTHER!"


—


[January 18, 1641, Euphratea]


—Seven years ago, a hundred-year war involving two nations, the Euphrates Kingdom and the Tigris Empire—later known by the term [Euphrates-Tigris Hundred-Year War]—had ended with the ultimate victory of the Euphrates Kingdom, which subsequently invaded the Tigris Empire. After the war concluded, the technology of the Euphrates Kingdom advanced with incredible speed. The person responsible for this technological development was none other than the scientists under the name of the Queen, Elizabeth nī Euphratean—who was currently holding her 90th birthday party in her palace.


At present, the Queen was inside her castle, specifically in the royal throne room bathed in golden light radiating from the numerous chandeliers above. The room was decorated with luxurious carpets and elaborate ornaments, and at the end of the room, atop a magnificent throne, sat Queen Elizabeth taking a rest. 


Her loyal servant, Sir Alden, an old man with a stooped back and a heart full of unwavering devotion who had originally come from the Systemist temple to serve the royal household, entered the room, his footsteps echoing against the polished marble floor. He approached the Queen with a bow, resting one hand over his aging heart—paying homage to the Queen.


Alden was already far too old to serve as the Queen's attendant; his hair had turned grey and he could barely stand strong, yet his loyalty to the Queen made him forget his age.


Queen Elizabeth heard Alden's footsteps and gently opened her eyes.


Looking at Alden's gaze with a calm composure, the Queen smiled warmly and amicably,


“Ah… Alden, good to see you.”


“Your Majesty, the preparations for your birthday party are complete. The guests have gathered in the Main Hall, all awaiting your presence.”


“Your Majesty… may I… assist you in walking?”


Even though his age was not much different from the Queen's, he still wished to help Her Majesty walk; such loyalty truly deserved praise.


“Thank you, Alden. Your loyalty has been my strength all this time.”


“It is an honor to receive such words from Your Majesty the Queen.”


Alden approached the Queen and carefully offered his hand. Queen Elizabeth slowly stood up, took Alden's hand, and together they walked slowly toward the corridor leading to the main hall—where the guests were waiting.


As they walked down the corridor, Alden asked,


“Did Your Majesty also… invite them?”


“Them?”


“Ah… do you mean those mad scientists from the Empire?”


“Correct… Your Majesty.”


“I have sent invitations to them through palace envoys. The guests must surely be looking forward to it as well, now that seven years have passed since our Kingdom's victory over the Empire...”


Alden merely nodded softly in understanding.


After walking for a moment, the two of them finally arrived at the main hall, greeted by the bright smiles of the nobles and ministers of the Euphrates Kingdom.


One neatly dressed man with blonde hair stepped forward to approach the Queen and Alden. Standing right before the Queen, he bowed his body—a mark of respect,


“Happy birthday, Your Majesty…”


“Thank you, Lord Lucius. I am glad you could attend my event today.”


“It is truly an honor for me to come, Your Majesty. I could not possibly miss the birthday party of the future grandmother of my child…”


“Oh, how is your son? Where is he now? Should I call Anna? It seems she is still in her room meeting with the priests; I wonder since when she started dealing with the temple.”


“Oh, there is certainly no need to trouble Princess Anna, Your Majesty. My son is nearby, walking around looking at the palace.”


“Ah… I do not find it troublesome at all, Lord Lucius. Perhaps Anna would also like to see your son.”


And so, the Queen and Lord Lucius spoke about Princess Anna's future with the son of the Baron family. The Queen and her guests were enjoying this joyful moment.


The venue of this birthday celebration was the main palace of the Euphrates Kingdom. Although called the main palace, it was smaller compared to palaces in other regions scattered across Euphrates. This palace only had 1 throne room, 1 main hall, 1 reception room, 2 bedrooms for the Queen and Princess, and 5 servant rooms.


Right beneath the palace underground lay a secret room, a laboratory unknown to anyone—not even the Queen herself.


The laboratory was filled with various intricate scientific instruments. In the center of the room stood a large workbench made of old wood, covered with precision measuring tools, mechanical data processing devices, and other complex instruments. Along the walls were shelves filled with experimental apparatuses—seemingly completed inventions—each labeled with a name.


Large machines adorned with gears filled the corners of the room, with chains and pulleys dangling down the walls.


The walls were made of iron metal and coated with layers of rust, conveying a sense of great age. Dim light from oil lamps attached to the surrounding walls provided faint illumination, creating a mysterious aura throughout the room. The air felt humid with a distinct, subtle metallic smell.


Copper piping ran along the walls and out of the room, connecting various equipment to a large steam engine. The sound of hissing and rumbling steam flowing through pipes created the distinctive sonic backdrop of this laboratory.


Inside the room were three siblings consisting of two women and a man with short black hair heavily streaked with grey, sitting in a wheelchair. One of the women, with short, unkempt white hair, was sleeping on a bed. The other was a blonde woman possessing beauty fit for royal nobility; she was applying makeup in front of a mirror, dressed in a black party gown with a camera hanging around her neck. Her crimson eyes glowed brightly as she turned her face away from the mirror and looked at the man in the wheelchair,


“Brother Zeeran, a few days ago, the Queen invited us to her party held this afternoon. Are we going?”


“Yes, we must go…”


“If we don't show up, that old hag will treat us like criminals. We have no choice but to go.”


Zeeran Twilight, a 30-year-old man currently working as the chief scientist of the Euphrates Kingdom, was a former high general of the Tigris Empire who had retired from the military due to his paralyzed legs. He was also a sufferer of severe post-war trauma illness and temporal dissociation.


“And look at you, Liezt, already all dressed up to go while still asking… dolling yourself up so beautifully… are you trying to seduce some rich noble man?”


“Though it's not like we need the money…”


In front of Zeeran was Liezt Twilight, a 29-year-old unemployed woman with a gorgeous appearance that could make anyone mistake her for a noble from the Euphrates Kingdom. But in reality, she hailed from the Tigris Empire, just like Zeeran.


“Not at all! I just wanted to look pretty... besides, this is a big event, there's no special reason!”


Liezt's face flushed red at her brother's words. Eventually, she ignored Zeeran and turned to Azure, a girl lying on the bed wearing steel armor fitted onto her legs.


Azure Twilight, a 30-year-old girl who could be described by the phrase [The Lazy Genius], was a scientist, just like Zeeran. Although Zeeran also worked as a scientist, nearly 90% of the technology possessed by the kingdom over the past seven years was Azure's creation.


“What do you think, Sister Azure?”


“I am…… so sore, not interested ……in going either.”


Azure's voice sounded weak, almost choked. Her eyes looked listless, and her white hair appeared tangled and unkempt, adding a gloomy impression to her appearance. Her face looked pale, likely from exhaustion. She wore a white lab coat.


Restlessly, Zeeran turned his wheelchair toward a somewhat dark area and slowly reached around the shelves, searching for something seemingly important. His expression reflected anxiety over losing something,


“I am certain the Queen has prepared an unpleasant reception for us; just prepare your ears…”


“But if the Queen violates our contract, perhaps we should eliminate her immediately and take over this Kingdom.”


“Don't say such terrible things! The Queen might not be friendly to us, but that's only natural—we used to be her enemies… we're fortunate enough to have a place to stay...”


“A place to stay? Didn't Zyfel arrange all of this?”


The Zyfel Zeeran mentioned was Zyfel Twilight, the sixth sibling among them and one of the younger sisters deeply loved by all of them. She was also the wealthiest person in the world who controlled the entire global economy, including the Euphrates Kingdom. Currently, she was not in the kingdom; she was on a journey to find a cure. The last time she took leave of her siblings was a year ago, saying she wanted to trace the paths of alchemy.


By the way, the [Twilight] family consisted of 7 siblings, with the eldest sibling (Zeeran) being male and all six younger siblings being female.


“Well… yeah, true.”


“—Come on, Sister Azure! This is a once-in-a-lifetime chance for you to enjoy the Queen's birthday party! We might never be able to attend it again!”


“Ahh…… I'm, ……tired.”


Liezt tried to help her stand up. With a tired face and feeling somewhat reluctant, Azure took Liezt's hand to be helped. Liezt tried to support Azure's body against her shoulder, and slowly Azure finally agreed to stand, even though she looked extremely lazy.


Zeeran glanced over at the quiet spot right beside his wheelchair. His fourth sister, Zell, was sitting right there beside him on her wooden stool, quietly observing them with her familiar gentle demeanor. 


“Make sure you take Zell along with you as well,” Zeeran said calmly, turning his wheelchair slightly toward them. “She has been staying inside this dark laboratory for far too long. A change of air will do her good.”


At his words, the laboratory fell into a sudden, heavy silence.


Azure and Liezt stiffened. For a fraction of a second, the two sisters exchanged a quiet, lingering stare with each other before Liezt's expression softened into a bright, affectionate smile. Stepping right up beside Zeeran's wheelchair, Liezt reached down toward the stool beside him and gently pulled Zell's hand, helping her up to her feet.


“Of course, Brother Zeeran,” Liezt said softly as she held Zell's hand. “Come along, Zell... let's get ready and go to the party together.”


A subtle sense of satisfaction settled over Zeeran as he watched Liezt carefully help Zell up and escort her alongside Azure. 


“You go ahead first. I haven't taken my medicine yet.”


“Oh… in that case, we'll head out first. Goodbye, Brother Zeeran!”


Full of enthusiasm, Liezt pulled Azure's hand with one arm while gently guiding Zell's hand with the other, leading them toward the heavy iron door of the laboratory.


“Ugghh……”


Zeeran merely glanced at them briefly, his hands still fumbling around. The tables became messy under his touch, before Liezt and Azure fully exited the laboratory,


“Oh right.. Azure…”


“Don't overdo it.”


Zeeran delivered his warning, his tone signifying concern,


Azure merely nodded, though she didn't fully understand what her brother meant by his warning to 'not overdo it'. 


After Azure and Liezt left the laboratory, silence enveloped the room. Now there was only the sound of a large CRT television and the increasingly audible rumble of the steam engine.


While continuing to search for his medicine, Zeeran heard the sound of the television inside the room broadcasting a scene where Queen Elizabeth was giving an announcement to her people from the palace balcony. Her voice sounded firm and full of authority.


"My beloved people of this Euphrates Kingdom. Today, I celebrate my birthday, and once again, our kingdom remains at peace."


"However, among us today, there are those attempting to destroy that peace! They are terrorists rising against the Kingdom! They cause terror and danger everywhere merely to satisfy their egos! I have captured several members of these terrorists and will execute their sentences on this very birthday!"


"There will be no mercy for terrorists!"


After delivering that speech before the crowd, a parade march passed through carrying several people whose faces were covered with cloth. It appeared they were the captured terrorists, causing everyone along the parade route to step aside to make way.


Zeeran was still searching for his medicine around the shelves. Upon hearing the Queen's speech, he clicked his tongue in annoyance,


"The Queen keeps spouting nonsense,"


"Several members of the organization have been captured. It seems a major shift will occur in the Kingdom today; Erika must have already made her move as well."


"I shouldn't linger searching for medicine; they will be here shortly."


An annoyed Zeeran reached into the pocket of his lab coat, his hand gripping a clump of medicine vials.

Taking his dosage with a glass of water, he calmly turned his wheelchair toward the entrance. After a moment, several heavy footsteps echoed from outside the corridor.

“They're here.”

Instead of a knock, an explosion blew down the heavy iron door. Soon after, several individuals clad in eerie black attire entered, all wearing bird-beak masks.

“Captain, this looks like a secret room,” one reported, scanning the apparatus.

“This room isn't on our map; perhaps the Owls missed it.”

“It looks like a laboratory. You guys check it out, there might be clues.”

While they spoke, Zeeran calmly rolled his wheelchair out of the shadows to face them. His posture remained composed, his sharp black eyes devoid of fear.

“Uninvited guests breaking down doors in my laboratory.”

Zeeran's sudden appearance startled them; they instantly trained their rifles on him.

“I am Zeeran Twilight,” he stated plainly, his voice carrying quiet, unwavering authority. “Ex-Supreme Commander of the Tigris Empire and Patriarch of House Twilight.”

The lead gunman let out a harsh, mocking scoff.

“Zeeran Twilight, you say?” the captain sneered. “The Supreme Commander of Tigris was a military legend. You're a crippled scientist stuck in a chair. You look nothing like a conqueror.”

Zeeran did not flinch or raise his voice. Under his code of absolute pride, lying was an act of cowardice, and hiding truth implied fearing consequences.

“Whether your mind is capable of comprehending the truth is immaterial to me,” Zeeran answered coldly. “I do not hide who I am.”

“Are you a palace scientist? What is this place? What are you researching here?” the captain demanded, pressing his gun barrel against Zeeran's forehead.

Without a second of hesitation or deception, Zeeran laid out the facts directly.

“I am the chief scientist for Euphrates, operating from a facility undisclosed even to Queen Elizabeth. I am currently manufacturing specialized chemical compounds, refining mechanical diagnostics, and monitoring continental supply logistics.”

The captain stared at him, bewildered by the total, unvarnished disclosure. To the captain's deceitful mindset, someone laying out their entire identity and operation so openly could only be a delusional lunatic or an arrogant fool trying to bluff.

“You think giving me a grand speech will save you, old man?” the captain barked, shifting his gun and firing a shot straight into Zeeran's leg.

*bang*

A sharp gasp escaped Zeeran's lips as blood seeped into his trousers, but he kept his head upright, his gaze fixed on the gunman with cold detachment.

“Answer me properly without the fairy tales! Last chance!” the captain snarled, leveling the barrel back between Zeeran's eyes.

Zeeran looked directly into the gun barrel, his face remaining entirely serene.

“I have given you the exact truth of my name and my work. If your deceitful nature forces you to disbelieve reality, that is a flaw in your intellect, not mine.”

“Oh, is that so—”

“Stop!” another squad member interrupted nervously.

“What if he really is a Twilight? The Hawks will be furious if we mess with them.”

“Captain… look at him. He's paralyzed and weak. The Twilights are monsters on the battlefield. This guy is just a delusional fool.”


*Boom!*


Suddenly, an explosion resonated from outside, making them all hurry.


“Hey, they've made their move. Come on, let's get out of here quick.”


“Roger, Captain!”


"Still, I will show no mercy to the people of Euphrates," he said, pulling the trigger of his gun. Several gunshots followed.


Zeeran did not move an inch, blood trickling from his chest as his heart ceased beating. Upon hearing the explosion sound, the five individuals abruptly rushed out of the room.


Zeeran, still in his wheelchair, lay helpless with no sound emerging from him. 


The room now fell silent.


***


Azure and Liezt were walking toward the Queen's palace. Liezt held a comb, standing behind the short Azure. Liezt looked delighted combing her hair, but Azure kept fidgeting, making it difficult for Liezt. At this moment, from anyone else's perspective, Azure appeared to be the younger sister.


"Liezt…..stop it….why do you carry that thing everywhere anyway?"


"Stay still, Sister Azure! If you don't stay still, it's hard for me to comb! And your hair is messy again! I put so much effort into combing your hair!"


"Uugh….”


Liezt was combing Azure's tangled hair, though Azure seemed to dislike the treatment. Ever since she was little, Azure never liked dressing up; to her, it was just a waste of time,


–If you still have time to dress up, that time would be better spent researching how to create a teleportation machine! So I wouldn't have to walk so far!


That had always been her answer whenever told to dress up or groom herself.


"Enough…… Liezt ……stop it, my energy will be completely drained…… before we even reach the ……party."


"Precisely because we're going to a party, that's why you have to dress up!"


*Boom*


Suddenly they heard an explosion. Azure's eyes immediately shot toward the source of the sound; her instincts told her something dangerous was happening,


"Liezt…… my hunch…… feels bad ……I will go back and check on Brother Zeeran. You…. don't drop your guard."


Liezt's tone of voice suddenly changed, her red eyes glowing brightly, her cheerful facial expression shifting to serious—like a completely different person. Liezt smirked as she said to Azure,


"Don't worry, sister… I'll go check the source of that explosion."


Liezt and Azure parted ways on the road. Azure returned to their laboratory, while Liezt headed toward the crowd.


The atmosphere there was extremely chaotic: people running in fear, a little child crying alone, someone sitting in a corner with a deeply miserable face.


“Erika, you've made your move, haven't you?”


Liezt merely grinned broadly; she loved thrilling situations like this. Hearing people's screams made her heart blossom with excitement. The adrenaline inside her began to surge, and an uncontainable curiosity filled Liezt's heart.


Ever since the past, Liezt had always faced situations like this. Her previous job was to listen to humans scream, fear, and listen to everything about themselves. You might think Liezt was a psychologist…


Back when the Euphrates-Tigris Hundred-Year War was still raging—a horrific war that seemed endless.


Liezt Twilight was a secret spy agent from the special division [Twilight] formed by the Tigris Empire. Liezt was the finest spy during wartime. Besides being a spy, she was also tasked with interrogating prisoners of war from the Euphrates Kingdom.


Her interrogation methods were extremely effective and always hit the mark. In the espionage world, Liezt frequently utilized her disguise abilities—ranging from clothing and personality down to her voice. Her disguise capability was beyond reason; she could adopt over 100 personalities and adapt them to any situation. When she went into disguise, no one could track her down.


Liezt's interrogation method was playing with her target's psychology; she could alter her personality to match her target's fear or feelings. If she felt the target was stubborn or impossible to talk to, Liezt would resort to violence and torture. 


***


Azure ran in a hurry, passing by people quickly and heading toward the secret room leading to the underground laboratory path. Her expression was deeply anxious—the first time she had shown such an expression in the past 7 years.


“This smell…”


Azure ran down the corridor and arrived before the iron door of the laboratory, which turned out to be blown open. Azure entered the room and saw Zeeran unconscious in his wheelchair with several gunshot wounds to his chest. Blood was still flowing from his wounds.


Azure immediately looked for bandages to administer first aid to Zeeran, beginning to care for him carefully. Azure appeared confused about how to treat him, as she truly never understood how to administer first aid or treat wounds.


“At a time like this…… I wish Zyfel were here ……hang in there, brother……”


Azure, struggling to care for Zeeran's wounds, began fumbling through the shelves. She searched among the shelves labeled [Resiliexcuratio Medicine]—a universal medicine specifically designed to stabilize Zeeran's health, created by Zyfel Twilight.


Without a second thought, Azure immediately administered the medicine to Zeeran, picking up a glass of water and feeding it to her brother, yet there were still no signs of life in Zeeran's body. However, the bleeding stopped instantly; the effect of this medicine was indeed extraordinarily effective. Zyfel Twilight, besides being the richest person in the world, served as a medical doctor, and was the best in the world.


Shortly after, the entire room suddenly went pitch black. Azure fell silent in shock, unable to utter a single word.


—Instantly, the room fell silent.
