High above the sea of shattered concrete and the glowing roots of the World Tree, the air was bitter cold and thin.

The metallic-bodied entity hovered silently amidst the grey ash clouds, its broad silver wings extended in absolute stillness. Devoid of eyes, mouth, or facial features, its head of polished white plate metal tilted slightly toward the horizon. Floating directly above its crown, a brilliant ring of pale white light spun with smooth, rhythmic precision, emitting a continuous, low-frequency hum that vibrated through the ambient static of the ruined world.

A ripple stirred the cloud cover. 

Emerging from the fog came another celestial form, enveloped in a matching halo of radiant light above her head and gripping a colossal, ornate metallic spear that crackled with latent energy. It bore silver wings and a structured metallic chassis, but unlike the featureless face of the observer hovering before her, its helmet parted to reveal a sculpted face that remarkably resembled a human woman. Her smooth ivory features appeared carved from stone, and her golden eyes gleamed with cold, unblinking authority as she came to a halt in mid-air.

The featureless, haloed entity did not speak in human tongue. Instead, its core flickered with a soft amber pulse, emitting a series of low, metallic murmurs—a rhythmic sequence of acoustic clicks, static chirps, and synthetic frequency pulses that echoed in the sky.

The woman-faced haloed entity adjusted her grip on the heavy spear, her golden eyes narrowing slightly as she addressed her counterpart directly.

"Sister Metatron... what?" she asked, her voice clear, melodic, yet entirely devoid of warmth. "you found her with mortal?"

Sister Metatron tilted her featureless metallic head. In response, a harsh hum of electronic noise modulated from her throat plate, followed by two short, mechanical clicks.

"how about the Longinus?" the spear-bearing entity continued, her halo pulsing softly as she inspected the distorted atmospheric readings below. "no?"

Sister Metatron gave a singular, fluid rise and fall of her silver shoulders, her halo spinning slightly faster while a soft whistle of white noise drifted from her chassis.

"and the Legion? how is 'The Harvest'?"

Sister Metatron's chest core pulsated with a soft blue rhythm, producing a short burst of rhythmic metallic clicks and low frequency hums.

The woman-faced entity tilted her head, her golden eyes reflecting the data stream as her brow furrowed subtly.

"Still processing...? Ah, separating into Hunting and Harvester divisions." She nodded slowly, resting her massive spear against her armored shoulder as she turned her gaze toward the vast horizon where Babylon's Harbor lay shrouded in mist. "hmm, i will report this to the higher ups."

With a swift tilt of her wings, the spear-bearing haloed entity turned and vanished into the upper stratosphere. Left alone in the quiet expanse of the sky, Sister Metatron paused, her featureless metallic head turned toward the dark clouds before her halo hummed softly, gliding silently downward to monitor the moving survivors below.

***

Far beyond the physical realm of soot and shattered stone, Princess Anna opened her eyes.

She did not feel the cold floor of the palace underground, nor the stench of blood from the day she orchestrated the Queen's assassination. Instead, a gentle, golden light bathed her face.

Anna slowly sat up, her breath hitching as she looked around. 

She was standing inside an endless, cathedral-like labyrinth. High marble archways stretched into an impossible distance, lined with towering bookshelves that climbed into vaulted ceilings of shimmering crystal. Millions of ancient leather-bound tomes, glowing tablets, and metallic scrolls rested along the shelves, smelling faintly of parchment, old wood, and ozone. Between the endless stacks stood countless pristine wooden doors of varying designs, each carved with ancient cuneiform script.

"Where... am I?" Anna whispered, stepping forward.

Her boots made no sound against the polished white marble floor. Wandering deeper through the grand corridors, she passed doorway after doorway, each leading to glimpses of swirling light and unseen worlds beyond. It felt less like a building and more like an ocean of compressed history.

Eventually, the towering book stacks opened up into a spacious, circular central court. Sunlight streamed down from an open dome overhead, illuminating a sunlit garden outside the high arched windows. 

In the very middle of the room sat a luxurious plush velvet couch. 

Sitting comfortably upon it was a striking woman with long, fiery red hair that fell in cascading waves over her shoulders. Crimson eyes gleamed with ancient intelligence beneath delicate brows, and her long, pointed ears subtly twitched at the sound of Anna's approaching footsteps. 

She was casually holding an open hardcover book in one hand, while a steaming cup of tea rested on a small mahogany table beside her.

Anna paused at the edge of the carpet, her defensive instincts flaring as she sought to understand her surroundings.

"Who are you?" Anna asked, taking a cautious step closer. "Is this... a dream?"

The red-haired woman didn't look up immediately. She slowly turned a page of her book, took a delicate sip from her teacup, and set it down onto the saucer with a soft *clink*.

Only then did her crimson eyes rise to meet Anna's gaze.

"Ah, En-gurnun," the woman said, her voice smooth, deep, and echoing with quiet amusement. "welcome to Ubbshu-Ukkinna."

Anna frowned slightly at the ancient title. "En-gurnun...? I am Princess Anna. Who are you?"

The woman set her book down onto her lap, leaning back against the velvet cushions with an enigmatic smile.

"You may call me En-sirsir... or Ruby Valentine, or Abramelin Valentine if you prefer. Call me whatever pleases you," she replied casually, gesturing softly with her hand. "I was the Second Entu, once the ruler of the Elves, and the one who forged the blade that grew into The Great Tree of Abramelin."

Ruby took another small sip of her tea, her crimson eyes sweeping across the quiet, cavernous hall.

"En-gurnun is your Entu title, just like the rest of us had ours," Ruby added smoothly, glancing toward the shadowy archways that branched off from the central lounge. "As for the other five Entu... they are probably still sleeping."

Anna blinked, a sudden look of confusion crossing her face as she mentally ran through the math.

"Wait..." Anna paused, tilting her head. "I am the Eighth Entu. If you are standing here, and I am here... that makes two of us. If there are five others sleeping, that only makes seven in total. Shouldn't there be six others sleeping?"

Ruby's expression darkened slightly, her enigmatic smile fading into a solemn gaze. She lifted one slender hand and pointed her finger toward a quiet, dark obsidian door set deep into the far corner of the court.

"The First Entu was erased and punished," Ruby said quietly, her tone dropping into a serious whisper. "She still stays in her chamber over there."

Ruby lowered her hand and turned her gaze back to Anna, her crimson eyes gleaming with a subtle warning.

"A word of advice, little En-gurnun... don't bother her," Ruby warned. She paused, swirling the remaining tea in her cup before adding with a light shrug, "Besides... she rarely ever goes outside anyway."

Setting her saucer down, Ruby leaned back against the plush velvet headrest, looking over at Anna with a playful, dry smirk. 

"Anyway, Anna..." Ruby continued, her voice dripping with non-hostile, lighthearted sarcasm, "...you are so brave that you didn't even discuss it with us first and just merged the fucking seven realms into one place. My Great Tree will get scratched... though my people might survive, at least."

Anna raised her chin, her eyes narrowing with unyielding resolve as she met the former Elven ruler's gaze.

"That's not my fault that you all left humanity behind," Anna replied smoothly, her voice firm and without a trace of hesitation. "I did what is necessary, or humanity will go extinct. As the human Entu, I must keep my priorities."

"Yes, because if humanity died today, it takes time to reincarnate them because the Underworld is currently sealed!" a tiny, high-pitched ethereal voice interrupted from above. Fluttering down on translucent, luminescent wings was a tiny spirit queen no larger than a fairy. "The goddess is missing, and I can't contact her!"

Before Ruby could respond, a low, reverberating tremor hummed through the marble floor of Ubbshu-Ukkinna.

From the shadows behind the high archways, a dark metallic clatter echoed with sudden, overwhelming presence. A colossal, obsidian-black chain—heavy, ancient, and tipped with a lethal, razor-sharp spearhead—burst into the light, its intricate divine runes flaring with a deep, pulsing radiance as it responded to Anna's voice.

With fluid, serpentine speed, the massive chain lunged forward. It slithered effortlessly around Anna's waist like a living viper, coiling up across her chest and shoulder in a sudden, breathless instant. Heavy metallic links rasped softly against her clothing, wrapping snugly around her body until the spear-tipped head hovered beside her neck, poised and vigilant like a serpent guarding its master.

Ruby's crimson eyes sparkled with genuine excitement as she tracked the living steel settling over Anna's shoulders. Setting her teacup down onto the saucer with an eager *clack*, a wide, delighted grin spread across her face.

"I like you!" Ruby declared, her voice bubbling with lively enthusiasm as she leaned forward. "We are going to have a very good relationship."

Before Anna could process Ruby's sudden enthusiasm, a series of heavy, ancient *clicks* echoed down the infinite corridors of Ubbshu-Ukkinna.

One by one, the carved wooden doors lining the high arches swung open. Bright light spilled into the central court as several figures stepped out into the hall, yawning softly and stretching their slender limbs after ages of deep slumber.

Anna's breath hitched as she realized that every single one of them was a humanoid female—her predecessor Entu of the past eras:

A tall woman stepped out bearing distinct demonic traits, her elegant curved obsidian horns gleaming under the crystal light as her thin tail swayed lazily behind her. Beside her stood a remarkably short Entu rubbing her sleepy eyes with a quiet yawn, followed by a sleek feline beastkin Entu whose soft animal ears twitched at the sudden noise. Hovering gracefully above them was a tiny, fairy-sized Spirit queen with translucent wings shimmering like twilight mist, and finally, another beastkin Entu emerged, her long, cascading hair shimmering with vibrant, glowing elemental light that pulsed like liquid fire.

Before Anna could even form a question, the beastkin Entu with the glowing elemental hair locked her eyes on Anna and gasped with pure, unbridled excitement.

"A new little sister!" she squealed happily.

Without a second's hesitation, she bounded across the marble floor and threw her arms around Anna in a fiercely tight, overwhelming embrace. Anna gasped in surprise, but before she could pull back, her face was completely plunged and practically choked between the woman's massive, soft bosom.

"Mmmph—?!" Anna muffled helplessly, her cheeks burning red as her eyes widened in shock. The dark, serpent-like chain coiled around her shoulders clattered with soft, metallic confusion as the rest of the Entu giggled warmly at the chaotic welcome.

After a few breathless moments, Alexandrite finally released her grip with a cheerful laugh. Anna stumbled back slightly, gasping for air while rubbing her glowing red cheeks.

Yet, as she looked across the court at the lively group, a gentle surge of ancient memory and spiritual resonance washed through her mind. Without anyone uttering a single introduction, Anna found essence instinctively knowing all of their names, titles, and legacies—Emerald Caleste, Quin Sapphie, Luna Diamond, Jenneate Garnet, and Alexandrite Novah. The sanctuary of Ubbshu-Ukkinna connected their souls, making them instantly familiar as if they had known each other for lifetimes.

"Alex, give the poor girl some room to breathe," Emerald chuckled in her light, bell-like voice, hopping onto a plush stool near the central table.

The mood in the court quickly settled into a relaxed, playful atmosphere as the predecessor Entu began chatting, laughing, and lightheartedly teasing one another like old friends reuniting after a brief nap. 

Seeing everyone gathering together, Luna—the horn-adorned Demon Entu—smiled softly and stepped away toward the sanctuary's lounge kitchen. A few moments later, she returned carrying a polished silver tray filled with steaming teacups and a freshly brewed pot of aromatic tea.

Luna carefully served a cup to everyone around the table, placing a steaming mug into Anna's hands. Wrapped in her coiled, dark chain and sitting among the legendary guardians of the past, Anna took a quiet sip, feeling a surprising warmth and comfort settle over her heart.

As they all settled comfortably into the plush velvet couches around the central table, the predecessor Entu began introducing themselves properly one by one:

"I am Emerald Caleste, the Third Entu—En-sig of the Dwarves," Emerald said proudly, swinging her short legs softly off the edge of her seat.

"Quin Sapphie, Fourth Entu—En-babbar," Quin added lazily, flicking her feline ears as she curled into the soft cushions. "Welcome to the lounge, little human."

"Luna Diamond, Fifth Entu—En-hulal," Luna offered with a gentle bow of her head, her dark wings folding gracefully behind her as she held her teacup.

"Jenneate Garnet, Sixth Entu—En-giznu," the fairy-sized Spirit Entu piped up, hovering right beside a teacup nearly half her size.

Ruby chuckled dryly, reaching out with one slender finger to casually poke Giznu's tiny, soft cheek. "She is the one who lets mortals reincarnate whenever she feels like it," Ruby introduced with lighthearted sarcasm.

Giznu gasped indignantly, her tiny cheeks puffing up in anger as she swatted fiercely at Ruby's finger. "What!? You are the one who made True Death into a weapon, are you for real?!" she snapped back. But because of her tiny fairy size, her furious outburst came out as a muffled, squeaky mumbling that just sounded overwhelmingly cute.

"And I'm Alexandrite Novah! Seventh Entu—En-guran!" Alex beamed, her hair pulsing with vibrant elemental light. "The one right before you came along!"

Ruby leaned back against her velvet headrest, swirling her tea with a nostalgic, quiet smile as her crimson eyes took in the full circle of women.

"It has been millennia since all of us Entu were gathered together like this," Ruby murmured, her voice carrying the weight of uncounted ages. "Tens of thousands of years... each of us sleeping through our respective epochs while the worlds turned below. To have every active Entu sitting at the very same table..." She took a slow sip, her smirk softening with genuine warmth. "...it's a rare sight indeed, little En-gurnun."

Before anyone could reply, the cozy warmth of the central court evaporated into a sudden, icy chill.

*CREAK...*

A slow, heavy sound echoed down the vaulted library corridors as the dark obsidian door shifted on its hinges. The cheerful chatter at the table died in an instant.

Every head snapped toward the quiet, shadowy corner of the court.

From within the dark chamber, a subtle, cold aura spilled forth, accompanied by a quiet, deliberate step against the marble floor.

Ruby's smile vanished. Her teacup froze halfway to her lips, her crimson eyes narrowing into a sharp, unblinking glare. Beside her, Emerald, Quin, Luna, and Alexandrite all stiffened in absolute silence as the chamber of the erased and punished First Entu stirred.
