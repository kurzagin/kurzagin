The silence that settled over the ruined plaza was unnerving, yet strangely peaceful.

For the first time since the sky turned black and the subterranean sanctuary was torn open, the surviving fifty humans could breathe without the immediate roar of metallic beasts or the suffocating pressure of impending death. Fires flickered in shallow metal barrels, casting warm amber light over the weary faces of Crows soldiers, Ravens operatives, and civilian refugees huddled together beneath makeshift canvas shelters.

They didn't know what had transpired in those final chaotic moments of the battle. They didn't know who the mysterious beastkin warriors with curved blades were, nor where they had vanished into the night. No one knew why the haloed anomaly had abruptly withdrawn instead of finishing the slaughter, nor where the remaining steel horde had scattered. 

Yet, against all logic, the ruin felt safe. A bizarre, fragile sanctuary had formed in the shadow of the colossal, silver-glowing World Tree.

Zeeran Twilight stood near the edge of the camp, his boots planted firmly on the cold concrete. He flexed his fingers and shifted his weight from one foot to the other, still coming to terms with the miraculous restoration of his legs. Beside him, Azure stood vigilant, her armless sockets quiet, her pale blue eyes scanning the perimeter while her internal diagnostics ran low-power scans.

Near the base of a tilted brick wall rested the eight-foot transparent Twilight Crystal cube. Inside its ice-blue stasis field, Liezt Twilight remained suspended, completely frozen in time, the tree branch pinning her torso locked harmlessly in place. Her vital signs were static—neither worsening nor improving—held in absolute equilibrium by the crystal's emergency protocols.

Erika stepped up beside Zeeran, a clean strip of cloth tightly bound around her forehead where blood had dried. Beside her stood Belle, adjusting her spectacles while holding a battered clipboard. Her violet eyes were heavy with fatigue, but Erika's gaze was sharp as she looked over the resting crowd. Old J.J sat on an overturned supply crate nearby, cleaning ash from his weathered shotgun alongside Dr. Griffin and Derek, Zack, and Victoria of The Ravens.

Zeeran turned to face the gathered leaders. It was time.

"Gather everyone close," Zeeran said softly, his voice carrying a calm authority that drew immediate attention. "We need to talk."

Erika, Belle, J.J, and the three Ravens officers stepped forward, forming a tight circle around Zeeran and Azure near Liezt’s crystalline pod. The surrounding refugees and Crows soldiers leaned in, listening intently.

"We survived the night," Zeeran began, looking each of them in the eye. "And right now, it feels safe. The monsters are gone, the sky is still, and we finally have a moment to rest. But make no mistake—this safety is an illusion."

He pointed toward the shattered rubble and the depleted crates of rations stacked nearby.

"Our underground sanctuary is completely destroyed," Zeeran continued, his tone sharp and pragmatic. "Our reserves are running dangerously low. If we stay here, sitting in the ash beneath this tree and waiting for answers, we won't survive. Stagnation is slow execution."

Erika crossed her arms, her bandaged forehead tightening as she gazed off toward the distant, darkened horizon beyond the city ruins.

"If we can't stay here... then we have to move far away from the epicenter," Erika said firmly. "Antarctica. Look at what's happening around us. This colossal World Tree, the spatial distortion, the emergence of those metallic monstrosities—it all erupted right here, near the heart of Euphrates and Tigris. The anomaly is rooted in this region. But beyond these lands lies an unknown horizon."

She gestured toward the south. "Antarctica is isolated by thousands of miles of ocean. It’s far removed from the continental fault lines and the immediate reach of the World Tree's canopy. It's worth pursuing."

Erika looked directly at Zeeran, expecting his instant tactical approval. 

Instead, for the first time in his entire life... Zeeran hesitated.

His sharp grey eyes stared blankly at the ground, his mind spinning through endless strategic calculations that kept collapsing into dead ends. 

*What are the odds?* he thought bitterly.

He looked up at the night sky. In just a few short hours, the sky would turn grey and morning would arrive. Out in the open daylight, they would be sitting ducks. The metallic enemies possessed wings and could fly effortlessly through the clouds, diving down to impale survivors with massive metallic spears. Their standard tactical rifles and bullet rounds were utterly useless—bullets simply ricocheted off the creatures' dense silver alloy hulls. 

Worse still, Zeeran remembered the terrifying clash with the haloed entity. Azure had thrown her absolute best high-velocity kick—a strike powerful enough to shatter reinforced bunker walls—and it hadn't even left a scratch on the haloed monster's defense. 

Zeeran's thoughts flickered back to the mysterious beastkin group—the dragon-horned warrior woman and her silk-clad squad wielding curved blades. They had sliced through the steel beasts like paper and vanished. But no matter how hard Zeeran tried to analyze it, he couldn't connect anything. Who were they? Why had they intervened? Were they allies, or just another unknown faction roaming the broken world? There were too many unknown variables.

Zeeran took a slow breath, forcing his voice to remain steady. "If we need to get to Antarctica... and if we need to reach the Ark... we need to move fast. Before daylight exposes us."

Old J.J let out a dry, gravelly sigh, rubbing the back of his neck. "Moving fast sounds great, kid, but let's be real. In a frontline break, I'm only gonna be a burden to you all. I can't run or dodge like you young folk. My specialty is weapons and dealing arms—not charging into battle against flying steel demons."

Derek of The Ravens stepped forward, shaking his head grimly. "We're in bad shape too. My squad is almost completely out of gasses and fuel for our tactical gear. We can't maintain high-mobility maneuvers without fresh supply canisters."

Hearing the rising concerns, Azure stepped forward, her pale blue eyes glowing softly.

"The objective remains valid," Azure stated in her calm, analytical tone. "In fact, basically everyone needs to go to Antarctica regardless. The Antarctic garrison was built alongside the Twilight family's primary central laboratory and deep-tech facility. It contains the raw materials, diagnostic docks, and manufacturing units required to rebuild our infrastructure—and repair my chassis."

She looked at Zeeran. "And as I verified through Liezt's stasis pod, the Ark hidden in Dock 7 at Babylon Harbor is built from the exact same impervious Twilight Crystal matrix. It is preserved and ready for deployment."

The heavy weight of the mission settled over the group. Moving fifty battered survivors through monster-infested ruins to a coastal harbor, low on gas, short on ammo, with an arms supplier who couldn't fight... the tension was thick enough to suffocate them.

Suddenly, a quiet, matter-of-fact voice broke through the grave silence.

"Guys..."

Everyone turned their heads. 

Belle was standing beside Erika, holding her clip-board with one hand while using her index finger to carefully push her spectacles up the bridge of her nose. Her expression was deadpan yet completely serious.

"...we need food."

A brief silence hung in the air. 

Erika blinked. J.J froze. Derek, Zack, and Victoria stared at her in utter disbelief. Then, J.J let out a sudden, boisterous bark of laughter, shaking his head as a few Crows soldiers chuckled softly behind him. Erika couldn't help but crack a weary smile, letting out a soft sigh as the suffocating tension in the air dissolved.

"She's right," Erika said, her tone lighter. "We can't sail anywhere on empty stomachs."

Zeeran looked at Belle, then around at his smiling allies, feeling the cold weight in his chest lift just a fraction. He nodded firmly.

"Alright," Zeeran declared, a faint smile touching his lips. "We scavenge what food and fuel we can carry, we pack Liezt's stasis cube, and we head straight for Babylon Harbor. Let's move!"
