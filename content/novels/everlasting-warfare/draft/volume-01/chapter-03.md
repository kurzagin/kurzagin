[Three Days After Maintenance]


■ ■ ■


I opened my eyes and saw the sight of an unfamiliar white ceiling, one I had never seen before. Yet that ceiling brought back memories of the past; I used to see it so often when I was a child.


A memory flashed through my mind, a quiet recollection of my younger sisters gathered together in the Twilight Room. The responsibility of protecting my family and restoring our rightful standing in this broken world rested entirely on my shoulders.

I am Zeeran Twilight, Ex-Supreme Commander of the Tigris Imperial forces and Patriarch of House Twilight. Every action I take, every strategic calculation I design, is built solely for the preservation and absolute dominance of my family.

Three years ago, when Erika first met me, she was the crown princess of the Euphrates Kingdom.


Yes, she was the rightful heir to the royal throne, until the royal court replaced her, choosing Anna to be the heir instead.


When she first met me, she didn't dare look me directly in the eye, keeping her head constantly bowed. Back then, she proposed a contract. She wanted us to help her assassinate the Queen and Anna—but there was no way I would grant that, right?


Erika was merely a fugitive princess back then, hunted down by the entire royal army, and we sheltered her in our underground base.


So what made her more valuable than the Queen and Anna? If we granted her request by killing the Queen and Anna, who would rule the kingdom? Erika? How could her subjects possibly accept that? The kingdom would devolve into chaos and disorder.


Erika, not knowing what to do, pleaded for us to simply assist her—helping with everything necessary to assassinate the Queen and Anna. Upon hearing this, I recognized the potential that would arise in the future and agreed.


Oh, about the contract……

I refer to this contract as the [Twilight Contract]. As the leader of Twilight, I distributed business cards containing hidden messages with our contact information across every corner of the kingdom. Only intelligent people could reach out to us. Naturally, the royal court never found out about this, because they were fools.


Besides using business cards, we also spread the word orally through select individuals.


 —For anyone in need of help, come to Twilight.


And that was how I forged countless contracts with people in need of assistance. We would do anything from assassination, money laundering, protection, support, serving as assistants, mediators, supplying weapons, and so on—but always for a reward that satisfied me.


Yesterday, I intentionally let myself be shot by members of The Crows so that Azure would join the game. Did you really think Azure would sit idly by knowing I risked my life like that purely for profit? Azure wouldn't allow it, which is why I didn't tell her anything about this plan.


In this plan, I would capitalize on the contract previously established between Twilight and Erika, regarding…

 —If The Crows, intentionally or unintentionally, cause injury to a member of Twilight or interfere with Twilight operations, the contract will be deemed null and void, and Twilight reserves the right to eliminate or neutralize all members of The Crows upon termination of the contract.


This meant that if The Crows harmed or interfered with any of us, we would reclaim everything we had ever given them. Furthermore, since the members of The Crows were themselves the product of our assistance, killing all members of The Crows still fell within the scope agreed upon by both parties when the contract was made.


I provoked the members of The Crows who entered my lab so one of them would drop their membership pin, enabling Azure to identify the perpetrator. Those members of The Crows were recruits who didn't understand much about us yet. I was the one who handpicked them; everything had been meticulously planned ever since the contract was first drafted. It was a gamble with my life—fortunately, they didn't shoot me in the head, or I could have died right then and there.


If Azure learned that The Crows breached the contract, she would immediately carry out the executions. But that wasn't what I planned; I deliberately planted a warning in Azure's mind so she wouldn't end up slaughtering all members of The Crows.


Yes, all of this was indeed planned…… though some parts relied on luck, making it a flawed plan. It wasn't like me to be like this……


Upon opening my eyes, I found myself in a medical ward. IV tubes were attached to my arm. Looking to my right, an elderly man in medical attire was inspecting a diagnostic parchment. I tried to sit up, but my chest throbbed violently.

“Do not move,” the old man said neutrally. “Your mechanical heart core—the Maschinenherz—was grazed by the bullet. It is impossible to repair or replace such a core here, but the heavy concentration of Vigortio in your blood stream stabilized your vital signs just enough to keep you alive.”

Maschinenherz core... grazed? And Vigortio?

The man set the parchment onto the desk, revealing a face deeply etched in my memory—grey hair, wire-rimmed glasses, and piercing blue eyes.

“Griffin,” I said coldly, my eyes narrowing.

“Your condition is critical,” Griffin stated, ignoring my glare. “The Maschinenherz core is compromised, and the toxicity of long-term Vigortio consumption is reaching end-stage physical paralysis. The temporary chemical surge in your system will allow you brief mobility, but your body cannot sustain this.”

“I do not require your medical concern, Griffin,” I answered evenly, my voice cold and calm. “The state of my body is the direct result of your White House experiments.”

Driven by sheer force of will and the temporary physical boost from Vigortio active in his system, Zeeran pulled himself up from the bed, tearing out the IV tubes attached to his arm. Grasping the wall for support as his legs trembled under the strain, he moved toward the door. He knew the temporary surge would not last long, but he needed to oversee the command room and enforce the contract.

■ ■ ■

When Zeeran entered the room, he walked with his hands groping along the wall, his legs visibly trembling, lacking strength. Seeing Zeeran like that, Azure immediately stood up from her seat and ran toward Zeeran, helping him walk to sit in a chair.


“Brother… are you alright?”


“Don't worry, Azure. Ordinary bullets like that won't kill me.” 


Azure supported Zeeran's arm over her shoulder, striving to help him walk. Once there, Zeeran sat in the chair and motioned for Erika to sit beside him. Azure climbed onto Zeeran's lap.


“Azure… I'm paralyzed, you know?”


Azure ignored Zeeran's words and remained seated on his lap with a pout. Azure was somewhat upset with Zeeran for keeping his plans secret and causing her worry.


Realizing this, Zeeran panicked slightly, but since his legs couldn't support Azure's weight, he shifted backward so Azure could sit on the front section of the chair.

Which only made his younger sister pout even more.


“Well? What kind of new contract are you offering?”


Erika, who had remained silent, slowly approached the chair beside Zeeran. Her gaze was intense, staring sharply into Zeeran's eyes; her silence let everyone know she was furious. It seemed she had already anticipated this outcome in her mind.


“Our previous contract was accidentally breach—”


“I don't care whether it was intentional or not. What if you're lying?”


“...”

“My apologies, I don't wish to drag this matter out any further.”

“To repeat, I am proposing a new contract between The Crows and Twilight.”

“The new contract will still follow the terms of the old one; I simply want this incident to be treated as though it never happened.”


“In that case, I will act as though this incident never happened. But in return, The Crows must allow Twilight to intervene in everything that occurs within The Crows from now on.”

“Starting from plans, logistics, activities, and everything else within the scope of The Crows' organization.”

“Twilight's position must be at the very top of the hierarchy.”


Erika merely gritted her teeth, her facial expression no longer concealable…… her rage boiled over, yet she didn't dare voice it.


Erika had always detested Zeeran; to her, Zeeran was the single least trustworthy person in Twilight. Erika often contemplated the possibility that Zeeran had ulterior motives for The Crows. She assumed Zeeran would pull something like this and had prepared to counter it, but she never expected Zeeran to move so quickly.


“In other words… Twilight is taking over The Crows?”


The statement escaped Erika's lips, sweat glistening on her face as she looked at Azure in fear.


“Correct.”


Zeeran answered in an arrogant tone, tilting his chin up, as though looking down at Erika with disgust.


Ever since childhood, Zeeran was deeply supremacist—he regarded 'Twilight', which was actually his family surname, as the name of a race or tribe, meaning he viewed Twilight as the supreme race above all other humans.


Unable to say anything more, Erika merely nodded her head.

“Very well.”


Erika's pride was trampled; she wanted to burst into rage, but she couldn't gamble the lives of all of The Crows over her ego.


“Belle, please bring me parchment and a quill.”


Erika called out to Belle—Isabelle Rätsel, her confidante and closest friend. She had twin-braided grey hair and exhibited a clumsy demeanor as she jogged to the door, making her appear unreliable.


“Okay!”


Moments later, Belle returned with a leather parchment and a quill set… then handed them to Erika. Erika began preparing to write. A new contract between Twilight and The Crows was drafted once again.


Finishing the writing, Erika unpinned the single bird-wing pin from her chest, pricked her index finger with the needle point, and pressed a bloody thumbprint onto the document—just as she had done in the past.


“It is done.”


Erika handed the pin to Zeeran, who arrogantly mimicked the action; together, the two of them sealed the fate of The Crows with their blood.


Silence enveloped the room; the agreement weighed heavily upon the members of The Crows who stood powerless, bewildered, unable to grasp the magnitude of what had transpired.


–The contract was established and agreed upon by both parties.


Once the contract was officially signed by both parties, Zeeran noticed there were no windows or sunlight in the room, realizing they were deep underground.


"Erika, prepare two rooms for me and Azure. We will stay overnight here," he instructed, his eyes sweeping the surroundings.

 
"--And retrieve my wheelchair from Griffin's place."


“Understood.”


Erika stood up and promptly executed Zeeran's request, leaving to prepare quarters for him and Azure. The room fell into immediate stillness as everyone present dispersed simultaneously; no one dared break the silence.


Once Erika returned with the wheelchair, she guided Azure and Zeeran to their respective rooms. Both rooms were similar—plain, cluttered with scattered tools, and smelling faintly of scrap iron. Nonetheless, Zeeran acknowledged that given their underground location, these rooms were more than adequate.


"I still have business to attend to. If you need anything, simply contact anyone you encounter, and your request will be taken care of immediately."


With those parting words, Erika departed, leaving Zeeran accompanied by the quiet underground surroundings. However, Belle approached her, and Erika directed her with a simple order.


"Belle, follow me to the HQ Room."


Belle immediately followed behind Erika without a word as they walked down a dimly lit corridor. At the end stood a door, which Erika opened to reveal a space starkly contrasting the previous rooms. The room beyond was decorated with various cute, charming plushies, creating a delightful atmosphere.


Erika walked to the center of the room and pressed a button on one of her rabbit plushies. A rumbling sound echoed as a secret door appeared, leading to an even deeper subterranean layer. Erika and Belle descended the stairs; Erika paused briefly to hit another switch on the wall. The room illuminated, revealing its true purpose—a training facility equipped for target shooting and combat practice.


Everything about the room was custom-designed to vent Erika's rage, a testament to her vengeful nature.


This room served as a secret sanctuary to hone her combat skills and release the inner turmoil raging inside her. The plushies and innocent-looking room above concealed Erika's true self from the other members.


"DAMN IT! WHAT IS WRONG WITH THAT GUY!!!???"


Erika's furious scream echoed throughout the room as she unleashed a flurry of kicks against the wall. All the pent-up emotion built since her earlier encounter with Zeeran exploded into an unfiltered display of rage.


"HE INTENTIONALLY MADE ME LOOK LIKE SUCH A LOSER!"


Belle, unfazed by Erika's abrupt shift in temperament, remained calm. Erika's vengeful and hot-tempered side was no secret to Belle, who had witnessed it before; no other member knew of it.


"I'm certain he planned on getting shot by our members, making it look as though we breached the contract first,"


Belle commented, still astonished by Zeeran's resilience. She couldn't comprehend how Zeeran survived being shot near his heart. By all logic, he should have been dead, yet he defied logic itself.


"Still, I was shocked that he survived in that condition," Belle added, her analytical mind processing the situation.


"I'm going to kill him tonight."


The gravity in Erika's eyes was unmistakable. She harbored a burning desire to end Zeeran's existence as quickly as possible.

Belle simply adjusted her glasses with precision, nodding in agreement with Erika's plan to take Zeeran's life.

■ ■ ■

Finally, night fell. The underground corridor was blanketed in a tense silence as Erika, cloaked in shadow, crept forward with a dagger concealed behind her thigh, every step heavy with lethal intent. The door to Zeeran's room creaked open, revealing Zeeran seated at a table, absorbed in a weathered-looking book.

Erika's resolve faltered as she observed that Zeeran was still awake.

Zeeran, without turning around, spoke calmly as though he knew Erika was coming, sending a chill down her spine.

"I've been waiting for you, Erika."

Her killing intent hung frozen in the air by the unexpected turn of events.

"How did you–?"

"I know. Your footsteps echo differently when you're plotting something. Poetic, isn't it? The leader of The Crows, scheming an assassination in the dark."

Erika struggled to find her voice, her panic halting her in her tracks for a moment.

"I know you better than you think, Erika. Now, why don't you put that dagger away and let's talk."

Zeeran closed his book and turned toward Erika.

The room seemed to shrink around them, the atmosphere thick with unspoken tension. Erika, disarmed by Zeeran's terrifying composure, took a deep breath, attempting to recalibrate her emotions.

"Why, Zeeran? Why betray me, betray The Crows? We trusted you!"

Zeeran finally looked up, his gaze piercing right through Erika's face.

"Betrayal? Ha! I've never allied myself with anyone. I bend everyone to my will for my own goals. And right now, that goal is ensuring the happiness of all my siblings, including Azure."

The air in the room grew heavy with tension as Erika confronted Zeeran, her anger surging.

"You want your siblings to be happy? Siblings, you say!? You just use your siblings as weapons to kill people!"

Her voice rose, echoing with high intensity as she accused Zeeran of manipulating his brother for his own gain.

Erika continued, her fury reaching a fever pitch,

"You always want something! You've been scheming ever since we made that damn contract! You sent two of your spies to watch us! You never trusted me from the start! I knew this would happen!"

Her words echoed across the room—a tempest of frustration and hatred.

Zeeran struck back with equal force, his voice slicing through the high-pressure atmosphere.

"How dare you lecture me about my relationship with my sibling! You? The person who wanted to kill her own sister for the royal throne?"

His voice thundered, a fierce counterattack in the verbal battle they engaged in.

"You think you're any better? You were willing to spill blood, betray trust, all for a crown! Your throne is built on the blood of others, and you call me a manipulator? Take a look in the mirror!"
 "You're the one who breached the contract by sending Sakura and Rosette away! They're part of Twilight!"
 "The contract was crystal clear—there was a rule that every member must be reported to Twilight! Yet you kept Griffin a secret!"

As Zeeran uttered those words in rage, his schizophrenia flared up; he saw something behind Erika—a giant eye watching him. It left him feeling cornered and pressurized, as if someone were spectating his every move right now. Zeeran sweated profusely, yet he feigned composure inside, a desperate act of intimidation against his conversational opponent.

The room felt smaller as the weight of their conflicting motives bore down upon them. The trading of accusations heightened the intensity, setting the stage for an impending clash between Erika and Zeeran—a battle of words reflecting a deeper, darker conflict lurking beneath the surface.

"You've always been obsessed with Azure, haven't you?" Zeeran pointed toward Erika's grey hair; as an Euphratean, Erika should have had blonde hair.
 "You want to be part of us, don't you?" 

Stung by his words, Erika suddenly brandished the dagger she had been hiding and lunged to stab Zeeran,

"SHUT UP!"

Zeeran was nearly stabbed; fortunately, he moved quickly and the dagger only struck the front frame of his wheelchair.

"Azure won't sit idly by with someone who hurts me. Do you want to trade all members of The Crows for me?" 

Zeeran, hiding his cold sweat, put on a tough act to intimidate Erika. However, Erika seemed unfazed by it.

Erika smiled, a sudden idea forming in her mind,

"In that case... I won't kill you."

"I'll alter a thing or two in your brain through surgery, and then you'll become my puppet. Azure won't notice a thing." 

"I know you're just bluffing. There's no technology capable of doing that."

"Is that so? There is someone… one person."
 "You surely know who."

Zeeran tried to scan Erika's face, guessing what she was talking about, as cold sweat broke out even heavier on his face, indicating fear and the unexpected revelation he had realized.

"Frankenstein!?”
 “Do you have Frankenstein inside The Crows?"

Zeeran uttered a name—Frankenstein, a disciple of Zyfel Twilight. He was the most skilled doctor who had learned directly from Zyfel himself, but when Zyfel decided to travel, Frankenstein abandoned Twilight. 

Erika merely smiled in silence. As she stepped closer, her eyes locked onto Zeeran with unwavering resolve. But then, she sensed someone watching from behind the door. Erika concealed her dagger once more and glared at Zeeran with intense anger.

The giant eye behind Erika now faded away; Zeeran felt relieved when Erika hid her dagger again, and spoke arrogantly,

"Why? Didn't you want to operate on my brain?"

"You don't know the state of the world right now, do you? Yeah... you remain arrogant because you don't know what's out there."
 "Take a look at the outside world tomorrow. Soon." Her advice cut through the air as Erika promptly left the room, regaining her composure.

Erika exited, suppressing her rage, leaving behind palpable tension. As the door closed, she bumped into Azure in the dark corridor.

"Ah! Senior! Good evening! I was just checking on Zeeran's health. Now, I'm heading off to my room. See you later!" she said hurriedly.

Azure remained silent, her eyes fixed on Erika as she vanished into the shadows.

She entered Zeeran's room, a somber silence filling the air. The room echoed with Zeeran's sighs and heavy breathing, his frail figure struggling in his wheelchair.

Azure spoke softly, her voice soothing amidst the stillness,

"Brother, you're acting tough. Your body is already weak, and it grew even weaker after being shot."

Maintaining a careful distance, Azure observed Zeeran's struggle, understanding that at this moment, he would not accept help from her.

Between coughing fits, Zeeran insisted,

"This is necessary."

Azure, eyes filled with worry, voiced the fear gripping her heart,

"But when will this end? There's nothing left. If you leave, I'll be alone. I don't want to be alone."

Zeeran reached for the medication on his desk, explaining with a heavy heart,

"....." Zeeran paused, wanting to say something but hesitating,
 "My Maschinenherz—"

 "—is grazed," Azure added.

Zeeran was stunned. How did Azure know? Perhaps Azure had met Griffin and was informed by him.

'Damn Griffin,' he thought to himself. Zeeran took a deep breath,
 "From the start, that Maschinenherz should have been crafted from Twilight Crystal."

Azure looked into Zeeran's eyes, her cold expression replaced by a piteous look. After taking in the room for a moment, she suddenly spotted a familiar blade lying on the table. Azure walked over to the desk.

Without hesitation, she gripped the dagger, intending to stab herself. As she raised the blade high and swiftly attempted to plunge it into her heart–

Zeeran immediately used his own hand to catch the blade, despite having no protection on his palm and Azure's strike being lightning-fast. The blade sliced open Zeeran's palm; before it could penetrate any deeper, he knocked it aside and threw it far away.

Zeeran's face strained against the pain, blood dripping from his hand, yet he neither screamed nor showed signs of agony. 
 "No, don't do that."

Azure gasped in shock when Zeeran discarded the dagger and got injured in the process. Panic and confusion washed over her face as she tore a strip from her lab coat and wrapped it around Zeeran's wounded hand.

"Your Maschinenherz is grazed…… I can give you mine."


 "No! Everything I've done would be useless if you did that!"

Zeeran held the Vigortio in his trembling hand, a faint glimmer of hope returning to his weary eyes. As he swallowed the pill, he instantly felt a familiar surge of vitality coursing through his veins—a comforting sensation.

Azure stood before him with a mixture of wonder and hurt in her eyes. Her voice trembled as she spoke,

"That's Vigortio……" she whispered, Azure's gaze fixed directly on Zeeran's eyes.

"I thought…… all Vigortio…… had been burned……” 
 “---I… can never guess…… your plans, Brother.”
 “Out of nowhere you plan this and that…… never telling me… suddenly I'm dragged in……”
 “What if it fails because of me?”
 “......”
 “Brother Zeeran… really is the smartest at planning things in our family.”
 "...But...... I feel left behind."
 “Zyfel and Chizu are traveling to find a cure for you, Zephyr's whereabouts are still unknown, and Liezt…… even if she seems to just mess around, she's actually gathering intel on Zephyr's tracks from everyone she meets.”


Zeeran was stunned; it wasn't like Azure to speak so much. Had he gone too far in keeping Azure out, only to abruptly drag her into his plan without a word?

“As for me…”
 "All I can do is... kill people, just a tool for killing."
Azure lowered her head while gently squeezing Zeeran's hand, her expression making Zeeran feel deeply guilty.

Zeeran stroked Azure's cheek and lifted her head so their eyes met,
 “I'm sorry,”
 “I couldn't think of any other way… This was the only way for all of you to live happily after I leave you all behind.”
 “Taking over The Crows will allow you to control other nations quickly, and then the New World Order can be implemented soon after.”
 “You won't have any enemies left.”
 “And finally I can rest in peace—”
 “Just a little longer.”

The New World Order was an ambitious plan Zeeran Twilight had harbored since childhood. He wanted to rule the entire world, becoming the center of global governance and trade; in his view, eternal peace could only be achieved if the whole world was ruled by Twilight.

This plan was already known to Azure and all his other younger siblings. Zyfel was instrumental in helping him with this plan; thanks to Zyfel's enterprises spanning mining, pharmaceuticals, and banking, Zyfel had successfully seized control of all world currencies from behind the shadows.

“New World Order…… huh?”

Azure simply listened to Zeeran, rendered speechless upon realizing that everything Zeeran did was for their happiness. She understood what it felt like to have an eldest brother who desperately wanted to see his younger siblings happy.

After a while, they arrived at a mutual understanding once more. Instead of returning to her own room, Azure curled up on Zeeran's chest to sleep in Zeeran's room. Ultimately, they slept together.

■ ■ ■

—When morning came, the area outside Azure and Zeeran's room suddenly turned chaotic, as they heard countless footsteps running back and forth.

“The Ravens have returned.”
