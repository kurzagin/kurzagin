[4 Days After Maintenance]

■ ■ ■

A member of The Crows scouting on the surface reported an unexpected development. He stated he had received a message from a black crow carrying a red ribbon around its leg.

The Crows used crows to communicate and exchange messages, and only one division possessed crows with ribbons on their legs—yes…

Special Division: The Ravens

The crow wore a red ribbon on its leg,
 “Victoria's crow, huh?” Erika noted, observing the crow's leg, whereupon a member of The Crows handed her a sheet of white paper.

Erika scanned the paper, reading it silently to herself. She scrutinized every word carefully, knowing that information from The Ravens could contain hidden codes. While Erika read the document, all the members of The Crows present in the room began preparing everything, as if instinctively knowing what to do upon receiving the report.

 “They will arrive in half an hour. From the report, there don't seem to be any hidden codes.”
 “However, it says they spotted a Metallic-Bodied Monster. What is the meaning of this?”

Erika shook her head in bewilderment. What did this [Metallic-Bodied Monster] they saw refer to? Were they merely exaggerating their report? Or was it a secret code?

The members of The Crows prepared several canisters that appeared to contain gas, placing them along the edge of the room. Each canister bore a different label and color.

The first was a blue-labeled canister marked [Zack], the second a red-labeled canister marked [Victoria], and the last a green-labeled canister marked [Derek].

Those were the names of the members of The Ravens division; it appeared these canisters had been deliberately prepared.

 “Everyone, prepare your gas masks! We must be ready before they arrive!”

Belle gathered all the gas masks, set them on the central table, grabbed two masks, and headed toward Erika.

 “Moe, put this on!”

 “Thank you, Belle—”
 “Oh, please give masks to those Twilights as well.”

 “Understood!” Belle picked up two more gas masks, just as Azure and Zeeran arrived in the room. Zeeran was in his wheelchair, being pushed by Azure.

Zeeran looked toward the gas canisters, examining the color labels and text on them, before asking,

 "Is this Griffin's creation? A complex gas mixture. I'm certain this gas is toxic to the rest of you, but safe for the person whose name is written on the canister."
 “However, Griffin's inventions always result in side effects for the user.”
 “Let me guess—they probably can't survive without inhaling this gas.”

Azure pushed Zeeran's wheelchair closer to Erika and Belle.

 “You certainly know your stuff, don't you?”

 “A single glance is all it takes. You didn't report information about these gas canisters to us either, did you?”
 “It turns out The Crows have breached the contract multiple times.”

Erika gritted her teeth, but she didn't deny it and calmly responded to Zeeran,

 “Apologies. Even though The Crows has grown into a large organization, our operational experience is still limited.”
 “So one or two details inevitably get overlooked in our reports.”

Erika tried to remain polite in front of the members of The Crows, as well as before Azure and Zeeran.

 “Hmm…”
 “No matter. We have already formalized a new contract.”
 “So I will treat past violations as non-existent.”

 “Thank you for your understanding,” Erika replied with a bow of her head.

Azure positioned Zeeran's wheelchair at the round table in the middle of the room, then sat beside her brother, remaining silent without saying a word. Her eyes appeared weary.

Zeeran simply waited in his chair, his eyes scanning the entire room, taking in everything present and taking place. Zeeran carefully evaluated every member of the organization; seeing their pins reminded him of Erika's report regarding their organizational structure.

According to Erika's report several months ago, within The Crows organization, several divisions played crucial roles in executing their missions.

The first division was Black Pigeon, serving as the main offensive unit. Members of this division were combat specialists responsible for executing strike operations.

Meanwhile, the second division was White Pigeon, which focused on healthcare and medical treatment. This division comprised medical experts ready to render medical aid to team members in the field.

The next division was Black Owl, acting as the reconnaissance unit. They were intelligence specialists adept at gathering critical info to support mission success.

The White Owl division was the scientist unit, responsible for developing new technologies required for their operations.

The primary division and command center was The Eagle, consisting of founding members and leaders of other divisions—Erika included. They managed missions and oversaw funding and equipment.

In addition, there was a special division, The Ravens, created by Erika herself. This division comprised only three members who were exceptionally skilled in assassination and theft, tasked with eliminating key targets prior to major operations.

However, Erika omitted several crucial secret details, such as her experiments with the gas, as well as the existence of Griffin and Frankenstein.

—Belle handed the gas masks she prepared to Azure and Zeeran, and the two put them on. Now everyone present was wearing a mask. Then, a short grey-haired man clad in all-black approached the gas canisters along the edge of the room. He glanced toward Erika, who responded with a nod—a signal to unseal the canisters.

Once the caps were opened, the gases immediately dispersed into the air, filling the underground space with three distinct gas colors. Peculiarly, the three colors did not blend, repelling one another instead.

Shortly after, the room's door burst open violently, revealing a tall, heavily built man in a black cloak with dark brown hair. He wore a gas mask which he suddenly yanked off and threw aside; his breathing was labored, as if near death. Behind him stood a woman and a shorter blonde man, all three in identical shape.

The black-haired woman with glowing red eyes also ripped off her gas mask the instant she entered, followed by the blonde man doing the same.

 “Well, well... looks like our special division ran into trouble too, huh?”
 “Where have the three of you been for the past three days?”

Erika looked at them with a mocking expression and tone.

 “Your tone is as unpleasant as ever, Chief.”

 “Are you two alright? Zack? Derek?” The black-haired woman inhaled deeply, drawing all the red gas into her lungs. She was worried about the two men.

 “I'm fine. What about yourself, Victoria?” The dark brown-haired man inhaled as well, drawing all the green gas into his lungs.

Although each seemed to pull only one color of gas into their lungs, in reality they inhaled all three gases simultaneously; once inside their respiratory tracts, incompatible gases were automatically expelled. This was because the three of them were products of past human experimentation led by Erika involving those very gases.

 “My breathing is still heavy.”

Victoria continued panting, working to stabilize her breathing, while the two men slumped into chairs.

 “Derek, are you safe? How is your injured leg?”

 “After inhaling this gas, my regeneration is recovering, so it's no problem.”
 “Outside we met several members of the Black Pigeon division. Please help them as well. I didn't bring them inside because they lost their gas masks.” Derek informed Erika, whereupon Belle immediately organized assistance, instructing several members to head out and tend to them.

Zack, Derek, and Victoria rested in their seats, staring intently at the unfamiliar figure of Zeeran. However, they appeared to already know Azure. Still, all three bore expressions of displeasure.

 “You're… Twilight, right?”
 “Why are there two Twilights here?”
 “Didn't you guys use to refuse to show up?”

Zack's gaze locked sharply onto Zeeran's eyes, his tone hostile; he clearly harbored deep suspicion toward the Twilights.

 “Watch…… your…… tone……”

Despite her weary eyes, upon hearing Zack's voice, Azure snapped awake and alert, glaring deeply at Zack. Hearing him, Zack fell silent immediately, not uttering another word.

Zeeran gently patted his younger sister's head, trying to soothe her as Azure looked visibly annoyed.

“You're The Ravens division, aren't you?”
“Your power seems dependent on those gases, considering your friend's injured leg healed right up.”
“The green gas—likely rapid regeneration.”
“Ha! Just another typical masterpiece from Griffin. Never changed since back then.”

Zeeran tilted his head up, his expression displaying his signature disgust.

Zack's face contorted in anger at Zeeran's insufferably arrogant shift in expression.

“What can a cripple like you do anyway?”

Hearing that, far from being offended, Zeeran merely smirked,

 “Indeed, I can do nothing with a body like this.”
 “My body is extremely weak; even sitting up properly takes great effort.”

 “......In the past…”
 “Someone like you…… would be dead instantly….”

 “Stop it. Now is not the time for bickering; there is a far bigger problem we must address first.”

Derek's voice broke up the quarrel, leading Zack to finally avert his eyes from Zeeran and Azure.

After a few minutes, the gas canisters were sealed again, whereupon the short man operated a vacuum device to extract all remaining gas in the room. Following that, ten individuals dressed in all-black walked in, bearing single-wing bird pins on their chests. They were evidently the Black Pigeon division members encountered by The Ravens.

Their condition was far worse than when The Ravens first arrived; their expressions were terror-stricken as if they had witnessed something horrifying. Belle escorted them to sit in the middle of the room.

Zeeran observed them, puzzled by their expressions,

 “Why do you look like that?”
 “What on earth did you see?”

The ten members gave no response to Zeeran's question, seemingly deeply traumatized. All of them lowered their heads; some clutched their faces in fear, while others stared blankly into space.

Erika joined them after finishing with the other members, surveying the group as she attempted to analyze the situation.

 “Zack, explain the details of your report regarding…”
 “The Metallic-Bodied Monster.”

Zack looked at Erika, his expression hardening into utter seriousness,
—The emergency meeting commenced.

 “Just as the name implies,”
 “It began three days ago, when The Crows launched a full-scale assault on the Queen's palace in Euphratea. We were tasked with investigating scientists tied to royal technology and were traversing the underground passages—specifically the royal drainage system.”
 “While still on the mission, the sun suddenly lost its light, and we woke up two days ago at the mission site.”
 “While in the drainage system, we heard engine noises. Investigating and peering at the surface from below, I spotted a mysterious creature—one I had never laid eyes on before.”
 “The creature was toweringly tall, its body reflective. I'm not entirely certain, but its body seemed made of a material as hard as steel, or harder.”
 “And we didn't just see one; there were many. They roamed the ruins of the destroyed city. I don't know if the city's destruction was caused by them or not. Bottom line: they are not creatures humans can fight.”
 “They possessed wings—they looked like bird wings to me, but when those wings struck down, they pulverized the earth.”

Zeeran listened intently, a theory forming in his mind that seemed most plausible regarding the creatures,

 “They are likely biological weapons from another nation.”
 “Steel bodies and steel wings are likely equipment fitted onto humans, or—”
 “Those humans themselves.”

Hearing this, Erika deemed Zeeran's explanation the most logical for now. If she could conduct gas experiments to grant abilities beyond human physical limits, then it was hardly impossible for another nation to execute even grander experiments.

 “Then how did you encounter them?”

Erika asked regarding the ten Black Pigeon division members who remained mute.

 “Yesterday, those creatures weren't in sight, so we attempted to reach the surface and send a message via Victoria's crow.”
 “After walking through the destroyed city for a while, we ran into them, and ever since then, those guys have been like this.”

Erika placed a hand on her chin, reflecting on all possibilities that could transpire. She began guessing about the mysterious entity, but suddenly one of the Black Pigeon members encountered by The Ravens spoke—

 “Bu...bullets... don't... work...”

Hearing that, Zeeran instantly wondered if the mysterious creatures were made of Twilight Crystal. The ammunition used by The Crows was merely ordinary bullets, whereas Azure possessed a pistol with specialized rounds—one of the Twilight Device Collection items, the Twilight Bullet. These bullets were vastly superior to the ones used by The Crows.

Meanwhile, Twilight Crystal was a transparent crystal discovered by Azure in a cave in the Tigris region long ago.

 “If bullets don't work, then you must use stronger weaponry.”
 “I know someone who can provide it. You surely know him, Erika—”
 “---J.J.”
 “Correct. He is your arms supplier.”
 “His location is north of the Queen's palace.”

 “But what if he died in the attack?”

 “If we don't go there, there's no other way to defeat them.”
 “From Zack's account, it seems those creatures don't attack underground.”
 “Ma'am, do you plan to stay here until you die?”
 “I suggest you take half of your current remaining forces.”

Suddenly Erika slammed her hand onto the table, her face shocked and annoyed at Zeeran's suggestion.

 “No! Half our forces, you say!?”
 “They have wings! If we take a large force, we'll just become open targets for them!”

Zeeran stared into Erika's eyes, involuntarily smirking with pleasure. Adrenaline coursed through him; this was the first time Erika had truly pushed back against him since last night. Zeeran had indeed planned to use half of The Crows' forces as bait for the creatures to gauge their strength. Erika realized this, leading Zeeran to conclude that Erika was slightly smarter than most ordinary humans.

 'Not bad, not bad,' he thought to himself.

 “If you're so terrified of that, then I'll assist you—”
 “Azure will accompany you.”

Zeeran tried to convince Erika; furthermore, he wanted to test whether Azure's [Frost Blade] could work against them, since the weapon was crafted from Twilight Crystal—a material harder than anything else.

A member of The Eagle chimed into the conversation,

 “Moe, it seems worth a shot.”
 “With Chief Azure present, our safety is guaranteed.”
 “Because no one can defeat her, even if they're from another nation.”

 “Still… half our forces is a massive number in our current situation.”

Erika bit her fingernail, trying to weigh whether they should execute the plan or not.

Seeing Erika's hesitation, Zeeran immediately invoked yesterday's Twilight agreement.

 “According to the contract—”

 “Stop it, I know.”

Erika sighed, her mind still swirling with doubts. After a few moments, Erika finally stood up, raising her hand to give orders to all members of The Crows.

“We will set out tonight. There's no need to wait long.”
 “According to intel from the special division, they aren't on the surface today.”
 “We don't know when they'll return, so we had better hurry.”
 “Prepare everything necessary!”

Following that, everyone departed the room to prepare for the mission.

Azure rose from her chair and pushed Zeeran's wheelchair back to his room. Upon arriving, Zeeran gently pulled Azure's hand, drawing her close before whispering softly into her ear,

“Your priorities are only three people.”
 “J.J… Erika, and try to locate Liezt's whereabouts.”
⃟“If something strange happens, flee and abandon the battle.”

Hearing this, Azure immediately looked shocked and slightly hurt, thinking her brother lacked faith in her abilities.

“Don't be sad like that…”
⃟“It's not that I don't trust your strength, Azure..”
⃟“It's just that we still don't know who the enemy is.”
⃟“We need to gather more intel first.”

“Understood… Brother.” Azure nodded her head.

Azure reactivated her leg armor, then reached for a dagger sheath attached to the back of her leg suit—a steel sheath shimmering white with blue accents. Azure slowly unsheathed the dagger, revealing a diamond blade with a transparent crystal set in its center, giving the blade a hollow appearance. The transparent crystal was a Twilight Crystal—a highly versatile material.

This blade was named [Frost Blade] by Azure—a dagger specifically engineered with advanced technology utilizing Twilight Crystal. The weapon's mechanism was intricate: Twilight Crystal, uniquely featuring a blunt nature, served as a vessel to contain Antarctic frost. This cold was then accumulated and transferred to the blade edge, bestowing it with ice attributes such that any object coming into contact with Frost Blade would instantly freeze.
