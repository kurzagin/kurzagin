*Thus it was inscribed upon the tablet of the high desert; thus was the deed recorded in the annals of the wasteland:*

When Utu blazed mercilessly in the high heavens,  
When the desolate sands burned into a shimmering inferno,  
Across the scorched expanse walked the titan of mortal flesh,  
Across the blinding sands walked **ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)**.

He moved with the leisurely, unbothered stride of a king without equal.  
He walked as a man who owned the sky above,  
He trod as a man who commanded the earth below.  
A simple black shirt strained against his colossal chest;  
A heavy metallic necklace clinked rhythmically upon his collarbone.  

As the desert gale caught his wild mane of red hair,  
The blazing rays of Utu ignited every burning strand—  
Igniting his head into a roaring bonfire of crimson flame,  
Scorching the very air that dared surround him.  
Beneath his heavy brow, glowing red eyes scanned the empty waste,  
Scanning the desolate horizon with absolute, heroic dominance.

There was no rush in his step.  
There was no defense in his stance.  
There was no guard raised to the heavens.  
There was only him.

---

Then did the desert floor shudder with violent tremors.  
Then did the deep earth groan beneath the march of iron.

*Thump. Thump. THUMP.*

The dunes exploded inward like a shattered vessel.  
It was no mere ambush of beasts—it was an ocean of steel.  
By the thousands, screeching Metallic-Bodied Monsters erupted from the sand,  
By the thousands, mechanical abominations swarmed like disturbed insects.  

Steel carapaces clattered in violent frenzy,  
Razor-sharp iron limbs chittered against the stone,  
An ocean of cold red optical visors locked upon his solitary form.  
The horizon drowned beneath a writhing sea of iron,  
Surging forward to crush the lone titan into dust.

At the apex of the swarm, a colossal Metallic-Bodied Monster coiled its frame,  
A grand leviathan of steel lunged from the sky.  
Its razor-sharp claw descended like a falling guillotine,  
Its iron weight aimed to split the earth beneath his feet.

**ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** stopped his leisurely walk.  
He dropped into no martial stance.  
He braced no leg; he guarded no neck.  
He stood as an unshakeable monument carved of pure manhood.

He tilted his head upward to the burning sky.  
His crimson eyes gleamed with raw, heroic amusement.  
He looked upon the descending leviathan;  
He looked upon the endless tide of metal surging behind it.

"Even though I hate King Enki..."  
His deep, thunderous voice echoed over the howling gale.  
"Even though I hate King Enki..."  
His thunderous voice drowned the screeching of the horde:  
"I hate you abominations even more!"

Without shifting his footing by a single millimeter,  
Without bending a knee or drawing back his heel,  
**ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** raised his right hand casually into the path of the descending claw.

*BOOM!*

A shockwave of compressed air and sand detonated for miles.  
The leviathan’s colossal strike smashed into his open palm—  
And stopped completely dead in its tracks.  

The kinetic backlash exploded outward like a dying star.  
In a single instant, his black shirt shattered into a cloud of torn threads,  
Scattered to the five winds of the desert waste.

Exposed beneath the blinding eye of Utu,  
His physical frame swelled with terrifying divine force.  
Muscles coiled, expanded, and bulged beyond mortal limits,  
Turning his massive torso into an impenetrable fortress of sinew.  

Across his flexing chest, across his broad shoulders,  
Across his massive arms, stylized Sumerian cuneiform tattoos ignited.  
They flared into a blazing, blinding crimson glow—  
The merciless sunlight feeding the fire of his skin,  
Until he shone as a living deity of endless war.

Etched into his massive biceps were the ancient wedge-shaped glyphs for strength: **𒆗**.  
Etched into his forearms were the ancient wedge-shaped glyphs for raw power: **𒀉**.  
Across his chest flared the sacred symbols of divine decree: **𒈨**.  
Across his shoulders burned the glyphs of heroism: **𒉆𒌨𒊕**,  
And the mark of absolute, unyielding greatness: **𒃲**.

Carved across his broad, massive back,  
The primary stylized cuneiform glyph flared with overwhelming divine power:

# **𒌨𒊕**

For before his rise, no tongue had spoken the word of Hero.  
Before his flesh stood firm, the concept of Savior held no meaning in mortal thought.  
He was the absolute origin, the primordial blueprint of the Hero model in humanity;  
Every archetypal hero, savior, and protector born in the ages after  
Is but a faint shadow, merely mimicking his eternal image.

> *Born from nothing in the dirt of the earth,*  
> *Without lineage, crown, or royal birth.*  
> *He climbed the bloody mountain through pain and strife,*  
> *To reach the absolute pinnacle of human life.*  
>  
> *Before his mortal fists, the heavens bowed low,*  
> *The pantheon acknowledged what mortals now know.*  
> *Enki bestowed the sacred ME decree,*  
> *Forging his Shumu for eternity:*  
>  
> ***𒌨𒊕***

**ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** sought no technique.  
**ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** cared for no martial form.  
Form was for the weak who begged for leverage;  
Stance was for the fragile who feared the blow.

Gritting his teeth with a raw, brutal smirk,  
He pulled his left fist back into the desert heat.  
He threw a single, stanceless punch straight into the giant leviathan’s chest.

*CRACK-BOOM!*

His bare fist caved in the reinforced steel carapace like wet paper.  
The sheer, unbridled force blasted through the monster's core,  
Detonating its metallic spine into flying splinters.  
A shockwave ripped through the air behind it,  
Obliterating fifty more beasts in a violent shower of shattered iron and sparks.

As thousands more mechanical bugs surged forward to swamp him,  
As the ocean of metal rushed to bury his body,  
**ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** just grinned, his crimson eyes flaring with divine fire.

"Bring an eternity of steel!"  
**ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** roared with a terrifying, wild laugh.  
"Bring an eternity of steel!  
My fists will shatter every last one of you before the sun ever dares to set!"

---

*Silence returned to the desert waste.*  
*Silence reclaimed the scorched sands.*

The roaring swarm was no more.  
The screeching horde had turned to dust.

A colossal mountain of twisted metal towered high above the dunes,  
A towering hill of caved-in carapaces and severed iron limbs,  
Smoking quietly beneath the scorching glare of Utu.

At the very peak of the metallic pile sat **ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)**,  
Reclining comfortably amidst the mountain of wrecked iron.  
His bare, muscular chest was coated in dust and faint metallic oil;  
His tattoos glowed with a quiet, ember-like crimson light;  
His wild red hair blew gently in the desert breeze.

Reaching casually into the pocket of his torn pants,  
He pulled forth a thick cigar.  
Placing it between his lips,  
He raised his thumb, flicked a spark off crushed armor, and lit the leaf.

He took a long, slow drag into his mighty lungs,  
Exhaling a cloud of gray smoke toward the high blue sky,  
Looking out over the quiet horizon—  
The undefeated titan upon his throne of metal.

---

Yet the desert sky did not remain silent.  
The scorching wind suddenly froze in mid-air.

From high above the ash-laden clouds, a solitary presence descended without sound.  
No wing beat against the gale; no thruster scorched the air.  
Hovering silently amidst the shimmering heatwaves floated a being of terrifying order—  
Its broad silver wings extended horizontally with motionless, rigid perfection,  
While levitating above its featureless, polished white metal head plate  
Spun a brilliant, ringed halo of pale light, turning with smooth, mechanical precision.

**ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** lowered his cigar.  
His glowing crimson eyes locked onto the hovering, featureless anomaly.

A deep, ancient memory stirred from the depths of forgotten ages,  
A realization echoing through his blood as though he had waited for this moment for an eternity—  
As though every battle, every corpse, and every century of waiting  
Had been nothing more than a prelude to this single arrival.

A raw, dangerous smirk spread across his lips beneath the curling smoke.

*"So..."* **ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** whispered, his thunderous voice vibrating with ancient anticipation.  
*"You have finally come."*
