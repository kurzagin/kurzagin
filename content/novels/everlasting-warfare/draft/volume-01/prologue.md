—And on that day, the entire Earth was plunged into darkness without light for just a few seconds. After they regained their sight, all light returned, and the sun reappeared, but in the sky hung strange writing. They did not know what language the text was written in, but all of them—miraculously—understood its meaning.

「1 Day After Maintenance」

They all looked up at the sky, wondering in confusion,

What is the meaning of Maintenance?

■ ■ ■

The Euphrates Kingdom has been known by various countries due to 「Babylon's Harbor」, which is extremely famous among the wealthy. This place is also a trade hub for several countries in the world, with all kinds of businesses operating here, making it the busiest place in the world.

Babylon’s Harbor is located at the mouth of two rivers: the Euphrates River and the Tigris River.

Here, one main attraction of the harbor stands out: 「The Ark」, a multi-deck cruise ship docked at the pier, built with extreme luxury designed to showcase its magnificence. The Ark features a striking modern design with gleaming gold accents, opulent furnishings, and cutting-edge technology. But even after a year since its construction, The Ark has yet to sail into open waters. According to rumors, The Ark possesses an internal defense and warship system, kept secret by the government.

During a press conference and interview, the shipbuilders made a shocking declaration when asked about the vessel's security, firmly answering,

 —Not even God could sink The Ark

It was a somewhat arrogant statement on their part, yet it made everyone feel pleased and confident in The Ark's safety.

But let us wrap up our talk about this ship,

After taking a look at the ship, you only need to walk a short distance from the coastline, and there will be a large sign reading 「Sphinx Casino」, a casino favored by the wealthy. Walking a little deeper into one of the nearby alleys, there are various prisons used to detain slaves.

A slave trading market—immensely favored by the rich. Every slave being sold was captured from the Tigris Empire, ranging from commoners to former nobles. Those who lacked education were trained and given basic knowledge so they could help complete various tasks for their prospective owners. Former nobles were auctioned off, as they possessed higher quality than other slaves. Some female slaves boasted beautiful faces, while others possessed quite high intelligence.

After entering the slave trade area, you will find a wide variety of shops selling bladed weapons such as swords, knives, spears, and so forth.

True to the harbor's slogan,

—In this harbor, you can do whatever you want, without any constraining rules. The only rule is to have fun.

As long as the actions of these people do not harm the kingdom, whatever they wish to do is completely permissible; but if their actions begin to offend the kingdom, the kingdom's law enforcers will not sit idly by.

So far, no anarchic acts have occurred in Euphrates other than those by a terrorist organization calling themselves [The Crows], an organization that always operates behind the shadows. As the largest criminal organization to ever exist in the world, they frequently conduct transactions in Babylon’s Harbor, yet they also consistently manage to escape whenever the kingdom begins taking action.

You must be wondering: why does the Euphrates Kingdom have a place like this? Why does this country have a place to entertain the rich? Why does this place allow so many illegal activities? Who rules the Euphrates Kingdom? Why has The Ark never set sail? And various other questions…

Now, I will tell you what you wish to know…
