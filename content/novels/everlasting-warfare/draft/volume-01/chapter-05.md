Night had settled over the realm, but without stars or moon—only a heavy, suffocating darkness that hung like a funeral shroud over the surface world.

Erika stepped out from the underground drainage exit first, her gloved fingers tightly gripping her sidearm. Behind her followed Azure, her bare feet silent against the cold stone, with Zack, Derek, Victoria, and a strike squad of The Crows filing out into the open air.

The moment their eyes adjusted to the surface, a collective shiver ran through the squad.

The world outside was terrifying.

What had once been majestic stone thoroughfares, bustling market squares, and towering spires of Euphratea was now a horrifying expanse of ruined desolation. Entire avenues had collapsed inward like crushed bone. Massive fissures tore through the asphalt and concrete, swallowing carriage ways and building foundations whole. Smoke rose in faint, ghost-like wisps from shattered windows, and the silhouette of the sky looked completely wrong—distorted by distant ash and uncanny static.


 “Gods above...” Victoria murmured, her voice trembling beneath her gas mask. “Is this... the same city we walked in just days ago?”

⃟“Visual navigation is completely useless,” Derek muttered, squinting into the fog. “The landmarks are gone. The main watchtower has been halved, and the eastern bridge is dust.”

Erika pulled a crumpled paper map from her coat pocket, spreading it across the hood of an abandoned, burnt-out vehicle chassis. Azure stepped closer, tilting her head as her quiet eyes scanned the faded ink.

⃟“We can't rely on visual roads,” Erika said coldly, though her hand shook slightly as she pinpointed their coordinate. “If we follow where the roads *used* to be, we'll walk straight into a ravine. We trace the old sewer lines on this map. J.J's bar was built right above the northern drainage valve.”

⃟“J.J...” Zack grunted, checking the chamber of his rifle. “If that stubborn old bastard is dead, we're coming back empty-handed.”

⃟“He won't die easily,” Azure said softly. Her voice was calm, yet carried an eerie certainty.

The group began moving cautiously through the ruins. Every shadow seemed like a lurking threat; every squeak of metal in the wind made the squad raise their weapons in sheer terror. They braced themselves for the horrifying Metallic-Bodied Monsters that Zack had described—the winged, bullet-proof abominations that had slaughtered the royal guard.

Yet, as they wandered through district after district, an unsettling silence gripped the city.

There were no metallic monsters. No winged beasts roosting on crumbled roofs. No echoing metallic screeches. Nothing except the hollow whistling of the wind through broken masonry.

⃟“It's too quiet...” Zack whispered, sweat trickling down his temple beneath his mask. “They were everywhere three days ago. Where did they go?”

⃟“Don't let your guard down,” Erika warned. “Keep moving towards the coordinates.”

After nearly an hour of tense, nerve-wracking navigation through twisted rebar and shattered glass, they finally reached the northern alleyway specified on Erika's map.

There, nestled between two leaning structures that looked ready to collapse at any second, stood the remnants of J.J's Bar.

The building was completely wrecked. The front facade was caved in; half of the wooden neon sign hanging overhead was snapped in two, dangling by a single copper wire that creaked in the breeze. The heavy oak door lay splintered on the ground, and the bar's upper floor had collapsed inward into a heap of charred timber and shattered glass.

⃟“It's destroyed...” a Crow squad member muttered in despair.

⃟“Inside. Now,” Erika commanded, stepping over the threshold with her gun drawn.

Azure led the way into the dimly lit interior. Dust floated thick in the stagnant air. Broken bottles covered the floorboards, smelling faintly of spilled whiskey and aged liquor. Tables were overturned, chairs smashed to splinters.

Yet as Azure stepped into the center of the ruined tavern, her eyes froze upon the back wall behind the bar counter.

Erika, Zack, and the rest of the team stopped dead in their tracks, stunned into complete silence.

Hanging pristine against a wall covered in soot and cracks was a large framed painting.

While everything around it—the liquor shelves, the oak counter, the ceiling beams—was crushed, burned, and shattered, the painting did not bear a single scratch. Not a speck of dust clung to its canvas, nor was there any smoke damage on its gilded frame. It looked as if an invisible barrier had shielded it from the apocalyptic destruction that had leveled the rest of the district.

The artwork in the Renaissance and ancient style depicted a mortal man collapsing onto the ground, desperately gripping a long white spear barehanded in a futile attempt to arrest its thrust. The man's hands were severely torn and bleeding profusely. Descending from above was a majestic white entity—clad in white armor, with pale white skin, long white hair, and brilliant, shining silvery-white wings spreading outward. Its head and face were completely unknown, rendered as an entity of pure, radiant light.

⃟“What... what is that?” Derek breathed, staring at the canvas in awe. “How is this the only thing untouched?”

⃟“It's impossible...” Erika whispered, stepping closer to examine the frame. “The fire didn't even scorch the wood... what kind of painting is this?”

Azure said nothing. She simply stepped forward and stared longer at the painting, her unblinking eyes fixed quietly upon the winged entity and the white spear.

*CREAK.*

Suddenly, a loud, heavy mechanical clatter echoed from beneath their feet.

Everyone jolted in shock, instantly raising their firearms and pointing them down at the floorboards.

A square section of the wooden floor right in front of the bar counter began to rise. A hidden trapdoor swung upward with a heavy thump, revealing a dark underground staircase lined with iron rungs and dim yellow emergency lights.

From the dark opening, a gray-haired man wearing a grease-stained leather apron and a scarred eye patch poked his head out. He held a heavy wrench in one hand and smoked a half-burnt cigar in his mouth.

It was J.J.

⃟“Put those useless toys away before you shoot your own toes off,” J.J grunted, scanning the startled faces of Erika, Azure, and The Crows.

⃟“J.J!” Erika gasped, lowering her pistol in shock. “You're alive!”

J.J didn't smile. He looked up at the shattered roof of his bar, his single eye widening with sudden, intense urgency as he gestured wildly down into the dark cellar.

⃟“Get inside! Move your asses underground right now!” J.J barked in a harsh whisper. “They will be coming again.”

Without waiting for a second invitation, Erika signaled the squad. One by one, The Crows, Zack, Derek, and Victoria scrambled down the narrow iron ladder into the darkness below. Azure moved last, her silent steps descending gracefully before J.J slammed the heavy hatch shut.

*CLANG!*

The iron doors sealed tightly with a series of heavy mechanical deadbolts locking into place. The muffling effect was instantaneous—the whistle of the cold surface wind vanished, replaced by the deep, rhythmic hum of ventilation turbines buried within the earth.

J.J led the group down a long, reinforced concrete corridor lit by low-hanging yellow industrial bulbs. Heavy steel pipes and electrical conduits ran along the ceiling, leading toward a pair of massive, double-hinged blast doors.

As the blast doors hissed open, the group emerged into a sprawling, bunker-styled underground facility.

It was J.J's secret weapon factory.

Racks of heavy firearms, custom rifle chassis, and specialized ammunition crates lined the concrete walls. Industrial lathes, metal-casting furnaces, and automated assembly rigs hummed with activity, filling the cavernous space with the sharp scent of gun oil, ozone, and hot steel. Massive reinforced pillars supported the vaulted ceiling, engineered to withstand surface bombardment.

While the members of The Crows stared around the subterranean armory in astonishment, Azure walked forward with calm familiarity. She had long known about this secret underground factory and its heavily fortified, bunker-styled layout.

Azure stopped near an assembly workbench where half-built rifle barrels lay scattered. She turned her cool, unblinking gaze toward the veteran gunsmith.

⃟“You never mentioned that you knew about the creature we faced,” Azure said quietly, her voice cutting through the hum of the machinery.

J.J stopped in his tracks. He pulled the half-burnt cigar from his lips, exhaling a thick cloud of grey smoke toward the ceiling. His single eye narrowed as he looked back at Azure, his scarred face hardening.

⃟“Because I didn't believe it,” J.J grunted in a low, gravelly voice. He turned to face Azure and Erika, resting a calloused hand against the edge of the workbench. “A monster with a steel body and wings that can pulverize solid stone? Sounded like the drunken ramblings of a terrified scout.”

He exhaled another stream of smoke, his expression darkening.

⃟“I didn't believe a word of it... until I saw one of those bastards with my own eyes.”

Erika leaned in, her brow furrowing deeply. “A creature like that... what is it?”

⃟“Based on its appearance,” J.J said, tapping his cigar against a metal tray, “it matches the old tales of *Aethernia*—an ancient alien entity from outer space. I don't know the exact details. It's just a story passed down through generations in my family.”

He gestured with his thumb back toward the ceiling, where the undamaged painting hung upstairs.

⃟“That painting up in the bar... it depicts that very story. But I always thought it was nothing more than a myth. Just a legend from my ancestors.”

J.J turned and walked toward a heavy vault door at the back of the factory. He keyed in a combination on a mechanical dial, pulling open the thick iron door to reveal a deeper, climate-controlled inner chamber. Erika, Azure, and the squad followed him inside.

The inner room was lined with specialized weapon racks. Mounted along the stone walls were various handcrafted warbows, forged from heavy composite alloys and reinforced limbs. Resting in quivers beside them were rows of arrows tipped with translucent, faceted crystal heads.

J.J picked up one of the arrows, holding its gleaming tip under the dim overhead light.

⃟“The arrowheads are made from Twilight Crystal,” J.J said quietly.

Azure's eyes widened slightly in genuine surprise. She had known for some time that J.J had been secretly experimenting with raw Twilight Crystal in his armory, attempting to harness its extreme hardness for weaponry.

Seeing her expression, J.J let out a bitter chuckle, tossing the crystal-tipped arrow back into its rack with a dull clatter.

⃟“Don't get your hopes up, Azure,” J.J grunted, looking at her with his single eye. “They're useless against those things. I already tried shooting one of these arrows straight into its chest when the city fell. It couldn't even scratch the surface. It couldn't penetrate the creature at all.”

Azure tilted her head, her quiet eyes narrowing as she stared at the crystal arrowhead in the rack.

⃟“You're stupid, J.J,” Azure said, her tone flat and completely unsparing.

J.J blinked, thoroughly caught off guard. “...What did you call me?”

⃟“Twilight Crystal has a naturally blunt property,” Azure pointed out, crossing her arms. “Why would you shape a blunt crystal into an arrowhead and expect it to pierce armor?”

J.J froze mid-exhale. His single eye widened, and the half-burnt cigar nearly slipped from his teeth as realization hit him like a thunderbolt. He slapped a heavy, calloused hand against his forehead with a groan of frustration.

⃟“...Ah. Hell's fire,” J.J muttered, rubbing his face in utter embarrassment. “I forgot.”

Erika cleared her throat, breaking the awkward silence in the vault.

⃟“Regardless of your failed arrow experiment,” Erika said firmly, “our mission remains unchanged. J.J, you are coming with us back to The Crows' underground base. We need your armaments expertise, and staying in this ruined sector alone is suicide.”

J.J looked around his vault, taking a final long puff from his cigar before stubbing it out against the rack frame.

⃟“You don't need to tell me twice, Chief,” J.J grunted, slinging a heavy leather travel sack packed with essential tools and schematics over his shoulder. “If we're moving, we need to move now. The faster we travel through the underground drainage networks, the less likely we are to encounter those winged bastards. They stick to the open skies on the surface.”

He turned back toward the vault door, securing the dial before gesturing toward the exit staircase.

⃟“We head back upstairs, grab what we can carry, and drop into the northern sewer line immediately.”

The group moved quickly back through the blast doors, ascending the iron ladder back into the ruined tavern above. Standing amidst the shattered oak tables and charred rubble under the dark surface sky, Erika, Azure, J.J, and The Crows strike squad readied their weapons and gear, preparing for the swift journey back underground.

They stepped out of the splintered tavern entrance onto an expansive open field—a ruined, desolate plaza where majestic stone thoroughfares had collapsed into crushed rubble and cracked asphalt roads. The Crows' underground base was still miles away across dangerous terrain; they were far from safety.

Then, without warning, the sky went pitch black.

A colossal, suffocating shadow plunged the open plaza into darkness. The air temperature dropped violently, shattered by a deafening, rhythmic roar—the thunderous screech of hundreds of steel wings slicing through the atmosphere.

High above the shattered spires, the sky was literally choked with them—hundreds of Metallic-Bodied Monsters swarming in tight formation like a cloud of steel locusts.

Before anyone could make a run for cover, the swarm swooped down directly into the open field.

*BOOM! CRAAAASH!*

Metallic wing-blades struck the ground like falling meteors, smashing through concrete and crushing vehicle chassis. A quadrupedal steel beast crashed directly into the ranks of The Crows, its razor-edged limbs slashing through the air. Screams of terror and agony tore through the plaza as squad members were tossed across the asphalt, their frantic gunfire ricocheting harmlessly off the creatures' seamless armor.

Pandemonium erupted across the open field.

J.J didn't hesitate for a single second—the veteran gunsmith was already sprinting faster than anyone else, clutching his schematics bag and bolting frantically across the ruined plaza toward the distant drainage valve.

Meanwhile, Erika froze dead in her tracks.

Her hand gripped her pistol so tightly her knuckles turned white, but she couldn't pull the trigger. Overwhelmed by a turbulent storm of emotions—paralyzing fear, burning hatred for the abominations slaughtering her people, and sheer helplessness—she stood frozen as blood spattered the cracked asphalt. The Crows were not safe. They were trapped out in the open.

Amidst the screaming and brutal carnage, Azure stood completely still.

She stepped between Erika and a descending steel beast, her quiet voice cutting effortlessly through the chaos.

⃟“Move, Erika,” Azure commanded softly. “Get them to the drainage lines. Now.”

Erika snapped out of her trance with a ragged gasp. Swallowing her terror and hatred, she forced her trembling legs to move, barking desperate orders to Zack, Derek, and Victoria to drag the surviving squad members toward the distant escape hatch.

As the panicked group scrambled away across the open ground, Azure remained alone in the center of the battlefield.

Slowly, deliberately, Azure reached down to her leg suit. With a soft pneumatic hiss, she disengaged her leg armor's safety limiters, deactivating the heavy restraints around her shins and ankles to unleash her full mobility.

Her face remained completely unfazed, unhurried, and cold as ice. Not a single flicker of fear touched her quiet eyes.

Standing amidst the swirling ash and screaming wind, Azure began to meticulously analyze every detail of the attacking swarm.

Up close, their anatomy was deeply unsettling. They possessed no faces—where a head should be, there was only a seamless, featureless slab of polished plate metal, devoid of eyes, nose, or mouth. Even more striking, many of them were not humanoid at all. Several bore quadrupedal steel frames, multi-jointed metallic limbs, and razor-sharp wing blades that pulverized brick and mortar upon contact.

Azure didn't feel the slightest urge to run.

To her, fleeing was unnecessary. This open field was a perfect chance to observe the enemy firsthand, test her strength, and gather the critical intel her brother needed.

Azure reached down to the twin steel sheaths shimmering white and blue against the back of her legs.

*CLACK-CLICK.*

Her slender fingers engaged the dual mechanical release catches. Instantly, an intricate array of internal brass and steel gears began to rotate inside the sheath housings with a rhythmic, mechanical clatter. Miniature teeth clicked into alignment as internal containment valves unlocked in rapid succession.

*WHISHHHHHHH—!*

A dense plume of sub-zero, freezing gas seethed violently from the ventilation slots along the hilts, coiling around her wrists in thick clouds as the trapped Antarctic cold surged into the weapons' frames. Frost instantly spiderwebbed across the shattered pavement beneath her feet.

Azure drew her twin daggers in a single, fluid motion.

The transparent Twilight Crystals embedded in the centers of the dual blades ignited with an ethereal, radiant blue glow. The extreme sub-zero cold accumulated within the crystals transferred to the razor edges, turning the surrounding humid air into shimmering frost as the brilliant blue light illuminated the featureless steel faces of the descending swarm.

Holding the twin Frost Blades in a reverse dual-wield grip, Azure tilted her chin upward—her expression remaining cold, composed, and utterly deadly.
