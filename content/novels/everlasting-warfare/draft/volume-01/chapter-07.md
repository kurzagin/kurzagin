The world shattered in a deafening cascade of falling stone, iron rebar, and choking gray dust.

The giant metallic spear had torn through thirty feet of reinforced subterranean concrete as if it were parchment. Shockwaves hammered through the underground command hall, throwing tables upside down, shattering fluorescent light fixtures, and violently knocking Crows operatives off their feet. Emergency alarms wailed in broken, distorted shrieks before dying into silence.

As the dense cloud of pulverized rock began to settle, a massive, jagged crater gaped in the center of the ceiling.

Above the breach, framed against the night sky, stood a single figure.

It was the metallic-bodied creature with the Halo.

It stood motionless at the edge of the crater on the surface, looking straight down into the underground sanctuary. The pale white halo floating above its featureless head spun with slow, mechanical rhythm, casting an eerie light across its silver armor plates. Its broad, static wings remained fully extended, completely unfazed by the smoke billowing around it.

It was entirely alone. No swarms of quadrupedal beasts accompanied it. No wing-blade monsters circled the sky. Just one solitary, divine terror gazing down upon the nest of surviving humans.

Every soul in the room froze. Paralyzing, primal terror seized the room. Crows soldiers stared up into the featureless white faceplate, their breath catching in their throats, unable to lift their rifles or utter a sound.

Azure was the first to react.

Despite her severed arm sockets sparking with faint blue electrical arcs, her expression remained utterly cold and devoid of fear. The moment the ceiling shattered, Azure planted her legs and stepped into the open center of the command hall, putting herself between the gaping crater and the fleeing survivors.

⃟“Take my brother!” Azure commanded, her voice cutting through the panic like ice, her silver leg armor shimmering as she stepped forward. ⃟“I will hold it!”

Erika snapped out of her shock as Azure's voice echoed. Her eyes darted from the gaping ceiling hole to Zeeran in his wheelchair. For a split second, Erika considered throwing Zeeran over her shoulder, but her smaller frame couldn't possibly bear his physical weight while fleeing uphill. Grabbing the rubber handles of Zeeran's wheelchair, Erika planted her boots and began frantically wheeling him toward the dark, narrow subterranean bypass corridor.

⃟“Secondary exit! The northern emergency ventilation shaft!” Zeeran shouted toward the panic-stricken room, his hands gripping the armrests tightly. ⃟“Do not attempt to fight it! Fall back now!”

⃟“Evacuate! Everyone to the secondary tunnels!” Erika screamed at the top of her lungs as she shoved Zeeran's heavy chair into the side passage. ⃟“Move your legs, damn it! Move!”

J.J didn't need to be told twice. The veteran gunsmith had already dropped his heavy gear and was sprinting toward the ventilation shaft alongside Zack, Derek, and Victoria of The Ravens.

Above them, the haloed anomaly moved.

It didn't drop with a crash. It simply drifted downward through the breach, levitating into the underground hall with absolute, silent grace. The moment its metallic feet touched the concrete floor, the air pressure inside the sanctuary plummeted.

As the creature stepped forward to exterminate the trapped survivors, Azure lunged into action. With her suit compressing her mass to normal weight, her 400kg-capable muscles delivered explosive speed. She delivered devastating roundhouse kicks and sweeping heel strikes aimed at the creature's head and chest. Her strikes barely made the anomaly flinch, but the raw kinetic force disrupted its movement, drawing its cold, featureless faceplate entirely onto her.

Screams echoed off the underground walls as the remaining Crows scrambled frantically into the exit behind Erika and Zeeran. Behind Azure, Crows who failed to move fast enough were executed in swift, brutal strikes.

⃟“Retreat complete! Azure, fall back!” Erika yelled back into the hall, struggling with all her strength to keep pushing Zeeran's heavy wheelchair up the steep incline of the emergency escape shaft.

Hearing the call and confirming Zeeran was safely inside the shaft, Azure disengaged instantly. Pivoting on one foot, she launched into a high-speed sprint, her dampened legs carrying her with terrifying velocity up the concrete tunnel after them.

Erika kept wheeling Zeeran with every ounce of muscle in her body, her lungs burning as she shoved the heavy chair up the winding incline. Just behind them, Azure caught up, her vigilant eyes scanning the darkness behind them as the agonizing screams in the main hall faded away.

***

Five minutes later, a heavy iron hatch on the surface burst open.

Zeeran, Azure, Erika, J.J, The Ravens, and roughly half of the surviving Crows spilled out into the cold night air, gasping frantically for breath. They collapsed onto the jagged rubble of the ruined surface city, their bodies trembling from exhaustion and fear. Out of the hundreds of operatives who had once populated the sanctuary, only a meager fraction remained alive.

Night had fully fallen. It was midnight.

The surface world was bathed in absolute, suffocating darkness. The ruined skyscrapers stood like silent black tombstones around them, the ash-laden wind howling through shattered glass frames.

J.J slumped against a collapsed concrete pillar, clutching his bleeding ribs. ⃟“We... we made it out...” he gasped, his voice shaking violently. ⃟“Is that monster following us?”

⃟“It's staying in the lower base to finish off the stragglers,” Victoria whispered, her face pale as snow as she leaned on her sniper rifle. ⃟“It won't take long...”

Erika collapsed over the handles of Zeeran's wheelchair, her breath coming in ragged hitches as tear tracks cut through the soot on her cheeks. Her legs trembled from the strain of wheeling Zeeran up the long subterranean shaft. She looked back at the small group of surviving Crows. Half their brotherhood had been wiped out in less than an hour.

Zeeran sat in his wheelchair, his gaze fixed blankly on the shattered asphalt beneath him. His mind was racing at dangerous speeds, and he began rapidly mumbling under his breath, completely lost in his own head as his knuckles turned white against his armrests.

⃟“I miscalculated the tracking speed... I assumed surface military nodes were the priority...” Zeeran mumbled frantic streams of tactical calculations, his eyes wide and unblinking. ⃟“I can re-adjust. If I pull the remaining Twilight assets from the sub-Antarctic vaults... if I contact Liezt and re-arm Azure with hyper-dense alloy sleeves... we force a bottleneck battle at the mountain passes...”

He was feverishly muttering contingency plans aloud, recalculating political leverage, resource allocations, and tactical sacrifices in a desperate effort to reclaim control of the war.

Then, Azure spoke.

She wasn't looking at Zeeran, nor was she looking back at the subterranean hatch. She was standing at the edge of the ruined plaza, her head tilted directly upward toward the sky.

⃟“Brother,” Azure said softly. Her tone was devoid of its usual calm precision. For the very first time in her life, there was a tiny, unmistakable tremble in her voice. ⃟“Look up.”

Zeeran blinked. He slowly lifted his head, turning his eyes away from his lap and toward the night horizon.

During the daytime, sunlight, thick grey clouds, and atmospheric glare had rendered it invisible—it blended seamlessly into the bright sky. But now, in the pitch-black darkness of midnight, it was impossible to miss. 

Because it was emanating a radiant, otherworldly glow.

Zeeran's breath hitched. His eyes widened until his pupils shrunk to pinpricks.

Rising from the center of the continent—stretching from the shattered horizon into the infinite dark of the cosmos—was a structure of unfathomable magnitude.

It was a tree.

A massive, colossal, primordial World Tree.

Its trunk was wide enough to swallow entire mountain ranges, composed of ancient, silver-shimmering bark that pulsated with slow, rhythmic veins of luminous white light. It reached up, up, up—piercing straight through the Earth's atmosphere, breaching the stratosphere, and extending into the endless void of space itself.

Branching out across the midnight sky were titan limbs, broad enough to cast shadows across oceans. Suspended from those branches were massive leaves—each leaf the size of a city—forming a brilliant, silver-glowing canopy that illuminated the dark sky and upper clouds like a radiant aurora. They reached into the skies, into the stars... into heaven itself.

The tree was not an anomaly. It was not a weapon. It was a domain. Emanating its divine light into the darkness, the world-spanning canopy stood as an omnipotent testament to a reality far beyond human comprehension.

Zeeran sat completely frozen in his wheelchair.

The wind howled softly over the ruins, carrying the scent of ozone and ancient ash, but Zeeran heard none of it.

His mind went dead silent.

All the grand formulas he had written, all the political web-spinning he had engineered, all the lives he had manipulated, and all the long-term gambits he had meticulously built over years—shattered in an instant like fragile glass under a hammer.

He had spent his entire life believing he was the ultimate grandmaster. He had studied every strategy, calculated every casualty, and mastered every subtlety of power. He had carefully positioned his pawns—Azure, Erika, The Crows, the royal court, the Twilight Contract—preparing to maneuver them across the board to conquer his enemies and rule the realm.

But as he stared up at the titan tree reaching into heaven, a cold, crushing realization pierced straight into his soul:

Everything he was planning was completely useless.

He was planning the pawn to rule the chessboard.

But the board itself was changed.

***

Before the suffocating weight of that realization could fully settle into Zeeran's chest, the ground beneath the ruined plaza shook with violent tremor.

*BOOM—!*

The secondary emergency hatch shattered into flying metal shrapnel. Emerging from the dust cloud, the haloed anomaly rose into the midnight sky, hovering silently above the wreckage. But it was no longer alone. Behind it, dozens of metallic-bodied beasts—quadrupedal hounds of steel and flying wing-blade monsters, none bearing halos—poured out of the breach like a ravenous swarm, locking their featureless heads onto the exhausted survivors.

⃟“They're on us!” J.J yelled, raising his shotgun with trembling hands. ⃟“Form a defensive line!”

Without a second of hesitation, Azure lunged into motion.

Despite her armless shoulders sparking with cold blue electrical arcs, Azure drove her legs into the concrete. Her silver leg armor gleamed as she launched herself directly into the vanguard of the horde, her dampened weight translating raw muscle into explosive velocity. 

*CRUNCH!*

A fluid roundhouse kick smashed into the jaw of an leaping steel beast, snapping its metallic neck and hurling it into two trailing monsters. Azure spun in mid-air, unleashing a rapid succession of devastating axe-kicks and heel-sweeps. Her legs moved as a lethal blur, repelling the swarming beasts away from Zeeran's position.

But the sheer numbers were overwhelming. 

A massive, quadrupedal beast flanked her defense, diving straight toward Zeeran. Its heavy steel body smashed directly into the side of Zeeran's wheelchair.

*CRASH-CRUNCH!*

The reinforced wheelchair was utterly obliterated, its titanium frame buckled and its wheels snapped into twisted scrap. Zeeran was thrown violently onto the hard asphalt, tumbling across shattered glass and jagged rubble.

Pain flared through his spine as his head bounced off the cracked pavement. Warm blood trickled down his temple into his eye, blurring his vision with dark red hues. Exhaustion, blood loss, and physical trauma rushed over him all at once. His eyelids grew heavy; his consciousness was rapidly slipping into a dark abyss.

Through the hazy fog of his fading sight, Zeeran forced his eyes to stay open.

He saw Azure being struck squarely by the heavy tail-blade of a flying anomaly. The crushing blow sent her armless body hurtling forty feet through the air, slamming violently into a ruined brick wall.

*THUD!*

Bricks tumbled over her limp frame. She hit the pavement hard and did not get back up. The faint shimmer of her silver leg armor flickered and died, leaving her motionless in the rubble, completely fainted.

*Azure...* Zeeran thought, his heart cold with desperate helplessness.

On the brink of blacking out, his blurred vision caught movement in the dark mist beyond the plaza. 

Distant silhouettes flickered along the crumbling rooftops before leaping down into the ruined plaza. A group had arrived.

Zeeran's fading eyes strained to make out their forms through the haze. They wore exquisite layered armor over elegant folded silk garments—dark lacquered shoulder plates bound with crimson silk cords, wide-sleeved outer robes wrapped tightly at the waist by broad woven sashes, and fitted leg greaves. Every single warrior carried a slender, single-edged curved blade sheathed in a lacquered wooden scabbard at their left hip. 

Though they moved with lethal grace, they looked remarkably young, their faces unblemished and calm. Most striking of all were their physical forms: while entirely humanoid, the members of the group bore rich beastkin traits. Sharp wolf ears twitched atop their hair, bushy fox tails and sleek feline tails swayed behind their coats, and their primal eyes glowed softly in the dark as they unsheathed their blades in perfect unison to intercept the swarming steel beasts.

At the center of the silhouettes stood a striking figure—the squad's leader, a dragon woman. Twin curved dragon horns protruded elegantly from her forehead, catching the faint celestial glow of the World Tree above, and shimmering reptilian scales accented her temples, jawline, and wrists. 

Step by step, the dragon woman stepped past the clashing blades and began walking closer to where Zeeran lay trapped in the rubble. 

Zeeran watched her silhouette approach, his heavy eyelids fluttering as her golden, slitted eyes met his fading gaze. But before she could fully reach him, before he could grasp who she was or comprehend what was happening, the crushing darkness pulled him under completely—and Zeeran fainted into absolute silence.
