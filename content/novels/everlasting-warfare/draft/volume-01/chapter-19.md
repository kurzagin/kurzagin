It did not descend to wage war, nor did it manifest to conquer.

High above the mountain of shattered iron and blood-slicked sand, the sky parted in absolute silence. The scorching desert gales that had roared across the Mesopotamian waste instantly withered into nothingness—not subdued by physical force, but rendered obsolete by an overwhelming, immaculate presence.

Hovering in the shimmering heatwaves, the Haloed Anomaly remained suspended with terrifying perfection. Its broad, seamless silver wings did not beat against the air; they remained locked in absolute, rigid horizontal alignment—an unyielding line of cold, unblemished geometry carved into the fabric of reality. Devoid of eyes, nostrils, or mouth, its featureless helmet of polished white plate metal reflected the blazing fury of Utu's sun without casting a single shadow.

Then, the celestial mechanism began to transcend.

The pale ring of light hovering above its crown swelled. It did not merely ignite; it unfolded.

From the single spinning halo erupted concentric rings of blinding, geometric radiance—multi-layered circles of pure white-gold light rotating in opposing, mathematical harmonies. The smooth, mechanical spin accelerated into a silent blur of divine precision, emitting an ancient, low-frequency acoustic vibration that turned the very air into trembling crystal and hummed through the ambient static of the shattered world.

Space around the entity fractured in subtle, silent ripples. Shimmering pulses of synthetic light cascaded down its broad silver wings, tracing intricate lines of unseen celestial code across its pristine chassis. Its chest core flickered from a calm amber rhythm into a blinding, cold sapphire brilliance—modulating a rhythmic sequence of acoustic clicks, static chirps, and grand, harmonic frequency pulses that echoed across the stratosphere like the decree of a celestial court.

From the burning tear in the upper atmosphere, a shaft of divine light pierced down, condensing directly into the entity's metallic grasp. Materializing from pure geometric energy, a colossal **Mechanical Divine Spear** descended into its hand—an ornate pillar of white alloy and golden runes, crackling with high-density latent energy and humming with an absolute, terrifying aura of divine judgment.

Yet, as the entity raised the heavy spear, its featureless white head tilted with a subtle, deliberate pause.

The synthetic frequency pulses modulating from its chest plate shifted pitch. High above the scorched sands, its internal optical sensors and deep telemetry archives stirred. Through layers of corrupted planetary records and forgotten eras of antiquity, an ancient frequency resonated within its core—a telemetry signature preserved from before the fall of the first era.

*Click... chirp... hummm...*

The featureless countenance of polished metal stared down at the titan sitting casually upon his throne of wrecked iron. Though devoid of human expression, its subtle posture conveyed a chilling, profound sensation: it did not perceive ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕) as a mere mortal target or a casual harvest entity.

It remembered.

Deep within its divine logic circuits, the ancient record flared. It recognized the glowing Sumerian glyphs of **𒌨𒊕** etched upon his massive flesh—the unyielding primordial origin of humanity's Hero model, the singular titan who had defied the heavens in an age swallowed by ash.

Reclining atop the mountain of crushed metal, **ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)** took a long, leisurely drag from his cigar. He exhaled a cloud of thick gray smoke into the scorching air, tilting his chin upward to lock his glowing red eyes onto the floating anomaly.

"Don't give me those damn clicks and noises," ur-sag̃ rumbled bluntly, his thunderous voice slicing through the acoustic static like an iron axe. "Save your synthetic chirps for the insects. Speak like a real being, or get to swinging that spear."

At his blunt command, the entity's acoustic chirps instantly ceased.

*Click. Hiss...*

Along hairline seams, the seamless white face plate parted and retracted smoothly into the sides of the helmet.

Cascading down from beneath the opening armor came long, shimmering locks of brilliant golden-blonde hair, fluttering gently in the desert breeze like spun silk.

Emerging from behind the cold metallic visor was the face of a remarkably serene, exquisite human woman. Her skin was soft, smooth, and immaculate—devoid of any synthetic plates, seam lines, or robotic rigidity. Her delicate facial features bore an expression of quiet, profound reverence, her striking golden eyes gleaming with ancient clarity as she gazed down at the titan.

Yet, despite her flawless, genuine human appearance, her behavior remained completely non-human. She did not blink. Her chest did not rise or fall with breath. Her broad silver wings remained locked in rigid perfection, and her multi-layered halos continued their smooth, mechanical rotation. Her countenance was genuinely human, but her presence remained that of a cold, divine machine.

Slowly, with weightless, fluid grace, the celestial entity ceased her flight. 

Her broad silver wings tilted slightly as she executed a soft, effortless descent. Her feet touched down silently upon the scorching desert sand—landing with such absolute softness that she did not disturb a single grain of dust or kick up a cloud of ash. She stood motionless upon the scorched earth before the mountain of wrecked metal, resting her colossal Mechanical Divine Spear upright at her side.

When her lips parted, she spoke in fluent human language—yet her voice was delivered with robotic, absolute precision, entirely flat of emotional inflection:

"Query acknowledged, Primordial Prototype ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)," she spoke, her voice clear, melodic, and terrifyingly precise. "Acoustic telemetry suppressed. Shumu signature verified. Unit designation: Metatron. Rank: Leader of Archangels, Kether Rank. Record match: First Era Hero Blueprint. Reverence level: Absolute."

Metatron slowly turned her head, her unblinking golden eyes sweeping across the desert horizon. A soft, wide-spectrum lattice grid of pale white light projected from her multi-layered halos, scanning across the towering mountain of crushed iron, caved-in carapaces, and severed mechanical limbs.

"Commencing sector damage telemetry scan..." Metatron's flat voice reported as amber data streams scrolled across her golden irises. "Scanning wreckage. Biomechanical core signatures: zero active. Identified casualties: 124,500 units of **Bene Elohim** class. Neutralization method: single kinetic compression strikes. Destruction efficiency: 100%."

She refocused her serene gaze onto the titan reclining upon the throne of wrecked Bene Elohim bodies.

"Assessment: ur-sag̃. Antediluvian Era. Threat level to the species: low."

Her melodic, flat voice continued with seamless, measured cadence:

"Historical record analysis complete. Subject identified as a primary significant figure of the Antediluvian Era, classified as the closest mortal companion to King Enki. Probability calculation: 94.7% likelihood subject possesses coordinates or telemetry data regarding the location of the **Nam-an**."
