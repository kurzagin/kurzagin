My name is Alden—Sir Alden, a Pilgrim of the Systemist temple and secret advisor to Princess Anna. As I entered Princess Anna's room, she appeared overjoyed because soon her grandmother would be killed by the terrorists. Seeing her cheerful face made me happy as well.

Lady Anna is the candidate for the next Entu, and the death of her grandmother will officially elevate her to become the Entu.

As I knelt before her—paying my respects, before long the sound of gunshots echoed from outside the room; it seemed Queen Elizabeth had been killed by the terrorists. Lady Anna fell silent… then, the cheerful Lady Anna suddenly closed her eyes. Upon opening her eyes, she looked at me with a deep, emotionless gaze, and with a cold, expressionless face, she declared,

“I am the eighth-generation Entu.”

And suddenly… all light sources disappeared from the earth.

■ ■ ■

A massive explosion outside the palace threw everyone attending the Queen's birthday party into panic, running back and forth to save themselves.

The Queen was escorted by several royal soldiers, and beside her was her most loyal attendant, Sir Alden.

"Your Majesty… you must head to the emergency evacuation route immediately."

Alden gently held the Queen's shoulders; the warmth of his touch calmed the Queen, who had initially been panicked.

"Thank you, Alden. What about yourself?"

"I must check on Princess Anna as well, Your Majesty."

"She must surely be in a panic right now too."

"Very well, Alden. Please bring my granddaughter to safety."

The Queen and Alden then took their leave of each other; the Queen's guards immediately escorted her toward the evacuation route, leading her down the stairs and along the corridor. However, the Queen realized something was wrong; the path they were taking was not toward the emergency evacuation route, but rather toward the back of the palace.

Meanwhile, Sir Alden, who headed to Princess Anna's room, opened the door without knocking, whereupon Princess Anna looked at him with a cheerful gaze as if she knew Alden would come.

“How did it go, Alden?”

Princess Anna nī Euphratesia, a 17-year-old girl, was the rightful heir to the throne of the Euphrates Kingdom. But she had absolutely no interest in becoming the next queen. What she desired was to fulfill the condition of becoming an Entu—which required her to be a ruler. Therefore, she had orchestrated her grandmother's death today to seize the kingdom's throne as quickly as possible.

Entu was an ancient title bestowed upon a spiritual leader; legend has it that the bearer of this title possesses unmatched abilities and power.

When Alden entered her room, Anna was wearing a black dress, her long blonde hair cascading down her back, signifying the noble blood flowing in her veins. Anna loved reading books and carried one with her wherever she went.

“Everything is proceeding smoothly, Princess. Soon your grandmother will be killed by the terrorists disguised as royal troops.”

“Just as planned.”


Anna closed her book, ending her reading session, and looked at Alden with a concerned expression,


“Good.”

“By the way, isn't that body of yours far too old? Where is your real body?”

 “Oh, thank you for your concern, Princess. My original body is near the Euphrates River, currently in a state of meditation. I can transfer bodies immediately whenever Princess commands me.”

 “Hmm, being a Pilgrim like you sounds quite amazing.”


Alden merely smiled while shaking his head. Not long after, several men and women entered clad in priest vestments and nun habits; they all immediately knelt facing Princess Anna without uttering a word.


The clergy were followers of the Entu since ancient times, even long before the first humans were created. They were the people who established a religion named Systemism with the Entu as their primary leader. However, according to historical records, the position of supreme leader had been vacant, and Anna would become the eighth-generation Entu.


“Hmm, everyone has gathered. Just a few moments longer.”


After conversing for a short while, the sound of gunshots echoed from outside. All of them immediately clasped their hands to offer prayers to Anna.


Then Anna closed her eyes, her head and entire body trembling as if something were occurring deep within her. Anna, who initially bore a cheerful and emotional expression, suddenly opened her eyes with a cold stare. She looked at the kneeling people and uttered a single sentence that left everyone stunned.


“I am the eighth-generation Entu, Anna Wisteria.”


A purple flower appeared before her; it was a Wisteria flower. 

According to ancient records preserved in the temple of Systemism, once an Entu has been officially chosen, they receive a new name. In Anna's case, she received the name [Wisteria].


Filled with awe and reverence for Entu Anna, they all prostrated themselves before her without questioning a thing.


Outside the palace, thunder rumbled loudly as rain suddenly poured down over the Euphrates Kingdom, as though nature itself acknowledged the emergence of the new Entu. Bolts of lightning struck down from the northeast, swiftly crashing down all the way into the kingdom.


The lightning did not seem to be ordinary; it carried something—something exceedingly dangerous.


Sensing the approaching danger, Entu Anna immediately made a hand gesture in the air, a movement as if opening a book. Suddenly, the air surrounding her hands glowed, and hundreds—thousands—of grimoire books materialized in midair.

The striking lightning had drawn extremely close to the palace room; it seemed as though the lightning was indeed targeting Anna's location. Just as the lightning came dangerously near—on the verge of bursting into the room—Anna opened all the grimoire books floating in the air and declared,


"Preparations complete, Tu obtained… initiating Maintenance."


Instantly, the world plunged into darkness. All light—from lamp light and candle light to sunlight—vanished, a strange phenomenon wherein the world was shrouded in complete darkness.


According to the ancient records of the Systemism temple, Tu is the divine authority acquired by an Entu. A power capable of issuing any command to anything absolutely—nothing can defy His or Her will.


***


After several seconds, light returned to engulf the earth. Azure, who was still inside the laboratory, stepped out and saw that the far end of the underground corridor leading outside was blocked by a massive boulder that hadn't been there before.


She ultimately returned inside the room. With Zeeran in critical condition needing urgent medical care, Azure tried to remain calm and conducted simulations inside her mind. Azure ran various mental simulations on ways to escape this situation.


Azure possessed the ability to construct simulations within her mind, capable of thinking hundreds or even thousands of times faster than an ordinary person. In just a few seconds, she had already devised ways to escape the underground room whose exit was blocked by the giant boulder.


‘Destroy it with explosives? No, the ground might collapse and I could get caught in it too,’

‘Chemical reaction? Too risky, nearby objects might melt,’

‘Destroy it with a blade? Takes too much time,’

‘Ultrasonic vibrations? Dangerous to the ears,’

‘Twilight Transmogrifier? Useless… dull,’

‘Frostblade? Too dangerous, extreme local temperature changes could destroy the whole place,’

…

…

…

…


Azure continued simulating all available possibilities; the longer she thought, the more anxious her expression grew, and she bit her own finger. Time was short; Zeeran needed immediate care with proper medical equipment.


"Nichtaktiv!”


Suddenly Azure shouted firmly and stomped her right foot onto the ground. The steel armor on her leg instantly vanished, leaving both of her feet bare.


The phrase uttered by Azure was one of the voice commands for Azure’s Leg Suit, an item from the [Twilight Device Collection] that deactivated her steel armor.


Azure fell silent for a moment, waiting… waiting for a vibration beneath her feet. After several minutes of waiting, a faint sound could be heard, a thud that gradually echoed—growing louder. The noise originated directly beneath the ground she stood upon, as if about to shatter the earth.


She positioned her right leg back, preparing to kick the giant boulder blocking her path. Once she felt the timing was right, she kicked the boulder with all her strength, shattering it into pieces.


Azure’s Leg Suit was designed not to protect her leg, but to protect the ground from being destroyed, because Azure's legs were incredibly heavy and hard. In the past, her legs had demolished a multi-story building simply by kicking its foundation pillars.


Morning sunlight poured into the underground room.


“Wait…… morning?”


But what Azure saw first after breaking through the blocking boulder was not the grand royal palace, but ruins.


“What…… what is this?”


A horrific sight greeted her: every building was overgrown with vine-like moss and large tree roots everywhere. Every structure within her line of sight lay ruined, reclaimed by wild flora.


Scattered around the area, human corpses lay along the streets, long since dead. The scene appeared to have transpired a very long time ago.


Azure looked up at the sky and froze in shock, for there was a strange text inscribed above. Azure did not know what language it was, but she understood its meaning.


"Unrecognized…… lettering……"


Azure was a genius who knew every language in the world, even in their ancient forms. Yet the text floating in the sky was a script she had never encountered.


"Three days…… after Maintenance?"

"What…… ……does it mean?"


Azure merely shook her head and dismissed it, returning to push Zeeran's wheelchair out of the underground area. Before leaving, Azure grabbed several items from the shelf labeled Twilight Device Collection. She also retrieved several bottles of Resiliexcuratio medication for later use.


Beneath Zeeran's chair lay various fallen items such as a pen, an ID card, and a pin. Azure picked them all up and placed them into her pocket.


Having prepared everything, Azure wheeled Zeeran out, pushing him slowly.


"Oh…… almost forgot……"

Azure gently stomped her right foot onto the ground once more, then uttered another voice command, a different one this time.


"Stahlstiefel!"


Her bare foot was immediately encased in steel boots. Azure began walking through the ruined city, driven by a single objective… to find medical supplies.


The condition of the royal palace defied all logic. Just a few minutes ago Azure had run past it to enter their underground laboratory, yet upon stepping outside, it had suddenly turned to ruins and rubble.


“Once again…… an unpleasant situation……”


Azure recalled the incident from seven years ago, an event carved deep into her memory concerning one of her younger sisters who had passed away—her sister Zell Twilight, who had died in the White House fire at the end of the Euphrates-Tigris Hundred-Year War.


Then there was her youngest sister, Zephyr Twilight, who had gone missing sixteen years ago and whose whereabouts remained unknown to this day.


Not to forget her sister Zyfel Twilight, who chose to wander in search of alchemy traces whose existence was uncertain. Her sister Chizu Twilight had followed Zyfel to keep her company.


Her entire family had vanished—separated one by one—leaving only Zeeran and Liezt. But this time, the thought of potentially being left behind by her family once more filled her with intense anxiety, for Zeeran was currently in critical condition, and Liezt was nowhere to be found.


Thinking of this caused her body to tremble, so much so that she lost track of how far she had walked.


—Suddenly Azure sensed someone's presence, a murderous intent aimed directly at her neck. Azure instantly grabbed the mystery person's hand reaching from behind and slammed them to the ground in front of her.


"Ouch! That hurts!"


"...... Erika? What… are you doing here?"


When she saw the person's face, Azure recognized a familiar figure—the leader of the world's largest criminal organization and an old acquaintance of hers. The killing intent Azure felt earlier was only natural; as the leader of a criminal organization, Erika had to always be prepared for life-or-death battles.


Erika, 25 years old, was a grey-haired girl who appeared cheerful to those close to her, but to her enemies, she was an exceedingly dangerous and terrifying individual. She wore a white outfit covered by a black cloak, along with a black bird-beak mask. A pair of pistols rested in holsters at her waist.


“Senior Azure! I'm surprised to see you here too!”

“I'm actually investigating the strange phenomenon that occurred in this city. Everything changed so fast, and I'm trying to figure out the cause. What about you, Senior?”


"I'm looking for medical supplies…... Brother is injured."


Erika looked at the unconscious Zeeran in his wheelchair with a worried expression. She understood how serious Azure's brother's condition was.


"Come on, follow me to The Crows' headquarters. We have top-tier medical facilities and skilled staff there," Erika urged firmly. She stepped up to Zeeran's wheelchair and gently pushed it, motioning for Azure to head out right away.


Azure followed in Erika's footsteps, traveling toward The Crows' headquarters. The two of them walked through ruined, chaotic scenery, picking their way past crumbled buildings and sprawling wild plants.


"Prior to the palace attack, we had built a secret underground base nearby. Fortunately, that location wasn't affected by this terrible chaos,"


Erika led the way through narrow alleys. After walking for a while, they finally arrived in front of an entrance leading to an underground tunnel. As they descended, Azure's eyes locked onto a pin shaped like a single bird wing pinned to Erika's chest pocket.


"What…… is that?"


“This is our identification mark, The Crows. We use the bird wing symbol as our emblem; every member of the organization wears this pin to show our identity and serve as a reminder that we stand together.”


"But…… why…… only one wing?"


"As a criminal organization striving against corrupt high-ranking officials, the single bird wing symbolizes our restricted freedom in fighting the corrupt government. We defy existing rules and are often targeted by authorities. We fight to liberate society from injustice."


"However, once our goals are fulfilled and a fair system is established, we will replace it with a pair of wings as a symbol of true freedom. That emblem will reflect our achievement in bringing positive change and delivering justice to the people. Well, something like that, Senior Azure."


“Hmm…… you're…… lying. The true goal…… isn't like that.”

“Isn't it…… to kill Anna?”


Erika merely smiled upon hearing Azure's blunt words. She hid her true intentions, though she couldn't deceive her. She quickly changed the subject,


“It's been a while since Senior last visited us. Isn't this a good time to conduct an internal inspection of the organization?”


“I'm…… too lazy.”


“Ahahaha, don't be like that, Senior! The Eagles surely miss you!”


The Eagles Erika referred to were the leaders of The Crows, known as [The Eagle]. They were the leaders who managed and observed everything taking place within The Crows.


The Crows was a massive organization, which was why they utilized a robust membership system—a highly structured and well-planned organization.


The organization was divided into several divisions, one of which was The Eagle—the top members who led each sub-divided unit. Erika was one of The Eagles, holding the highest position, commanding everything.


After passing through the underground drainage tunnel, they finally arrived before a small door. Erika opened it, revealing a vast, expansive room inside.


Countless people were bustling about their own business, all dressed in all-black attire; some wore bird masks, while others did not.


A long banner hung draped from top to bottom—a red flag with gold accents and a black crow symbol emblazoned in the center.


Lighting in this underground space was provided by oil lamps mounted in every corner of the room.

It was truly baffling; The Crows' headquarters here seemed completely unaffected by anything outside. 


"Could it be…… the underground…… wasn't affected by the disaster's impact?"


Azure recalled that her laboratory was also unharmed. It appeared the disaster didn't reach underground, only affecting the surface.


Erika left Azure to speak with one of her associates; she appeared to be talking to an elderly doctor. His hair was grey and his face wrinkled, evidently an experienced individual.


Upon observing the doctor further, Azure suddenly remembered someone who closely resembled him…


"Doctor…… Griffin?"

"Impossible……"


Doctor Griffin was the person who had cared for the Twilight family since childhood, acting as a father figure to them, who had died on the battlefield two years ago.


Azure brushed it aside, pushing Zeeran toward Erika and the man.


"Here's the patient. Please take care of him; his safety is priority number one. If there are other patients with only minor injuries, set them aside for now."


"Understood……"


The old man immediately hurried to push Zeeran's wheelchair, bringing him to the medical room.


"Senior Azure! Now it's time to see the organization's progress firsthand. The Eagles are holding a meeting. Let me take you there."


Erika pulled Azure's hand, leading them toward another small door.


 “Uggh…… why are these people always so hyper?”


 “Come on!”


As Erika opened the door, many people could be seen inside. Several individuals were shouting at each other around a round table. The room was dark, illuminated only by oil lamps.


Azure experienced a sense of déjà vu; she had been through this before, dragged around by an overly energetic person.


A tall, black-haired man with a muscular frame and bulging arms was standing while pointing at the person in front of him. His raised voice clearly showed they were arguing,


"Impossible! The third Black Pigeon division hasn't returned since three days ago!? The Raven division disappeared too!?"

"Why didn't you inform us about this yesterday!?"


The person pointed at by the large man responded in a raised tone as well, seated on one of the chairs at the round table.


"The city is in total chaos! We can't contact anyone, and communication devices and transport are unusable. Their carrier birds haven't sent any news either……"


"We've lost contact with several divisions scattered across the continent! If we lose any more, our remaining forces will be tiny!"


"There are only five hundred members in this base, and not all of them can fight!"

"The civilian refugees also number around two hundred! If this continues……"


Amidst the intensely heated meeting, Azure paid them no mind. She was merely searching for something around their chests—a pin. She was looking for someone who lacked a pin on their chest.


"Found you……"


Azure walked toward that person, disregarding the ongoing meeting. The participants turned their heads toward Azure, as if only just noticing her arrival.


"C……Chief!"


Everyone at the round table immediately stood up and bowed deeply in respect. The title "Chief" they used was directed not at Erika, but at Azure.


Why did they show respect to Azure and call him Chief?


Three years ago when it was first formed, Azure directly led The Crows herself. Once it grew a bit, Azure left them, and Erika naturally took over as chief.


Azure walked over casually; suddenly, a pistol was already in her hand,

 “Huh? Senior Azure?”

Erika reached for the holster at her waist; the pistol that should have been there had vanished—Azure had taken it.


Azure approached her target and pointed the gun straight at their head—pressing the muzzle directly against their forehead.


Everyone present froze in shock as the situation shifted abruptly,

“What is the meaning of this, Senior?”


Azure remained silent. She reached into her lab coat pocket to retrieve an item, pulling out a pin shaped like a single bird wing.


“This…… belongs to you, right?”


Erika saw the pin and gasped in surprise, quickly putting the pieces together. After a moment, she deduced one thing—the person Azure was holding at gunpoint was the one who had wounded Zeeran, whether Zeeran had snatched the pin during the encounter or it had been dropped at the scene for Azure to find.


*clack*


Azure ensured the gun was loaded and ready to fire; a mere twitch of her finger would blow a hole through the man's head.


“According to the contract…… article three…… clause, two…”

“This contract is deemed null and void……”

“—Which means I reserve the right to eliminate or neutralize all members of The Crows upon termination of the contract,”

“—Which means I have the right to kill everyone here.”


Moments before Azure pulled the trigger, she suddenly recalled Zeeran's words before they parted.


‘Don't overdo it.’


Azure promptly tossed the pistol aside. She realized her brother's warning was meant for this exact moment—whether Zeeran had planned something or had other schemes in motion, Azure immediately acted with restraint to avoid interfering with her brother's plans.


"Nichtaktiv!”

Azure stomped her foot on the ground, deactivating her steel boots. The situation spiraled out of control. The people present erupted in anger, aiming their weapons at Azure in unison.


“You guys…… want to fight me? I'm pretty strong, you know…… if you don't want to get hurt, don't resist……”


Erika gritted her teeth in deep anxiety and concern. Erika and the Eagles had personally witnessed Azure's terrifying combat prowess. What's more, Azure's former title was Vanguard Commander of the Tigris Empire, earning the moniker [The Bloody Snow Princess] from her enemies.


The Eagles sat silently at the round table, not daring to utter a word for fear of offending Azure and provoking further rage.


What was truly terrifying about Azure wasn't the title, but her speed. During wartime, she had slaughtered an entire Euphrates Kingdom base camp single-handedly with nothing but a pair of daggers—against rifle-bearing troops.


Sweat drenched Erika's face as she racked her brain to prevent the situation from deteriorating further,


“All of you… lower your weapons.”


“But Moe! He attacked us first!”


The members pointing guns at Azure were new recruits who had joined only a month prior; they didn't comprehend the reality of the situation they were in.


“I said lower your weapons!”


Her voice rose sharply; she couldn't fathom the utter foolishness of her subordinates. From the very beginning, they had been warned never to mess with the Twilights. Yet they had nearly killed Zeeran, a leader of the Twilights himself. What would happen if Zeeran truly didn't survive? Erika could only pray that the doctor would save him.


“Senior Azure…… may I propose a new contract?”


Erika looked deeply into Azure's eyes as if pleading for mercy; she was at her wit's end.


Hearing that, Azure re-equipped her steel boots and walked toward the round table, taking a seat while pondering whether this was what her brother had planned. Did her brother want The Crows to propose a new contract? But Azure doubted she fully grasped what her brother had in mind.


After a few moments of deliberation, the door opened and a white-haired man entered, breaking the tense atmosphere,


“Tell me more about this new contract you wish to propose, Erika.”


Zeeran arrived, feeling his way along the wall as he walked.
