High above the screaming chaos and blood-soaked carnage of the ruined plaza, one creature stood completely apart from the wild, ravenous horde.

It was perfectly humanoid in posture, hovering silently in mid-air amidst the swirling ash. Unlike the frenzy of metallic wings beating wildly around it, the creature's broad silver wings remained completely motionless—extended horizontally with absolute, rigid perfection. It floated in the sky with zero vibration or aerodynamic effort, as though the laws of gravity had simply ceased to apply to its form.

Levitating directly above its head was a brilliant, ringed halo of pale white light, spinning slowly with smooth, mechanical precision.

Its head was a seamless helmet of featureless, polished white plate metal—devoid of eyes, nose, or mouth. Yet despite its blank countenance, its subtle tilt conveyed a chilling sensation: it was not participating in the slaughter. It was observing. 

It was staring directly down at Azure.

Azure's cold gaze locked onto the haloed entity. Recognizing it as an overwhelming presence, Azure didn't hesitate. She bent her knees and drove her bare feet into the cracked asphalt.

*BOOM!*

Unleashing the full, uninhibited power of her leg suit, Azure shot into the air like a streak of blue light, launching herself straight toward the floating anomaly.

In mid-flight, her razor-sharp eyes identified a hair-width gap between the creature's neck and shoulder plate.

Closing the distance in a fraction of a second, Azure lunged forward, plunging her twin Frost Blades deep into the narrow armor seam. She forcefully twisted both hilts, unleashing the maximum sub-zero Antarctic discharge. Frost erupted violently within the joint gap, instantly encasing the creature's chest and shoulder in a thick, crystalline shell of radiant blue ice.

The creature didn't flinch. It didn't utter a sound.

With terrifying, casual speed, the entity's metallic hand shot out and clamped around Azure's throat like an iron vice, snuffing out her momentum instantly.

*CRUNCH-CRACK-SNAP!*

Before Azure could react, the creature tightened its grip around her wrists. With effortless, godlike physical strength, it crushed Azure's prosthetic arms. Metal plates buckled, internal gears shattered into scrap, and the glowing Twilight Crystal hilts exploded into dust.

Without even turning its featureless face, the haloed anomaly tossed Azure away like a useless bug.

Azure hurled through the air, crashing violently into the ruined plaza below. She tumbled across the cracked asphalt, skidding through dust and shattered glass before coming to a halt.

Azure lay on her back, her severed, crushed prosthetic sockets sparking with short-circuited electrical arcs and cold blue smoke.

Her quiet eyes narrowed. A cold realization washed over her: *this creature was an anomaly.* It was something far beyond normal physical limits or conventional warfare.

In her mind, her brother Zeeran's soft whisper echoed with crystal clarity:

*“Your priorities are only three people… J.J… Erika, and try to locate Liezt's whereabouts. If something strange happens, flee and abandon the battle.”*

Azure lifted her head, surveying the battlefield.

The ruined plaza was a nightmare of blood and metal. The Crows were being hunted like cattle. Giant metallic spears rained down from the sky, impaling fleeing squad members to the asphalt. Quadrupedal steel beasts crushed bodies underfoot, their razor wing-blades severing limbs in a horrific display of carnage. Blood spattered across the shattered cobblestones, painting the rubble red.

Scanning the devastation, Azure noted that J.J was already gone—the veteran gunsmith had successfully dived into the northern drainage valve to save his skin.

Then, Azure's gaze landed on Erika.

A few yards away, Erika lay motionless amidst the rubble, knocked unconscious by a nearby shockwave impact. Blood trickled down her pale forehead, her pistol lying uselessly beside her hand as a quadrupedal beast turned its featureless head toward her.

Azure didn't hesitate.

Using her powerful leg suit, Azure scrambled to her feet instantly. She sprinted across the blood-slick pavement, her bare feet blurring as she closed the distance to Erika.

Arriving at Erika's side just as the beast lunged, Azure slid her foot beneath Erika's waist and kicked upward with flawless precision.

Erika's unconscious body floated smoothly up into the air.

Azure maneuvered beneath the falling form, catching Erika expertly across her upper back and neck. Balancing Erika's weight securely across her shoulders, Azure adjusted her posture to steady her unconscious burden.

She had no arms left to hold her. But her legs were unbroken.

Digging her toes into the cracked asphalt, Azure unleashed an explosive burst of speed, sprinting armless across the blood-soaked plaza with Erika on her back—fleeing the apocalyptic nightmare toward the underground sanctuary.

Night had fully enveloped the realm by the time Azure navigated the ruined city corridors. The surface world was a suffocating expanse of pitch black darkness. As she sprinted through the shadows, Azure constantly tracked the sky, her quiet eyes scanning the thick ash clouds above to ensure they were not being trailed by the swarming monsters.

Navigating hidden drainage routes and subterranean bypasses, she finally reached the heavy steel blast doors of The Crows' underground base.

As the security doors sealed behind them, the horrifying reality of the expedition laid bare.

Out of the full strike squad that had set out for J.J's bar, only a tiny fraction had returned alive. Aside from Azure and the unconscious Erika, only J.J and The Ravens division—Zack, Derek, and Victoria—had made it back. Every other member of The Crows had been slaughtered on the surface.

J.J collapsed onto a wooden bench near the entrance, exhausted to death. He leaned his head against the concrete wall, panting heavily, his skin slick with cold sweat and grease.

Zeeran Twilight was waiting in his wheelchair at the corridor junction. He quickly rolled forward to welcome them back, but the moment his eyes fell upon Azure, his usual calm composure vanished instantly.

Azure stood before him armless. Her prosthetic arms had been completely crushed and torn away at the shoulder joints, leaving twisted metal plates and short-circuited wiring sparking with blue embers.

Zeeran panicked. His eyes widened in rare agitation, and he began mumbling frantically to himself, his voice trembling as he tried to process the sight.

⃟“No... no, no... her arms... dismantled...?” Zeeran muttered, rubbing his forehead frantically as his mind raced. “Where are the spare parts...? Is there a reserve unit in the lower underground laboratory...? No... the primary stock... back in Antarctica... there are many spare parts in Antarctica...”

Forcing himself to regain control, Zeeran wheeled his chair into the center of the subterranean command room. He turned to face the battered survivors, his tone growing grim and urgent.

⃟“Everyone, gather round,” Zeeran ordered. “We must start an emergency meeting immediately. Tell me everything you saw on the surface.”

Azure stepped forward first. In her flat, unhurried voice, she reported her findings:

⃟“The enemy includes a humanoid anomaly,” Azure stated calmly. “It hovers in mid-air with non-flapping wings, crowned by a glowing ringed halo. It possesses no face. It resisted the Antarctic frost of the Twilight Crystal without flinching... and easily crushed both of my prosthetic arms before throwing me aside.”

Next, The Ravens reported. Zack, Derek, and Victoria recounted the nightmare on the surface—the quadrupedal beasts, the flying wing-blade monsters, the terrifying rain of metallic spears, and the utter slaughter of their surface forces.

As the reports concluded, Erika stirred on a nearby medical cot and slowly opened her eyes.

Regaining consciousness, the horrific memories of the open plaza rushed back into her mind—the blood, the screams, the destruction of her squad. Erika stumbled off the cot, her gaze locking directly onto Zeeran's face.

Erika marched straight up to Zeeran's wheelchair and **slapped him violently across the face.**

*CRACK!*

The force of the blow echoed through the silent room, turning Zeeran's head to the side.

⃟“This is all your fault!” Erika screamed, tears of grief and rage streaming down her face. “You convinced me to send half our forces onto the surface! You promised Azure would protect them! They're all dead because of you!”

Zeeran didn't retaliate. He slowly turned his bruised face back to look at Erika, his eyes heavy with solemn remorse.

⃟“...It was my fault,” Zeeran confessed quietly. “I miscalculated.”

Heavy silence settled over the room. No one spoke; the grief of their losses hung suffocatingly in the air.

After a few quiet moments, Zeeran paused. His gaze drifted thoughtfully toward the floorboards, his brow furrowing as a sudden, unsettling realization struck him.

⃟“If the enemy possesses such overwhelming strength... and such terrifying speed...” Zeeran spoke softly, his voice cutting through the silence like an icy blade, “...why were you able to reach all the way back here at all?”

The question hung in the air like a death sentence.

*Scritch... click... scritch...*

Tiny, metallic scraping noises suddenly echoed from the concrete ceiling overhead.

The entire room went dead silent. Nobody dared to breathe.

Zeeran's eyes widened in sheer horror. With explosive reaction speed, he threw his body forward out of his wheelchair, forcefully shoving Azure out of the way!

*CRRRRASH—!*

A massive metallic spear tore through the reinforced concrete ceiling, shattering iron beams and stone in a blinding shower of dust and debris!

The underground base was broken open.

They had been found.
