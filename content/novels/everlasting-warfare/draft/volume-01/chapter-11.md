The cozy warmth that had filled the central court of Ubbshu-Ukkinna vanished in a single breath, replaced by a cold, suffocating weight that pressed down upon the high marble pillars.

*CREAK...*

The massive obsidian door shifted fully open. From the shadowy threshold of the dark chamber, the quiet sound of wooden wheels turning against the polished floor echoed down the cathedral hall.

Princess Anna watched with wide eyes as a figure rolled into the light of the central court.

Sitting in a dark, intricately carved wooden wheelchair was a woman whose presence commanded an inexplicable, overwhelming gravity. Her long, flowing hair was a striking blend of deep dark obsidian and glowing amethyst purple that shimmered like cosmic dust under the crystal dome. 

Yet, her appearance bore the agonizing marks of severe, ancient retribution. 

Clean white cloth bandages wrapped around nearly every inch of her body—coiling around her neck, wrists, arms, waist, and legs beneath a simple dark robe. Most shocking of all, embedded directly in the center of her chest was a single, ancient dagger. The hilt and blade glowed with a faint, pulse of ethereal violet light, permanently plunged into her breast. It did not bleed, nor did it fall—it remained lodged deep within her flesh like a permanent, unremovable divine punishment forged into her very soul.

Anna's breath caught in her throat. 

Before Anna could utter a single word, a loud, synchronized rustle of fabric shattered the silence.

Without a second's hesitation, Ruby Valentine dropped from the plush velvet couch onto the marble floor. Lowering her head until her forehead pressed flat against the white stone, the proud former ruler of the Elves prostrated herself in absolute, unflinching submission.

Beside her, Emerald Caleste, Quin Sapphie, Luna Diamond, and Alexandrite Novah instantly followed suit. The warm, chaotic laughter of moments ago dissolved into terrifyingly strict discipline as the predecessor Entus dropped to their knees and pressed their foreheads against the marble floor. Hovering low above them, tiny Giznu folded her pair of translucent spirit wings tight against her back and dipped low toward the ground, her small head bowed in silent, solemn reverence beside Ruby. 

Not a single one of them dared to lift their eyes. The quiet lounge had transformed into a court of absolute, solemn allegiance.

Anna stood entirely alone amidst the kneeling figures, frozen in place. 

Inside her mind, the ancient spiritual resonance of Ubbshu-Ukkinna hummed. When she had met Ruby, Emerald, Quin, Luna, Jenneate, and Alexandrite, the sanctuary's library of souls had instantly flooded her mind with their names, titles, and legacies. But as Anna looked upon the woman in the wheelchair, Ubbshu-Ukkinna returned nothing. 

Where her name and history should have been, there was only a terrifying, absolute void—an empty abyss erased from the records of existence. Anna possessed full access to the sanctuary's vast knowledge, yet she could not identify the figure before her.

From her prostrated position on the floor, Ruby raised her voice slightly, her tone stripped of all dry sarcasm and filled with profound, trembling respect.

"What has prompted Your Majesty to venture outside her chamber...?" Ruby spoke smoothly, her voice echoing off the marble arches. "Is there something troubling Your Majesty? What has bothered your rest?"

The woman in the wheelchair—Amethyst Lorelei—did not speak immediately. Her deep amethyst eyes swept slowly across the court, resting for a brief moment upon Anna's standing figure before drifting back to the kneeling Entus.

The moment Ruby finished speaking, the rest of the Entu eagerly chimed in with strict, submissive devotion, offering their service in hushed, earnest voices.

"Your Majesty... would you care for fresh tea?" Luna offered quietly, her demon wings folded tightly against her back as her forehead remained pressed to the floor. "I can brew a new pot immediately!"

"Or perhaps warm bread, Your Majesty?" Emerald added quickly, her short frame bowing as deeply as possible. "I can prepare it at once!"

"If Your Majesty requires a message delivered or an errand carried out across the sanctuary..." Alexandrite's voice bubbled with nervous reverence, her glowing elemental hair dimming in respect. "...please grant us the honor of your command!"

"Whatever Her Majesty desires, we shall perform without delay," Giznu squeaked softly, hovering low to the ground as her translucent wings fluttered in hushed reverence.

Quin nodded in silent agreement beside them, her cat ears pinned flat against her head in complete deferential stillness.

Amethyst parted her lips, attempting to respond to their eager offerings. But as she tried to form words, her voice was halted and deeply troubled. What escaped her throat was a shaky, rough rasp—a fragile, labored sound that seemed to strain against the ancient punishment lingering in her chest.

Seeing Her Majesty struggle to speak, Ruby Valentine kept her head low and spoke with gentle, reverent care.

"Your Majesty... may I have permission to draw closer?" Ruby asked softly, her tone filled with quiet devotion.

Amethyst paused, her amethyst eyes gazing down at the Elven ruler before giving a slow, faint nod.

Ruby carefully rose from the marble floor, taking small, deliberate steps toward the wheelchair. She knelt down beside the dark wooden frame, leaning her ear close to Amethyst's bandaged lips. 

Amethyst leaned in slightly, the glowing violet dagger in her chest pulsing faintly as she whispered a few quiet, raspy words directly into Ruby's ear. 

Ruby's crimson eyes widened subtly as she listened. When Amethyst finished, Ruby bowed her head deeply once more.

"Understood, Your Majesty," Ruby murmured softly.

As the wooden wheels rolled into the darkness, the massive obsidian door shifted shut behind them with a quiet, heavy thud, leaving the court in solemn, breathless silence.

With the door closed, the suffocating gravity lingering over the hall finally began to lift. One by one, Emerald, Quin, Luna, and Alexandrite slowly exhaled and stood up from the marble floor, dusting off their robes with solemn quietness. 

Ruby turned and walked back into the central lounge, her expression unusually serious. She crossed the polished floor and walked straight toward Anna, stopping just a few steps in front of her.

Anna looked at the Elven ruler, her brow furrowed with lingering questions. "Who was she, Ruby? Why couldn't Ubbshu-Ukkinna tell me anything about her?"

Ruby took a slow breath, her crimson eyes meeting Anna's gaze with deep gravity. 

"Her Majesty... Amethyst Lorelei... was the First Entu, En-zagin," Ruby revealed quietly. "And long before you carried the burden in this era, Anna... she was the ruler of humanity."

Anna's eyes widened as the revelation settled over her heart.

"The message she whispered to me just now... was meant for you, En-gurnun," Ruby continued softly. "She asked me to pass her words directly to you."

Ruby paused, delivering Her Majesty's message with absolute solemnity:

"'I apologize... I couldn't save humanity last time. My action made me punished, and made humans left behind.'"

Anna froze, her breath catching as the heavy weight of the First Entu's confession resonated through the vast library of Ubbshu-Ukkinna.
