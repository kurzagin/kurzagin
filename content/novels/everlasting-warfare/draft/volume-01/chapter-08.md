Silence hit Zeeran before consciousness did.

It wasn’t the quiet of a calm night, nor the muted stillness of the underground sanctuary before the breach. It was a heavy, stagnant, suffocating silence—the kind that follows absolute devastation.

Zeeran opened his eyes.

The dark, starless sky above was split wide open by the celestial, silver glow of the colossal World Tree. Its colossal branches still stretched into the cosmos, glowing with faint, divine light that cast long, grotesque shadows across the ruined surface plaza.

Zeeran blinked, forcing his hazy vision to clear. His head throbbed faintly where it had bounced against the shattered concrete, but the agonizing, soul-crushing paralysis that had bound him to a wheelchair for years... was gone.

He felt his fingertips. They twitched smoothly, responding to his nerve impulses without delay. He flexed his wrists, his arms, his chest—every muscle, every joint felt light, warm, and revitalized. There was no lingering numbness, no dead weight dragging down his lower body. His physical body felt entirely, inexplicably healthy.

Zeeran gasped softly, his chest expanding with deep, clear lungfuls of ash-tinged air. 

*I... I can feel my legs.*

A wave of shock surged through his mind, instantly followed by a fierce, instinctual urge. He didn't know what miraculous power had mended his broken body during his unconsciousness—whether it was the lingering aura of the glowing World Tree or the strange beastkin warriors who had appeared at the edge of the darkness—but he wasn't going to lie in the dirt any longer.

Zeeran dug his hands into the rough, glass-strewn asphalt. 

He pushed. 

His arm muscles tightened, and his knees pulled inward beneath his torso. For the first time in years, his feet planted firmly onto the solid ground. He gritted his teeth, shifting his weight forward, and pushed upward.

His knees wobbled for a fraction of a second before locking into place.

Zeeran Twilight stood up.

He stood fully upright, on his own two feet, elevating his posture above the ruined floor. A surge of raw emotion welled up in his throat. For years, he had been a strategist trapped in a broken shell, forced to rely on wheels and the physical protection of others. Now, he was finally standing up—not just physically, but stepping forward under his own strength to face the nightmare of this shattered world.

He took a slow, tentative breath, steadying his posture as he began to observe the situation around him.

The sight before him was grim and horrific.

The entire plaza looked like a battlefield graveyard. 

Scattered across the cracked pavement and collapsed masonry lay the fallen bodies of Crows soldiers, Ravens operatives, and civilian refugees. Tactical coats were soaked in crimson blood, and broken rifles littered the ground alongside crushed supply crates and thick clouds of ash. 

It appeared, at first glance, as though an entire massacre had swept through the plaza.

Zeeran’s sharp eyes darted across the blood-stained plaza, searching for immediate threats. 

*Where are the monsters?*

There were no active creatures left. No haloed anomaly glided through the air; no quadrupedal steel beasts prowled the shadows. Instead, the area was littered with the remnants of the horde. 

Several metallic beasts lay scattered around the rubble, but they were no longer moving. Their sleek silver plates were cleanly bisected, cut through by razor-sharp strikes that had sliced through dense alloy like paper. Severed mechanical limbs, detached tail-blades, and halved torsos lay frozen on the asphalt, completely inactive and devoid of life. Whatever had fought them off—those young beastkin warriors with their folded silk armor and curved single-edged blades—had left the metallic abominations severed and dead.

Yet, despite the slaughter of the horde, the fkin tree was still there.

Zeeran lifted his gaze slightly, staring at the colossal titan of a tree reaching into heaven. Its silver-shimmering bark pulsated in a slow, silent rhythm, completely indifferent to the mountain of human corpses beneath its canopy. It stood as a permanent, immovable monument to the new reality.

Zeeran turned his head frantically, his heart hammering against his ribs as a cold panic seized his chest. 

*Azure—!*

His eyes swept across the bloodied plaza until they locked onto the base of a ruined brick wall forty feet away. 

Half-buried beneath a pile of heavy fallen masonry lay a fragile, armless figure. Her silver leg armor was scuffed and dull, its faint shimmer completely dead; her silver hair was matted with blood and dust. She wasn't moving.

"Azure!" 

The name tore from Zeeran's throat. 

Without a moment of hesitation, without even thinking about his newly restored legs, Zeeran broke into a sprint. His boots slammed against the shattered asphalt as he ran straight toward her, closing the distance in frantic, desperate strides.

Reaching the crumbled wall, he immediately dropped to his knees. He carefully pushed away the heavy bricks and shattered concrete blocks resting over her chest and legs, clearing the debris as fast as his hands could move. Beneath the rubble, Azure lay cold and motionless, her armless sockets sparking with faint, dying static. 

Zeeran reached out, gently cradling the back of her head with one hand while using his fingers to softly tap her blood-dusted cheek.

"Azure... Azure, wake up," he whispered gently, his voice trembling with concern. "Please, open your eyes."

For a long moment, she didn't respond. Then, her long silver eyelashes fluttered softly. Azure let out a weak, shallow breath as her eyelids parted, her pale blue eyes slowly focusing through the haze of unconsciousness.

The moment her gaze settled on Zeeran, her eyes widened in profound shock.

She saw him kneeling over her—his posture firm, his back straight, resting on his own knees with no wheelchair in sight. The frail, bound master she had spent her entire life protecting was holding her up with full physical vigor.

"Brother...?" Azure’s soft voice carried an unprecedented note of utter disbelief. "You... you are standing...?"

"I'll explain later. I don't fully understand it myself," Zeeran said hurriedly, though a small, reassuring touch remained in his eyes as he helped her sit up against the wall. He quickly looked around the quiet, blood-soaked plaza once more before turning his urgent gaze back to her. "Azure, did you see them? Did you see a group?"

Azure blinked, her brow furrowing slightly in confusion. "A group?"

"Right before I blacked out... I saw silhouettes leaping off the rooftops," Zeeran explained, his voice low and intense. "They were wearing lacquered armor over silk robes... beastkin warriors carrying curved blades. A dragon-horned woman was leading them. When I woke up just now, all the creatures were cleanly cut into pieces, the horde was gone, and everyone else..." He swallowed hard, glancing back at the corpses of their allies. "...everyone else was dead."

Zeeran looked directly into her pale blue eyes. "Did you see where they went? Or who they were?"

Azure remained quiet for a brief moment, her eyes scanning the dark skyline before looking back at Zeeran. She shook her head slowly. 

"I... I do not know, Brother," Azure admitted softly. "A heavy impact knocked me into the masonry, and my systems went offline almost instantly. I saw nothing."

Offering her his hand, Zeeran helped support her as they stood up together. Though her arm sockets still flickered with dead plasma arcs and her legs were stiff from strain, Azure maintained her footing alongside him, standing side-by-side amidst the ash and devastation.

As they stood together, Azure turned her sharp blue eyes toward the carnage littered across the plaza. She scanned the severed silver torsos, the sliced mechanical limbs, and the shattered quadrupedal hulls strewn across the asphalt.

Her expression darkened slightly as she made a critical observation.

"Brother," Azure murmured, her voice laced with quiet vigilance. "The haloed entity... is not among them."

Zeeran froze, his eyes narrowing as he followed her gaze. She was right. While dozens of quadrupedal steel beasts lay cleanly cut and inactive in pools of dark synthetic oil, there was no sign of the single divine, white-haloed creature that had breached their underground base. It hadn't been slain here; it was completely gone.

Before Zeeran could ponder the anomaly further, a weak, trembling groan echoed from beneath a pile of overturned supply crates near a shattered pillar.

"Ugh... n-no..."

Zeeran and Azure's heads snapped toward the sound simultaneously.

"Erika!" Zeeran called out.

They moved toward the source together. Zeeran reached the crates first, shoving aside the splintered wood to reveal Erika curled up on the ground. Her face was pale, her arms pulled tightly against her chest in shock. Above her left temple, a deep gash spilled a line of dark crimson blood down her forehead, coating her cheek and brow.

"Erika," Zeeran said gently, kneeling beside her.

Erika's eyes fluttered open, glassy and unfocused, before widening slightly as she recognized his face. "Z-Zeeran...? Azure...?" she stammered, her voice shaking violently as she clutched her head. "Is... is everyone...?"

"Don't move," Zeeran instructed calmly. 

Reaching down, Zeeran gripped the inner hem of his linen coat. With a sharp, practiced pull, he tore a long strip of clean cloth from his own garments. Folding the cloth tightly, he leaned in and pressed it gently over the bleeding wound on Erika's forehead, carefully wrapping it around her head and securing it with a firm knot.

"You're bleeding, but you're alive," Zeeran whispered reassuringly, adjusting the bandage. "Hold still. We're right here."

Erika took a deep, unsteady breath, using Zeeran's offered hand to carefully pull herself up. She winced at the dull throb in her forehead, but seeing Zeeran standing firm on his own two legs gave her a sudden surge of focus amid the horror.

"We need to check the bodies," Erika said, her voice filled with quiet desperation. "There might still be people alive under the wreckage."

Zeeran nodded. "Let's search. Fast."

Leaving Azure to keep watch at the plaza's perimeter, Zeeran and Erika began moving systematically through the sprawling wreckage, checking the fallen bodies scattered across the concrete.

To their immense relief, not everyone had perished.

Behind a collapsed concrete barricade, Erika spotted movement. Old J.J was leaning heavily against a shattered support beam, coughing up soot and blood, but very much alive. His shotgun was bent, but he gave a weak, breathless grin as Zeeran pulled him to his feet.

"Damn... kid," J.J wheezed, staring in disbelief at Zeeran's legs. "Look at you... standing tall..."

A few yards away, movement stirred beneath a pile of ruined canvas tents. Derek, Zack, and Victoria of The Ravens emerged, bruised, battered, and bleeding from minor claw wounds, but alive. They had taken cover when the initial strike slammed into the plaza, surviving the horde's rampage by seconds.

Emerging from a nearby reinforced medical hatch was Dr. Griffin. Draped in his dark, soot-stained coat and clutching his brass-bound leather case of specialized chemical formulations, the chief scientist stepped out onto the ash-streaked concrete, his unreadable, analytical gaze sweeping across the carnage before joining The Ravens.

As Zeeran, Erika, and J.J continued shouting across the dark plaza, more silhouettes began crawling out from the shadows—from broken subterranean ventilation shafts, shattered drainage ditches, and collapsed doorways. 

They were the surviving Crows members and refugees who had evacuated the underground base. 

One by one, the survivors gathered around Zeeran in the center of the ruined plaza. Men and women with torn tactical coats, bloodied bandages, and hollow expressions huddled together under the silver glow of the World Tree. 

Erika quickly conducted a headcount, her eyes sweeping over the quiet crowd.

"How many?" Zeeran asked, stepping up beside her.

"About fifty," Erika replied softly, her voice heavy with grief yet relieved. "Out of hundreds... only about fifty Crows members and refugees survived."

Zeeran looked over the remaining fifty survivors. Their base was destroyed, their leadership fractured, and the world irrevocably altered—but they were alive. Standing tall in the ash, Zeeran prepared to guide them forward.

***

While Zeeran and Erika gathered the survivors in the center of the plaza, Azure had moved out to secure the eastern perimeter. 

Stepping carefully over cracked glass and fallen steel beams, her silent gaze scanned the shadow-draped ruins of a collapsed five-story building at the edge of the square. The lower floors had caved in, leaving a dark, cavernous hollow beneath the tilted concrete slabs.

Something inside the shadow caught her attention. 

It was not metallic beast armor, nor human debris. It was emitting a subtle, rhythmic hum—a low-frequency vibration that resonated directly with Azure’s internal core scanner.

Approaching the hollow beneath the collapsed building, Azure stepped inside. 

Lying partially covered under twisted rebar, ash, and thick overgrown roots was a massive, perfectly geometric structure. It was an eight-foot cube forged entirely from smooth, transparent **Twilight Crystal**, glowing softly with faint blue and gold circuit lines along its facets.

Azure’s eyes widened slightly as her internal diagnostic sensors picked up the familiar energy signature. Recognition hit her instantly.

It was the **Twilight Transmogrifier**.

It was her own engineering creation—a compact shape-shifting device of Twilight Crystal that she had designed. In moments of extreme danger, the Transmogrifier could activate its *Tactical Aegis* protocol, expanding its crystalline lattice into an indestructible sanctuary.

Azure stepped closer, wiping a layer of gray concrete dust off the front polished crystal surface. Looking through the transparent Twilight Crystal wall, her sensors registered a young woman with distinctive silver-black hair and delicate Twilight facial features, suspended completely frozen inside a thick, ice-like stasis field.

It was **Liezt Twilight**.

A thick, jagged branch protruded directly across Liezt's chest and stomach. Wanting to assess the full structural damage, Azure walked around to the rear side of the crystal cube. 

Peering through the back transparent wall, Azure saw the rest of the horror. The cube had expanded directly around the base of a thick, mutated tree trunk—one of the lesser anomaly vegetations sprouting around the World Tree. Liezt’s back was pressed violently flat against the main wooden trunk, creating a devastating squeeze against the branch pinning her front.

Azure stood silent, conducting a swift simulation within her mind as her eyes traced the angle of the wood. In just a few seconds, her mind reconstructed the exact sequence of events that had transpired during the blackout.

‘The mutated tree erupted violently from the ground… Liezt was caught directly between the trunk and a growing branch…’

‘The crushing pressure was about to bisect her torso… tearing through flesh and bone to split her completely in half…’

‘In that split second before her body was severed, Liezt activated the Transmogrifier…’

The conclusion was clear. Liezt had triggered the device’s *Tactical Aegis* protocol at the ultimate moment, expanding the crystal around her and locking her body into an emergency cryo-stasis field. Suspended in crystal ice and stasis fluid, her life signs were barely flickering—holding her near-fatal injury frozen in time to keep her from dying.
