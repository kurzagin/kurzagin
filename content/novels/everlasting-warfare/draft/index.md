Zeeran panics seeing Azure armless, mumbling about spare parts in Antarctica. During an emergency intel meeting, Erika awakens and slaps Zeeran in grief and rage, which Zeeran accepts as his miscalculation. Just as Zeeran realizes the enemy allowed them to escape to locate their base, a giant metallic spear shatters the ceiling—breaching their sanctuary. | [chapter-06.md](./volume-01/chapter-06.md) |
| **07** | Chapter 7: The Shifted Chessboard | Chapter | The underground base is breached as the haloed creature gazes down from above. Azure steps forward to hold off the creature while Erika wheels Zeeran to the surface since her smaller frame cannot carry his weight. Upon reaching the surface at midnight, Azure points out a colossal glowing World Tree reaching into heaven, shattering Zeeran with the realization that his grand strategic calculations are useless. As the haloed entity and a swarming horde pursue them, Azure fights furiously until Zeeran's wheelchair is destroyed. As Zeeran loses consciousness, a group of young beastkin warriors clad in lacquered armor and silk robes arrive, led by a dragon-horned warrior. | [chapter-07.md](./volume-01/chapter-07.md) |
| **08** | Chapter 8: Standing in the Ash | Chapter | Zeeran awakens in the ruined plaza, discovering his paralyzed body fully healed. He stands up on his own two feet—finally standing up for himself. He observes the area, seeing no active creatures and finding severed, inactive metallic bodies, while the glowing World Tree towers in the night sky. He runs to Azure and gently wakes her up; she is deeply shocked to see him standing. Azure answers she didn't see the mysterious group, and they stand together. Observing the bodies, Azure notices that the haloed entity is not among the severed creatures. They hear Erika groaning beneath debris and rush to her side, where Zeeran tears a piece of his linen coat to bandage Erika's bleeding forehead. Zeeran and Erika then search the bodies for survivors, finding J.J, The Ravens, and surviving Crows refugees, totaling about 50 survivors. Meanwhile, Azure inspects a ruined building on the perimeter and discovers her custom-built Twilight Transmogrifier expanded into a crystal Aegis pod. Walking around to the back, Azure runs a diagnostic simulation revealing Liezt Twilight frozen in cryo-stasis, trapped between a tree trunk and a branch that nearly split her in half, holding her alive in suspended animation. | [chapter-08.md](./volume-01/chapter-08.md) |
| **09** | Chapter 9: The Unknown Horizon | Chapter | Gathering the 50 survivors, Zeeran calls a meeting to address their imminent doom without resources. Erika proposes going to Antarctica, but for the first time, Zeeran hesitates—weighing the grim odds against flying spear-wielding monsters immune to bullets and Azure's kicks, while failing to connect the mysterious beastkin. Azure reveals a military-grade Ark at Babylon Harbor intact due to Twilight Crystal's immunity. J.J admits he will be a burden in combat, while The Ravens lack gas supplies. Azure notes everyone needs Antarctica as it houses their main lab. Finally, Belle adjusts her glasses and points out they need food, breaking the tension with laughter before they prepare to move. | [chapter-09.md](./volume-01/chapter-09.md) |
| **10** | Chapter 10: Divine Whispers in the Sky | Chapter | High above the ash clouds, Sister Metatron meets a spear-wielding haloed entity with a woman's face, who reacts to telemetry about a mortal, the Longinus, the Legion, and 'The Harvest' status before reporting to higher-ups. Meanwhile, Princess Anna awakens inside Ubbshu-Ukkinna—a vast, infinite library filled with books and doors. Reaching the central court, Anna meets a red-haired, red-eyed Elven woman resting on a couch who sips her tea, greets her as En-gurnun, and introduces herself as En-sirsir (Ruby Valentine). When Ruby notes five other Entu are sleeping, Anna questions the math. Ruby reveals that the First Entu was erased and punished, staying inside her chamber and rarely going outside, warning Anna not to bother her. Ruby then teases Anna with playful sarcasm about her reckless bravery in merging all seven realms without consulting them, remarking that her Great Tree will get scratched, though her Elven people will probably survive. | [chapter-10.md](./volume-01/chapter-10.md) |
| **11** | Chapter 11: Her Majesty's Presence | Chapter | As the dark obsidian door creaks open, the erased First Entu—Amethyst Lorelei—emerges into Ubbshu-Ukkinna's central court in a wheelchair. Wrapped head-to-toe in bandages and bearing a glowing, unremovable knife permanently embedded in her chest, her presence instantly shatters the warm lounge atmosphere. Ruby Valentine and all predecessor Entus immediately prostrate themselves in profound reverence, leaving Princess Anna bewildered as Ubbshu-Ukkinna's soul resonance fails to reveal Amethyst's identity. Addressing 'Her Majesty' with strict humility, the Entus offer tea, bread, and obedience. When Amethyst struggles to speak with a shaky, rough voice, Ruby asks permission to draw near, receives a whisper, and wheels her back to her chamber. Afterwards, Ruby reveals to Anna that Amethyst was the original ruler of humanity and passes on her message: an apology for failing to save humanity, getting punished, and leaving humans behind. | [chapter-11.md](./volume-01/chapter-11.md) |
| **12** | Chapter 12: The Iron Path to Babylon | Chapter | As dawn approaches, Erika and The Ravens lead the advance team toward Babylon's Harbor, only to find the main coastal highway completely destroyed and torn apart by colossal World Tree roots. Blocked by a massive tree trunk, Erika banters via radio with Zeeran, who scouting from afar in his deep baritone scope position guides them through the climb. Reaching the river channel, Azure uses her specialized leg suit to execute a monumental leap across the water gap and interface-kicks the dock control panel to lower the massive steel drawbridge. Erika radios Zeeran to bring the fifty refugees safely across. Once aboard, Azure guides Erika step-by-step through the complex engine ignition sequence, sparking the colossal Twilight Crystal core to life with grandiose force. The Ark glides backward out of Dock 7, turns its prow toward the open sea, and slowly sets sail into the morning horizon—leaving Babylon behind. | [chapter-12.md](./volume-01/chapter-12.md) |
| **13** | Chapter 13: Sanctuary in Motion | Chapter | As the Ark sets sail across the open ocean, the exhausted survivors finally find a moment to rest. Azure informs Erika of the ship's vast number of empty quarters, but Erika insists on keeping everyone housed close together to maintain security, heating, and morale. In their assigned room, The Ravens suffer from severe respiratory strain and frantic coughing, prompting Dr. Griffin to intervene with a few of his last therapeutic gas canisters. Meanwhile, Azure directs Belle to the emergency food storage filled with processed rations, which Belle inspects with a small team. With J.J already fast asleep in his room, Azure joins Zeeran in his quarters, where the two share a quiet moment of reflection and rest. | [chapter-13.md](./volume-01/chapter-13.md) |
| **14** | Chapter 14: The Burden We Carry | Chapter | In the quiet privacy of the command quarters, Zeeran feeds Azure hot broth as the Ark sails southward. During their intimate moment, Azure notes Zeeran's recent closeness with Erika, prompting Zeeran to dryly ask by what parameter she means before clarifying that Erika's organizational skills are essential for leading the group. Azure then recounts how she liberated Liezt's stasis cube the night before by severing the tree trunk sections protruding outside the indestructible crystal lattice. Unable to fit the massive 8-foot cube through narrow cabin doors, Azure secured Liezt intact in Deck Two's central concourse with heavy cargo cables, drawing strength from their bond as they head toward Antarctica. | [chapter-14.md](./volume-01/chapter-14.md) |
| **15** | Chapter 15: The Frozen Silence | Chapter | Teleported into the middle of the ocean, the mage almost drowns as her hijacked body parts the sea to rescue survivors under attack by hundreds of Metallic-Bodied Monsters. After teleporting the survivors and the screaming man back to Kaka's cliff, her total mana depletion triggers an emergency passive magic vanish, leaving everyone and Kaka behind. | [chapter-15.md](./volume-01/chapter-15.md) |
| **16** | Chapter 16: Stranded on the Cliff | Chapter | Immediately following the sudden spatial teleportation from the ocean abyss, Zeeran tumbles onto the snow-covered Antarctic cliff. He witnesses the strange girl in the gray cloak vanish in a flash of blue light, leaving behind a bewildered crow. After confirming Azure and Liezt's stasis pod are safe, Zeeran takes the lead to guide the group toward their underground research lab, while Erika stays behind a moment to take Kaka along as her new companion. | [chapter-16.md](./volume-01/chapter-16.md) |
| **17** | Chapter 17: The Golden Descent | Chapter | The expedition ascends the side staircases of the golden Ziggurat Tower, modeled after ancient Etemenanki architecture with cross-stairways converging toward the center. Leading the group, Zeeran notes that Rosette and Sakura White might be inside if they survived, to which Erika sarcastically reminds him that he sent them as spies, and that she caught on long before maintenance and shipped them to Antarctica where her 700 Crow troops were stationed on a mission. Upon reaching the central apex, Zeeran reveals the main platform is a massive mechanical elevator designed to descend into the subterranean facility. Erika decides to split the survivors into two groups, opting to remain behind with the second group while Zeeran and Azure take the first. | [chapter-17.md](./volume-01/chapter-17.md) |
| **18** | Chapter 18: The Vanguard of the Wasteland | Chapter | ur-sag̃ (𒌨𒊕)—the primordial origin of the Hero model in humanity before whom no meaning of Hero existed—confronts an endless swarm of Metallic-Bodied Monsters in the desert. After declaring his unshakeable, eternal endurance against the horde and throwing brutal stanceless punches, the narrative skips to the aftermath: ur-sag̃ sits atop a colossal mountain of shattered metallic bodies, calmly pulling a cigar from his pocket and lighting it under the desert sun. | [chapter-18.md](./volume-01/chapter-18.md) |
| **19** | Chapter 19: The Transcendent Law | Chapter | High above the scorching Mesopotamian wasteland, the Haloed Anomaly undergoes a divine transcendence. When ur-sag̃ bluntly dismisses its acoustic chirps, its face plate retracts to reveal a serene blonde female face. Metatron—Leader of Archangels, Kether Rank—softly alights upon the desert sand, scans the surrounding wreckage to identify over one hundred thousand destroyed Bene Elohim, and evaluates ur-sag̃ as an Antediluvian Era figure closest to King Enki who likely holds knowledge of the Nam-an's location. | [chapter-19.md](./volume-01/chapter-19.md) |
