## ⚔️ Major Factions & Powers

1. **[Tigris Empire](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/tigris-empire.md)**: An ancient imperial superpower blessed with vast resources and an iron-fisted culture. Worshiping the Elder Triad (**Enki**, **Enlil**, **An**) and venerating **Gilgamesh**, Tigris historically won every war with raw physical strength and ancient tactics. Because of their unbroken history of victories and stubborn refusal to look at foreign innovations, they arrogantly believed they would always win without firearms until their defeat in 1634.
2. **[Euphrates Kingdom](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/euphrates-kingdom.md)**: A modernist sovereign power centered around **Euphratea**. Formed **~900 years ago** when rebellious Tigris youth fled across the river to escape traditionalist elders, Euphrates citizens intermingled with foreign nations, worshipping **Marduk** and **Ishtar**. Known for iconic blonde hair and red eyes (blessings of Ishtar's beauty and Marduk's fierce ambition), they spent centuries in quiet research and engineered muskets specifically for the 100-Year War.
3. **[Twilight Family](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/twilight-family.md)**: A prestigious noble bloodline led by patriarch Zeeran Twilight (`ZR4N-4`) and his six sisters (`ZR3-4` Azure, `LZT-4` Liezt, `ZLL-4` Zell, `CZ-4` Chizu, `ZFL-4` Zyfel, `ZP-4` Zephyr), represented by their noble crest of a snake eating its own tail (**Ouroboros**). All subjects were stripped of their birth names upon entry into the White House; Zeeran named each sibling from their serial codes and derived their family surname from their assigned research ward, the **Twilight Room**. Lacking official birth records, all family members' ages are estimated, and all siblings officially chose **29 February (leap year)** as their shared birthday. They purposefully chose this quadrennial date so they wouldn't waste time celebrating every year—allowing each member to dedicate their undivided attention to their duty, work, and ambitions, before reuniting exactly four years later to gather and recount everything they have achieved.
4. **[White House](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/white-house.md)**: A top-secret human experimentation black-site deep in imperial forests, disguised as an orphanage. Founded on veneration of **Gilgamesh**, its classified mission is to engineer the "next Gilgamesh"—a supreme human asset of godlike physical strength—through brutal conditioning and [Vigortio](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/vigortio.md). Subjects entered sequentially: Zeeran (`ZR4N-4`) at ~6 yrs old, Azure (`ZR3-4`) +1 yr later, Liezt (`LZT-4`) +1 yr later, Zell (`ZLL-4`) & Chizu (`CZ-4`) +1 yr later, Zyfel (`ZFL-4`) +1 yr later, and Zephyr (`ZP-4`) +6 months later.

## 📜 Key Systems & Lore Mechanics

- **Technological & Ideological Disparity**: The split began **~900 years ago**. Euphrates embraced modernist innovation and spent centuries researching, developing muskets as a new weapon system for the war (first encountered by Zeeran at age ~20), whereas the resource-wealthy Tigris Empire maintained strict religious traditionalism, pure-blood doctrine, and heavy melee armor to their detriment.
- **[Tigris-Euphrates 100 Year War](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/timeline/tigris-euphrates-100-year-war.md)**: A century-long conflict starting in **1534** and ending in **1634** with the defeat of the Tigris Empire, resulting in land cessions to Euphrates and the displacement of House Twilight to Euphratea.
- **Discovery of [Twilight Crystal](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/twilight-crystal.md) & Genesis of Twilight Devices**: Discovered in Tigris caverns by Zeeran Twilight when Azure was 14 years old, the transparent, energy-dampening crystal became the foundation for all Twilight R&D—beginning with **[Azure's Leg Suit](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/azures-leg-suit.md)** engineered by 14-year-old Azure to dampen her hyper-dense 200 kg mass per leg.
- **Post-War Monetary Hegemony — [Euphry](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/technology/euphry.md)**: The supreme global currency unit established post-1634 and produced around the **[World Treasure Company](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/factions/world-treasure-company.md)**. Originally a modest apothecary clinic, it exploded into a global economic titan under the ruthless genius of **[Zyfel Twilight](file:///root/projects/vxnus-studio/EverlastingWarfare/lore/characters/zyfel-twilight.md)** ("The Gilded Plague") after she manufactured and cured an itching pestilence among Euphrates nobility. Secretly governing the institution under revered honorifics (*"Young Lady"*, *"Madame"*, *"Mademoiselle"*), Zyfel mints physical gold coins and paper banknotes bearing Ex-Queen Elizabeth's portrait alongside a subtle, micro-engraved House Twilight **Ouroboros** crest, enslaving continental trade to her sovereign wealth.
