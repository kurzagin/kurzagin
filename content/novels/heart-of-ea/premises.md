### Heart of Ea

The world of Ki remains scarred by The Flood, a devastating divine conflict that occurred 6,000 years ago, known as "Enuma Elish". The result is a planet almost entirely submerged under a toxic, heavily polluted ocean, making drinkable water a commodity of extreme scarcity.

The Catastrophe: The Flood and Abyss Energy

The conflict of Enuma Elish was fought between the Nun-Gal-En-Ne (The Great Prince), led by Marduk, and the Primordial Gods, commanded by Tiamat. Though Tiamat was defeated and sealed in the Deep Sea, her containment was imperfect. Her leaking power, dubbed Abyss Energy, contaminated all upper waters and triggered two sequential calamities:

- **The Creeping Tide (~6,000 to ~4,000 BP)**: A multi-century, progressive rise in ocean levels that submerged coastlines and drowned 90% of Ki's landmass over two millennia. This gradual deluge allowed humanity time to pool global resources and construct their ultimate sanctuary vessel, *The Preserver of Life*.

- **Marine Metamorphosis (~4,000 BP)**: As ocean contamination reached a critical saturation point ~4,000 years ago, marine life mutated into monstrous Flood Creatures.


The victorious gods subsequently vanished, their existence sacrificed to forge the seals that bound Tiamat. This act brought the undated Mythical Age to a definitive close. All subsequent history is recorded in the current post-war calendar era, **ADV** (After Deluge/War), with present day standing at 2,000 ADV.

Humanity’s Final Bastion: The Preserver of Life
  
Humanity's last hope rests upon The Preserver of Life, a colossal vessel designed as the ultimate sanctuary against the Flood Creatures. This massive ship is home to three interconnected, yet often conflicted, internal factions:

1. Eridu Capital: The core operational hub and central citizenry. They manage technology, farming, and daily sustenance, having been the original developers of the vessel. They are the most neutral faction, open to all ordinary citizens and young people seeking a faction.
    
2. Zigurrat Society: Responsible for all military and defensive operations aboard the vessel.
    
3. Ur-Ilani Covenant: The custodians of history and knowledge, they also maintain a Marduk cult. Their deep understanding of The Flood earns them deference from the military ("They know more about what we don't"), yet their overall relationship with the other factions is strained.

*Special Sub-Branch & External Faction:*
- **The Flood Army**: A specialized military branch within the Zigurrat Society dedicated exclusively to youth aged 17 to 25 fielding Flood Weapons.
- **Cult of Tiamat**: An external fanatic faction operating in the open ocean outside the vessel. They view Abyss Energy as a divine blessing and seek to break Marduk's seal by retrieving the shattered Tablet of Destinies.

Demographics & Divine Lineages

- **Ishtar's People (Uruk Descendants)**: During the Mythical Age in ancient Uruk, the goddess Ishtar found baseline humanity aesthetically plain and bestowed animal ears and tails upon her subjects as divine "decorations". Over centuries of generational training, these traits evolved into enhanced physical instincts (balance, directional hearing, spatial reflexes). They reproduce like baseline humans (with a 50/50 inheritance chance when interbreeding) and are fully integrated without discrimination across all factions aboard *The Preserver of Life*.
    

Sustenance and Defense

The survival of the factions hinges on effective resource management and defense:

- Sustenance: Eridu Capital manages the distribution of the Golden Apple, a fruit gifted by the god Ea, which is humanity's sole natural source of food and hydration. Though simple to cultivate, the scarcity of land necessitates the use of Golden Serum, an injectable derivative, for efficient distribution.
    
- Defense (Flood Weapons): For millennia, humanity defended the Ark using kinetic artillery and divine sanctuary barriers. However, as oceanic Abyssal density intensified over time, Flood Creatures mutated into hyper-regenerative Kraken and Leviathan grades. Starting ~900 years ago (~900 BP / 1100 ADV), researchers began reverse-engineering salvaged Kraken biological cores, though they did not yet understand the core's true nature or constraint logic. 


The Peril of the Flood Weapons and The Flood Army

Flood Weapons are a necessary evil, yet they pose a severe internal threat. They possess their own malevolent consciousness, linked to the unconscious mind of Tiamat. The weapons' twisted constraint logic imposes a tremendous mental burden on their users, ultimately causing them to contract the Flood Disease and mutate into the very Flood Creatures they are fighting.

When early Flood Weapon prototypes were first delivered to the Zigurrat Society ~900 years ago (~900 BP), The Flood Army did not exist yet; the military comprised standard adult and elderly personnel. When these adult soldiers attempted to wield the weapons, they died horrifyingly without understanding what killed them or why. 

After 400 years of tragic research trials, scientists stabilized **Constraint Logic** ~500 years ago (~500 BP / 1500 ADV). They discovered that the weapons' constraint logic strictly permits only youth aged 17 to 25 to bond with them due to neural plasticity. Upon confirming this through trial testing on young adults ~500 years ago, the Zigurrat Society formally established **The Flood Army** as a dedicated branch to wield Flood Weapons. Though indispensable for survival, the Flood Army receives no public recognition and its members are often viewed by protected citizens as mere tools or even enemies.

- The Admiral (Supreme Commander of The Preserver of Life): The enigmatic higher-ups of everything aboard the Ark to whom all three factions defer for major decisions. Revered deeply across generations, elders recall meeting him in their childhood, yet the Admiral himself genuinely appears not to know his origin or age when asked. Centuries of vessel documents bear his mark. While Eridu Capital's investigations into his identity only yielded further confusion, the Ur-Ilani Covenant warns "Don't try," and the Zigurrat Society pragmatically follows his commands. Recently, his growing closeness with Dr. Triad and Soraishi has made other factions fear he is leaning too far toward the military, heightening political tensions aboard the vessel.

- Soraishi (Chief Captain of the Flood Army): A kind leader deeply committed to her team's safety. Her Flood Weapon is named The Forgiver. Its ironic constraint logic dictates that it only wants to be used by a person who values souls, despite its inherent power to absorb and explode souls. Currently at Stage 3 / Phase 3 contamination (The Calling). Her assigned Threat Rank (Leviathan grade) reflects the catastrophic hazard level she would pose if she ever succumbed to Stage 5 / Phase 5 terminal transformation.
    
- Dr. Triad (Zigurrat Society Commander): A woman whose intense mentality earned her a seat of command. Her weapon, The Betrayer, has a twisted logic: it can only be used by a user who does not want to use it, suggesting a perpetual internal struggle during combat. Currently at Stage 4 / Phase 4 contamination (Physical Metamorphosis), she uses *The Betrayer* to sever her own physical mutations. Her Threat Rank (Leviathan grade) denotes her potential threat class upon full Stage 5 / Phase 5 transformation.
