The **Cult of Tiamat** is a radical faction operating outside the societal boundaries of *The Preserver of Life*. Unlike the rest of humanity, who view The Flood as an apocalyptic catastrophe and God Marduk's sealing of Tiamat as salvation, the Cult believes that the submerged world is the natural evolution of Ki.

### Philosophy & Worldview
- **The Blessing of Transformation**: The Cult rejects the notion that the ocean is polluted or contaminated. To them, the sea and Abyss Energy are sacred gifts of Tiamat designed to remake humanity into superior aquatic entities. Physical mutations—such as webbed digits, gills, and scaled skin—are celebrated as holy ascensions rather than afflictions.
- **Absence of The Calling**: Because the Flood Disease and its agonizing "Calling" phase stem from human psychological and bio-spiritual rejection of the ocean, Cult members—who embrace the sea and Tiamat completely without internal conflict—do not experience The Calling phase or traditional rejection sickness. 
- **Natural Order over Defiance**: They view humanity's technological resistance (such as *The Preserver of Life* and *Golden Apple* cultivation) as futile defiance against the primordial mother. True adaptation requires embracing the sea.
- **Immunity to Dehydration**: Through complete bio-spiritual acceptance and continuous communion with Tiamat's residual power, cult members process sea water directly without suffering dehydration or rejection, having completely shed reliance on the *Golden Apple* or *Golden Serum*.

### Capabilities & Tactics
- **Mastery of Flood Creatures**: Rather than fighting the beasts born of Abyss Energy, cultists swim alongside, ride, and command Flood Creatures. They utilize aquatic leviathans as mounts, beasts of war, and aquatic scouts.
- **Amphibious Mastery**: Exceptional swimmers capable of diving into extreme ocean depths without conventional submersibles or diving gear.

### Ultimate Goal
The Cult's primary objective is to recover all scattered and shattered fragments of the **Tablet of Destinies**—the ancient cosmic relic used by God Marduk 6,000 years ago during Enuma Elish to bind and seal Tiamat in the Deep Sea. The Cult believes that by assembling the shattered fragments, they can shatter Marduk's seal and fully release Tiamat to complete the world's aquatic transformation.
