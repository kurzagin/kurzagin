The **Flood Army** is a specialized military branch of the **Zigurrat Society** dedicated exclusively to fielding youth between the ages of 17 and 25 to wield **Flood Weapons**.

### Origin & Establishment

When research into salvaged Kraken cores began ~900 years ago, early weapon prototypes were developed without a clear understanding of their hidden constraint logic. At that time, the Flood Army did not exist, and the military personnel of the Zigurrat Society were typical adult and elderly soldiers. 

When these adult officers and veterans attempted to wield the prototype weapons, they suffered immediate, fatal rejections—dying without knowing what killed them or why. Subsequent research revealed that Flood Weapons could only be safely wielded by young minds aged 17 to 25. Once trial testing on youth confirmed this rule, the Zigurrat Society formally established **The Flood Army** to recruit and field this specific age group.

Though indispensable for humanity's survival, the Flood Army receives no public recognition, and its young members are often viewed by protected citizens as mere tools or even dangerous outsiders.
