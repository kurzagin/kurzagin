The **Zigurrat Society** is responsible for all military and defensive operations aboard *The Preserver of Life*.

### History & The Creation of The Flood Army

Approximately 900 years ago, when researchers first attempted to convert salvaged Kraken biological cores into weaponry, they handed early prototypes over to the Zigurrat Society for combat integration. At the time, the Zigurrat Society relied on standard adult and elderly military personnel. 

Without understanding the core's hidden constraint logic, the initial testing proved catastrophic: adult soldiers died horrifying deaths upon attempting to wield the weapons, without anyone understanding why. 

Once later research determined that Flood Weapons strictly require wielders aged **17 to 25**, trial testing on youth succeeded. The Zigurrat Society then established **The Flood Army** as a specialized branch within its command structure, specifically dedicated to deploying youth vanguard soldiers equipped with Flood Weapons.

### Relation to The Admiral

The Zigurrat Society maintains a strictly pragmatic attitude toward **The Admiral**. Unlike Eridu Capital's obsessive identity investigations or the Covenant's mystical taboos, the martial high command sees no point in probing his unknown origins. For the Zigurrat commanders, the equation is simple: as long as the Admiral possesses the authority to issue decisive strategic commands that keep the fleet safe, they obey without question.

Recently, the Admiral's close relationship with Commander **Dr. Triad** and Flood Army Chief Captain **Soraishi** has drawn the military leadership even closer to high command. While this grants the Zigurrat Society unprecedented executive backing, it has heightened friction with Eridu Capital and the Ur-Ilani Covenant, who view the alignment as dangerous military favoritism.
