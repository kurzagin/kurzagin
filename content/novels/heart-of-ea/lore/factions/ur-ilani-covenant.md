The custodians of history and knowledge, they also maintain a Marduk cult. Their deep understanding of The Flood earns them deference from the military ("They know more about what we don't"), yet their overall relationship with the other factions is strained.

### Key Leadership: High Priestess Geme-Marduk

The Covenant is led by 17-year-old High Priestess **Geme-Marduk**, a direct bloodline descendant of **Marduk** carrying the heritage of ancient Babylon. Despite her youth, her divine lineage grants her exclusive access to classified pre-Flood tablets, Tiamat sealing authorizations, and sacred astrolabes that no elder scholar can unlock.

### Relation to The Admiral

While the Covenant brings all major theological and historical decrees to **The Admiral** for supreme approval, they take a stern, almost taboo stance regarding his unknown identity. When questioned by Eridu researchers or inquisitive citizens about why the Admiral appears in ancient logs spanning centuries, Geme-Marduk and the Covenant offer a cryptic, absolute warning: *"Don't try."* Geme-Marduk firmly believes the Admiral is a fellow survivor of ancient Babylon.

Despite their reverence for his mandate, the Covenant's council is increasingly alarmed by the Admiral's frequent visits to Zigurrat Commander **Dr. Triad** and Flood Army Captain **Soraishi**. Whenever reports arrive of the Admiral spending hours at the military barracks, High Priestess Geme-Marduk grows visibly tense and quiet. While the High Council interprets her silent tension as a grave theological warning against military favoritism, in reality, Geme-Marduk is simply pouting and lonely, wondering why the Admiral visits the military deck so often but rarely visits her in the Grand Sanctuary.
