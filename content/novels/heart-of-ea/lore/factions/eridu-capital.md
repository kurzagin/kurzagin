The core operational hub and central citizenry aboard The Preserver of Life. They manage technology, farming, and daily sustenance, having been the original developers of the vessel. They are the most neutral faction, open to all ordinary citizens and young people seeking a faction.

Eridu Capital manages the distribution of the Golden Apple, a fruit gifted by the god Ea, which is humanity's sole natural source of food and hydration. Though simple to cultivate, the scarcity of land necessitates the use of Golden Serum, an injectable derivative, for efficient distribution.

Additionally, following centuries of dangerous research initiated ~900 years ago, Eridu Capital successfully stabilized Flood Weapons ~500 years ago, manufactured from biological materials salvaged from Kraken-grade Flood Creatures.

### Key Leadership & Relation to The Admiral

Under the technical leadership of 20-year-old prodigy **Dr. Vane** (Chief Scientist & Head of R&D), Eridu Capital strictly maintains high ethical standards regarding civil survival: while their historical research into biological cores and Flood Weapons pushed dangerous boundaries, Eridu maintains an unshakeable iron rule that **Golden Serum will never be commercialized or sold**.

As the administrative and scientific backbone of *The Preserver of Life*, Eridu Capital brings all major civil and resource decisions to **The Admiral** for supreme authorization. Over the centuries, Eridu's data ministries conducted exhaustive investigations to uncover the Admiral's true identity, though every attempt yielded contradictory results and dead ends. Today, Dr. Vane and Eridu administrators express growing unease regarding the Admiral's close relationship with Zigurrat Commander **Dr. Triad** and Flood Army Captain **Soraishi**, fearing that supreme leadership is leaning too heavily toward military priorities and destabilizing civil governance.
