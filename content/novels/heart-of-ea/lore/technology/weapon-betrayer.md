The Flood Weapon wielded by Dr. Triad (Zigurrat Society Commander).

The Betrayer has a twisted logic: it can only be used by a user who does not want to use it, suggesting a perpetual internal struggle during combat. Beyond combat, Dr. Triad uses *The Betrayer* for the agonizing task of severing her own Phase 4 Flood physical mutations as soon as they manifest—a process as painful as self-amputation—to maintain her human appearance beneath her bandages and oversized white cloak.
