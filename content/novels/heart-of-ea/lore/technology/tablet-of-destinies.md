# The Tablet of Destinies

**The Tablet of Destinies** is a primordial artifact of immense cosmic authority. During **Enuma Elish** (~6,000 BP), the tablet served as the ultimate weapon and key of binding utilized by **Marduk** and the **Nun-Gal-En-Ne (The Great Prince)** to subjugate **Tiamat** and seal her within the Deep Sea.

---

## ⚡ The Sealing & Shattering

To forge an inescapable binding upon Tiamat, Marduk channeled the divine authority of the Tablet of Destinies. Upon driving the seal into the ocean floor, the supreme strain of containing Tiamat's boundless malice caused the artifact to fracture into several shattered shards across Ki's abyssal depths.

---

## 🌊 The Cult of Tiamat's Objective

Operating outside *The Preserver of Life*, the fanatic **Cult of Tiamat** views Abyss Energy as a sacred baptismal gift. The Cult's primary objective is locating and recovering the scattered fragments of the Tablet of Destinies. 

They believe that by reversing the tablet's binding logic or destroying the remaining shards, Marduk's seal will collapse entirely, allowing Tiamat to awaken and reclaim the world of Ki.
