Following centuries of dangerous research initiated ~900 years ago, humanity successfully stabilized **Flood Weapons** (~500 years ago), manufactured from biological materials salvaged from Kraken-grade Flood Creature cores. However, the use of these crucial instruments is strictly restricted to individuals between the ages of 17 and 25.

### Research History & Fatal Discovery

When research into the Kraken core began ~900 years ago, researchers did not yet understand its true bio-spiritual nature or its connection to Tiamat's unconscious mind. Upon crafting early prototype weapons, they handed them over to the **Zigurrat Society** for military testing and defense. 

At that time, the Flood Army did not exist; the Zigurrat Society's military was composed of standard adult and elderly soldiers. When these adult personnel tried to wield and test the weapons, the rejection was swift and fatal—they died mysteriously without anyone understanding what had happened or why.

Through painstaking trial and continuous research, scientists eventually discovered that the core's hidden **Constraint Logic** strictly dictates that only young minds—specifically those aged **17 to 25**—possess the necessary neural plasticity to bond with the weapon without immediate brain death or neural collapse. Upon verifying this rule through trial testing on youth, the Zigurrat Society established **The Flood Army** as a dedicated branch to wield these weapons.

### Early Youngster Feedback & Weapon Consciousness

Centuries ago (during the early stabilization era ~500 years ago), the Eridu capital scientists received direct feedback from the early young wielders who were deployed with prototype weapons:
- **Perceived Consciousness & Eerie Presence**: Wielders reported feeling a tangible, eerie consciousness residing inside the weapons.
- **Weapon "Rules" & "Requirements"**: The youngsters noted that specific weapons possessed idiosyncratic "rules" or strict operational "requirements" that had to be met to activate or control them.

### Scientific Notes & Hypotheses

Based on this qualitative feedback from the early youth wielders, Eridu capital scientists formally classified and named this lingering consciousness phenomenon as **Tiamat's Echo**. Their findings include:
1. **Compact Flood Creatures**: Scientists noted that the Kraken consciousness remained deeply embedded within the weapon's forged biological form as part of **Tiamat's Echo**. In essence, a **Flood Weapon is a compact, weaponized version of a Flood Creature**.
2. **The Age Restriction Hypothesis**: While scientists successfully established the empirical 17–25 age boundary required for neural survival, they hypothesized that this age restriction itself might originate directly from **Tiamat's Echo** (her subconsciousness), though they remain uncertain of the exact mechanism.
3. **Role-Based Naming System (Eridu R&D Convention)**: Under R&D Chief Scientist **Dr. Vane**, Eridu Capital rejected sterile alphanumeric serial codes (e.g., `FW-004`) in favor of assigning each stabilized Flood Weapon a dramatic narrative **role** based on its constraint logic and psychological archetype—such as ***The Betrayer***, ***The Forgiver***, ***The Heretic***, ***The Bishop***, ***The Clown***, and ***The Executioner***. Though military pragmatists initially teased Vane for her fiction-inspired naming scheme, the role designations proved eerily accurate at mapping how each weapon's malevolent consciousness demands to be used.

### Nature and Risks

Flood Weapons are a necessary evil, yet they pose a severe internal threat. They possess their own malevolent consciousness, linked to the unconscious mind of Tiamat. The weapons' twisted constraint logic imposes a tremendous mental burden on their users, seeping abyssal corruption into their nervous system and triggering the dreaded [Flood Disease](/codex/bestiary/flood-disease). Over extended deployments, wielders experience extreme thirst, an irresistible calling toward the ocean, and progressive metamorphosis into the very Flood Creatures they are fighting.


### Constraint Logic: Inviolable Coherence and Fairness

While Flood Weapons impose bizarre, paradoxical, or seemingly insane psychological demands upon their wielders—forcing individuals into excruciating internal conflicts—their underlying mechanics are ruthlessly coherent and fair. 

Key characteristics of this covenant include:
- **Absolute Coherence**: Every weapon operates under rigid internal rules rooted in Tiamat's unconscious psyche. However surreal or punishing the prerequisite may seem, it functions as an unwavering law rather than erratic malice.
- **Inviolable Fairness**: If a wielder genuinely fulfills the weapon's prerequisites, the weapon faithfully unleashes its power without deceit or hidden penalties beyond the stated price.
- **Immutable Requirements**: Throughout recorded Eridu history and Zigurrat Society records, **no Flood Weapon has ever been recorded changing its requirements mid-term**. Once a weapon's constraint logic manifests and bonds, its terms never shift, move the goalposts, or arbitrarily demand new sacrifices mid-battle.
