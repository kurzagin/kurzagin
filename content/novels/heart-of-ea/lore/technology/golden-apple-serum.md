Eridu Capital manages the distribution of the Golden Apple, a fruit gifted by the god Ea, which is humanity's sole natural source of food and hydration.

Though simple to cultivate, the scarcity of land necessitates the use of Golden Serum, an injectable derivative, for efficient distribution.

Notably, while Golden Serum successfully sustains ordinary metabolic life, it is powerless against the unnatural, scorching thirst brought on by the [Flood Disease](/codex/bestiary/flood-disease). Infected individuals crave real, unadulterated water, yet with fresh water virtually extinct across the flooded world, victims suffer uncontrollable torment that drives them toward the toxic ocean.
