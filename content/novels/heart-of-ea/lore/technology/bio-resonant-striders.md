# Bio-Resonant Abyssal Striders

The **Bio-Resonant Abyssal Striders** (popularly known as **"Abyssal Striders"** or **"Kraken Treaders"**) are specialized bio-mechanical combat footwear developed through a joint initiative between **Eridu Capital** engineers and **Zigurrat Society** quartermasters. Engineered specifically for **The Flood Army**, the Striders allow wielders of [Flood Weapons](/codex/technology/flood-weapons) to stride, dash, and maneuver directly across the surface of Ki's toxic Abyssal ocean.

---

## 🔬 Development & Bio-Mechanical Synergy

Prior to the deployment of the Striders, Flood Army combat units were restricted to deployment bays, ship hulls, or buoyant pontoon barges—rendering them highly vulnerable to Leviathan-grade wave crests and deep-water ambush. 

Attempts to develop pure mechanical repulsors failed due to the erratic, hyper-dense fluidic physics of [Abyss Energy](/codex/bestiary/abyss-energy). Conversely, raw biological Kraken tissue proved unpredictable and seeped severe bio-spiritual corruption into soldiers' limbs.

The breakthrough came from merging both disciplines:

1. **Passive Bio-Buoyancy (Kraken Epidermal Sole)**:
   - The lower sole is lined with stabilized epidermal membrane harvested from low-tier Kraken cores.
   - Naturally tuned to Ki's ocean density, the membrane locks onto surface tension, allowing the wearer to stand and walk across abyssal fluid passively without expending external power.

2. **Active Polarity Harness (Eridu Micro-Stabilizer)**:
   - An integrated ankle-and-leg exoskeleton channels low-voltage electrical pulses through the sole.
   - When stimulated, the Eridu dampeners force the membrane to violently displace water underfoot, enabling instantaneous high-speed dashes, wave jumps, and evasive surface slides.
   - Crucially, the harness acts as a neural filter, suppressing [Tiamat's Echo](/codex/technology/flood-weapons#scientific-notes--hypotheses) within the tissue to protect the wearer from direct foot-to-fluid infection.

---

## ⚡ Combat Mechanics & Strategic Impact

The deployment of Bio-Resonant Striders drastically transformed ocean surface combat tactics for the Zigurrat Society:

* **Surface Mobility**: Enables 3D footwork across liquid battlefields—letting soldiers dodge incoming Kraken tentacle strikes, close distance for short-range Flood Weapon strikes, and leap across wave crests.
* **Environmental Shielding**: Keeps the wielder's body elevated above toxic ocean water, significantly reducing direct fluid contact and slowing the onset of [Flood Disease](/codex/bestiary/flood-disease).
* **Low Energy Footprint**: Passive flotation requires zero battery draw; energy cells are consumed only when triggering high-velocity **Surface Bursts**.

---

## ⚠️ Overclock Hazard & Contamination Risk

While the Striders provide unprecedented agility, they carry an operational risk:

* **Resonance Overload**: If a soldier repeatedly overclocks the Eridu harness for consecutive high-speed dashes, the dampening field briefly collapses.
* **Corrosive Leakage**: During overload windows, residual Abyssal energy from the Kraken sole leaks into the user's nervous system, causing immediate thermal feedback and accelerating their neurological contamination level.
