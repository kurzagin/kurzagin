The Flood Weapon wielded by Soraishi (Chief Captain of the Flood Army).

Its ironic constraint logic dictates that it only wants to be used by a person who values souls, despite its inherent power to absorb and explode souls.

## Soul Absorption & Execution Ritual

*The Forgiver* grows significantly more potent as it absorbs human and abyssal souls, converting their lingering essence into radiant energy. Because of this property, Flood Army soldiers undergoing terminal transformation (Stage 4/5 of the Flood Disease) choose to submit to Soraishi's blade rather than turn into mindless Flood Beasts. By being consumed by *The Forgiver*, their souls become fuel for their captain, allowing them to protect their remaining squadmates even after death.

The execution occurs in total silence on isolated outer decks—no words spoken, with *The Forgiver* already unsheathed as the doomed soldier steps forward.
