# The Preserver of Life

**The Preserver of Life** is the colossal sanctuary vessel harboring the remaining fragments of human civilization. Constructed over a two-thousand-year mobilization effort during **The Creeping Tide** (~6,000 to ~4,000 BP), the vessel serves as humanity's floating metropolis, fortress, and holy refuge amidst the toxic Abyssal oceans of Ki.

---

## 🏗️ Structural Architecture & Decks

Spanning tens of kilometers in length and height, *The Preserver of Life* is divided into three distinct operational domains:

1. **Eridu Capital (The Nucleus Core)**:
   - The central urban, agricultural, and civic hub.
   - Houses the sacred **Golden Apple Gardens** and the pharmaceutical complexes synthesizing **Golden Serum**.
   - Governed by civilian administrators, engineers, and agronomists.

2. **Zigurrat Society (The Perimeter Shield)**:
   - The heavily fortified outer hull, battle decks, and military command towers.
   - Operates defensive barrier systems, kinetic point-defense batteries, and deployment bays for the **Flood Army**.

3. **Ur-Ilani Covenant (The High Sanctum)**:
   - The cloistered, highly restricted upper towers and archival vaults.
   - Maintained by monastic scholars preserving pre-Flood historical records, astronomical data, and Marduk veneration shrines.

---

## 🛡️ Life Support & Defense Systems

- **Hydro-Desalination & Golden Sustenance**: Because upper ocean waters are saturated with toxic Abyss Energy, traditional water purification is impossible on a global scale. Humanity relies on **Golden Serum** derived from the Golden Apple trees to sustain life and maintain hydration without ocean reliance.
- **Barrier Containment**: Divine barrier wards maintained by the Ur-Ilani Covenant defend the hull from physical impact by Kraken-tier threats, while Flood Army vanguard units deploy externally to suppress aquatic swarms. If a Leviathan grade attacks the Ark directly, Commander Dr. Triad (ex-Flood Army, the strongest wielder) steps out to engage and hold off the titan.
