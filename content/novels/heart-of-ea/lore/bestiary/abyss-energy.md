# Abyss Energy & Primordial Contamination

**Abyss Energy** is the term designated by modern Eridu scientists to describe the unrefined, hyper-dense divine radiance radiating from the imprisoned Primordial Goddess **Tiamat**. 

---

## 🌊 Origin & The Creeping Tide

Following the conclusion of **Enuma Elish** 6,000 years ago (~6,000 BP), Marduk and the **Nun-Gal-En-Ne (The Great Prince)** bound Tiamat within the deepest trenches of Ki's abyssal oceans. However, the divine seal was imperfect. 

Over centuries, Tiamat's unconscious malice began to bleed into the surrounding waters as **Abyss Energy**. This persistent contamination triggered two sequential planetary disasters:

1. **The Creeping Tide (~6,000 to ~4,000 BP)**: A multi-century expansion of planetary water volume, drowning 90% of Ki's landmass over two millennia.
2. **Marine Metamorphosis (~4,000 BP)**: Saturation reaching critical density, causing natural sea life to mutate into hostile **Flood Creatures**.

---

## 🔬 Biological Impact & Mutations

Abyss Energy alters organic life at the cellular and spiritual level:

- **Kraken & Leviathan Core Synthesis**: As marine organisms absorb Abyssal density over centuries, their vital organs compress into dense biological energy cores capable of hyper-regeneration.
- **Flood Disease**: Humans exposed to unshielded Abyss Energy or bonded with Flood Weapons suffer progressive neural decay, spiritual erosion, and eventually physical metamorphosis into monstrous sea entities.

---

## ⚡ Energy & Weapon Integration

Despite its lethality, Abyss Energy is the sole catalyst capable of destroying high-tier Flood Creatures. **Flood Weapons** utilize harvested Kraken cores to channel Abyssal force against mutated horrors, imposing a heavy psychological **Constraint Logic** upon their wielders.
