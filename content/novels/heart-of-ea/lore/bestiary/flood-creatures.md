Monstrous entities created when leaking Abyss Energy from Tiamat's imperfect containment in the Deep Sea contaminated all upper waters and transformed almost all aquatic life into monstrous Flood Creatures.

Materials salvaged from Flood Creatures are used to manufacture Flood Weapons. When wielding Flood Weapons, or through direct contact with toxic ocean water, humans can contract the insidious [Flood Disease](/codex/bestiary/flood-disease). The weapon's twisted constraint logic and abyssal resonance place an immense mental burden on their users, ultimately causing victims to experience uncontrollable ocean longing and mutate into the very Flood Creatures they are fighting.

## Threat Classifications & Grades

Eridu tactical doctrines and Zigurrat defense archives classify Flood Creatures into three distinct grades based on abyssal density, scale, and combat lethality:

### 1. Swarm Grade
- **Profile**: Small in individual physical stature, but appearing in relentless, overwhelming multitudes.
- **Behavior & Threat**: Exceptionally hostile, vicious, and frantic. Swarm-grade entities exhibit hyper-aggressive predatory instincts, swarming Ark drainage pipes, outer hull plating, and scouting craft.
- **Tactical Assessment**: Due to their sheer numbers, sudden ambushes, and feral hostility, Swarm grades are extremely hazardous in confined or exposed settings despite their small mass.

### 2. Kraken Grade
- **Profile**: Medium-sized oceanic abominations with reinforced chitin, dense muscular mass, and sprawling abyssal tendrils.
- **Behavior & Threat**: Noticeably sluggish in movement and lacking burst speed compared to the frantic Swarm grade.
- **Tactical Assessment**: Individual Kraken-grade entities are easier to anticipate and defeat than an unrelenting swarm, provided long-range suppression is maintained.
- **Flood Weapon Source**: The biological cores, neural conduits, and resilient organic material of Kraken-grade specimens serve as the primary raw source material for forging [Flood Weapons](/codex/technology/flood-weapons). Their dense abyssal resonance provides the necessary conduit to Tiamat's unconscious mind while remaining stable enough for human neural synchronization.

### 3. Leviathan Grade
- **Profile**: Colossal, planet-scale abyssal titans of legendary proportions dwelling within the darkest oceanic trenches.
- **Behavior & Threat**: Ancient, catastrophic world-shapers. They do not merely hunt—their sheer passage creates tidal shifts and seismic abyssal disturbances.
- **Tactical Assessment**: **Standard forces cannot conquer or slay a Leviathan.** No conventional armament, defensive battery, or standard Flood Weapon squad can defeat them. When a Leviathan surfaces, standard doctrine dictates retreat. However, **Dr. Triad**—the strongest wielder among the military (ex-Flood Army, currently Zigurrat Commander)—is the sole exception capable of directly engaging a Leviathan to defend *The Preserver of Life* if it attacks the Ark, though even she does not slay the titan.
