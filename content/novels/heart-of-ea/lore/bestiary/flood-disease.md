**The Flood Disease** (also referred to by Ark medical officers as *The Deep Sickness*, *Abyssal Contagion*, or *The Calling*) is an insidious biological and psycho-spiritual affliction affecting humans aboard the Preserver of Life. Born of the leaking Abyss Energy from Tiamat's degraded containment seal in the Deep Sea, it breaks down the barrier between human consciousness and the primordial ocean.

> [!NOTE]
> **Root Cause & Ocean Rejection:** The affliction and its agonizing "Calling" phase stem from human psychological and bio-spiritual rejection of the abyssal ocean—the subconscious attempt of human minds to resist and push back against abyssal resonance while carrying internal conflict. Conversely, fanatics like the **Cult of Tiamat**, who accept the ocean completely without internal resistance or rejection, do not experience "The Calling" phase or standard rejection sickness.

---

## Vectors of Infection & Acceleration Factors

The contagion does not spread through typical airborne bacteria; rather, it is contracted through direct exposure to Abyss Energy and oceanic contamination:

1. **Direct Contact with the Ocean**: Touching raw ocean water or coming into contact with contaminated sea spray along the lower hull and maintenance ports.
2. **Submersion & Swimming**: Entering or falling into the toxic, heavily polluted sea yields near-instantaneous contamination at cellular and neural levels.
3. **Wielding Flood Weapons**: Because Flood Weapons are forged from salvaged Flood Creature remains and bound to Tiamat's unconscious mind, their twisted constraint logic places immense mental strain on the wielder, slowly seeping Abyssal resonance directly into the nervous system.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Cumulative Acceleration Rate:** Stage progression is not fixed. Repeated or prolonged exposure to contaminated water over time—or a single incident of excessive, high-density saturation—drastically accelerates the speed of infection. While a lightly exposed crew member may take months to progress from Stage 1 to Stage 3, a soldier subjected to frequent sea-spray during frontline combat or submerged during lower-hull breaches will skip dormant phases and rapidly deteriorate into Stage 4 metamorphosis within cycles or hours.

### Symptom Progression & Stages / Phases

The disease progresses through distinct clinical and behavioral stages (also designated as Phases in military records). Progression speed varies heavily based on cumulative contamination load; higher exposure exponentially reduces the time window between stages. Due to severe shortages of fresh water aboard the Ark, early symptoms are tragically common and difficult to quarantine before irreversible damage occurs.

### Stage 1 / Phase 1: The Dormant Onset (Unnoticed Contamination)
- **Manifestation**: In the initial period, infected individuals are completely oblivious to their condition.
- **Diagnostics**: Vital signs appear stable; standard Ark blood work registers only trace galvanic fluctuations easily mistaken for common exhaustion or baseline radiation from the lower decks.
- **Behavior**: Patients continue their daily shifts aboard Eridu Capital, Zigurrat Society posts, or the Flood Army vanguard without detecting anything amiss.

### Stage 2 / Phase 2: The Scorching Thirst & Desalination Craving
- **Manifestation**: Victims develop a sudden, agonizing, and unquenchable internal burning thirst.
- **Treatment Inefficacy**: Standard rations of **Golden Serum** (derived from Ea's Golden Apple) fail to alleviate the parched throat. While Golden Serum sustains metabolic nourishment, it cannot quench the metaphysical "dryness" eating at the patient's cells.
- **Desperation**: Because clean, drinkable fresh water is a scarce commodity aboard the Preserver of Life, victims try desperately to hold back their agony. The burning sensation mounts until conventional logic begins to fray.

### Stage 3 / Phase 3: The Calling (The Deck Trance & Ocean Longing)
- **Manifestation**: The disease targets the victim's subconscious mind, synchronizing their brainwaves with the abyssal hum of Tiamat.
- **Behavioral Trance**: Without consciously realizing what they are doing, victims sleepwalk or drift toward the outer decks and observation balconies of the Ark.
- **The Longing**: Standing at the railing, they stare down into the pitch-black waves, feeling an overwhelming, magnetic pull. They describe the polluted deep sea not as a toxic grave, but as a warm, welcoming cradle calling them "home."
- **The Leap**: Many afflicted crew members and citizens lose their struggle with the call; numerous individuals succumb to the trance and leap into the dark ocean below before security or comrades can intervene.

### Stage 4 / Phase 4: Physical Metamorphosis (Aquatic Transmutation)
For those individuals restrained, confined, or possessing sufficient willpower to resist hurling themselves off the Ark's deck, the disease mutates the physical body to adapt to the abyssal habitat:
- **Dermal Alterations**: The skin loses melanin, turning mottled slate-gray or luminescent pale blue, while drying out into hardened scales.
- **Appendages**: Interdigital webbing forms between the fingers and toes; fine cartilage fins pierce through forearms, calves, and along the spine.
- **Respiratory & Ocular Shifts**: The eyes cloud into nictitating, light-sensitive black lenses; vestigial gill slits split along the neck.

### Stage 5 / Phase 5: Full Transmutation (Flood Creature Emergence)
- **Terminal Metamorphosis**: Complete dissolution of human identity and anatomy. The victim's skeleton and musculature warp beyond recognition, yielding a fully transformed, predatory **Flood Creature**.
- **Loss of Self**: All memories, emotional ties, and human consciousness are overwritten by the hive-mind resonance of the Deep Sea. Once this stage is reached, containment or termination by the Flood Army is the only protocol. Transformed individuals present extreme hazard classifications, up to Leviathan grade depending on Abyssal saturation.

## Ark Protocols & Confinement Measures

1. **Deck Patrol Curfew**: The Zigurrat Society maintains 24-cycle guard patrols along open-air catwalks and observation gantries to apprehend crew members exhibiting the "Ocean Stare."
2. **Quarantine Ward 0**: Any citizen or soldier exhibiting unresponsive gaze, excessive Golden Serum intolerance, or cutaneous webbing is immediately isolated under high-level restraint.
3. **Flood Army Monitoring**: Because Flood Army soldiers (ages 17–25) wield Flood Weapons continuously, commanders like Dr. Triad and Soraishi regularly inspect their units for early tremors, unquenchable thirst, and involuntary deck wanderings.
