Following the conclusion of Enuma Elish, leaking Abyss Energy caused a multi-century global inundation known as **The Creeping Tide**. 

Unlike a sudden cataclysm, the oceans rose progressively across two millennia (~6,000 to ~4,000 BP), slowly drowning coastlines and major inland landmasses. 

Because the deluge occurred over centuries rather than overnight, humanity possessed the time and foresight to pool global engineering knowledge. Led by the ancestors of Eridu Capital, nations unified to construct **The Preserver of Life**—a colossal self-sustaining city-vessel engineered as humanity's final refuge against total submergence.
