The cradle world of Ki was forever altered by a cataclysmic divine conflict that occurred 6,000 years ago, known universally as **Enuma Elish**.

The conflict pitted the **Nun-Gal-En-Ne (The Great Prince)**, commanded by Marduk, against the Primordial Gods, led by Tiamat. Though Marduk ultimately defeated Tiamat and sealed her in the Deep Sea using the Tablet of Destinies, the containment seal was imperfect. 

Her leaking divine malice—dubbed **Abyss Energy**—began to slowly contaminate the oceanic realm. The victorious gods subsequently vanished, their physical forms sacrificed to forge and maintain the seals, bringing the Mythical Age to a definitive close 6,000 years ago.
