Over millennia of continuous leakage from Tiamat's deep seal, Abyssal density in the ocean intensified exponentially. Flood Creatures mutated into hyper-dense, colossal Kraken and Leviathan grades capable of instant regeneration, rendering conventional kinetic weapons completely obsolete.

As divine sanctuary shields began to fail, humanity faced extinction. Approximately 900 years ago (~900 BP), **Eridu Capital** researchers initiated dangerous experimentation to reverse-engineer salvaged Kraken biological cores, though they did not yet comprehend the core's underlying consciousness or constraint logic. 

Early weapon prototypes were created and handed over to the **Zigurrat Society** for military testing and defensive deployment. At that time, The Flood Army did not exist, and the Zigurrat Society's military forces comprised standard adult and elderly personnel. When these adult soldiers attempted to test and wield the weapons, the bio-spiritual rejection was absolute: they died horribly without anyone knowing what had happened or why.

After centuries of tragic trials and continuous research, scientists stabilized **Constraint Logic** around 500 years ago (~500 BP). They discovered that the core strictly requires young minds aged **17 to 25** who possess the necessary neural plasticity to bond with the weapon without immediate brain death. 

Upon confirming this rule through trial testing on youth, the **Zigurrat Society** formally established **The Flood Army** as a specialized branch within its military structure, deploying youth vanguards to wield Flood Weapons and ensure humanity's survival.
