Around 4,000 years ago (~4,000 BP), two thousand years after Tiamat was sealed, oceanic Abyssal energy reached a critical contamination threshold. 

The toxic pollution triggered mass genetic and spiritual mutations throughout marine ecosystems, transforming ordinary sea life into the first predatory **Flood Creatures**.

During this initial era, humanity successfully defended *The Preserver of Life* using conventional weaponry—heavy kinetic artillery, naval batteries, electrified hull plating, and fading divine sanctuary barriers left behind by Ea and Marduk. Because early Flood Creatures were lower-tier specimens, conventional kinetic ordnance was sufficient to protect the fleet.
