Chief Captain of the Flood Army. A kind leader deeply committed to her team's safety.

- **Threat Rank:** **Leviathan grade** (Projected hazard class if fully transformed upon reaching Stage 5 / Phase 5 terminal metamorphosis)
- **Transformation Phase:** **Phase 3 / Stage 3** (The Calling / Deep Abyssal Synchronization)

Her Flood Weapon is named **The Forgiver**. Its ironic constraint logic dictates that it only wants to be used by a person who values souls, despite its inherent power to absorb and explode souls.

## Personality & Archetype

- **Archetype:** Earnest, compassionate young leader (Amiya-like). Wholly trusting of the Admiral and deeply committed to the survival and morale of the Ark's crew.
- **Combat Demeanor:** Resolute and self-sacrificing. She treats combat as a solemn duty rather than a thrill, taking full moral responsibility for unleashing souls through *The Forgiver*.

## Lore: The Silent Executioner

Beyond her role as Chief Captain on the battlefield, Soraishi bears a heavy, agonizing secret burden known only within the ranks of the Flood Army: she is the designated executioner for her own comrades.

### The Grim Logic
When Flood Army soldiers reach the terminal stages of the Flood Disease (Stage 4 and beyond), their physical metamorphosis into mindless, predatory Flood Beasts is inevitable. To the soldiers, turning into monsters that threaten the Ark and their surviving squadmates is an unacceptable tragedy. However, because *The Forgiver* absorbs and converts souls into raw power for Soraishi, her comrades derived a cold, pragmatic logic: if their death is unavoidable, dying by Soraishi's blade allows their souls to fuel *The Forgiver*, making their captain stronger so she can continue protecting the fleet. To them, it is the last meaningful act of service they can offer.

### The Unspoken Ritual
The execution follows an unspoken protocol born of mutual respect and silent grief:
- **Silent Recognition:** After a battle, when a soldier has clearly crossed the threshold of severe contagion—skin scaling, nictitating eyes, or the abyssal trance—no one mentions it. There are no dramatic goodbyes, no formal declarations, and no pitying glances. The squad acts as though nothing has changed, preserving the dying soldier's dignity.
- **The Solitary Deck:** Late into the cycle, Soraishi walks to an isolated outer deck alone. She unsheathes *The Forgiver* in advance and stands waiting in the ocean mist.
- **No Final Words:** The doomed soldier arrives on the deck alone. Neither Soraishi nor the soldier speaks a single word—no final wishes, no apologies, no comfort. Words would only shatter the fragile composure holding them together.
- **The Strike & Absorb:** In absolute silence, Soraishi delivers the final blow. *The Forgiver* drinks the comrade's soul, integrating their strength into the weapon while leaving Soraishi to carry the crushing weight of their memory alone.
