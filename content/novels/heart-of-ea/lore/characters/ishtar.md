# Ishtar — Goddess of Love, Beauty, and War

**Ishtar** (also known as *Inanna*) is the ancient Mesopotamian deity of love, beauty, passion, war, and fertility. In the Mythical Age, Uruk served as the central seat of her divine worship and power.

---

## 🎨 The Divine Bestowal of "Decorations"

During the Mythical Age prior to **Enuma Elish** (~6,000 BP), Ishtar observed baseline humanity and expressed disdain for their visual monotony. Declaring that the original creator gods lacked artistic flair and imagination, Ishtar decided to "decorate" the citizenry of her beloved Uruk.

Out of divine vanity and a desire to see her subjects embody vibrant, captivating aesthetics, she endowed the people of Uruk with living animal traits—most notably **animal ears and tails** across a wide variety of animal motifs (felines, canines, foxes, bovines, and birds).

---

## 🏛️ Legacy and Modern Heritage

Though Ishtar vanished alongside the rest of the pantheon after sealing Tiamat at the conclusion of the Mythical Age, her aesthetic gift endured through generations:

- Her blessed descendants are known as **Ishtar's People** or **Uruk Descendants** (colloquially referred to as *Beastfolk*).
- While Ishtar's original intent was purely cosmetic, her descendants over centuries adapted their bodies to harness these physical traits, transforming decorative ears and tails into heightened physical instincts, kinetic balance, and spatial reflexes.
- In modern **2000 ADV**, shrines and ancient records preserved by the **Ur-Ilani Covenant** honor Ishtar's aesthetic flair, while her descendants serve with distinction across all operational branches of *The Preserver of Life*.
