Zigurrat Society Commander & Veteran Vanguard. An ex-Flood Army member who remains active in military defense, Dr. Triad is recognized as the single strongest wielder among the Ark's forces.

- **Threat Rank:** **Leviathan grade** (Projected hazard class if fully transformed upon reaching Stage 5 / Phase 5 terminal metamorphosis)
- **Transformation Phase:** **Phase 4 / Stage 4** (Physical Metamorphosis / Abyssal Greatsword Resonance)
- **Role & Combat Exception:** As the strongest active wielder, Dr. Triad is the sole defender capable of directly engaging Leviathan-grade titans if they attack *The Preserver of Life*. While Leviathans cannot be slain, she holds the line and repels their assault to protect the vessel.

Her weapon, **The Betrayer**, has a twisted logic: it can only be used by a user who does not want to use it, suggesting a perpetual internal struggle during combat.

## Physical Metamorphosis & Concealment

Despite being classified at Phase 4 contamination, Dr. Triad displays no visible mutations. In reality, whenever a physical transformation attempts to manifest (such as monstrous scaling or mutated appendages), she relentlessly cuts off every visible physical transformation herself using **The Betrayer**.

Because the Flood transformation is linked to her body as living tissue, severing it inflicts agony identical to self-amputating her own limbs. To hide the raw wounds and remaining scars from her crew and the Admiral, she keeps her body tightly wrapped in bandages beneath her oversized white cloak. This ritual of self-mutilation allows her to maintain a human facade and function normally, masking her excruciating reality behind her playful, unbothered demeanor.

## Personality & Archetype

- **Archetype:** The overwhelmingly powerful, unbothered older sister ("Onee-san"). Speaks casually and playfully, effortlessly swinging *The Betrayer* with one hand.
- **Combat Demeanor:** Nonchalant to the point of teasing Leviathan-class horrors, yet fiercely protective of the Admiral and younger comrades. While she does not kill Leviathans, she steps into the tide without hesitation whenever the Ark is threatened.
