# Ea — The Beneficent Father

**Ea** (also known as *Enki*) is the ancient deity of wisdom, freshwater, and creation within the Eridu pantheon. He is revered as the father of **Marduk**, supreme commander of the **Nun-Gal-En-Ne (The Great Prince)** during Enuma Elish.

---

## 🍎 The Gift of the Golden Apple

Long before the outbreak of **Enuma Elish** (~6,000 BP) and the ensuing catastrophe of **The Flood**, Ea looked upon mortal humanity with profound compassion. Recognizing their physical vulnerability in a world of volatile primordial forces, Ea bestowed upon early human settlers a divine blessing: the sacred seed of the **Golden Apple**.

This miraculous flora possessed the unique capacity to distill raw terrestrial vitality into pure, nourishing sustenance and life-sustaining hydration. 

---

## 🏛️ Legacy Aboard The Preserver of Life

Though Ea vanished alongside the other gods following the sealing of Tiamat at the end of the Mythical Age, his gift remains humanity's sole salvation:

- **Eridu Capital** cultivates the descendants of Ea's original Golden Apple trees within the central hydroponic domes of *The Preserver of Life*.
- The **Golden Serum** synthesized from these fruits forms the foundation of human survival, shielding citizens from the toxic, Abyssal-polluted waters outside the vessel.
- The **Ur-Ilani Covenant** maintains sacred shrines dedicated to Ea, honoring him as the true Architect of Human Preservation.
