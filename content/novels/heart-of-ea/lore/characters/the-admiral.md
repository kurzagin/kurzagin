# The Admiral — Supreme Authority of The Preserver of Life

**The Admiral** is the undisputed supreme authority aboard *The Preserver of Life*. Standing above all political divisions, he is the final arbiter to whom all three major factions—**Eridu Capital**, the **Zigurrat Society**, and the **Ur-Ilani Covenant**—bring their most critical decisions, territorial disputes, and strategic crises. 

Despite holding absolute command over humanity's final sanctuary vessel for as long as written history records, no living soul knows who he actually is or where he came from. Yet, he is deeply and universally respected by citizens of all walks of life.

---

## ⌛ The Generational Paradox

One of the most bewildering phenomena surrounding the Admiral is his ubiquitous presence across time:

- **Collective Memory:** If you speak to any elder aboard the ship—grandmothers and grandfathers from the oldest residential sectors—they will invariably recall meeting the Admiral in person when they were young children, looking exactly as he does today.
- **Centuries of Documentation:** Shipboard charters, judicial verdicts, and vessel construction logs spanning centuries all bear his personal seal and signature. 
- **Genuine Ignorance:** When directly questioned about his longevity, origin, or identity, the Admiral displays no evasiveness or malice. Instead, he appears genuinely unaware, answering with sincere confusion as if he honestly does not know how long he has lived or why his mark appears on centuries of archives.

## 🏛️ Faction Perspectives & Institutional Reactions

The three major factions hold wildly divergent attitudes toward the Admiral's mysterious nature:

| Faction | Official Stance | Operational Mindset |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eridu Capital** | *Obsessive Investigation* | Eridu scientists and archivists have repeatedly attempted to uncover his biological lineage, medical records, and historical origins. Every investigation has yielded dead ends, contradictory data, and deeper confusion. |
| **Ur-Ilani Covenant** | *Solemn Warning* | The Covenant's archivists and Marduk cultists refuse to investigate, offering a blunt, universal directive to anyone who asks: *"Don't try."* |
| **Zigurrat Society** | *Pragmatic Obedience* | The military high command takes a purely functional stance. As long as the Admiral provides clear leadership and tactical commands that keep the vessel afloat, his true identity is irrelevant—they follow his orders implicitly. |

---

## ⚔️ Mounting Political Tensions

In recent cycles, a noticeable shift in the Admiral's personal routine has unsettled the Ark's political delicate balance:

- **Closeness with the Military:** The Admiral has grown noticeably close to **Dr. Triad** (Commander of the Zigurrat Society) and **Soraishi** (Chief Captain of the Flood Army). Whether reviewing patrol reports on the bridge or spending quiet hours on the outer decks, he is frequently seen in their company.
- **Factional Friction:** This deepening bond has fueled growing suspicion among **Eridu Capital** and the **Ur-Ilani Covenant**. The non-military factions perceive the Admiral as becoming overly supportive of the Zigurrat Society and the Flood Army, fearing a potential tilt toward martial dominance. This perceived bias has severely strained inter-factional relations and tensed up the political climate across *The Preserver of Life*.
