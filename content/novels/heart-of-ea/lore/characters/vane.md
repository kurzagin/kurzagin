**Dr. Vane** is the Chief Scientist and head of the Research & Development Division within **Eridu Capital**. Despite being only 20 years old, her sheer scientific brilliance elevated her to the highest technical seat in Eridu, granting her authority over Kraken core stabilization, Flood Weapon research, and Golden Serum refinement aboard *The Preserver of Life*.

- **Role & Specialization:** Chief Scientist of Eridu Capital's R&D Division. Oversees bio-engineering, Abyssal core containment, and life-support logistics.
- **Contamination Status:** **Phase 0 / Clean** (Enforces mandatory daily biometric decontamination protocols for herself and her entire lab staff to prevent Abyssal contagion).

---

## Personality & Archetype

- **Archetype:** Brilliant, hot-tempered Tsundere Technocrat. She takes immense pride in Eridu's technological superiority and works herself to exhaustion, yet blushes instantly whenever the Admiral drops by her lab unannounced.
- **Secret Fiction & Light Novel Addict:** Beneath her crisp lab coat and authoritative technocrat persona, Vane is an unapologetic, secret addict of fantasy light novels and serialized web fiction. She stays up until the crack of dawn reading melodrama, romance-fantasy tropes, and grand heroic sagas hidden behind stacks of biometric logs. When caught reading by the Admiral, she frantically claims she is conducting "deep research into human narrative psychology under high-stress conditions."
- **Dramatic Role Naming for Flood Weapons:** Vane passionately refuses to give Flood Weapons sterile alphanumeric serial numbers like `FW-009`. Instead, she insists on giving every stabilized weapon a dramatic narrative role—such as ***The Betrayer***, ***The Forgiver***, ***The Heretic***, ***The Bishop***, ***The Clown***, or ***The Executioner***—arguing that a biological weapon's constraint logic directly reflects a tragic character archetype in a story. She will get aggressively defensive and spout light novel tropes if anyone questions her naming system.
- **Ethical Iron Line:** While Eridu Capital's historical research on biological cores and Abyssal materials is notorious for pushing dangerous boundaries, Vane maintains a fierce, unshakeable moral code regarding basic survival resources: **Golden Serum is never to be commercialized or sold**.
- **Relationship with The Admiral:** Deeply fascinated by the Admiral both scientifically and personally. She frequently invents "mandatory tech check-ups" or "calibrations" as excuses to get the Admiral to visit her workshop. When she catches the Admiral spending time with Dr. Triad or Soraishi, her competitive, flustered side explodes into sharp tsundere protests.
