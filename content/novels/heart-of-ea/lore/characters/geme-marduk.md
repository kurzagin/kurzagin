**Geme-Marduk** is the High Priestess of the **Ur-Ilani Covenant** and the supreme spiritual authority aboard *The Preserver of Life*. Despite being only 17 years old, she stands at the apex of the Covenant due to her holy lineage as a direct bloodline descendant of **Marduk**, carrying the divine heritage of ancient Babylon.

- **Role & Specialization:** High Priestess & Arch-Keeper of the Celestial Archives. Holds exclusive authorization over classified pre-Flood tablets, Tiamat sealing rituals, and Abyssal depth history.
- **Divine Blessing:** As a direct descendant of Marduk, her bloodline grants her natural immunity to passive Abyssal contamination (Phase 0) and the unique ability to activate ancient divine artifacts like the *Astrolabe of Marduk*.

---

## Personality & Archetype

- **Archetype:** Solemn, regal High Priestess with a secret, lonely heart. Externally, she speaks with absolute dignity, grace, and ancient authority, commanding respect from elder scholars and military generals alike.
- **Belief Regarding The Admiral:** She firmly believes that **The Admiral** is a fellow survivor of ancient Babylon, linked to the divine mandate of Ea and Marduk. Because of this, she holds immense, near-sacred reverence for him.
- **The Grand Political Misunderstanding:**
  - Whenever the High Council reports that the Admiral is spending time at the Zigurrat Society barracks with **Dr. Triad** or **Soraishi**, Geme-Marduk becomes noticeably stiff, silent, and tense.
  - **Public Interpretation:** The Covenant scholars, Eridu technocrats, and military commanders interpret her stern tension as a solemn theological protest, believing she is warning the ship that *"the Admiral's military favoritism is angering the heavens."*
  - **The Reality:** In truth, her internal reaction is purely personal and sweet—she is internally sulking and lonely, thinking: *"The Admiral went to drink tea with Dr. Triad again today... Why does he visit the military deck every afternoon, but never comes up to the Grand Sanctuary to talk with me...?"*
