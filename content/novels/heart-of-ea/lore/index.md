# Ki & The Preserver of Life: Master Overview

The world of Ki remains scarred by The Flood, a devastating divine conflict that occurred 6,000 years ago, known as **Enuma Elish**. The result is a planet almost entirely submerged under a toxic, heavily polluted ocean, making drinkable water a commodity of extreme scarcity.

### The Catastrophe: The Flood and Abyss Energy

The conflict of Enuma Elish was fought between the Nun-Gal-En-Ne (The Great Prince), led by Marduk, and the Primordial Gods, commanded by Tiamat. Though Tiamat was defeated and sealed in the Deep Sea, her containment was imperfect. Her leaking power, dubbed Abyss Energy, contaminated all upper waters and triggered two sequential calamities:

- **The Creeping Tide (~6,000 to ~4,000 BP)**: A multi-century, progressive rise in ocean levels that submerged coastlines and eventually drowned 90% of Ki's landmass over two millennia. This gradual deluge allowed humanity time to mobilize resources and construct their ultimate sanctuary vessel, *The Preserver of Life*.
- **Marine Metamorphosis (~4,000 BP)**: As ocean contamination reached a critical saturation point around 4,000 years ago, marine life underwent deep genetic and Abyssal mutations, giving birth to the first monstrous Flood Creatures.

The victorious gods vanished after the war, their existence sacrificed to forge the seals that bound Tiamat. This act brought the undated Mythical Age to a definitive close. All post-war history is measured under the current calendar era, **ADV** (After Deluge/War), with present day standing at 2,000 ADV.

### Humanity's Final Bastion: The Preserver of Life

Humanity's last hope rests upon The Preserver of Life, a colossal vessel designed as the ultimate sanctuary against the Flood Creatures. 

Above all operational bodies stands **The Admiral**, the enigmatic supreme authority of the Ark to whom all three factions bring major decisions. Though deeply revered across generations—with elderly citizens recalling meeting him in their youth while centuries of archives bear his official mark—the Admiral genuinely appears unaware of his own origins or age when questioned. 

The ship is home to three interconnected, yet increasingly conflicted, internal factions:

1. **Eridu Capital**: The core operational hub and central citizenry. They manage technology, farming, and daily sustenance, having been the original developers of the vessel. They are the most neutral faction, open to all ordinary citizens and young people seeking a faction. Eridu Capital launched extensive archival and biometric inquiries into the Admiral's true identity, but their research only yielded deeper confusion.
2. **Zigurrat Society**: Responsible for all military and defensive operations aboard the vessel. Pragmatic in their approach, the Zigurrat command follows the Admiral's strategic directives without questioning his origins.
3. **Ur-Ilani Covenant**: The custodians of history and knowledge, they also maintain a Marduk cult. Their deep understanding of The Flood earns them deference from the military ("They know more about what we don't"), yet their overall relationship with the other factions is strained. When asked about the Admiral's identity, the Covenant issued a stark directive: *"Don't try."*

*Associated Military Branch & External Faction:*
- **The Flood Army**: A specialized military branch of the Zigurrat Society dedicated exclusively to youth aged 17 to 25 wielding Flood Weapons.
- **Cult of Tiamat**: A fanatic faction operating outside the vessel in the open ocean. They view The Flood and Abyss Energy as a divine blessing and transformation, ride and control Flood Creatures with no dehydration issues, and actively seek the shattered fragments of the **Tablet of Destinies** to release Tiamat from Marduk's seal.

Recent cycles have seen growing friction between the factions: the Admiral's increasingly close relationship with Zigurrat Commander **Dr. Triad** and Flood Army Chief Captain **Soraishi** has sparked widespread anxiety. Eridu Capital and the Ur-Ilani Covenant fear the Admiral is becoming overly supportive of the military high command, tensing up the political atmosphere aboard *The Preserver of Life*.


### Demographics & Divine Lineages

- **Ishtar's People (Uruk Descendants)**: Descendants of ancient Uruk endowed with animal ears and tails by the goddess **Ishtar** during the Mythical Age. While originally granted purely as cosmetic "decorations", generations of adaptation transformed these appendages into heightened physical instincts (balance, directional hearing, spatial reflexes). They reproduce like baseline humans (with a 50/50 inheritance chance when interbreeding) and are fully integrated without discrimination across all factions aboard *The Preserver of Life*.

### Sustenance and Defense

The survival of the factions hinges on effective resource management and defense:

- **Sustenance**: Eridu Capital manages the distribution of the Golden Apple, a fruit gifted by the god Ea, which is humanity's sole natural source of food and hydration. Though simple to cultivate, the scarcity of land necessitates the use of Golden Serum, an injectable derivative, for efficient distribution.
- **Defense (Flood Weapons)**: For millennia, humanity defended the Ark using kinetic artillery and divine sanctuary barriers. However, as oceanic Abyssal density intensified over time, Flood Creatures mutated into hyper-regenerative Kraken and Leviathan grades. Starting ~900 years ago (~900 BP / 1100 ADV), researchers began reverse-engineering salvaged Kraken biological cores, though they did not yet understand the core's true nature or constraint logic.

### The Peril of the Flood Weapons and The Flood Army

Flood Weapons are a necessary evil, yet they pose a severe internal threat. They possess their own malevolent consciousness—officially designated by Eridu capital scientists as **Tiamat's Echo**—linked directly to the unconscious mind of Tiamat. As a compact version of a Flood Creature, the weapon's twisted constraint logic imposes a tremendous mental burden on their users, ultimately causing them to contract the Flood Disease and mutate into the very Flood Creatures they are fighting. Yet, despite demanding seemingly insane or paradoxical rules of their wielders, Flood Weapons remain strictly coherent and fair—no weapon has ever been recorded changing its requirements mid-term.

When early prototypes were first handed to the Zigurrat Society ~900 years ago (~900 BP), The Flood Army did not exist yet; the military was composed of standard adult and elderly personnel. When these adult soldiers attempted to wield the weapons, they died horrifyingly without understanding what killed them or why. 

After 400 years of dangerous trials, researchers stabilized **Constraint Logic** around 500 years ago (~500 BP / 1500 ADV). They discovered that the weapons' constraint logic strictly permits only youth aged 17 to 25 to bond with them. After confirming this through trial testing on young adults ~500 years ago, the Zigurrat Society formally established **The Flood Army** as a dedicated military branch to field these youth vanguards. Though indispensable for survival, the Flood Army receives no public recognition and its members are often viewed by the protected citizens as mere tools or even enemies.


### The Biological Terror: The Flood Disease & Threat Classifications

Beyond external monster assaults, humanity faces a silent, insidious contagion known as the **Flood Disease**. Caused by direct contact with the toxic ocean, swimming in contaminated waters, or the continuous neural burden of wielding Flood Weapons, the affliction attacks both body and mind. 

Victims pass through progressive **Stages / Phases**—beginning with unnoticeable onset (Stage/Phase 1), deteriorating into agonizing, unquenchable thirst (Stage/Phase 2), followed by a hypnotic psychic longing pulling them to gaze at the sea from the Ark's decks (Stage/Phase 3). While many succumb and throw themselves into the abyss, those who resist experience physical transformations (Stage/Phase 4) developing webbed digits and aquatic fins before fully transmuting (Stage/Phase 5) into predatory Flood Creatures. 

In Ark archives, human wielders like Soraishi and Dr. Triad are assigned **Threat Ranks** (e.g. Leviathan grade) that represent their projected hazard class and destructive scale if they were to fully transform upon reaching Stage 5 / Phase 5 terminal metamorphosis.
