# Ishtar's People (Uruk Descendants)

**Ishtar's People**, historically known as **Uruk Descendants** (and colloquially referred to as *Beastfolk*), are a prominent sub-population of humanity on Ki distinguished by physical animal appendages—most commonly animal ears and tails.

---

## 🏛️ Mythological Origin

During the Mythical Age in the ancient city of Uruk, the goddess **Ishtar** looked upon humanity and judged their baseline appearance as bland and lacking creativity. Driven by her love for aesthetics, beauty, and individuality, she bestowed "decorations" upon the citizens of Uruk in the form of expressive animal traits.

---

## ⚡ Biological Adaptation: From Aesthetics to Instinct

* **Purely Cosmetic Beginnings**: When Ishtar first granted these features, they were purely ornamental divine gifts with no altered physiology or combat function.
* **Generational Evolution**: Over centuries—and especially during the environmental stress of *The Creeping Tide*—Uruk descendants realized that having these appendages provided latent physical potential. Through generational adaptation and training:
  * **Tails** were leveraged for superior kinetic balance, sharp maneuvering, and agile acrobatics.
  * **Ears** allowed for acute directional hearing, spatial awareness, and heightened environmental reflexes.
What began as divine vanity evolved into a distinct biological advantage.

---

## 🧬 Genetics and Reproduction

* **Human Physiology**: Ishtar's People reproduce identically to baseline humans. There are no hybrid/chimera mutations; offspring inherit distinct, coherent trait sets.
* **Inheritance Probability**: When an Uruk Descendant reproduces with a baseline human, the offspring has a **50/50 chance** of inheriting Ishtar's animal traits, determined by whichever parent's dominant genetic expression prevails.

---

## 🚢 Role Aboard *The Preserver of Life* (2000 ADV)

* **Full Social Integration**: Ishtar's People are heavily embedded throughout all levels of society on *The Preserver of Life*. They experience no social discrimination or prejudice.
* **Faction Distribution**:
  * **Eridu Capital**: Active in governance, engineering, agriculture, and civilian life.
  * **Zigurrat Society**: Highly valued in defensive operations where their heightened reflexes and agility excel.
  * **The Flood Army**: Wielders among Ishtar's People utilize their sharp instincts and spatial awareness to better navigate the chaotic demands of Flood Weapons.
  * **Ur-Ilani Covenant**: Revered as living links to the ancient city of Uruk and the goddess Ishtar.
